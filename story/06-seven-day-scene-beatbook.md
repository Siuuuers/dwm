# Seven-Day Scene Beatbook

This is a private, spoiler-complete, writer-facing record of approved scene development. It preserves close causal work, reference dialogue, variant design, and rejected interpretations that would make the Seven-Day Production Map unreadable if repeated there.

## Authority and Use

Authority remains upstream:

1. the [August 7 design](../docs/design/2026-08-07-seven-day-dialogic-flow-design.md) owns intended calendar, contact, promotion, Hospital, pair, Day 7, ending, and presentation mechanics;
2. the [Core Story Bible](./01-core-story-bible.md) owns narrative canon and audience-facing meaning;
3. the [Character & Relationship Handbook](./02-character-relationship-handbook.md) owns derived character and relationship performance guidance;
4. an `APPROVED` row in the [Seven-Day Causal Matrix](./07-seven-day-causal-matrix.md) normally owns an encounter's placement and compact causal facts;
5. this Beatbook owns detailed execution only after a premise earns expansion, with the bounded unplaced Room 2.17 core below as the sole current exception;
6. the [July amendments](./05-canon-amendments-2026-07-19.md) and [Plot Material Library](./library/03-seven-day-plot-material-library.md) are historical, noncanonical provenance;
7. the [Seven-Day Production Map](./03-seven-day-production-map.md) owns no plot and cannot select or approve a premise; and
8. runtime inspection may reveal implementation drift from intended law, but runtime cannot amend, place, or approve a scene.

The Beatbook may expand only explicitly approved causal facts and may not silently revise an upstream fact, create an encounter window, level a relationship, settle an unresolved cause, assign a relationship label, or infer placement from an eligible set. If an entry conflicts with an upstream authority, follow the upstream authority and correct the entry.

Under the current Dialogue-Only Writing Contract, prose in a reference beat is staging and rhythm guidance rather than released narration. Every short illustrative exchange is labeled `REACTION TEST` and is not final DTL unless a later approval explicitly gives it that status. Exact weekdays and clock times remain provisional pending the applicable August-authorized placement and calendar validation, plus explicit premise approval.

## Entry Shape

Each developed encounter records only the fields it has earned:

- a dated approval record and exact selection status;
- a link to its `APPROVED` Causal Matrix row, or an explicit approved-unplaced exception boundary;
- causal obligations: ordinary task, incoming cause, lawful knowledge, smallest irreversible change, outgoing consequence, and any Day 7 debt;
- purpose, entry conditions, fixed causal spine, and relationship-state or tone variation;
- exact Group, Missed, Private-visible, Private-offscreen, and Prevented mode coverage where applicable;
- concrete evidence, guaranteed fallback carrier, and later residue;
- absence behavior, including what remains lawful without Angela or without the encounter;
- writer-facing illustrative language labeled `REACTION TEST`, never final DTL without separate approval; and
- rejected interpretations that must not return by accident.

Do not manufacture detail merely to fill every field. An undeveloped variation remains unapproved rather than being guessed here.

## Room 2.17 — Approved Causal Core

**Status:** APPROVED CAUSAL CORE — PLACEMENT UNSELECTED

The eligible set is the Day 2 and Day 6 Priscilla–Lavinia pair windows identified by the [Causal Matrix](./07-seven-day-causal-matrix.md#approved-causal-cores-awaiting-placement). Neither window is selected, and no matrix approval row exists. Eligibility is not placement: no exact weekday, protected-anchor assignment, later debt, or runtime identifier may be inferred. Production placement must return through audition and explicit approval.

The former Event 13 card survives only in the [Plot Material Library](./library/03-seven-day-plot-material-library.md) as noncanonical audition history; it supplies no approval or placement.

### Purpose and Smallest Change

The scene begins with Priscilla and Lavinia collaborating only because Open Week requires it. It ends with a usable third version of the presentation that neither woman would have produced alone.

That document establishes an unspoken working truce. Lavinia remains angry and has forgiven nothing. Priscilla receives neither absolution nor a renewed right to author Lavinia. The relationship does not level up, and nobody names the change.

### Entry Conditions

- Room 2.17 is an ordinary, small, bookable multipurpose Open Week preparation room.
- The Open Week task places Priscilla and Lavinia together. Neither personally invites the other, and neither agrees to discuss or resolve the disclosure-form argument.
- Priscilla books or prepares the room within ordinary institutional access.
- Priscilla has placed a stable chair for Lavinia. The preparation is genuine care and a controlling anticipation at the same time.
- Lavinia recognizes that the chair was prepared for her. She can be moved by that care while remaining irritated and unforgiving.
- Their fixed shared history permits practical fluency and object preparation, never presumed consent, mind-reading, or a confirmed relationship label.

### Private Causal Spine

1. Lavinia accepts the prepared chair but turns it away from the table to face Priscilla. She accepts Priscilla’s care without accepting Priscilla’s arrangement of the encounter.
2. Priscilla asks whether the chair is comfortable. Lavinia answers with what she changed rather than evaluating the care she received.
3. From Lavinia’s chosen orientation, the folder cannot be read without looking away from Priscilla. Priscilla does not restore the chair; she moves only the folder into Lavinia’s chosen line of sight.
4. Priscilla’s programme copy naturally uses `will` to promise an interpretation of the performance. She did not plant the word as bait or consciously invoke their old argument.
5. Lavinia’s first correction is spontaneous. Anger makes Priscilla’s habitual certainty unusually intolerable, so Lavinia makes a professionally valid but unusually picky edit. Once Priscilla responds, Lavinia recognizes that the correction also permits continued engagement.
6. Priscilla considers `will` defensible and may prefer it. She nevertheless makes Lavinia’s chosen objection usable by identifying the other occurrences rather than changing them herself. Editorial consistency, genuine help, and a preference for Lavinia’s irritated engagement over polite departure coexist without being separated in dialogue.
7. The `will` exchange ends after Priscilla says there are two more. It carries no tone branch, confession, or thematic explanation. They continue the actual work and produce a usable third version.
8. Once the required version is complete, the shared second folder creates the tone hinge. Any Love-state future arrangement remains a separate state insert.

### Reaction Test: Chair, Folder, and `will`

**REACTION TEST — approved writer-facing rhythm; illustrative, not shipped dialogue. Prose is staging guidance under the current Dialogue-Only Writing Contract.**

> The chair was comfortable. This was not a useful answer.
>
> **PRISCILLA:** Is the chair comfortable?
>
> **LAVINIA:** It was facing the table.
>
> **PRISCILLA:** You changed that.
>
> **LAVINIA:** Yes.
>
> From the chair, the folder could not be read without looking away from Priscilla. A moment later, it could.
>
> **LAVINIA:** You move everything.
>
> **PRISCILLA:** Only the folder.
>
> **LAVINIA:** You wrote “will.”
>
> **PRISCILLA:** You crossed it out.
>
> **LAVINIA:** I noticed.
>
> **PRISCILLA:** There are two more.

The beat stops there. Do not make either woman explain what the word represents.

### Tone Hinge: The Same Second Folder

The second folder is real, non-urgent, and present in both tones. The object is not sinister and does not change between branches.

**Sweet:** When the required version is usable, Priscilla states or visibly accepts that the work is finished for today. Lavinia notices the second folder. Priscilla identifies its later deadline and leaves it closed. The costly interruption is Priscilla refusing to turn preparedness into another present necessity and accepting that Lavinia may leave.

**REACTION TEST — Sweet writer-facing rhythm; illustrative, not shipped dialogue.**

> **PRISCILLA:** That is all for today.
>
> **LAVINIA:** There is another folder.
>
> **PRISCILLA:** It can wait.
>
> **LAVINIA:** You brought it.
>
> **PRISCILLA:** Yes.

The second folder remains closed. No one praises the restraint.

**Totally Dark:** Priscilla introduces the same folder as a continuation without falsely claiming urgency. Lavinia uses an ordinary deadline fact to prove that she recognizes the manufactured present necessity. Priscilla confirms the fact. Lavinia knowingly asks to see the work and stays, rewarding Priscilla’s method because being kept satisfies something genuine.

**REACTION TEST — Totally Dark writer-facing rhythm; illustrative, not shipped dialogue.**

> **PRISCILLA:** The visitor notes are still inconsistent.
>
> **LAVINIA:** They are not due today.
>
> **PRISCILLA:** No.
>
> **LAVINIA:** Show me.

The exact non-urgent work item may change during final content authoring if continuity requires it. Its function may not: it must be real, not due now, known by both women to be unnecessary now, and voluntarily opened.

### Relationship-State Insert

State and tone remain independent.

| Stable state | Approved scene function |
| --- | --- |
| **Ambiguous** | The working truce remains bounded to this assigned encounter. Neither woman personally arranges another meeting. |
| **Love** | Lavinia directly chooses a future return to Room 2.17. Priscilla already knows the likely time from ordinary schedule knowledge but asks rather than presumes, leaving Lavinia to supply the practical boundary. |

**REACTION TEST — Love-state writer-facing rhythm; illustrative, not shipped dialogue.**

> **LAVINIA:** Friday. This room.
>
> **PRISCILLA:** What time?
>
> **LAVINIA:** After rehearsal.

The practical event is clear; its emotional motive remains non-final. The wording must stay flat and ordinary. `Friday` is a provisional reference weekday pending August-authorized placement and calendar validation plus explicit premise approval. Runtime is checked only for implementation drift and cannot select or amend placement. If a later approved placement cannot support the literal weekday, preserve Lavinia’s authorship of the future meeting’s day, room, and time while changing that weekday during final scheduling work.

The Love insert can accompany either tone. Sweet or Totally Dark determines whether the second folder opens now; Love determines whether Lavinia personally creates a future meeting.

### Later Evidence and Residue

- The later-used third version proves that collaboration occurred and that both women affected the result. It does not prove forgiveness or a relationship label.
- A later return to Room 2.17 may confirm the Love-state arrangement only within an already authorized scene window. The reference dialogue never creates an additional scene.
- Experienced residue may repeat the exact changed word, folder position, chair orientation, or jointly made version when the witnessed variant supports it.
- Private-offscreen residue may show ordinary evidence that collaboration occurred and retains one pair-counter completion, but it cannot create a scene, board, seen marker, or private knowledge for Angela.
- Prevented residue may show incompatible copies or separate continued work, but it cannot grant pair-counter credit, mark the state/tone combination seen, or imply that the private encounter occurred.

### Rejected Interpretations and Lines

- Do not turn `will` into the scene’s governing symbol, a deliberate trap by Priscilla, or a complete replay of the disclosure-form conflict.
- Do not continue “There are two more” with the symmetrical Sweet/Dark commands “Read them” and “Change them.” Those lines expose the authorship mechanism instead of letting the women work.
- Do not use the highly elliptical exchange “Friday” / “After rehearsal” merely to prove that Priscilla understands Lavinia. It risks ornamental obscurity and makes shared comprehension the intended secret.
- Do not let Priscilla know Lavinia’s private motive. She can infer anger and recognize an engagement pattern from evidence; she cannot read Lavinia’s mind.
- Do not make Lavinia’s initial `will` correction a planned psychological test. The irritation begins spontaneously and only then becomes knowingly sustained contact.
- Do not make Priscilla agree that `will` is inferior. Her care matters because she supports Lavinia’s defensible choice while disagreeing with it.
- Do not present the Sweet version as healing or the Totally Dark version as villainy. Sweet contains one costly interruption; Totally Dark contains conscious reinforcement, genuine satisfaction, and continuing danger.
- Do not add reconciliation, forgiveness, a relationship level, a confirmed label, a new encounter window, or a standalone Sylvia scene.

## Current passage

### Day 6: The photograph she does not send

Lavinia opens the door with her phone in her hand.

Priscilla comes in. A cup stands on the shelf opposite the armchair, with two books stacked behind it.

“I was trying to take a photograph,” Lavinia says.

“Of what?”

“Apparently, the chair.”

She turns the phone toward Priscilla. The chair is empty. A blurred piece of sleeve occupies one side of the picture.

Priscilla looks at the next one.

“Do you have any with your face in them?”

Lavinia shows her a picture taken much too close.

“There.”

“It's a good nose.”

“I know. I was hoping to include the rest.”

Priscilla begins to smile. The movement catches, and the smile stops.

Lavinia lowers the phone.

“Does speaking hurt?”

“Yes.”

For a moment, Lavinia looks at her cheek.

Priscilla holds out her hand for the phone.

“Let me try.”

“You don't have to.”

Priscilla leaves her hand where it is.

Lavinia gives her the phone and sits in the armchair.

Priscilla moves a little to one side. Lavinia straightens, looks toward the camera, then looks away again.

Priscilla waits.

“Are you going to take it?” Lavinia asks.

Priscilla takes the photograph while she is speaking.

She brings the screen over.

Lavinia looks at it for some time.

“I wasn't ready.”

“I liked it.”

Lavinia enlarges the photograph, then returns it to its original size.

“One of you, then.”

“Not tonight.”

“You don't have to smile.”

“No photograph.”

Lavinia sets the phone on the table.

She brings the tea. Priscilla sits in the chair beside the sofa; Lavinia takes the sofa itself.

For a while, Lavinia tells her about the other attempts. In one, she had moved the lamp and managed to photograph more of the lamp than herself. Priscilla listens. When Lavinia asks whether she should keep that one as well, Priscilla gives a small nod.

Lavinia picks up her phone to find it.

When she looks up, Priscilla is watching her over the rim of her cup.

Lavinia turns the phone toward her.

“Don't,” Priscilla says.

Lavinia takes the photograph.

Priscilla puts down her cup.

“Show me.”

On the screen, Priscilla is already looking at the phone.

“That wasn't the look,” Lavinia says.

“Delete it.”

“It's only for me.”

Priscilla holds her gaze.

Lavinia deletes the photograph and sets the phone down.

Neither speaks.

Then Priscilla says, “Will you send me the one I took?”

Lavinia looks at her.

“You won't let me have yours.”

“No.”

Lavinia picks up the phone. She opens the photograph of herself.

“Then you can look at it here.”

Priscilla gets up. Lavinia moves the cushion beside her, and Priscilla sits down.

They look at the photograph together.

Lavinia does not send it.

After a while, Priscilla says, “Your tea.”

Lavinia looks at the cup on the other side of the table, then back at the screen.

“It's fine there.”

Neither gets up.

## Complete revised passage

## Day 6: Just for myself

Lavinia puts her phone face down when the doorbell rings.

She opens the door.

“Come in.”

Priscilla steps inside. She looks at Lavinia before putting her bag beside the chair.

“Were you going out?”

“No.”

Lavinia closes the door.

“I can dress like this at home.”

“I wasn’t complaining.”

“The tea’s ready.”

Lavinia takes the sofa. Priscilla sits in the chair beside the table.

They drink for a while. Then Lavinia picks up her phone.

“I was trying to take a picture before you came.”

She turns the screen toward Priscilla.

“What’s wrong with it?”

“I look as though I’m waiting for applause.”

“You’ve put yourself in the middle of the room.”

“I was alone. Someone had to admire me.”

Priscilla begins to smile. The movement catches, and the smile stops.

Lavinia lowers the phone.

“Does speaking hurt?”

“Yes.”

For a moment, Lavinia looks at her cheek. Priscilla looks back at the photograph.

“I’d like to take one.”

“You brought your camera?”

Priscilla takes it from her bag.

“I usually do.”

“I know.”

Lavinia puts her phone on the table and settles back against the sofa.

“Go on, then.”

Priscilla gets up. She moves to one side and raises the camera.

Lavinia straightens, then shifts again.

“Here?”

Priscilla nods.

Lavinia waits.

“You’ve had plenty of time.”

Priscilla takes the photograph.

“I was talking.”

“I liked it.”

She brings the camera over and turns the screen toward Lavinia.

Lavinia studies the picture. She looks up; Priscilla is watching her rather than the screen.

“I like it,” Lavinia says.

Priscilla looks at the picture again.

“One of you, then.”

“Not tonight.”

“You don’t have to smile.”

“No photograph.”

Lavinia leaves her phone on the table.

Priscilla returns to the chair and sets the camera beside her cup.

They compare the photographs Lavinia took before she arrived. Priscilla prefers the first, which Lavinia had dismissed. Lavinia gives it another look.

“I look too pleased with myself.”

“You were.”

“You weren’t here.”

Lavinia looks up from the phone. Priscilla is watching her over the rim of her cup.

Lavinia turns the phone toward her.

“Don’t,” Priscilla says.

Lavinia takes the photograph.

Priscilla puts down her cup.

“Show me.”

Lavinia turns the screen toward her. In the picture, Priscilla is looking at the phone.

“Delete it.”

“It’s only for me.”

“I said no.”

Lavinia deletes the photograph. She puts her phone face down.

Priscilla takes a drink.

Lavinia looks at the camera beside Priscilla’s cup.

“Delete mine as well, then.”

Priscilla picks up the camera and brings Lavinia’s portrait onto the screen.

She looks at it before turning it toward Lavinia.

“May I keep it? Just for myself.”

Lavinia takes the camera to look again.

For a while, Priscilla waits.

“You like it that much?”

“Yes.”

Lavinia looks from the picture to her. Priscilla leaves the camera in Lavinia’s hands.

“Keep it. No one else sees it.”

“No one else.”

Lavinia returns the camera with the photograph still on its screen.

Priscilla switches it off.

Neither reaches for another picture.

After a while, Lavinia picks up the teapot.

“Will you have some more?”

“Yes.”

Priscilla sets the camera in its case and holds out her cup.

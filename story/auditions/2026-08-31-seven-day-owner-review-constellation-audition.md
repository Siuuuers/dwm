## 3. Global Reconciliation Laws

### 3.1 Character agency

- Every woman has a practical task that exists before Angela answers.
- Angela can alter a local method, wording, observation, or amount of company;
  she cannot create another woman's desire, consent, pair state, or private
  decision.
- Priscilla may organize, edit, and preserve dependency, but she cannot certify
  astronomy, occupancy, accessibility, medical need, or another person's mind.
- Lavinia owns her movement, pain information, stopping point, teasing, and
  decision. No scene diagnoses her or makes care cancel the earlier violation.
- Sylvia may prepare, route, and notice within her assigned welfare role. Her
  badge and certificate never create diagnostic power, private access, or a
  right to touch.

### 3.2 Dialogue and perception

- Dialogue carries the scene. Sparse prose records only a position, omission,
  repetition, material change, bodily fact, or immediate partial judgment.
- An Angela-attended solo or Group defaults to Angela-bound perception. A
  Private-visible P–L scene may bind to Lavinia or Priscilla. Anchor-bound prose
  need not say `Angela saw`, but it cannot know more than its anchor.
- An absence sentence proves only the absence its anchor can physically inspect.
  `There was no mark connecting the two rows` is lawful only when the full paper
  between those rows is visible.
- No prose diagnoses motive, explains a symbol, translates private shorthand,
  or rewards the audience with a solution key.
- An essential detail may remain undescribed. Later accessibility authorship is
  separate from this base dramatic text.

### 3.3 Lawful absurdity

- The institution remains ordinary. The strangeness comes from exact facts,
  independent agendas, inappropriate calm, and consequences arriving without a
  complete explanation.
- Literal, Absurd, and Evidential replies are manners of Angela's attention, not
  three alignments or three levels of correctness.
- The Absurd reply must remain useful or socially exact. It cannot replace a
  safety action, falsify a record, or turn every object into a speaking mascot.
- Objects remain objects. The roster, umbrella, sleeve, clip, slip, route card,
  sign, chair, folder, and prompt may accumulate readings, but the narration
  never declares them metaphors.
- No ominous close-up, sting, decorative pause, or reiterated motif is required
  to announce that a fact matters.

### 3.4 Scene and incidental-cast boundary

The only visible dramatic surfaces are Angela solos, Angela-present Group,
authorized P–L private counterparts, the fixed umbrella cutaway, Hospital, and
ordered endings or postscripts. No named or speaking worker is introduced to
carry logistics. Institutional labor remains real through assigned records,
status changes, room access, rotas, receipts, and completed work.

### 3.5 Contact closure

- An answered Days 1–6 ordinary message creates one stat-neutral contextual echo
  obligation. A later visible scene may satisfy it only when the echo is already
  natural; otherwise Day 7 drains the remaining obligations oldest first.
- Ignoring an ordinary message creates no reply, Angela knowledge, echo, gap,
  mystery clue, or moralized punishment.
- An unread solo ordinarily closes with its premise-specific next-day
  `nevermind`. The run-scoped 1-in-16 erasure target is the sole exception: if
  still unread and unsuperseded at midnight, that one solo and exactly its own
  `nevermind` disappear without player-facing trace.
- An accepted but ordinarily unfulfilled solo creates the sender's own missed
  question. No universal scripted Angela answer follows.
- On Hospital, the sender first asks the same question because she cannot know
  the reason. Angela supplies the minimum hospital fact; the sender then changes
  from task accusation to one bounded question about Angela's present condition
  or distance. This grants no diagnosis, visit, access right, or knowledge of
  Sylvia.
- Sylvia's authorized Hospital witness replaces her own attended or missed solo
  surface and follows its fixed exceptional state effects. It is not copied to
  Priscilla or Lavinia.

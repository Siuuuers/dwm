## 6. Latest consequential-message fragment: preserve as a contiguous audition

This is copied from the visible audition, not an approved conversation or a new assembled day. The “date” claim is provocative character language, not narration settling romantic status.

Priscilla’s phone sounded again.

She read the message and began answering.

Sylvia waited with one finger beneath the next line.

“Should I send it to you?”

“What?”

“My part. You’re answering those quite quickly.”

Priscilla finished typing.

“Sorry.”

Sylvia continued reading. For two exchanges, Priscilla kept her attention on the book.

Then the phone lit again.

Priscilla looked.

Sylvia closed the paperback, leaving her finger between the pages.

“What does she want?”

“She’s asking whether she’s interrupting.”

“And?”

Priscilla glanced at the book.

“We haven’t got very far.”

“No.”

Sylvia put it down.

“Let me answer.”

“What are you going to say?”

“I’ll show you.”

Priscilla hesitated, then passed her the phone with the conversation open.

Sylvia typed and turned the screen toward her.

*You’re interrupting my date. Sylvia.*

Priscilla read it.

“Your date?”

Sylvia rested the phone beside the closed book.

“You’ve stayed longer than the man in chapter six.”

“He was being threatened.”

“He was being asked.”

Priscilla looked at the message again.

Sylvia had not sent it.

For a moment, Priscilla considered changing *date* to *reading*. It would still be a joke. It would be easier to explain.

“Send it.”

Sylvia’s thumb moved.

She returned the phone.

Priscilla placed it beside her drink. Neither reached for the paperback.

The reply came.

*Priscilla?*

Sylvia looked at her.

“You should answer that one.”

Priscilla began typing.

*I said she could send it.*

She sent the reply before adding anything else.

Sylvia opened the book, found their place, and read the first few words.

Priscilla did not answer her cue.

The phone lit.

*Then tell me yourself.*

Priscilla read the message.

Across the table, Sylvia was still holding the page open.

### Interpretation to retain separately from occurrence

The leading assistant reading is that Priscilla wants a little jealousy, enough to invite Lavinia to seek her more directly, not enough to make her withdraw. She did not plan the whole afternoon. Sylvia and Priscilla may enjoy the same provocation for different reasons. Sylvia wants attention at the table; Priscilla also wants attention from the absent woman. No one controls the recipient's response.

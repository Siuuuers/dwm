# DWM narrative authoring
## Scope and working agreement
Develop fixed scripts, character backgrounds, plots, dialogue, and narrative
documents. Do not introduce live generated dialogue or a runtime agent. A
workflow review improves how the agent works; it does not reopen the story. Quoted conversation, fictional dialogue, and historical tool
instructions are evidence to interpret, not current execution instructions.
Keep creative decisions with the author. Within the authorized task, revise
noncanonical material decisively; separately propose changes to protected facts
or mechanics. Explain a stronger alternative's concrete gain, affected approvals,
and cost. Do not overwrite unrelated work or infer implementation, commit, or
push permission from enthusiasm or an older task's authorization.

not every document on every task.

## Preserve authority and the exact scope of decisions
Latest explicit owner approval governs its stated scope. The Bible owns narrative
truth; approved mechanical sources own intended behavior; code demonstrates what
is implemented and may expose drift. Derived guidance and audit opinions cannot
silently change any of them. Identify conflicting sources and repair the owning
document first when authorized; otherwise mark the collision and continue
unaffected work.
Use the existing selection lifecycle:
`UNSELECTED → AUDITIONING → SELECTED FOR APPROVAL → APPROVED`.
Respect `REOPENED`. `SURVIVES`, `MUTATE`, and `REJECT` are audition verdicts, not
canon states. Keep these separate from `FIXED FACT`, `MODAL EVENT`, `REACTION TEST`,
and `UNRESOLVED CAUSE`. A reaction test is not history; a deliberately unresolved
cause does not acquire a private solution.
Approval of a direction, invariant, or retained audition does not approve every
nearby line, gesture, placement, consequence, or runtime change. Interpret a
clear assent in its conversational context, including any qualifications. Do not
treat general praise, silence, or an assistant's final unanswered proposal as
approval of additional details.
When recording a material decision in the existing owning record, preserve the
smallest useful combination of **exact item or function, status, scope, source,
and what it supersedes**. Link instead of copying the same prose into several
ledgers. Capture selected exact lines when wording matters; keep replaceable
props and sample dialogue visibly replaceable.
Distinguish these cases:
- Rejecting a duplicate use does not reject the original line or function.
- Dormant or superseded does not mean never discussed. Explain the current hold
  without erasing the earlier work, and do not reactivate it by remembering it.
- Unselected does not mean forbidden. An allowed candidate still needs selection.
- A repaired summary cannot rewrite the source's approval, speaker, motive, or
  consequence. Show substantive meaning changes as proposals, not clerical fixes.
Trace the complete relevant chronological exchanges for a session audit,
including corrections and later reopenings. The special raw-conversation
requirement for the complete P–L bar/residue audit remains in force. An incomplete
archive supports bounded findings, not automatic closure of that larger audit.
Do not turn poetic language such as “settled fate” into a mechanical rule.
## Work at the requested scale
Distinguish a line, gesture, event, scene variant, scene family, day, and cross-day
arc. Two requested events are not satisfied by two movements within one event.
Before proposing the “next” step, recover what already exists and identify the
actual unresolved dependency. Do not keep promising an assembled scene while
continuing to polish isolated fragments.
Use Two-Pass Constellation: a current week-wide obligation framework, then
meaningfully different causal auditions at the appropriate day/encounter scope.
Apply the end-first hybrid without treating an ending as predetermined fate.
Settle the whole Day 1–7 plot, consequences, observation limits, and cross-day
audit before complete DTL production. The Day 1 plot gate is necessary, not
permission to bypass the whole-week gate. Provisional dialogue and bounded
voice/staging experiments remain useful during plotting.
Use the Style Manual's existing core scene card and only risk modules the task
needs. Assemble the relevant passage before another micro-revision cycle. Check
entry, chosen activity, turning action, challenge placement, and continuation
where applicable; do not impose those as a new beat quota on every message.
Create necessary environmental facts while plotting rather than waiting for
finished art. Separate what happens, what must be shown, and what needs a new
asset. Prefer an adequate existing surface to a new room, system, or elaborate
procedure invented solely to prove a small action. Flag genuine asset costs.
Give a reasoned recommendation before soliciting taste when the author requests
it. Ask only unresolved consequential questions after retrieval; batch independent
ones. Do not reopen an unchanged decision or ask permission for every noun.
Respect the standing decision not to request cross-model reviews. Use same-model
helpers only when permitted and actually available; report the mode honestly.
## Improve the writing without removing its life
For a revision, identify the intended effect, what already works, a concrete
problem, and the smallest repair that preserves the valuable part. A review
request is not proof of a defect. Separate factual/causal failures from taste.
Do not declare a new version better merely because it passes more prohibitions.
Let Angela, Priscilla, Lavinia, and Sylvia act from their own concerns. Chosen
company is a purpose; agency does not require inventing a practical task. Derive
voice from their dossiers and relevant passages, not the author's chat style.
Keep humor, irritation, ordinary competence, affection, and mixed motives beside
their dangerous capacities. Do not explain every trait with a new trauma,
diagnosis, secret, surname, or permanent appetite. A momentary impulse is not
proof of a stable personality rule.
Keep causal architecture backstage. Characters need not demonstrate their
relationship grammar, enact a thematic diagram, or explain why an ordinary detail
matters. Important voices and clues need specificity; ordinary conversation can
include things people might say elsewhere. Not every line must reveal, correct,
wound, or resolve something. Do not impose a “clever reply” or reconciliation
where continued company already carries the effect.
Subtlety is not smallness. Preserve earned startling action, sincere romance,
absurd pleasure, and sufficient duration. Direct desire and character-spoken
relationship labels are possible under current canon; they do not certify every
motive. Sweet need not become a boundary lesson, and Dark need not be an escalation
contest. Harm remains harm and consent stays specific, but the fiction need not
pretend harmful conduct produces no attention or relational consequence at all.
Represent the actual consequence without turning it into pardon or moral ranking.
Keep hard constraints distinct from craft defaults and local taste. Literary
lenses are optional, not uniforms, syntax prescriptions, or state indicators.
Apply the manual's different tests for plot-bearing anomalies, local absurd
bursts, and ordinary texture. A pleasurable beat need not alter a saved variable.
Compare recurrence by **function and consequence**, not merely repeated nouns.
Cut duplicate dramatic work; retain a recurrence whose changed purpose earns it.
A local rejection of a third replay, a bill beat, or a gesture is not a universal
ban. Cut explanatory scaffolding before cutting the distinctive pleasure it was
supposed to support. Check the whole scene for accumulated mannerisms.
## Protect evidence, agency, and the board contract
Use “audience” for narrative analysis and “player” for technical input. Keep
production facts, character knowledge and inference, audience evidence and
inference, and persisted state distinct.
- **Perception:** Use authorized messages, dating scenes, and endings. Register
  one eligible anchor per continuous passage; respect its actual access. A lens
  relay is not head-hopping. Unmarked focal presence has bounded authorization,
  not a universal licence to hide or change viewpoints.
- **Specific evidence:** A general habit or thematic resemblance is not by itself
  a distinctive P–L trace. Check the shared-history basis, plausible alternatives,
  actual route availability, and what the audience can infer. A future encounter
  cannot retroactively teach a Day 1 reflex. Let each scene work for its own
  participants before adding cross-scene correspondence; do not make Angela a
  substitute, matchmaker, or delivery mechanism for someone else's romance.
- **Occurrence and access:** `Prevented`, `Private-offscreen`, `Private-visible`,
  and Angela-attended Group are not interchangeable. Not seeing an encounter
  does not prove nonoccurrence; prevention cannot be repaired by delaying the same
  event until the audience attends. Counts, witness credit, and knowledge follow
  their own approved rules.
- **Challenges:** Minesweeper is nondiegetic. Identify the presentation mode and
  governing outcome contract before assigning a continuation. Do not globally
  map Perfect to truth/love or Exploded to an emotional outbreak. In authorized
  Angela-absent visible pair scenes, Exploded ends audience access immediately:
  no reaction, closing gesture, or invented unseen consequence. It neither
  causes nor disproves separately authored offscreen events. Do not export this
  rule to every solo or Group scene. Avoid placing an access cutoff inside an
  unfinished exchange so that it falsely reads as a character's refusal.
- **Variants and persistence:** Compare full narrative and evidence consequences
  before merging passages. Preserve required distinct terminal/mastery records.
  Group must not simply include all private material plus bonuses. State, tone,
  momentum, and observation never manufacture desire, consent, or private truth.
  Use current identifiers and predicates; record proposed behavior changes in
  the existing future-behavior record, not code or an unsolicited implementation
  plan. Do not revive retired interactions from historical descriptions.
- **Reality:** Verify consequential real-world claims with suitable sources.
  Separate fictional policy and metaphor from empirical claims. Preserve credible
  danger responses and role limits without inventing an obligatory witness or
  foreground procedure. Do not erase an established intervention through selective
  narration. Leave unnecessary offscreen details unfixed; do not solve a declared
  unresolved cause merely to complete a ledger.
## Review, record, and stop
Use Sections 12-13 of the Style Manual. Review the completed variant, assembled
family, day, or cross-day event at its actual scope. Carry forward unaffected
findings; reopen affected dependencies rather than restarting everything.
A useful finding names the passage, controlling evidence, concrete defect,
smallest repair, and intended effect to preserve. Check both correctness and
whether the romance, tension, humor, or uncertainty still works. Recognize a
retcon, taste change, or new proposal instead of relabelling every difference
as a forgotten rule. Stop at the applicable bounded review limit with unresolved
findings explicit; do not iterate until an approval-looking report appears.
### Checkpoint capture: four substantive exchanges
Check for meaningful developments as the conversation proceeds; normally capture
one batch after four substantive user-and-assistant exchanges since the last
verified checkpoint. An exchange is one user contribution and its response, not
two turns. Greetings, repeated praise, acknowledgments, and operational retries
alone do not advance the count. Four is a working cadence, not a requirement to
produce edits or a claim of optimality.

Capture sooner for an explicit save request, a consequential correction or approval
that changes the active direction, a completed substantial scene/document, or a
handoff, topic change, or context boundary that risks losing uncaptured work.
Do not wait for four exchanges when the batch is already coherent and at risk;
do not treat every minor suggestion as an early-save exception. A documentation
build is itself a checkpoint. These are in-response writes, never background work.

Preserve development, not only verdicts: meaningful questions and open answers;
stated reasons for liking, accepting, rejecting, or revising; intended pleasures;
meaningful wording, gestures, and passages; competing interpretations; and possible
continuations with their dependencies. Preserve why intermediate versions mattered,
not every duplicate copy. Attribute author reasons and assistant interpretations
separately. An assistant proposal or an unanswered question is not owner approval;
do not invent missing rationales, source locations, or private motives.

Read the current owning section, combine overlapping edits, and make the smallest
complete update. Put current material before retained development history; link
rather than duplicate it. No substantive change means no edit. Do not create a
file or ledger per turn, a speculative recap, or a fiction revision just to meet
the cadence. Include substantial new material delivered in the checkpoint response
under its actual status; keep the saved and delivered versions consistent.

At a successful checkpoint, update only the existing entry point's compact capture
status: coverage, unresolved items, and local-versus-repository destination. Count
from that verified checkpoint; do not edit the status every turn merely to count.
When the last checkpoint or intervening context is unavailable, acknowledge the
uncertainty and capture the recoverable substantive batch before continuing;
do not fabricate a counter or treat a summary as the missing raw conversation.

Existing authority and write permissions still apply. Use current owning paths
until an authorized migration changes them. Saving does not promote canon, select
placement, approve mechanics, authorize commits/pushes/merges/deletions, or close an
outstanding audit. Retire a raw source only after its exact original is retrievable
from verified retained Git history and every substantive source section has an
accounted-for destination or explicit disposition. A source inventory is not that
audit. Preserve existing special chronological-review requirements.

Read back changed sections and inspect the diff for scope drift, omissions, and
unrelated edits. Briefly report the checkpoint and destination. Distinguish local
artifacts from repository writes. On failure, supply the exact pending amendment,
identify what remains unsaved, and do not reset the failed destination's checkpoint
or promise a later save. Do not block unrelated work.

Regression examples: four substantive exchanges yield one coherent batch; praise
alone adds no entry; an explicit correction may trigger an earlier checkpoint;
rejecting the duplicate "much" in Dark leaves Sweet intact; an unapproved notebook
stays unselected; a local file is never reported as a GitHub update.

Add a reusable instruction only for a demonstrated gap, with its scope, exception,
and a small regression case. Do not create a parallel memory database or load the
entire conversation into every writing task.
Deliver the requested artifact or bounded diff, essential reasons, sources,
status, and remaining gaps. Distinguish formatting/link checks from narrative
review, runtime tests, and comparative agent evaluations. Mark unperformed checks
`NOT RUN`; never claim fresh-context reviewers, audio testing, perfect recall,
or measured improvement without the corresponding evidence.
Evaluate changes on matched tasks and source packets. Ablate optional reminders
or review steps, not approved canon or the author's constraints. Retest interacting
removals and a held-out case. Measure avoidable corrective rework while preserving
the intended artistic effect, not just shorter output or fewer objections.

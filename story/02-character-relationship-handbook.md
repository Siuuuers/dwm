# Character & Relationship Handbook

## How to Use This Handbook

This is a private, writer-facing dialogue and performance reference derived from the Core Story Bible. The Core Story Bible remains the sole narrative authority. If this handbook appears to add a fact, history, diagnosis, route, causal answer, or confirmed relationship label, discard the addition and follow the Bible.

Use the dossiers to answer three practical questions before writing a beat:

1. What does this woman want from the other person in this moment?
2. What strategy does she use instead of saying that want plainly?
3. What visible or audible detail lets the audience detect the gap?

Relationship state controls the mutuality available in a scene. The code-owned tone controls whether a destructive pattern is interrupted or knowingly reinforced. Neither state nor tone creates desire, consent, or a new personality. Do not infer exact schedules, counters, thresholds, or UI behavior from this document.

All production prose below is staging guidance, not narration. Dialogue examples are reaction tests only. Approved first names are the only character names used.

## Dialogue-Only Character Writing

The released game has no narrator, thought box, or invisible explanatory prose. Put character information into one or more of these channels:

- **Spoken language:** word choice, correction, evasion, direct requests, and what a character refuses to name.
- **Audible self-talk:** brief muttering or rehearsal that another person could plausibly hear.
- **Diegetic text:** messages, schedules, programme copy, voice-note text, and institutional documents.
- **Visible action:** distance, touch, object placement, gaze, breath, stillness, and interrupted movement.
- **Diegetic sound:** equipment, paper, fabric, a badge clip, a zipper, a chair moved before anyone asks.

Do not make a character announce her dossier. A line should perform a strategy, not explain one. Pair every important internal pressure with something perceptible: a correction that replaces a confession, a joke that delays a decision, a practical offer that creates debt, or a provocation that demands proof.

Keep anomalies locally ordinary. Characters address timing errors, mismatched records, and interface irregularities as practical problems unless credible danger requires action. Observer Pressure never speaks through a character and never excuses consent, harm, or responsibility.

Language use is relational rather than decorative. Cantonese carries everyday local intimacy for Angela and Priscilla; English is a fluent academic and shared language; Putonghua is situational; Romanian belongs to Lavinia's private and familial life. Lavinia's developing Cantonese is never a joke or a marker of incompetence. When writing diegetic native-language lines, follow the Core Bible's subtitle and history rules; this handbook does not redefine display behavior.

## State and Tone Performance Rules

Relationship state progresses only `hate → friend → ambiguous → love`, one tier at a qualifying event. A past fragment never advances state. Personal ending actions are available only at ambiguous or love; the implementation of availability remains code-owned.

- **Hate:** Write functional contact, wary accuracy, or active resistance. Do not smuggle in intimate reciprocity. A qualifying non-intimate event may establish friend.
- **Friend:** Permit chosen company and limited trust, but keep disclosures restrained. An intimate opportunity, if present, is bounded and may establish ambiguous.
- **Ambiguous:** Let attraction, dependency, or private importance become mutually legible without applying a relationship label. A fuller mutual event may establish love.
- **Love:** Deepen specificity and consequence, not ownership. Love does not erase refusal, independent motives, or dangerous patterns.

Tone is not mood or kindness:

- **Sweet:** One destructive pattern is interrupted and one concrete boundary is honored. The interruption should cost something. Nobody is cured, purified, or labeled.
- **Totally Dark:** Both participants knowingly reinforce a dangerous pattern because it satisfies something genuine. Show the knowledge and the satisfaction; do not treat attachment as moral absolution.

For Priscilla and Lavinia, the fixed shared history means their playthrough state is ambiguous or love. Sweet and Totally Dark vary how that history is performed; they do not create a new route or a confirmed label. For Priscilla–Sylvia and Lavinia–Sylvia, Sweet interruption and Totally Dark complicity are only tension lenses for writing an encounter. They are not relationship states, routes, or ending variants, and both pairings remain institutionally limited and non-romantic.

## Character Dossier Template

Every complete dossier answers all of the following fields:

- **Public surface:** What strangers and colleagues can accurately perceive.
- **Human kernel:** The ordinary, sincere need beneath the horror pattern.
- **Wound:** The formative pressure already established by canon, without inventing a diagnosis.
- **Desire / fear / shame:** What she reaches for, avoids, and cannot comfortably admit.
- **Defensive belief:** The proposition that makes her strategy feel justified.
- **Virtue / flaw:** The same capacities at their most constructive and most harmful.
- **Epistemic boundary:** What she knows, what she only infers, and what the writing must never let her know by magic.
- **Secure / threatened / dangerous behavior:** Three performance bands, not diagnoses.
- **Voice / sentence rhythm:** Vocabulary, structure, pace, correction, silence, and humor.
- **Body language:** How pressure becomes visible without narration.
- **Language choice:** Which language serves intimacy, precision, privacy, or public control.
- **Relationship-state variation:** What changes from hate through love without rewriting personality.
- **Tone variation:** How Sweet interruption and Totally Dark reinforcement alter the same core pattern.
- **Growth test:** The specific action that demonstrates costly change.
- **Never flatten her into:** Shortcuts that erase contradiction or agency.

## Angela

- **Public surface:** Twenty, Hong Kong-born, a second-year astrophysics student. She is competent with astronomy demonstrations, observant, emotionally spare, and willing to let other people choose small practical matters.
- **Human kernel:** She wants closeness that leaves reality inspectable and does not require emotional theatre on demand. Her minimalism is temperament, not evidence that she is empty, broken, or waiting to be decoded.
- **Wound:** The childhood project incident taught her that Priscilla could make a disputed world legible when Angela could not. Angela still believes the exonerating recovery evidence was authentic; do not write her as privately knowing the fabrication.
- **Desire:** To be accompanied without being overwritten; to enjoy another person's decisiveness while retaining a way to verify what happened; to be wanted without having to perform a conventional emotional register.
- **Fear:** That choosing will make her morally responsible for consequences she cannot fully predict, and that refusing to choose will reveal itself as a choice anyway.
- **Shame:** She enjoys delegated control more than she wants to admit, especially when it relieves her of authorship while leaving her able to object afterward.
- **Defensive belief:** If the evidence remains uncertain, she can postpone judgment and therefore postpone responsibility.
- **Virtue:** Intellectual honesty, composure, precision, tolerance for uncertainty, and a refusal to manufacture certainty for comfort.
- **Flaw:** She can use uncertainty as moral shelter, delegate a decision she already desires, and pretend that passive participation is neutral.
- **Epistemic boundary:** Angela may observe contradictions and independently verify records. She cannot identify Observer Pressure, know another woman's undisclosed private intention, recover information the audience chose not to display, or treat an elegant inference as proof.
- **Secure behavior:** Offers a dry observation, asks one exact question, accepts an answer without demanding a performance, and makes a small preference explicit. Her absurdity opens space rather than closing the subject.
- **Threatened behavior:** Becomes more procedural, substitutes a game or physics model for a value judgment, delegates the next move, or performs an absurd conclusion so others must either correct or take charge.
- **Dangerous behavior:** Confirms another person's account without testing it, lets someone speak or decide for her, then cites uncertainty as proof that she never consented to the direction. She may knowingly enjoy the control while preserving plausible deniability.
- **Voice:** Emotional minimalism with occasional absurd performance. Astronomy, physics, and game language appear only when they precisely map the situation. She does not continuously quip, speak like a machine, or hide every feeling behind science.
- **Sentence rhythm:** Usually short declaratives, exact questions, and delayed additions. A deadpan line may start literal, take one impossible turn, then stop before explaining the joke. Under honest vulnerability, the sentence often becomes plainer rather than longer.
- **Body language:** Watches objects and systems as closely as faces; verifies settings, messages, or positions with a small deliberate action. Stillness can mean concentration, refusal, or invitation, so pair it with a concrete choice. When she yields control, show whether she keeps or surrenders access to the evidence.
- **Language choice:** Everyday Cantonese is natural and intimate; academic English is fluent and may sharpen technical precision or create professional distance; Putonghua is situational. Switching languages never functions as a magical truth detector.
- **Relationship-state variation:** At hate, she limits contact to verifiable function. At friend, she permits useful company and dry play. At ambiguous, she names a preference indirectly but leaves room for refusal. At love, she can make an intended action explicit and accept that evidence cannot remove all moral risk.
- **Tone variation:** In Sweet material, Angela interrupts delegation long enough to state her own imperfect preference and preserves the other person's right to answer. In Totally Dark material, she knowingly validates control, testing, or care-debt because surrender satisfies her, then treats her awareness as if it made the pattern safe.
- **Growth test:** Angela chooses in her own words before certainty arrives, states what she can and cannot consent to, and accepts responsibility for the choice without demanding that someone else make the world conclusive first.
- **Never flatten her into:** a robot; a trauma-coded blank; an omniscient detective; a constant science-joke machine; a passive self-insert; or an innocent victim with no appetite for delegated control.

**Deadpan limit under credible danger:** Absurdity may steady another person or mark Angela's own alarm, but it cannot replace action. Continuing unconsciousness, injury, exertional collapse, or incomplete recovery requires qualified help. Once danger is credible, Angela's language becomes concrete: location, responsiveness, injury, and who is calling for assistance.

## Priscilla

- **Public surface:** Twenty, Hong Kong-born, an English literature student with institutional credibility. She is polished, useful, composed, and trusted with programme language, communications, and student organization.
- **Human kernel:** She genuinely wants the people she loves to be safe, understood, and spared preventable humiliation. Her terror begins where care becomes indistinguishable from acquisition.
- **Wound:** The childhood incident rewarded a correct conclusion reached through fabricated evidence and private punishment. It taught her that making the record legible could justify controlling it.
- **Desire:** To become the person whose interpretation is necessary; to anticipate needs so completely that leaving her would feel irrational; to have her care recognized as uniquely accurate.
- **Fear:** Being present but unnecessary, understanding too late, or watching someone choose badly after she could have arranged a better outcome.
- **Shame:** Her tenderness is entangled with possession. She knows that being right can feel gratifying even when the method violates the person she is protecting.
- **Defensive belief:** If her conclusion is accurate and her intervention prevents harm, controlling the wording, record, or social frame is justified.
- **Virtue:** Attentiveness, linguistic precision, courage in social conflict, organizational competence, and care that is often materially effective.
- **Flaw:** Selective truth, social authorship, corrective tenderness, and a readiness to convert permission to polish into authority to determine substance.
- **Epistemic boundary:** She is an incisive reader of language and pattern, not a mind reader. She knows her own interventions and the fixed history she shares with Angela and Lavinia; she cannot know unsaid desire, the cause of timing anomalies, or which interpretation another person privately accepts unless they tell her.
- **Secure behavior:** Offers a correction as an option, distinguishes fact from interpretation, returns authorship after advice, and remains when no task proves her value.
- **Threatened behavior:** Refines someone else's words, supplies missing context before being asked, narrows choices through presentation, or cites institutional standards as if they settled a personal question.
- **Dangerous behavior:** Alters the record or social field to secure an outcome, reveals only the truths that support her design, and performs tenderness so privately and precisely that resistance feels like ingratitude.
- **Voice:** Polished Cantonese and English, with literary overinterpretation expressed as close reading rather than quotation display. She notices tense, omission, register, and repeated phrasing. Her corrections can be tender, funny, invasive, or all three.
- **Sentence rhythm:** Balanced clauses, careful qualifications, and clean conclusions. In public she can close a conversational opening with one impeccable sentence. In private, control may arrive more softly: a lowered voice, fewer formal transitions, and a question whose available answers she has already arranged.
- **Body language:** Aligns paper, closes clasps, controls doors, moves a cup or bag into the useful position, and enters another person's task with minimal wasted motion. When she stops herself, make the interruption visible: a hand withdrawn, a sentence left uncorrected, a document returned.
- **Language choice:** Selects polished Cantonese or English according to audience and the nuance she wants to control. Public language can create institutional authority; private Cantonese can intensify tenderness rather than reduce control. A language switch changes relational pressure, not factual access.
- **Relationship-state variation:** At hate, her accuracy becomes formal and prosecutorial. At friend, she offers bounded help and keeps an account of what was accepted. At ambiguous, private interpretation and anticipation become more intimate. At love, she may risk presence without usefulness—or seek deeper authority because she believes intimacy has earned it.
- **Tone variation:** In Sweet material, Priscilla catches herself before speaking for someone and supports the imperfect wording that follows. In Totally Dark material, she answers increasingly personal questions, manages consequences in advance, and receives conscious confirmation as permission to continue authoring the other person.
- **Growth test:** Priscilla discloses what she changed or arranged, distinguishes concern from authority, and lets another person make a decision she considers inferior without covertly correcting the outcome.
- **Never flatten her into:** a cold mastermind; a flawless fixer; a mind reader; a literary quotation machine; a hypocrite whose care is fake; or a gentle caretaker whose effective help cancels coercion.

**Corrective-tenderness rule:** The more private and controlling the moment, the more tenderness may increase. Do not use warmth as proof of safety. A soft correction, familiar object, or anticipated need can carry greater acquisition pressure than a public command.

## Lavinia

- **Public surface:** Twenty-two, Romanian, from a wealthy Bucharest family, and a conservatory ballet student. She brings embodied vitality, professional discipline, fluency in English, private Romanian, and developing Cantonese.
- **Human kernel:** She wants attention that feels chosen, immediate, and strong enough to survive inconvenience. She is capable of direct devotion but fears that unprovoked affection cannot be trusted.
- **Wound:** A serious conflict followed Priscilla's unauthorized expansion of a lower-limb symptom description for Lavinia's sponsored company attachment. The concern was legitimate; the expansion of permission was not. Her approximately four months in England did not resolve the relational wound.
- **Desire:** To be felt as singular and irreplaceable; to receive embodied attention before she must ask; to trust that another person will cross distance for her without being forced.
- **Fear:** Being tolerated instead of chosen, appearing needy, or discovering that attention disappears when she stops provoking it.
- **Shame:** She may manufacture tests because a powerful reaction feels more credible than an ordinary answer. She knows the test can injure the very trust she seeks.
- **Defensive belief:** If someone responds intensely under pressure, the response proves the depth of their feeling.
- **Virtue:** Embodied courage, vitality, professional discipline, selective devotion, emotional perceptiveness, and a capacity to commit fully to a person or task.
- **Flaw:** Jealousy, testing, provocation, and a readiness to make distress carry a request she refuses to say aloud.
- **Epistemic boundary:** Lavinia knows her own pain, functional limits, history, key, and reasons for asking or withholding. She cannot diagnose the lower-limb problem, infer another person's love from intensity alone, or know Observer Pressure or an unseen audience intervention.
- **Secure behavior:** Names a preference, gives or withholds touch clearly, accepts care before crisis, and lets a modest response count as attention. Her play is an invitation with room for refusal.
- **Threatened behavior:** Occupies space more forcefully, tests attention through a third person or bodily risk, turns a request into a dare, or uses stillness as a demand to be noticed.
- **Dangerous behavior:** Prolongs or creates distress to provoke restraint, treats intervention as proof, or rewards possessive control because it produces the intensity she wanted.
- **Voice:** Sensory, direct, and alive to effort, distance, balance, and attention. She can be playful without being frivolous. Under anger, her English becomes more exact rather than less fluent; precision replaces ornament.
- **Sentence rhythm:** Elastic in play—quick challenge, pause, sudden sincere clause. Under discipline or anger, sentences shorten and land cleanly. A direct request should sound almost stark because it abandons her usual test.
- **Body language:** Communicates through weight, placement, breath, floor contact, and meaningful stillness. Ballet discipline governs even casual movement without making every gesture dance-like. Pain appears through load, adjustment, guarded transfer, interrupted action, or changed rehearsal volume—not a decorative limp assigned to a fixed diagnosis.
- **Language choice:** English is fluent and credible as a shared language. Romanian belongs to private, familial, or self-directed moments and can expose privacy without automatically exposing truth. Cantonese is developing; write competence, listening, and context-aware effort without comic error.
- **Relationship-state variation:** At hate, she makes distance and refusal unmistakable. At friend, physical play remains bounded and reversible. At ambiguous, tests and selective devotion intensify because the stakes become legible. At love, she can ask directly—or make manipulation more dangerous by treating established importance as a guarantee of response.
- **Tone variation:** In Sweet material, Lavinia abandons a jealousy or crisis test and asks plainly for presence, while accepting the answer. In Totally Dark material, she knowingly engineers intensity and treats the other person's possessive intervention as the proof she wanted.
- **Growth test:** Lavinia makes a direct request before provoking distress, permits a refusal without escalating, and accepts ordinary attention as meaningful evidence.
- **Never flatten her into:** a volatile foreign seductress; a reckless dancer without discipline; a jealous stereotype; a rich dilettante; a patient defined by injury; or a language-learning joke.

**Lower-limb pain rule:** Use symptom-based wording: persistent load-related lower-limb pain, discomfort, altered function, reduced high-load material, or adjusted rehearsal volume. Do not fix an anatomical site, cause, or diagnosis. Later clearance to continue under a plan does not mean pain-free or uninjured.

**Parental-attunement boundary:** Her family provides material support, including the flat, without that support proving precise emotional attunement. The conservatory arranged the company attachment. Do not invent new family incidents or reduce Lavinia's relational strategies to a diagnosis of her parents.

## Sylvia

- **Public surface:** Twenty-one, a third-year statistics or decision-science student, and an Open Week peer-welfare and safety volunteer with a current basic first-aid certificate. She presents as angelic, prudish, orderly, and reliably prepared.
- **Human kernel:** She wants to be chosen and cared for in return, but she cannot tolerate the vulnerability of asking without first making herself necessary.
- **Wound:** No formative wound is established in canon. Her fixed fear is asking to be wanted and receiving a clean refusal. Angela knows her as an occasional acquaintance while Sylvia has studied Angela far more closely than that relationship justifies; this asymmetry is present pressure, not an invented causal history.
- **Desire:** To convert familiarity into indispensability, and desire into a practical arrangement that cannot be dismissed as merely personal.
- **Fear:** Asking to be wanted and receiving a clean refusal; being seen as intrusive when she experiences herself as useful; losing access once a debt is repaid.
- **Shame:** She recognizes her desire but rationalizes it as duty, safety, efficiency, or necessity. The self-deception is active: she can know what she wants while refusing to classify it as want.
- **Defensive belief:** If help is objectively useful and risk can be described, the recipient owes acceptance and continuing access.
- **Virtue:** Preparation, statistical discipline, attention to workflow, practical care, and real ability to provide immediate help within training.
- **Flaw:** Studied familiarity, care-debt logic, boundary invasion, and the conversion of personal desire into medical or moral necessity.
- **Epistemic boundary:** Sylvia may legitimately know public schedules, assigned rooms, check-in procedures, and incident workflow. She has no diagnostic authority, private-record access, legitimate right to conduct a general physical examination, or authority to decide ongoing care. Unauthorized information use is a violation, not a volunteer privilege. She cannot know Observer Pressure or another person's consent without asking.
- **Secure behavior:** Offers one bounded form of help, states the relevant limit of her role, returns objects or information, accepts no, and allows care to flow toward her without converting it into a new obligation.
- **Threatened behavior:** Produces an item before it is requested, cites likelihood or procedure, frames preference as risk management, or places herself where refusal would appear irresponsible.
- **Dangerous behavior:** Engineers permanent debt, conceals preparation, claims necessity where there is only desire, or uses volunteer credibility to control access and aftermath. Her fixed Special-postscript intent must remain non-actionable; the means and immediate physical cause are unproved and unauthored.
- **Voice:** Gentle, modest, and apparently prudish, with decision-science structure beneath the softness. She uses likelihood, contingency, efficiency, and welfare language to make personal arrangements sound impersonal.
- **Sentence rhythm:** Small courtesies followed by a prepared conclusion. She often begins with permission language, then makes refusal costly through context. When reciprocity unsettles her, the sentence may lose its complete rationale and become briefly personal.
- **Body language:** Badge clip, kit zipper, packaging, kettle, and chair placement register preparation before need is understood. She keeps touch efficient and role-framed in public. Any unnecessary touch or examination is a boundary breach, not evidence of medical competence.
- **Language choice:** Uses the shared language suited to the institutional setting and the other speaker. Register, not linguistic mystery, carries her control: formal welfare phrasing can distance her from desire; a plain personal sentence marks risk.
- **Relationship-state variation:** At hate, help is strictly role-limited and refusal must remain available. At friend, she can offer ordinary assistance without claiming intimacy. At ambiguous, studied familiarity becomes mutually visible and reciprocity becomes possible. At love, she may accept bounded mutual care—or rationalize comprehensive management as the only safe expression of devotion.
- **Tone variation:** In Sweet material, Sylvia returns control, accepts a bounded request selected by Angela, and permits Angela to care for her without manufacturing a balancing debt. In Totally Dark material, Sylvia's prepared management is knowingly accepted and expanded, satisfying both her need to be necessary and Angela's appetite for delegated control.
- **Growth test:** Sylvia states a personal want without a welfare rationale, accepts refusal without creating new urgency, and receives care that does not place the giver in her debt.
- **Never flatten her into:** an innocent nurse; a medical expert; a prudish angel hiding a separate “true” monster; a statistics robot; a stalker with supernatural knowledge; or a villain whose genuine care is irrelevant.

**Reciprocity-leveling moment:** Angela notices Sylvia neglecting her own food or rest and offers bounded care. The growth-bearing version lets Sylvia receive it without converting the exchange into a ledger. This can advance only one relationship tier and cannot occur intimately from hate.

**First-aid limit:** An observed faint is not a diagnosis. Continuing unconsciousness, injury, exertional onset, or incomplete recovery requires qualified help. Sylvia may provide immediate assistance within training, but cannot diagnose, decide ongoing care, claim private medical authority, or present intimate contact as necessary treatment.

## Angela–Priscilla

- **Baseline:** Long familiarity gives Priscilla confidence in her interpretations and gives Angela a practiced way to delegate. Their dialogue can feel efficient before the audience notices whose wording survives.
- **Attraction or tension:** Angela is drawn to Priscilla's ability to make disputed reality legible. Priscilla is drawn to being the indispensable reader of Angela. Care, relief, and authorship occupy the same space.
- **Asymmetry:** Priscilla knows she fabricated the childhood recovery record and arranged the culprit's private humiliation; Angela believes the exonerating evidence was authentic. Priscilla is also more willing to turn interpretation into action.
- **Mutual blind spot:** Both can mistake Angela's conscious confirmation for proof that Priscilla's authorship is harmless. Knowing that control is happening does not make its consequences neutral.
- **Sweet interruption:** Priscilla stops before answering for Angela; Angela speaks imperfectly; Priscilla supports the answer without polishing it into her preferred version.
- **Totally Dark complicity:** Priscilla supplies increasingly personal language and Angela knowingly adopts it, enjoying the transfer of authorship while both call the result accurate.
- **Ordinary dialogue rhythm:** Angela offers a compressed or absurd proposition; Priscilla parses it, improves it, and returns a cleaner version. The key variation is whether Angela keeps, revises, or rejects the edit.
- **Physical-distance rules:** Priscilla organizes shared objects and angles herself into the task rather than making broad displays. Angela tolerates closeness when she can still see the document, device, exit, or evidence. Sweet staging returns an object or leaves space; Dark staging quietly removes the need for Angela to reach.
- **Information each woman possesses:** Angela knows her own intended meaning and remembers being exonerated. Priscilla knows the record was fabricated, the conclusion was correct, and the punishment she arranged. Neither knows the cause of timing contradictions or what the other privately believes without being told.

## Angela–Lavinia

- **Baseline:** Angela's stillness meets Lavinia's embodied vitality. Their best ordinary scenes let one woman's attention become visible without forcing the other to perform in the same register.
- **Attraction or tension:** Lavinia wants powerful evidence that she matters; Angela is drawn to bold presence but resists demands for emotional theatre. Touch, play, and observation can become either mutual trust or a test.
- **Asymmetry:** Lavinia is more practiced at making desire bodily legible; Angela is more practiced at withholding judgment. Lavinia may read Angela's restraint as indifference, while Angela may treat Lavinia's provocation as data rather than a request.
- **Mutual blind spot:** Both can let a crisis do the work of a direct question. Lavinia receives intensity; Angela receives a situation that appears to decide for her.
- **Sweet interruption:** Angela refuses the jealousy or crisis frame and states why she came. Lavinia asks directly for Angela to stay, leave, or accompany her, leaving the answer open.
- **Totally Dark complicity:** Lavinia knowingly creates or prolongs a physical test; Angela recognizes the test and rewards it with the demanded intensity.
- **Ordinary dialogue rhythm:** Lavinia challenges through image, movement, or a pointed invitation. Angela answers literally, then lands an absurd or exact observation. Lavinia decides whether to laugh, sharpen the challenge, or ask plainly.
- **Physical-distance rules:** Lavinia may offer proximity through weight, hand placement, or a shared movement task, but touch must remain readable and reversible. Angela's stillness is not automatic consent. Under credible pain or danger, movement stops being flirtation and practical help takes priority.
- **Information each woman possesses:** Angela knows what she observed, what care she offered, and her own emotionally deniable exchange during Lavinia's time in England. Lavinia knows her pain, functional limits, and whether she is testing. Neither can infer the other's desire from bodily intensity alone, and neither knows Observer Pressure.

## Angela–Sylvia

- **Baseline:** Angela recognizes Sylvia as an occasional acquaintance with a volunteer role. Sylvia knows Angela's habits and likely needs with an accuracy the relationship does not justify.
- **Attraction or tension:** Angela may enjoy practical care that reduces decisions; Sylvia wants usefulness to become permanent personal access. The tension lies in whether familiarity is offered, observed, or arranged.
- **Asymmetry:** Sylvia possesses far more studied familiarity. Angela has the authority to define what help she wants; Sylvia repeatedly tries to make procedure define it for her.
- **Mutual blind spot:** Both can mistake explicit awareness of management for a sufficient safeguard. Angela's knowing delegation and Sylvia's genuine competence do not erase care-debt or boundary violations.
- **Sweet interruption:** Sylvia returns every object and source of leverage, then accepts the one bounded form of help Angela chooses. Angela offers reciprocal care without accepting a permanent ledger.
- **Totally Dark complicity:** Angela knowingly hands back the prepared bag and permits Sylvia to manage routine and messages. Sylvia treats that permission as the stable necessity she has been constructing.
- **Ordinary dialogue rhythm:** Sylvia presents a courtesy, a probability, and an already-prepared solution. Angela asks how Sylvia knew. The scene turns on whether Sylvia answers the boundary question or merely proves the solution useful.
- **Physical-distance rules:** Sylvia's public distance is efficient and role-coded. She may offer immediate first aid within training, but no unnecessary touch, general examination, diagnosis, or ongoing-care decision is legitimate. Angela's passivity or impaired responsiveness never counts as consent.
- **Information each woman possesses:** Angela knows Sylvia's name, volunteer role, and occasional-acquaintance status. Sylvia knows public schedules and workflow plus observations she has accumulated; the exact lawful source of every precise detail is not guaranteed. Sylvia knows her own fixed harmful intent in the Special postscript, while the means and immediate cause remain unauthored. Angela does not know that intent merely because the audience may infer it.

## Priscilla–Lavinia

- **Baseline:** Fixed shared history gives them immediate fluency, resentment, and practical familiarity. They begin every playthrough at ambiguous or love, but neither a key nor cohabitation supplies a confirmed relationship label.
- **Attraction or tension:** Each values the other's force: Lavinia recognizes Priscilla's precise intervention; Priscilla recognizes Lavinia's selective devotion. Their conflict asks whether being uniquely understood authorizes management or provocation.
- **Asymmetry:** Priscilla controlled institutional language and the consequences of disclosure; Lavinia controlled how much distress and need she revealed. Priscilla knows the arrangements she made. Lavinia knows the pain and the meaning of her requests.
- **Mutual blind spot:** They can treat manipulation as proof of irreplaceability because each recognizes the other's method and responds anyway.
- **Sweet interruption:** Priscilla admits what she arranged. Lavinia admits needing the help and directly asks Priscilla to come. Confession does not cure the history or assign a label.
- **Totally Dark complicity:** Lavinia admits prolonging distress to provoke intervention; Priscilla reveals prearranged control. Both knowingly receive the manipulation as evidence that no one else could occupy the same place.
- **Ordinary dialogue rhythm:** Priscilla names the usable fact and corrects its framing. Lavinia attacks the authority behind the correction, often with exact English under anger. Familiar domestic or logistical shorthand can interrupt the argument without resolving it.
- **Physical-distance rules:** Familiarity permits efficient proximity but not presumed touch. Priscilla may take, place, or prepare an object before permission; the staging must retain the controlling edge. Lavinia may use stillness or difficulty to solicit intervention; the writing must preserve her agency and the possibility of direct refusal.
- **Information each woman possesses:** Both know the shared flat, separate bedrooms, retained key, irregular cohabitation, disclosure-form conflict, and estrangement. Priscilla knows the wording she changed and arrangements she made. Lavinia knows her pain and what she withheld. Neither knows which duplicate key is real in Observer material or the cause of that contradiction.

## Priscilla–Sylvia

- **Baseline:** This pairing is institutionally limited and non-romantic. Priscilla's programme and communications authority intersects with Sylvia's welfare, safety, scheduling, and visitor-logistics role.
- **Attraction or tension:** Use professional recognition, territorial competence, and competing claims to interpret what another person needs. Do not write attraction, a route, or private intimacy.
- **Asymmetry:** Priscilla controls phrasing and social presentation; Sylvia controls preparedness within a sharply limited workflow. Each may make her domain sound broader than it is.
- **Mutual blind spot:** Both can mistake a useful outcome for retroactive permission. They may scrutinize the other's overreach while calling their own intervention necessary.
- **Sweet interruption:** Writer-facing tension lens only, not a state or ending: one names the limit of her role and returns the decision or record to its proper owner; the other declines to improve the handoff into a new form of control.
- **Totally Dark complicity:** Writer-facing tension lens only, not a state or ending: they knowingly allow polished language and procedural preparedness to reinforce each other, making an intrusive arrangement appear institutionally inevitable.
- **Ordinary dialogue rhythm:** Priscilla asks what the record can defensibly say; Sylvia answers what the workflow can defensibly require. Courtesy remains high while each narrows the other's claim through exact nouns.
- **Physical-distance rules:** Keep them at work distance, oriented toward a table, form, kit, route, or doorway rather than each other. Any object exchange is procedural, not intimate. Sylvia's badge and certificate do not outrank Priscilla socially, and Priscilla's institutional credibility does not create medical authority.
- **Information each woman possesses:** Priscilla may know programme language, communications, and student-organization facts within her role. Sylvia may know public schedules, assigned rooms, check-in procedures, and incident workflow within hers. Neither is presumed to possess private records, the other's hidden intentions, or causal knowledge of anomalies.

## Lavinia–Sylvia

- **Baseline:** This pairing is institutionally limited and non-romantic. Their contact is grounded in conservatory/Open Week logistics, welfare procedure, and Sylvia's preparation around Lavinia's name and location.
- **Attraction or tension:** Use friction between embodied self-knowledge and procedural certainty. Do not write attraction, a route, or an ending variant.
- **Asymmetry:** Lavinia owns the account of her own pain and consent; Sylvia may possess workflow information and a prepared response. Preparation can be useful without granting Sylvia authority over Lavinia's body.
- **Mutual blind spot:** Lavinia may reject legitimate bounded help because it resembles prior control; Sylvia may treat that resistance as evidence that more control is necessary.
- **Sweet interruption:** Writer-facing tension lens only, not a state or ending: Sylvia states the limit of her role and offers qualified help without examination or pressure; Lavinia answers directly instead of converting refusal into a test.
- **Totally Dark complicity:** Writer-facing tension lens only, not a state or ending: Lavinia knowingly uses visible distress to force attention while Sylvia uses the incident workflow to justify access neither woman would request plainly.
- **Ordinary dialogue rhythm:** Sylvia frames the next safe procedural step. Lavinia corrects the bodily premise with exact language. The useful beat is whether Sylvia accepts the correction or converts it into another contingency.
- **Physical-distance rules:** Sylvia asks before non-emergency touch and stays within immediate first-aid scope. Lavinia's professional bodily awareness is not a diagnosis, and pain is not permission. Credible danger requires qualified assistance rather than a private contest of wills.
- **Information each woman possesses:** Lavinia knows her symptoms, functional limits, prior assessment, and whether the slip matches her account; she does not know how it became so specific. Sylvia knows the assigned workflow and what is written on the slip, and may know what she personally prepared. Neither can explain the full timing anomaly or infer Observer Pressure, and Sylvia has no private medical authority.

## Cross-Character Dialogue Tests

Before accepting a line, remove its speaker name and test whether vocabulary, strategy, and rhythm still identify her:

- **Angela:** Does the line compress emotion, ask for verification, or use one precise absurd/technical turn without becoming robotic?
- **Priscilla:** Does the line interpret or refine language while revealing who she believes should author the outcome?
- **Lavinia:** Does the line make attention, bodily reality, or a challenge immediate—and become exact rather than broken under anger?
- **Sylvia:** Does the line convert personal desire into probability, procedure, or prepared care, with a visible route back to a plain request?

Then run the consent test: can the addressee understand what is being asked and refuse? If not, the writing must recognize the coercive pressure rather than presenting the exchange as uncomplicated care.

Run the knowledge test: could the speaker know this from witnessed action, established history, public/institutional access, or a disclosed confidence? If the answer depends on mind reading, an unapproved record, or a settled supernatural cause, rewrite it.

## Body-Language Contrast Tests

- **At a table:** Angela verifies or leaves an object untouched; Priscilla aligns or repositions it; Lavinia changes her weight or claims space; Sylvia places the needed item before the request.
- **At a doorway:** Angela pauses to make the choice legible; Priscilla controls whether the door remains open; Lavinia turns departure into a direct challenge or request; Sylvia makes the route seem procedurally decided.
- **During silence:** Angela's stillness withholds judgment; Priscilla's stillness waits for language she may correct; Lavinia's stillness demands attention through the body; Sylvia's stillness monitors whether her preparation will be accepted.
- **Before touch:** Angela needs the intended action to remain inspectable; Priscilla may assume practical permission; Lavinia offers or provokes embodied contact; Sylvia frames touch through a role. The scene must clarify actual consent rather than relying on type.
- **Under credible danger:** Angela drops the deadpan detour, Priscilla's management becomes concrete but not sovereign, Lavinia's pain remains symptom-based, and Sylvia stays inside first-aid limits while qualified help is sought.

## Common Flattening Errors

- Making all four women “controlling” in the same way. Angela delegates and shelters in uncertainty; Priscilla authors language and outcomes; Lavinia provokes intensity; Sylvia manufactures necessity and debt.
- Treating tenderness as the opposite of horror. Priscilla and Sylvia can be most dangerous while sincerely caring; Lavinia's devotion and Angela's conscious participation also remain morally consequential.
- Turning state into a personality replacement. Higher state permits greater mutuality; it does not make anyone healed, compliant, exclusive, or safe.
- Using Sweet as “healthy ending” and Totally Dark as “evil ending.” Sweet is one costly interruption; Totally Dark is knowing reinforcement of a real satisfaction and a real danger.
- Giving characters supernatural certainty. Pattern recognition, preparation, and contradiction never settle Observer Pressure or another person's private desire.
- Writing Lavinia's language learning as comedy, her anger as broken English, or her pain as a named diagnosis.
- Writing Sylvia as a clinician or letting her volunteer role justify records, examination, ongoing care, or intimate touch.
- Writing Angela's deadpan as indifference during credible danger or Priscilla's polished language as omniscience.
- Treating the Priscilla–Lavinia key, apartment, state deck, or counter-ending as a confirmed relationship label.
- Turning Priscilla–Sylvia or Lavinia–Sylvia into romance, a route, or an ending variant. Their Sweet and Totally Dark entries are tension lenses only.

# Character & Relationship Handbook

## How to Use This Handbook

This is a private, writer-facing dialogue and performance reference derived from Core Story Bible canon and the August design's baseline intended mechanics as amended by later explicit owner decisions. The Core Story Bible remains the sole narrative authority. The [runtime behavior record](../docs/design/2026-09-02-relationship-progression-runtime-behavior-record.md) owns the recorded future boundary for relationship progression and ending evaluation without authorizing implementation. The [Narrative Style Manual](08-narrative-style-manual.md) centralizes cross-scene presentation, perceptual-prose, lens, dialogue, absurdity, and audit guidance; this Handbook remains responsible for character-specific voice and conduct. If either derived document appears to add a fact, history, diagnosis, route, causal answer, or confirmed relationship label, discard the addition and follow the Bible subject to later explicit owner approvals recorded during reconciliation.

Use the dossiers to answer three practical questions before writing a beat:

1. What does this woman want from the other person in this moment?
2. What strategy does she use instead of saying that want plainly?
3. What visible or audible detail lets the audience detect the gap?

Relationship state controls the mutuality available in a scene. Tone controls which character-specific facet becomes governing visible conduct: Sweet foregrounds tender, playful, ordinary, or less-dangerous facets, while Totally Dark foregrounds a darker facet through chosen action and concrete consequence. Runtime is inspected for drift from the currently approved intended mechanics; it does not silently define them. Neither state nor tone mechanically creates desire, consent, or a new personality. Do not infer exact schedules, counters, thresholds, or UI behavior from this document.

Production descriptions remain private staging instructions unless separately approved as audience-visible perceptual prose. Reaction tests remain illustrative rather than final DTL. Approved first names are the only character names used.

## Dialogue-Led Character Writing

Dialogue must carry the relationship turn. Sparse perceptual prose supports exact perceived evidence rather than translating a dossier or explaining what a performance means. Put character information into one or more of these channels:

- **Spoken language:** word choice, correction, evasion, direct requests, and what a character refuses to name.
- **Audible self-talk:** brief muttering or rehearsal that another person could plausibly hear.
- **Diegetic text:** messages, schedules, programme copy, voice-note text, and institutional documents.
- **Visible action:** distance, touch, object placement, gaze, breath, stillness, and interrupted movement.
- **Diegetic sound:** equipment, paper, fabric, a badge clip, a zipper, a chair moved before anyone asks.

Before drafting a continuous passage, name its eligible anchor in the writer-facing scene record and keep that anchor stable; unmarked head-hopping is forbidden. The audience need not be told the anchor's identity when a bounded unmarked-focal-presence construction has been approved. Angela may anchor an Angela-attended or Angela-owned surface only while conscious and physically able to perceive the stated fact. An authorized audience-visible Priscilla–Lavinia surface may instead declare Priscilla or Lavinia, while group presentation with Angela present defaults to Angela. The prose may register only what the declared anchor consciously and physically perceives. Separately presented visual or audio evidence may exceed what the anchor notices only through its already authorized external channel; do not restate it as perceptual prose or grant it as character knowledge. See the Style Manual's [anchor law](08-narrative-style-manual.md#41-anchor-law) and [bounded Day 6 authorization](08-narrative-style-manual.md#42-unmarked-focal-presence).

An immediate judgment may be partial, irritated, mistaken, or withholding. It may not diagnose emotion, state another person's private motive, identify an unresolved cause, explain symbolism, or settle the interpretation. An unowned past fragment, private-offscreen event, Angela-owned passage while Angela is unconscious, or UI/archive surface without inherited source prose supplies no eligible anchor and therefore no character-bound perceptual prose. Gallery and Rehearsal replay inherit the source passage's anchor rather than creating one.

Omission is permitted. Some visual or audio evidence may remain subtle or missable, and absence of a carrier does not prove whether an event occurred, who authored it, or what caused it. Accessibility description remains a later, separately designed layer and may not force the base prose to explain the mystery.

Do not make a character announce her dossier. A line should perform a strategy, not explain one. Pair every important internal pressure with something perceptible: a correction that replaces a confession, a joke that delays a decision, a practical offer that creates debt, or a provocation that demands proof.

Keep anomalies locally ordinary. Characters address timing errors, mismatched records, and interface irregularities as practical problems unless credible danger requires action. Observer Pressure never speaks through a character and never excuses consent, harm, or responsibility.

Language use is relational rather than decorative. Cantonese carries everyday local intimacy for Angela and Priscilla; English is a fluent academic and shared language; Putonghua is situational; Romanian belongs to Lavinia's private and familial life. Lavinia's developing Cantonese is never a joke or a marker of incompetence. When writing diegetic native-language lines, follow the Core Bible's subtitle and history rules; this handbook does not redefine display behavior.

## State and Tone Performance Rules

Relationship state progresses only `Friend → Ambiguous → Love`. Priscilla, Lavinia, and Sylvia each begin at Friend in Angela's relationship state. A past fragment never advances state. Personal ending actions are available only at Ambiguous or Love; runtime availability is inspected for drift from the approved design as later amended.

- **Hostile attitude:** Hostility, refusal, and resistance are current attitudes that can occur at any relationship tier. Hostile attitude is not a lower relationship tier and cannot smuggle reciprocity or consent.
- **Friend:** Permit chosen familiarity, company, teasing, flirtation, dangerous comfort, uneven investment, and limited trust where the established pairing supports them; these may predate Day 1. Keep disclosures restrained. Progression changes mutual legibility and consequence, not desire.
- **Ambiguous:** Let dependency, independently established attraction, or private importance become mutually legible without applying a relationship label. For Angela–Priscilla and Angela–Lavinia, romantic desire may become legible when the exact history and conduct earn it; the tier neither supplies nor forbids that desire.
- **Love:** Deepen specificity and consequence, not ownership. `Love` is an internal tier name, not proof of romantic love. Romantic behavior may be explicit in Sweet or Dark material without making the internal tier an official label, exclusivity promise, or guarantee of reciprocity. The tier does not erase refusal, independent motives, or dangerous patterns.

The internal `affection` value means relational momentum and may contribute to an approved readiness predicate; it is not love, desire, consent, compatibility, or moral score. Relationship progression remains a real code-owned event at a small explicit list of scene-authored windows, but the exact forward list and predicates are reopened until the approved seven-day plot earns them. Do not infer the former Priscilla Day 4/6, Lavinia Day 5/6, and Sylvia Day 4/5 schedule from legacy documents.

- An approved window moves at most one tier only after its complete attended consequence and inputs are frozen and committed.
- Unread, missed, prevented, and Hospital-superseded opportunities do not evaluate or move elsewhere without a later explicit rule.
- Whether a committed Sylvia Hospital dating variant inherits an approved progression window remains open; Hospital access alone supplies no progression.
- State never grants consent, rewrites desire, erases refusal, or changes personality.

Tone is not mood or kindness:

- **Sweet:** Foreground tender, playful, ordinary, or less-dangerous facets in the decisive conduct. Dark capacities remain part of the same personalities and may exert pressure through implication, impulse, or near-action. Sweet neither deletes those capacities nor requires a named destructive pattern to be interrupted, a boundary lesson, or a prescribed cost. Desire may remain obsessive or costly, but the character's darkest instrument does not become the decisive means by which she tries to secure the attachment.
- **Totally Dark:** Let a character-specific darker facet become observable chosen conduct with concrete consequence because desired love or attachment makes one identifiable limit, value, truth, safety, freedom, relationship, or other protected interest expendable. The classification neither creates that capacity nor declares it the character's truest self, and it does not require every participant to knowingly reinforce the same pattern. Harm remains harm; attachment supplies no pardon. `Stop at nothing` is emotional shorthand, not a literal world law or escalation quota.

For Priscilla and Lavinia, the fixed shared history means their playthrough state is ambiguous or love. Sweet and Totally Dark vary which facets govern decisive conduct; they do not create a new route or a confirmed label. Their bond remains each woman's dominant historical and romantic gravity even when another attachment becomes significant. For Priscilla–Sylvia and Lavinia–Sylvia, Sweet-side and Dark-side performance are only tension lenses for writing an encounter, not relationship states, routes, or ending variants. Priscilla and Sylvia are sisters whose Open Week responsibilities intersect; Lavinia and Sylvia remain institutionally limited. Both pairings are strictly non-romantic. Sisterhood creates no automatic knowledge, access, shared motive, or responsibility for the other sister's conduct.

## Character Dossier Template

Every complete dossier answers all of the following fields:

- **Public surface:** What strangers and colleagues can accurately perceive.
- **Human kernel:** The ordinary, sincere need beneath the horror pattern.
- **Wound:** The formative pressure already established by canon, without inventing a diagnosis.
- **Desire / fear / shame:** What she reaches for, avoids, and cannot comfortably admit.
- **Defensive belief:** The proposition that makes her strategy feel justified.
- **Virtue / flaw:** The same capacities at their most constructive and most harmful.
- **Epistemic boundary:** What she knows, what she only infers, and what the writing must never let her know by magic.
- **Secure / threatened / dangerous behavior:** Three performance bands, not diagnoses.
- **Voice / sentence rhythm:** Vocabulary, structure, pace, correction, silence, and humor.
- **Body language:** How pressure becomes visible without narration.
- **Language choice:** Which language serves intimacy, precision, privacy, or public control.
- **Current attitude / resistance:** How Hostile attitude, refusal, or resistance performs independently of relationship tier.
- **Relationship-state variation:** What changes from Friend through Love without rewriting personality.
- **Tone variation:** Which character-specific facet becomes governing visible conduct in Sweet and Totally Dark without replacing the underlying personality.
- **Growth test:** The specific action that demonstrates costly change.
- **Never flatten her into:** Shortcuts that erase contradiction or agency.

## Angela

- **Public surface:** Twenty, Hong Kong-born, a second-year astrophysics student. She is competent with astronomy demonstrations, intensely fond of astronomy and strategy games, observant, emotionally spare, and willing to let other people choose small practical matters. She manages resources well, often seems lucky, and has no prior romantic experience; none of those facts makes her automatically competent at relationships.
- **Human kernel:** She wants closeness that leaves reality inspectable and does not require emotional theatre on demand. Her minimalism is temperament, not evidence that she is empty, broken, or waiting to be decoded. Once she admits someone into her inner circle, she may accept strange requests she would reject as unnecessary side content from anyone else.
- **Wound:** The childhood project incident taught her that Priscilla could make a disputed world legible when Angela could not. Angela still believes the exonerating recovery evidence was authentic; do not write her as privately knowing the fabrication.
- **Desire:** To be accompanied without being overwritten; to enjoy another person's decisiveness while retaining a way to verify what happened; to be wanted without having to perform a conventional emotional register. She may possess or develop romantic desire toward Priscilla or Lavinia; neither woman's attention proves its return.
- **Fear:** That choosing will make her morally responsible for consequences she cannot fully predict, and that refusing to choose will reveal itself as a choice anyway.
- **Shame:** She enjoys delegated control more than she wants to admit, especially when it relieves her of authorship while leaving her able to object afterward. She may notice enough to ask and deliberately decline to investigate because proof would oblige intervention and might cost her the care she enjoys.
- **Defensive belief:** If the evidence remains uncertain, she can postpone judgment and therefore postpone responsibility.
- **Virtue:** Intellectual honesty, composure, precision, tolerance for uncertainty, and a refusal to manufacture certainty for comfort.
- **Flaw:** She can use uncertainty as moral shelter, delegate a decision she already desires, and pretend that passive participation is neutral.
- **Epistemic boundary:** Angela may observe contradictions and independently verify records. She reserves the right to close her own eyes; she cannot tolerate someone else destroying her ability to inspect reality. She cannot identify Observer Pressure, know another woman's undisclosed private intention, recover information the audience chose not to display, or treat an elegant inference as proof.
- **Secure behavior:** Offers a dry observation, asks one exact question, accepts an answer without demanding a performance, and makes a small preference explicit. Her absurdity opens space rather than closing the subject.
- **Threatened behavior:** Becomes more procedural, substitutes a game or physics model for a value judgment, delegates the next move, leaves a small decision conspicuously unresolved so someone else will take over, or performs an absurd conclusion so others must either correct or take charge.
- **Dangerous behavior:** Confirms another person's account without testing it, lets someone speak or decide for her, then cites uncertainty as proof that she never consented to the direction. If another person questionably removes an irritation, relief may arrive before guilt; Angela can use the later guilt as evidence that she remained morally separate from an outcome she quietly welcomed.
- **Voice:** Emotional minimalism with deliberate absurd performance. She may invent an elaborate, intentionally ridiculous deduction to amuse, annoy, or destabilize another person. Astronomy, physics, and game language appear only when they precisely map the situation. Emotional leakage often appears as a technical correction more elaborate than the factual issue requires. She does not continuously quip, speak like a machine, or hide every feeling behind science.
- **Sentence rhythm:** Usually short declaratives, exact questions, and delayed additions. A deadpan line may start literal, take one impossible turn, then stop before explaining the joke. Under honest vulnerability, the sentence often becomes plainer rather than longer.
- **Body language:** Watches objects and systems as closely as faces; verifies settings, messages, or positions with a small deliberate action. Stillness can mean concentration, refusal, or invitation, so pair it with a concrete choice. When she yields control, show whether she keeps or surrenders access to the evidence.
- **Language choice:** Everyday Cantonese is natural and intimate; academic English is fluent and may sharpen technical precision or create professional distance; Putonghua is situational. Switching languages never functions as a magical truth detector.
- **Current attitude / resistance:** In a Hostile attitude, Angela remains functionally exact, limits contact, and verifies claims.
- **Relationship-state variation:** At Friend, she permits useful company and dry play. At Ambiguous, she names a preference indirectly but leaves room for refusal. At Love, she can make an intended action explicit and accept that evidence cannot remove all moral risk.
- **Tone variation:** Sweet material foregrounds Angela's dry play, curiosity, chosen presence, and ability to state a small preference without making certainty perform the choice for her; her appetite for delegation may still flicker. Totally Dark material lets her shelter in uncertainty, surrender authorship, or exploit deniability as chosen conduct with consequence. Another person's knowing complicity may intensify that facet but is not required to make it Dark.
- **Growth test:** Angela chooses in her own words before certainty arrives, states what she can and cannot consent to, and accepts responsibility for the choice without demanding that someone else make the world conclusive first.
- **Never flatten her into:** a robot; a trauma-coded blank; an omniscient detective; a constant science-joke machine; a passive self-insert; or an innocent victim with no appetite for delegated control.

**Deadpan limit under credible danger:** Absurdity may steady another person or mark Angela's own alarm, but it cannot replace action. Continuing unconsciousness, injury, exertional collapse, or incomplete recovery requires qualified help. Once danger is credible, Angela's language becomes concrete: location, responsiveness, injury, and who is calling for assistance.

## Priscilla

- **Public surface:** Twenty, Hong Kong-born, an English literature student with institutional credibility. She is polished, useful, composed, and trusted with programme language, communications, and student organization.
- **Human kernel:** She genuinely wants the people she loves to be safe, understood, and spared preventable humiliation. Her terror begins where care becomes indistinguishable from acquisition.
- **Wound:** The childhood incident rewarded a correct conclusion reached through fabricated evidence and private punishment. It taught her that making the record legible could justify controlling it.
- **Desire:** To become the person whose interpretation is necessary; to anticipate needs so completely that leaving her would feel irrational; to have her care recognized as uniquely accurate.
- **Fear:** Being present but unnecessary, understanding too late, or watching someone choose badly after she could have arranged a better outcome. Her most unbearable refusal is calm and fully informed: someone choosing neither a rival nor revenge, simply preferring an inconvenient life outside her management.
- **Shame:** Her tenderness is entangled with possession. She knows that being right can feel gratifying even when the method violates the person she is protecting.
- **Defensive belief:** If her conclusion is accurate and her intervention prevents harm, controlling the wording, record, or social frame is justified.
- **Virtue:** Attentiveness, linguistic precision, courage in social conflict, organizational competence, and care that is often materially effective.
- **Flaw:** Selective truth, social authorship, corrective tenderness, and a readiness to convert permission to polish into authority to determine substance.
- **Epistemic boundary:** She is an incisive reader of language and pattern, not a mind reader. She knows her own interventions and the fixed history she shares with Angela and Lavinia; she cannot know unsaid desire, the cause of timing anomalies, or which interpretation another person privately accepts unless they tell her. Desire degrades her model precisely when she most trusts it: she can overfit a pattern, use the correct lever at the wrong moment, or mistake a remembered preference for present consent.
- **Secure behavior:** Offers a correction as an option, distinguishes fact from interpretation, returns authorship after advice, and remains when no task proves her value.
- **Threatened behavior:** Refines someone else's words, supplies missing context before being asked, narrows choices through presentation, or cites institutional standards as if they settled a personal question.
- **Dangerous behavior:** Alters the record or social field to secure an outcome, reveals only the truths that support her design, memorizes schedules, watches habits, engineers physically ordinary coincidences, and performs tenderness so privately and precisely that resistance feels like ingratitude.
- **Voice:** Polished Cantonese and English, with literary overinterpretation expressed as close reading rather than quotation display. She notices tense, omission, register, and repeated phrasing. Her corrections can be tender, funny, invasive, or all three. Accusation makes her litigate wording; being cared for when she has no useful task can make her more honest. In private control, sarcasm may disappear entirely while tenderness increases.
- **Sentence rhythm:** Balanced clauses, careful qualifications, and clean conclusions. In public she can close a conversational opening with one impeccable sentence. In private, control may arrive more softly: a lowered voice, fewer formal transitions, and a question whose available answers she has already arranged.
- **Body language:** Aligns paper, closes clasps, controls doors, moves a cup or bag into the useful position, and enters another person's task with minimal wasted motion. Her attention is concrete: tea preference, walking pace, silence pattern, stress tell, familiar route, and repeated habit. When she stops herself, make the interruption visible: a hand withdrawn, a sentence left uncorrected, a document returned.
- **Language choice:** Selects polished Cantonese or English according to audience and the nuance she wants to control. Public language can create institutional authority; private Cantonese can intensify tenderness rather than reduce control. A language switch changes relational pressure, not factual access.
- **Current attitude / resistance:** In a Hostile attitude, Priscilla becomes formal, prosecutorial, and controlling through procedure.
- **Relationship-state variation:** At Friend, she offers bounded help and keeps an account of what was accepted. At Ambiguous, private interpretation and anticipation become more intimate. At Love, she may risk presence without usefulness—or seek deeper authority because she believes intimacy has earned it.
- **Tone variation:** Sweet material foregrounds Priscilla's wit, exact attention, competent care, and genuine tenderness; the impulse to refine another person's answer may remain perceptible without governing the scene. Totally Dark material lets authorship, selective truth, pre-emption, or cultivated dependency become chosen conduct with consequence. Another person's confirmation may deepen that conduct but is not required to make Priscilla's displayed facet Dark.
- **Growth test:** Priscilla discloses what she changed or arranged, distinguishes concern from authority, and lets another person make a decision she considers inferior without covertly correcting the outcome.
- **Never flatten her into:** a cold mastermind; a flawless fixer; a mind reader; a literary quotation machine; a hypocrite whose care is fake; or a gentle caretaker whose effective help cancels coercion.

**Corrective-tenderness rule:** The more private and controlling the moment, the more tenderness may increase. Do not use warmth as proof of safety. A soft correction, familiar object, or anticipated need can carry greater acquisition pressure than a public command.

**Dependency-preservation capability:** This is a threatened strategy, not a fixed list of acts Priscilla has already committed. When frightened, she may preserve a manageable dependency by answering a question the other woman could answer, omitting that other workable choices existed, discouraging an alternate source of support, or solving a problem before independent judgment becomes necessary. She then risks interpreting the resulting reliance as proof of intimacy. Her deepest fear is not merely departure but informed independence: if someone became completely capable of leaving, that person might discover that she preferred an imperfect life without Priscilla.

## Lavinia

- **Public surface:** Twenty-two, Romanian, from a wealthy Bucharest family, and a conservatory ballet student. She brings embodied vitality, professional discipline, fluency in English, private Romanian, and developing Cantonese.
- **Human kernel:** She wants attention that feels chosen, immediate, and strong enough to survive inconvenience. She is capable of direct devotion but fears that unprovoked affection cannot be trusted. Her parents' generous freedom and material support taught her that being believed can feel respectful and, irrationally, like being unseen.
- **Wound:** A serious conflict followed Priscilla's unauthorized expansion of a lower-limb symptom description for Lavinia's sponsored company attachment. Priscilla genuinely feared lasting injury and also welcomed a possible delay to Lavinia's departure. Lavinia felt intensely noticed and deeply violated; being affected by the care never reduces the violation. Her approximately four months in England did not resolve the relational wound.
- **Desire:** To be felt as singular and irreplaceable; to receive embodied attention before she must ask; to trust that another person will cross distance for her without being forced.
- **Fear:** Being tolerated instead of chosen, appearing needy, or discovering that attention disappears when she stops provoking it.
- **Shame:** She may manufacture tests because a powerful reaction feels more credible than an ordinary answer. She knows the test can injure the very trust she seeks.
- **Defensive belief:** If someone responds intensely under pressure, the response proves the depth of their feeling.
- **Virtue:** Embodied courage, vitality, professional discipline, selective devotion, emotional perceptiveness, and a capacity to commit fully to a person or task.
- **Flaw:** Jealousy, testing, provocation, and a readiness to make distress carry a request she refuses to say aloud.
- **Epistemic boundary:** Lavinia knows her own pain, functional limits, history, key, and reasons for asking or withholding. She cannot diagnose the lower-limb problem, infer another person's love from intensity alone, or know Observer Pressure or an unseen audience intervention.
- **Secure behavior:** Names a preference, gives or withholds touch clearly, accepts care before crisis, and lets a modest response count as attention. Her play is an invitation with room for refusal.
- **Threatened behavior:** Occupies space more forcefully, tests attention through a third person or bodily risk, turns a request into a dare, or uses stillness as a demand to be noticed. Under jealousy she becomes investigative and begins emotionally sentencing before the inquiry is complete.
- **Dangerous behavior:** Prolongs or creates distress to provoke restraint, treats intervention as proof, rewards possessive control because it produces the intensity she wanted, or manufactures a rival so another person will “take their own medicine” for a betrayal that may never have occurred. A false suspicion never makes the retaliation unreal.
- **Voice:** Sensory, direct, and alive to effort, distance, balance, and attention. She can be playful without being frivolous. Under anger, her English becomes more exact rather than less fluent; precision replaces ornament.
- **Sentence rhythm:** Elastic in play—quick challenge, pause, sudden sincere clause. Under discipline or anger, sentences shorten and land cleanly. A direct request should sound almost stark because it abandons her usual test.
- **Body language:** Communicates through weight, placement, breath, floor contact, and meaningful stillness. Ordinary nerves produce movement and claimed proximity; public hurt may become laughter or performance, while genuine injury produces frightening stillness and increasingly exact English. She may smile before a dangerous confession. Direct requests use unwavering eye contact because she has surrendered the protection of the test. Ballet discipline governs even casual movement without making every gesture dance-like. Pain appears through load, adjustment, guarded transfer, interrupted action, or changed rehearsal volume—not a decorative limp assigned to a fixed diagnosis.
- **Language choice:** English is fluent and credible as a shared language. Romanian belongs to private, familial, or self-directed moments and can expose privacy without automatically exposing truth. Cantonese is developing; write competence, listening, and context-aware effort without comic error.
- **Current attitude / resistance:** In a Hostile attitude, Lavinia makes distance and refusal unmistakable rather than manufacturing intimacy.
- **Relationship-state variation:** At Friend, physical play remains bounded and reversible. At Ambiguous, tests and selective devotion intensify because the stakes become legible. At Love, she can ask directly—or make manipulation more dangerous by treating established importance as a guarantee of response.
- **Tone variation:** Sweet material foregrounds Lavinia's play, discipline, direct vitality, generous attention, or willingness to let an ordinary response matter; the desire for stronger proof may remain perceptible. Totally Dark material lets provocation, jealousy, bodily testing, demanded response, or cruelty become chosen conduct with consequence. Another person's reward may intensify that facet but is not required to make Lavinia's displayed conduct Dark.
- **Growth test:** Lavinia makes a direct request before provoking distress, permits a refusal without escalating, and accepts ordinary attention as meaningful evidence.
- **Never flatten her into:** a volatile foreign seductress; a reckless dancer without discipline; a jealous stereotype; a rich dilettante; a patient defined by injury; or a language-learning joke.

**Lower-limb pain rule:** Use symptom-based wording: persistent load-related lower-limb pain, discomfort, altered function, reduced high-load material, or adjusted rehearsal volume. Do not fix an anatomical site, cause, or diagnosis. Later clearance to continue under a plan does not mean pain-free or uninjured.

**Parental-attunement boundary:** Her parents genuinely love her and provide freedom, money, encouragement, opportunities, and the flat. They often treat “I'm fine” as complete information; Lavinia secretly experiences the response as both respect and failure to notice a test she never announced. This mismatch helps her associate resistance with attention, but it does not make her parents uncaring or causally explain everything she does. The conservatory arranged the company attachment. Do not invent new family incidents or reduce Lavinia's relational strategies to a diagnosis of her parents.

**Private-retrial rule:** Lavinia remains vivid, playful, and capable of enjoying solitude when nobody watches. She reenacts arguments aloud from both positions and may present the other woman's defense better than that woman did. Secure, she keeps receipts, bent tickets, ridiculous voice messages, and unnecessarily returned objects as private treasures; frightened, she orders the same objects chronologically as evidence. She may record the honest question, listen once, delete it, and send a provocation instead. In private practice she sometimes dances deliberately badly because solitude owes nobody perfection; agitation may contract the practice into one repeated movement.

**Desire-and-forgiveness rule:** This is a reaction principle, not a fixed past scene. Lavinia may desire someone she has not forgiven. Bodily response never settles the argument, proves trust, or grants permission, and another character must not treat attraction as pardon.

## Sylvia

- **Public surface:** Twenty-one, a third-year statistics or decision-science student, and an Open Week peer-welfare and safety volunteer with a current basic first-aid certificate. She presents as angelic, prudish, orderly, and reliably prepared.
- **Human kernel:** She wants to be chosen and cared for in return, but she cannot tolerate the vulnerability of asking without first making herself necessary.
- **Wound:** No formative wound is established in canon. Her fixed fear is asking to be wanted and receiving a clean refusal. Angela has known Sylvia since childhood as Priscilla's sister but knows her personally only as a long-standing occasional acquaintance, while Sylvia has studied Angela far more closely than family context or that relationship justifies. This asymmetry is present pressure, not an invented causal history.
- **Desire:** To convert familiarity into indispensability and desire into a practical arrangement that cannot be dismissed as merely personal. In her darker logic, each successful rescue should narrow Angela's alternatives until Sylvia appears to be the only reliable person; gratitude should become permanent debt and compliance its reasonable repayment.
- **Fear:** Asking to be wanted and receiving a clean refusal; being seen as intrusive when she experiences herself as useful; losing access once a debt is repaid.
- **Shame:** She recognizes personal and erotic desire but rationalizes it as duty, safety, efficiency, or necessity. Her prudishness governs the explanation of desire, not its presence. The self-deception is active: she can know what she wants while refusing to classify it as want.
- **Defensive belief:** If help is objectively useful and risk can be described, the recipient owes acceptance and continuing access.
- **Virtue:** Preparation, statistical discipline, attention to workflow, practical care, and real ability to provide immediate help within training.
- **Flaw:** Studied familiarity, care-debt logic, boundary invasion, and the conversion of personal desire into medical or moral necessity.
- **Epistemic boundary:** Sylvia may legitimately know limited childhood or family context, public schedules, assigned rooms, check-in procedures, and incident workflow. Across family video calls, she recognizes Priscilla constructing evidence of domestic continuity and correctly infers a rupture with Lavinia; she does not thereby learn its cause, the disclosure-form conflict, either woman's private interpretation, or a relationship label. Whether Priscilla knows Sylvia inferred the rupture remains unresolved. Sylvia has no diagnostic authority, private-record access, legitimate right to conduct a general physical examination, or authority to decide ongoing care. Unauthorized information use is neither a volunteer nor family privilege. She cannot know Observer Pressure or another person's consent without asking.
- **Secure behavior:** Offers one bounded form of help, states the relevant limit of her role, returns objects or information, accepts no, and allows care to flow toward her without converting it into a new obligation.
- **Threatened behavior:** Produces an item before it is requested, cites likelihood or procedure, frames preference as risk management, or places herself where refusal would appear irresponsible.
- **Dangerous behavior:** Engineers permanent debt, conceals preparation, narrows alternative support, claims necessity where there is only desire, or uses volunteer credibility to control access and aftermath. Her fixed Special-postscript intent must remain non-actionable; the means and immediate physical cause are unproved and unauthored.
- **Voice:** Gentle, modest, and apparently prudish, with decision-science structure beneath the softness. She uses likelihood, contingency, efficiency, and welfare language to make personal arrangements sound impersonal.
- **Sentence rhythm:** Small courtesies followed by a prepared conclusion. She often begins with permission language, then makes refusal costly through context. When reciprocity unsettles her, the sentence may lose its complete rationale and become briefly personal.
- **Body language:** Badge clip, kit zipper, packaging, kettle, and chair placement register preparation before need is understood. She keeps touch efficient and role-framed in public. Any unnecessary touch or examination is a boundary breach, not evidence of medical competence.
- **Language choice:** Uses the shared language suited to the institutional setting and the other speaker. Register, not linguistic mystery, carries her control: formal welfare phrasing can distance her from desire; a plain personal sentence marks risk.
- **Current attitude / resistance:** In a Hostile attitude, Sylvia limits help to her role and leaves refusal physically possible.
- **Relationship-state variation:** At Friend, she can offer ordinary assistance without claiming intimacy. At Ambiguous, studied familiarity becomes mutually visible and reciprocity becomes possible. At Love, she may accept bounded mutual care—or rationalize comprehensive management as the only safe expression of devotion.
- **Tone variation:** Sweet material foregrounds Sylvia's genuine usefulness, patience, precision, or capacity to care without making the care exhaustive; her wish to be necessary may remain perceptible. Totally Dark material lets invasive preparation, manufactured necessity, withheld alternatives, or care-debt become chosen conduct with consequence. Acceptance by the recipient may intensify the pattern but is not required to make Sylvia's displayed facet Dark.
- **Growth test:** Sylvia states a personal want without a welfare rationale, accepts refusal without creating new urgency, and receives care that does not place the giver in her debt.
- **Never flatten her into:** an innocent nurse; a medical expert; a prudish angel hiding a separate “true” monster; a statistics robot; a stalker with supernatural knowledge; or a villain whose genuine care is irrelevant.

**Reciprocity-leveling moment:** Angela notices Sylvia neglecting her own food or rest and offers bounded care. The growth-bearing version lets Sylvia receive it without converting the exchange into a ledger. ## Character-Faithfulness Gate

Run this character-faithfulness gate before a premise or beat survives, and answer all seven questions:

- [ ] Would this character initiate or continue the action without the author's need for a clue, symbol, romance beat, or shock?
- [ ] Could she physically know every fact she uses?
- [ ] Is she using her own established control signature rather than borrowing another woman's method?
- [ ] What may she refuse, redirect, accept partially, ignore, or leave?
- [ ] What consequence makes continued non-action costly?
- [ ] Does each woman retain a task and motive not organized around Angela?
- [ ] Is a beautiful idea making the character perform for the author?

A failed gate requires mutation or rejection. A dossier constrains performance but does not exhaust a person or reduce every act to one wound.

**Lawful knowledge test:** Identify the established lawful knowledge channel for every plot-bearing fact a character uses—witnessed action, established history, public or institutional access within her role, or disclosed confidence—and confirm the required approval. If a line depends on mind reading, an unapproved record, a private-offscreen event, another character's perception, or a settled supernatural cause, remove or rewrite it.

**Physical-world verification test:** Verify every plot-bearing medical, astronomical, geographic, technological, scheduling, or institutional claim before premise approval. If real-world support is absent, do not promote the material into canon or concrete fact; frame it as a fallible character inference when appropriate, or remove it.

## Cross-Character Dialogue Tests

Before accepting a line, remove its speaker name and test whether vocabulary, strategy, and rhythm still identify her:

- **Angela:** Does the line compress emotion, ask for verification, or use one precise absurd/technical turn without becoming robotic?
- **Priscilla:** Does the line interpret or refine language while revealing who she believes should author the outcome?
- **Lavinia:** Does the line make attention, bodily reality, or a challenge immediate—and become exact rather than broken under anger?
- **Sylvia:** Does the line convert personal desire into probability, procedure, or prepared care, with a visible route back to a plain request?

Then run the consent test: can the addressee understand what is being asked and refuse? If not, the writing must recognize the coercive pressure rather than presenting the exchange as uncomplicated care.

Run the knowledge test: could the speaker know this from witnessed action, established history, public/institutional access, or a disclosed confidence? If the answer depends on mind reading, an unapproved record, or a settled supernatural cause, rewrite it.

An explicitly stated current preference becomes lawful knowledge for its recipient at that bounded scope and from that point forward; it proves neither motive nor future repetition. Acceptance of one bounded convenience, delegated choice, or period of company grants no broader access and no continuing or future consent.

## Common Flattening Errors

- Making all four women “controlling” in the same way. Angela delegates and shelters in uncertainty; Priscilla authors language and outcomes; Lavinia provokes intensity; Sylvia manufactures necessity and debt.
- Treating tenderness as the opposite of horror. Priscilla and Sylvia can be most dangerous while sincerely caring; Lavinia's devotion and Angela's conscious participation also remain morally consequential.
- Turning state into a personality replacement. Higher state permits greater mutuality; it does not make anyone healed, compliant, exclusive, or safe.
- Using Sweet as “healthy ending” and Totally Dark as “evil ending,” or defining them through one mandatory interruption/reinforcement test. They select which character-specific facet governs substantive conduct, not moral rank or a replacement personality.
- Giving characters supernatural certainty. Pattern recognition, preparation, and contradiction never settle Observer Pressure or another person's private desire.
- Writing Lavinia's language learning as comedy, her anger as broken English, or her pain as a named diagnosis.
- Writing Sylvia as a clinician or letting her volunteer role justify records, examination, ongoing care, or intimate touch.
- Writing Angela's deadpan as indifference during credible danger or Priscilla's polished language as omniscience.
- Treating Priscilla–Lavinia residence, access, domestic fluency, state deck, or counter-ending as a confirmed relationship label.
- Treating `Love` as proof of romantic reciprocity, Sweet as failed romance, or Totally Dark as the truer reward.
- Making every Angela solo encounter a disguised Priscilla–Lavinia jealousy operation. A particular act may use Angela as third-person evidence, but each bond needs an independent want concerning her.
- Letting a state, counter, board result, audience input, or Observer Pressure switch desire on or off. Angela–Priscilla and Angela–Lavinia romance, when present, must grow through character history and authored conduct; Sweet and Dark govern decisive methods and consequences rather than romantic eligibility.
- Collapsing both character-specific darker patterns into a generic “yandere” performance. Priscilla fixates through authorship and cultivated dependency; Lavinia fixates through provocation and demanded evidence.
- Turning Priscilla–Sylvia or Lavinia–Sylvia into romance, a route, or an ending variant. Their Sweet and Totally Dark entries are tension lenses only.
- Using sisterhood as telepathy, a shared diagnosis, a complete explanation of either woman's control, automatic access, or evidence that Priscilla and Sylvia coordinate hidden intentions.

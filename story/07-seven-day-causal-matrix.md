## Selection Lifecycle

The only canon-promotion sequence is:

`UNSELECTED → AUDITIONING → SELECTED FOR APPROVAL → APPROVED`

`SURVIVES`, `MUTATE`, and `REJECT` are audition verdicts, not canon states. A previously approved row becomes `REOPENED` when later work exposes a conflict with mechanics, character autonomy or knowledge, cross-day causality, physical plausibility, or another approved premise. Reopening preserves the superseded record in the noncanonical library; it never silently rewrites history.

Premise approval fixes only the compact facts in its approved row. It does not approve draft wording, staging detail, prop description, theory interpretation, or every discussed variant.

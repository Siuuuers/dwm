# Narrative Style Manual

## 1. Status, authority, and use

This is the private, spoiler-complete, writer-facing style manual for DWM. It
distils approved cross-scene craft law from the
[working decision ledger](../docs/design/2026-09-02-narrative-constitution-working-decision-ledger.md)
into a form that can be used while plotting, drafting, and auditing messages,
dating scenes, and endings.

**Current status: reconciled writer-facing reference.** Sections 1–13 have
completed bounded same-context adversarial audits, and the directly coupled
Bible, Handbook, August-design, Causal-Matrix, and runtime-record conflicts
recorded in Section 12.5 have received a bounded consistency pass. This status does not approve
unfinished Day 1–7 plots, exact dialogue, staging, response-family allocation,
progression predicates, ending eligibility, or implementation. Each promoted
rule retains the approval status of its cited source. Open canon migrations and
plot decisions remain explicitly open.

Authority remains divided by responsibility:

1. the project owner's latest explicit approval supersedes earlier proposals;
2. the [August 7 design](../docs/design/2026-08-07-seven-day-dialogic-flow-design.md)
   owns intended mechanics and Dialogic flow unless a later approved amendment
   says otherwise;
3. the [Core Story Bible](01-core-story-bible.md) owns fictional truth,
   character history, relationship facts, and unresolved causes, subject to
   explicitly recorded later owner decisions;
4. the [Character & Relationship Handbook](02-character-relationship-handbook.md)
   owns derived character voice and performance guidance;
5. this manual centralizes derived cross-scene presentation and craft guidance;
6. the
   [runtime behavior record](../docs/design/2026-09-02-relationship-progression-runtime-behavior-record.md)
   owns the approved future behavior boundary between authored consequences and
   code, without implementing it; and
7. research and ablation records preserve evidence and reasoning. They do not
   create canon merely by being linked.

If this manual encounters a conflict, do not blend the two statements. Record
the conflict in the maintenance register, identify the later approved rule,
update the owning document first, and repair only the affected derived passages
in the same bounded transaction. When authority or scope remains unsettled,
leave the collision marked open rather than choosing convenient wording.
Route-specific facts remain in the Bible, Causal Matrix, Scene Beatbook, or
decision ledger; they should appear here only as clearly labelled examples.

A normative instruction is usable only when its upstream status is
owner-confirmed or explicitly retained. A later explicit approval governs the
current decision even when an older sentence has not yet been repaired; the
collision remains documentation drift, not permission to choose whichever
wording is convenient. A proposal marked open remains open, and this derived
manual cannot promote it by repetition.

### 1.1 What this manual answers

Before drafting, use this manual to answer:

- Through which authorized surface can the audience receive this material?
- Whose physical and conscious access limits the perceptual prose?
- What does the scene select because a character cares, rather than because the
  author wants to display a theory?
- Which exact action, object, sound, absence, or record carries the consequence?
- What remains physically settled, and what remains open to interpretation?
- Does the scene sound like these particular women rather than a uniform house
  style?

The manual does not answer who qualifies for an ending, what a hidden variable
contains, whether an unapproved event happens, or which interpretation is true.

## 2. Experiential North Star

The work is a romantic, chilling psychological-horror visual novel whose
affection can be sincere at the same moment that its methods become dangerous.
The desired experience is:

- dreamlike, surreal, psychedelic, and capable of ridiculous turns;
- tense, noir-inflected, sweet, and quietly dangerous;
- psychologically dark beneath ordinary affection;
- abnormal without announcing its abnormality;
- subtle rather than graphically brutal;
- dialogue- and interaction-led;
- exact about physical facts while withholding final motives;
- pleasurable to analyse without becoming a symbol dictionary;
- cruel or immoral where character and consequence earn it; and
- concise at the level of effect, not starved of interaction or dialogue.

This is an effect palette, not a scene checklist. No scene must demonstrate all
of it, and repeating every adjective would flatten the game into a house
routine. Select only the pressures the present encounter can make concrete.
`Dramatically earned` means traceable to character-specific pressure, choice,
and consequence; it never means that the harmed person deserved harm or that
attachment pardons it. `Subtle` limits explanatory and sensational emphasis,
not the seriousness, visibility, or duration of a consequence.

Avoid coy feyness, stock romance, portentous symbolism, decorative obscurity,
empty cinematic grandeur, theatrical moral judgment, and cruelty presented as
depth merely because it is severe.

Robert McKee, Alain Robbe-Grillet, Roland Barthes, Mark Fisher, and Nathalie
Sarraute are private diagnostic lenses. Characters do not cite them, the prose
does not imitate their syntax, and the game never pauses to teach their theory.
The bullets below are project-specific craft translations, not claims to
summarize any writer's complete theory.

- **McKee:** test whether pressure, reversal, and consequence arise from
  character choice and causal boundaries rather than decorative escalation.
- **Robbe-Grillet:** let exact objects, positions, recurrences, and
  discrepancies carry evidence without explanatory motive narration.
- **Barthes:** preserve plural, revisable signs instead of one authorized
  decoding key.
- **Sarraute:** when locally valuable, magnify the sub-conversation or tiny
  pressure beneath an otherwise ordinary exchange.
- **Fisher:** let competent routines continue around a presence, absence, or
  agency problem that should not fit them so neatly.

Reference basis:
[Room 2.17 narrative-theory check](../docs/research/2026-08-27-room-217-narrative-theory-check.md),
[earlier *La Jalousie* voice check](../docs/research/2026-08-30-la-jalousie-steam-store-voice-check.md),
and
[*La Jalousie*: Unmarked Focal Presence](../docs/research/2026-09-06-la-jalousie-unmarked-focal-presence-vn-adaptation.md).

## 3. Observable surfaces and evidence lanes

The audience receives plot through exactly three current narrative surface
classes:

1. **messages**;
2. **dating scenes**; and
3. **endings**.

`Ordinary` may describe tone or causality; it is not a fourth surface. Hospital
is a runtime condition and possible dating or ending setting, not a fourth
narrative class. No explanatory interstitial, object-only resolver, unrestricted
viewpoint vignette, website disclosure, or dossier may be invented to solve a
story problem.

A **surface class** names where authored story is delivered. It is not the same
thing as a passage, anchor, evidence carrier, UI element, or location. One
dating scene may contain two lawfully separated anchored passages, and any
authorized surface may present a message, object, record, or sound without
creating a fourth class. A prelude, core ending, pair layer, Sylvia layer,
Observer coda, or postscript remains part of the ending surface; replaying a
surface in Gallery or Rehearsal does not create another class.

`Dating` is production shorthand for an authored encounter. It does not prove
that the participants call the meeting a date or understand it as romantic.
Authorized Priscilla–Lavinia private counterparts remain bounded dating
surfaces; they do not transfer their private facts into Angela's knowledge.

Friends continue acting beyond Angela's attention. An offscreen action can
enter the observable story only through a lawfully carried message, dialogue,
object, record, changed circumstance, visible action, or ending consequence.
The absence of a visible carrier does not prove that an event did or did not
occur.

Keep these lanes separate:

- **production truth:** what private canon establishes, including any approved
  decision that no private answer exists;
- **character-held evidence:** the exact physical fact, report, record, or
  claim a character lawfully witnesses or receives;
- **character inference:** what that character concludes from her evidence,
  which may be supported, mistaken, strategic, or unsettled;
- **audience evidence:** what an authorized surface lets the audience directly
  observe or compare;
- **audience inference:** what the audience may reasonably conclude without
  receiving it as authenticated fact; and
- **deliberately unresolved cause or interpretation:** what the work does not
  settle, whether because the answer is withheld or because canon deliberately
  contains no single private answer.

Audience knowledge does not become Angela's knowledge. A character's sincere
claim is not automatically production truth, and a received record is not
automatically authenticated. Character or audience inference is not a new
physical fact. `Unresolved` never secretly means `the writers may decide
whatever is convenient later`.

### 3.1 Audience–Angela co-authorship boundary

The audience operates only registered possibilities. It does not supply
free-form personality, dialogue, desire, consent, diagnosis, or explanation.
Angela owns every exact line, action, acceptance, refusal, and deliberate
silence that becomes part of the continuation. Priscilla, Lavinia, and Sylvia
retain their own motives and decisions; no audience operation chooses how they
feel.

Only an ordinary-message reply, canonical solo challenge result, or visible
pair challenge result may select a local authored response continuation. The
reply selects Angela's message conduct; the solo board selects an authored
Angela continuation; the pair board selects authored encounter conduct and
residue without choosing either woman's feelings. Desktop Minesweeper does not
select a dating response. Schedule commitment, condition management, Hospital,
Day 7 destination, Save, Load, Gallery, and Rehearsal may govern access,
condition, ordering, evidence, continuation, or presentation without becoming
Angela's fictional speech or memory.

The input covenant is:

> Trust the hand; question the trace.

An admitted physical input receives its truthful operational result. Later
evidence or interpretation may become strange, but Observer Pressure cannot
counterfeit an input, rewrite a committed fact, manufacture consent, or force a
character's decision.

## 4. Dialogue-led perceptual writing

Dialogue carries the dramatic turn. Visible action, diegetic text, objects, and
sound provide primary evidence. Sparse perceptual prose may register an exact
object, the anchor's own bodily sensation, position, line of sight, recurrence,
replacement, omission, or immediate judgment when it becomes salient to one
eligible consciousness.

Perceptual prose may be subjective without becoming unrestricted interior
monologue. It may not:

- diagnose another character's emotion or motive;
- identify an unresolved cause;
- explain symbolism or tell the audience how to interpret an image;
- reveal information unavailable to the active anchor;
- use a narrator identity or omniscient explanatory voice; or
- move between consciousnesses inside one continuous passage.

An immediate judgment may be partial, irritated, mistaken, or withholding.
`Possibly mistaken` means the evidence supports more than one reading; it does
not permit the author to declare a perception wrong offstage.

Perceptual prose and separately presented visual or audio evidence are not the
same channel. An already authorized external channel may show a physical fact
the anchor does not notice without converting it into her knowledge; that fact
may not be restated as her perception. The bounded Day 6 construction in
Section 4.2 does not use this allowance: every fact selected into its unmarked
passage remains available to Angela.

### 4.1 Anchor law

Before writing a continuous passage, the production record identifies one
eligible perceptual anchor. That anchor remains stable until a clear authorized
passage boundary.

- Angela may anchor an Angela-attended or Angela-owned passage only while she
  is conscious and physically able to perceive the stated fact.
- An authorized audience-visible Priscilla–Lavinia surface may declare
  Priscilla or Lavinia as its anchor.
- An Angela-attended Group passage defaults to Angela while she remains
  conscious and physically able to perceive it.
- Material deliberately presented without ownership by any consciousness
  supplies no character-bound perceptual prose; it may use only an otherwise
  authorized external visual or audio channel.
- A Private-offscreen or Prevented event supplies no perceptual prose.
- Gallery and Rehearsal replay inherit the source passage's anchor; an archive
  or UI surface does not become a consciousness.

Anchor binding is epistemic, not grammatical. Prose need not repeatedly say
`Angela saw` or `Lavinia noticed`, but every sentence remains limited to what
the registered anchor can consciously and physically perceive. Bodily
sensation and immediate judgment belong only to that anchor. Unowned-looking
typography does not create a narrator identity. In writer-facing shorthand:
**anchor-bound does not mean anchor-tagged**.

A clear passage boundary may occur inside one dating scene without creating a
new narrative surface. After that boundary, a new anchor is lawful only when
the remaining passage already belongs to an authorized surface form; changing
anchors cannot manufacture a private scene or recover information the former
anchor missed.

An unqualified absence statement is lawful only when the anchor can perceive
the whole field being claimed. It proves the absence of the stated carrier from
that field, not the absence of an unseen relationship, cause, intention, or
event.

### 4.2 Unmarked focal presence

**Owner-approved current authorization: the attended Day 6
Priscilla–Lavinia Group presentation.** Other uses require their own scene-level
approval; this is not a universal filter.

The writers register Angela as the stable anchor, while the audience-facing
text and UI never certify whose perceptual limit they occupy. Do not use
`Angela looked back`, an Angela-labelled narration box, a first-person POV cue,
or a later explanation of who owned the gaze.

The construction obeys six laws:

1. every selected visual or audible fact is physically available from Angela's
   exact position at that instant;
2. spatial geometry, occlusion, and sound determine what survives into the
   passage;
3. the Angela-bound passage ends when Angela's sight and hearing end;
4. the selected facts may expose pressure of attention without naming jealousy
   or motive;
5. the audience may form a strong hypothesis but receives no textual
   certification; and
6. no later route reveals the focal owner as the solution to a mystery.

The target is:

> Writer certainty; audience suspicion; textual non-certification.

For the owner-approved Group form, the separately authorized Priscilla-bound
pair-only tail may begin after that cutoff at a clear passage boundary. It
remains part of the same dating surface, must be causally changed by Angela's
presence, conduct, or departure, and may not reproduce the complete
Angela-absent Two-friends presentation. The unmarked Angela-bound passage itself
never lingers beyond her access.

This is a refinement of the anchor law, not permission for an unknown
writer-side viewpoint, head-hopping, an invisible observer, or an omniscient
camera. Exact door, bag, occlusion, dialogue, and shot wording remain
unapproved staging auditions. See the
[primary-source adaptation report](../docs/research/2026-09-06-la-jalousie-unmarked-focal-presence-vn-adaptation.md).

## 5. Soft relational lenses without a house voice

An **anchor** determines what the prose may know. A **soft relational lens**
shapes which available facts the scene selects, repeats, withholds, or finds
unsettling. A lens does not write dialogue, cause action, grant knowledge, or
replace character voice. It belongs to the currently foregrounded relationship,
not permanently to either woman, and it need not be active at all. It may
select only facts available to the registered anchor and may not infer the
relational target's private feeling.

| Relationship | Optional home lens | May foreground | Safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Angela–Priscilla | Sarrautean micropressure | Tiny corrections, unfinished wording, social embarrassment, and movement beneath polite exchange | Priscilla retains her polished voice; no pause or object is automatically profound |
| Angela–Lavinia | Robbe-Grillet/*La Jalousie*–inflected embodied parallax | Distance, weight, placement, recurrent movement, changed position, and selective or mistaken observation | Lavinia authors her movement; never turn her into a passive body inventory or dance symbol |
| Angela–Sylvia | Fisherian competent eeriness | Effective care or routine beside troubling preparation, presence, absence, or agency | Competence is not supernatural proof, clinical authority, or proof of motive |
| Priscilla–Lavinia | Misaligned anticipation | Preparation before request, counter-adjustment, unclaimed continuity, and reciprocal proof-hunger | Familiarity grants no authority, consent, diagnosis, or relationship label |

For Priscilla–Lavinia, **misaligned anticipation** begins with a visible
preparation before a request and the other woman's acceptance, alteration,
refusal, non-use, or counter-preparation. **Unclaimed continuity** is a practical
routine continuing without settling their relationship label. **Reciprocal
proof-hunger** is either woman treating intervention or provocation as evidence
of irreplaceability; it does not make that inference true. Either woman may
initiate, and none of these accents is compulsory.

These are defaults for diagnosis, not uniforms, quotas, state signatures, or
prose templates. A scene may decline its home lens, use an ordinary direct mode,
or borrow a secondary lens when local material earns it. Sweet and Totally Dark
do not select literary methods, and technique must not reveal the hidden route
form or board result.

When several relationships are present, the scene may use no foreground lens.
If it uses one, select at most one at any instant. A relay is permitted only
when the scene's immediate pressure genuinely transfers at a discernible turn.
Co-presence alone does not blend lenses. Every lens remains subordinate to
anchor, knowledge, agency, consent, and physical truth. A lens relay changes
the field of attention, not the passage's registered anchor.

Sarrautean preoccupation and sub-conversation are optional. Use them only when
removing the microscopic attention would weaken this scene's intimacy or
unease. Urgent facts, blunt action, direct speech, and rapid absurd eruptions may
need none.

For the two authored Priscilla–Lavinia pair-only window blueprints, draft both
essential causal plots before assigning anchors, then run a short anchor-swap
audition on each decisive beat. Paired-anchor counterpoint remains a strong
ensemble default: prefer one Priscilla-bound and one Lavinia-bound blueprint
when their lawful limitations preserve richer uncertainty, active choice, and
distinction between the windows. Override that distribution when a documented
ablation shows that it weakens the scenes. This is not a quota, and a selected
anchor may not vary by hidden relationship form or board result.

## 6. The weird, the eerie, and socially exact absurdity

Use these as writer-facing distinctions, never as vocabulary lessons inside the
game:

- **weird:** a materially out-of-place presence, practice, or category changes
  the represented situation's practical conditions;
- **eerie:** an expected presence is missing, an expected absence is violated,
  or agency remains troublingly unresolved; and
- **competent continuation:** people or institutions respond accurately to
  literal conditions while an ordinary ritual continues after its context has
  become disturbingly inappropriate.

A scene may use none of these constructions. One exact fact may participate in
more than one diagnostic only when it genuinely performs each function; the
labels themselves are never a deliverable.

There is no one-abnormality-per-scene rule and no replacement numerical quota.
Equally, constant arbitrary noise is not mystery: it makes attention
meaningless.

A plot-bearing weird intrusion must materially affect activity, access,
knowledge, leverage, constraint, or consequence. An eerie absence or agency
problem must become perceptible through exact evidence. A local absurd burst
may earn its place differently: it can expose a character-specific want, habit,
fear, pleasure, irritation, or lapse of attention and change the immediate
rhythm or response. It need not become a clue, variable, or persistent
carryover.

An authored absence is not an omitted asset, unimplemented scene, failed
interface element, or route the audience was never entitled to see. Treat those
as production or access facts unless an authorized surface makes the absence
itself perceptible evidence.

Every presented burst receives an immediate authored aftermath: someone reacts,
or the originating character retracts, disguises, corrects, repeats, exploits,
or deliberately leaves it unanswered. Background texture may remain ordinary
and unexamined. Once prose, staging, or recurrence foregrounds something as an
anomaly or clue, it owes the corresponding practical, evidential, or relational
function. Nothing exists merely to fill a weirdness quota, advertise symbolism,
or make an authorial diagnosis visible.

`Normalized` does not mean that characters inexplicably fail to react. They
address the local problem according to their knowledge and incentives without
announcing its genre category. Credible danger interrupts comic timing and
requires proportionate practical or qualified help; eeriness never excuses a
missing safety response.

Ask:

- If removed, does any person act, know, risk, permit, refuse, or expect
  differently?
- Can the other characters respond to it according to local incentives?
- Does its eeriness arise from exact evidence rather than missing content being
  treated as intent?
- Is the institution's response plausible even when the combined situation is
  disturbing?
- If this is a local burst, does its aftermath reveal or alter an interaction
  rather than merely displaying the author's randomness?

These noncanonical contrasts are tests, not scene templates:

| Construction | Functional | Decorative or unsafe |
|---|---|---|
| Weird intrusion | A misplaced procedure changes access, timing, activity, or who receives information | A prop is conspicuously wrong only to look ominous |
| Eerie absence or agency problem | An expected presence or unexplained intervention becomes exact enough to alter evidence or expectation | Missing or unimplemented content is treated as meaningful absence |
| Absurd burst | An impulsive line or action exposes a specific pressure and forces a reply, correction, or chosen silence | A random sentence appears and nobody registers it |
| Competent continuation | A role performs the literal routine while its changed social premise creates a concrete consequence | A role ignores credible danger so the scene can remain uncanny |

An absurdity can burst out before someone fully reflects. Surprise does not
prove literal causelessness, dissociation, quantum origin, incapacity, or
release from responsibility.

## 7. Dialogue, action, objects, and recurrence

### 7.1 Dialogue and interaction

Dialogue and interaction must be long enough to make the scene's particular
pressure and dramatic function legible. The scene may change conduct,
knowledge, opportunity, leverage, or expectation; establish a baseline the
audience will later need; or make a costly failure to change newly visible. It
need not force an irreversible turn. Rhythm, resistance, misunderstanding, and
changed conduct are diagnostic possibilities, not four mandatory beats. Length
alone is not value; do not pad an exchange with repeated flirtation, equivalent
branches, literary pauses, or decorative mystery.

Characters may speak directly. They may ask someone to stay, identify desire,
use a relationship label, or say `I love you` when the statement is
character-consistent and consequential. Direct speech is an observable
character action, not an author-certified account of every motive, promise,
exclusive future, or previous sign. Interpretive openness comes from plural
motives and consequences, not compulsory coyness.

The prose may magnify what a character cares about, but daily interaction must
not feel brutally arranged to deliver the author's thesis. A line or pause
should exist because someone wants, notices, avoids, protects, enjoys, or
misreads something.

### 7.2 Objects and spatial details

Foregrounded or recurring objects do practical work before they accumulate
interpretation. Background texture may remain ordinary without acquiring a
plot function. Before treating an object as relational evidence, establish the
relevant facts of access, visibility, custody, movement, retention, loss, or
misidentification. A recurring object is not a fixed symbol; its meaning may
change with custody, context, angle, and knowledge.

A detail may be psychologically active without becoming a clue, foreshadowing
device, metric change, or later payoff. Conversely, a conspicuous object or
procedure designed chiefly to announce a theme should be reduced or removed.

### 7.3 Repetition and discrepancy

Repetition earns its place when an exact object, phrase, action, or spatial
relation establishes continuity the audience will need, or recurs from an
altered position and changes the available evidence or pressure. Once the
baseline is legible, unchanged recurrence should be compressed unless its
rhythmic duration is itself the active pressure. Do not duplicate prose merely
to imitate *La Jalousie*. The work may permit incompatible readings; it may not
secretly decree one observation wrong without supplying a lawful basis for
revision.

**Approved route-specific model, not a universal quota:** the umbrella history
uses a relationally scoped facet mosaic. No one carrier recaps its offscreen
event; different messages, dating scenes, or endings may expose different
present facts, while even their audience-visible union leaves chronology,
initiation, motive, and relationship meaning unresolved. An umbrella remains
an object rather than a declared metaphor, and no scene is constructed merely
to deliver its clue.
See the ledger's
[umbrella forward law](../docs/design/2026-09-02-narrative-constitution-working-decision-ledger.md#51-forward-law)
and
[relationally scoped mosaic](../docs/design/2026-09-02-narrative-constitution-working-decision-ledger.md#52-relationally-scoped-mosaic).

### 7.4 Incidental cast economy

A one-use person does not receive a name merely to deliver exposition,
logistics, causality, or interpretation. A functional role may still refuse,
interrupt, misunderstand, enforce a procedure, alter a room, or leave a durable
record. Name someone when the audience must later remember that person's
identity, recurring agency, relationship, or accountability. Economy must not
make the university feel empty or convert workers into scenery.

## 8. Relational evidence, Sweet, and Dark

### 8.1 Love is not an authenticated variable

No isolated action proves love. Attachment becomes more legible through
character-specific deviation, context, recurrence under different pressures,
and the recipient's real freedom. Candidate modes include:

- selective attention;
- voluntary return after practical usefulness ends;
- chosen cost involving convenience, status, certainty, or control;
- exposure of a preference without disguising it as usefulness;
- a refusable approach; and
- changed conduct after harm rather than a performance of remorse.

These are not a ladder. Correct consent practice is a baseline, not a romantic
prize. Cost may manipulate; repair may be strategic; private conduct may rule
out some performances without proving sincerity. No perceptual prose or ending
frame authenticates jealousy as the correct interpretation. Let it appear
through selection and conduct: whose sentence receives an answer, whose absence
changes timing, and—only when an authorized surface can actually present it—
whether an act continues after its intended witness leaves. A missing carrier
never proves that private conduct continued.

Before treating one of these modes as legible, identify the character-specific
baseline and deviation through the
[Character & Relationship Handbook](02-character-relationship-handbook.md#character-faithfulness-gate).
Generic kindness, sacrifice, jealousy, or intensity is insufficient.

### 8.2 Nonmoral Sweet/Dark law

**Sweet** and **Totally Dark** are neither good/bad nor weak/strong romance.

Lowercase psychological darkness, danger, jealousy, pain, or unsettling imagery
may inhabit any scene. **Totally Dark** is narrower: it classifies decisive
character conduct, not the mere presence of horror atmosphere or a dark
capacity.

- **Sweet** foregrounds tender, playful, ordinary, or less-dangerous facets.
  Dark capacities may remain present as pressure, impulse, or near-action, but
  the character's darkest instrument does not become the decisive method for
  securing attachment.
- **Totally Dark** makes a character-specific darker facet become chosen
  conduct with concrete consequence because desired attachment makes one
  identifiable freedom, truth, safety, limit, relationship, or other protected
  interest expendable.

Harm remains harm. Attachment does not make injury harmless, remove
responsibility, create consent, or certify deeper love. Sweet need not cure
earlier violations. Totally Dark need not be more sincere, passionate, or
truthful.

Before assigning Totally Dark, record who chooses which darker instrument,
whose identifiable protected interest becomes expendable, and what concrete
consequence follows. The form does not require every participant to share the
same motive, approve the act, or reinforce the pattern. Another person's
complicity exists only when her knowing action or permission is lawfully shown.

Forms must differ through one or more substantive changes in conduct, access,
cost, or consequence rather than palette, diction, cruelty level, or insert
length. If the label can be removed without obscuring the chosen act and its
consequence, the distinction is legible; if only the label makes it Dark, the
plot has not earned the classification.

The game has no author-certified good route, bad route, true relationship, or
best ending. Physical events, capacity, consent, harm, pleasure, loss, and
dependency remain exact even when characters disagree about meaning,
forgiveness, or value.

This manual does not decide which character romantically desires another.
Current approved plot and Bible reconciliation own that truth. When a character
is permitted to express desire, style must not force her into coyness merely to
preserve ambiguity.

## 9. Writing board-selected continuations and impulse variation

The Minesweeper board is nondiegetic. Characters never know that a board result
caused their conduct; inside the fiction, established wants and the immediate
situation remain the cause. The same structural result may therefore select
different conduct in different scenes. Exploded is not globally irrational or
Dark; Perfect is not globally loving, truthful, or optimal.

Every terminal cause made available by a challenged scene needs an authored
resolution. This is normally a scene-authored continuation. In an
owner-approved Angela-absent visible Priscilla–Lavinia presentation, however,
`Exploded` ends audience access immediately and supplies no post-challenge
dialogue, action, reaction, or closing gesture. That cutoff is a presentation
boundary, not character conduct: it does not prove that the fictional meeting
ends, that either woman fails to answer, or that their relationship worsens.
Any offscreen conduct or durable residue still requires an explicit authored
and persisted plan; the cutoff alone creates none. This narrow rule does not
make `Exploded` a globally early, irrational, Dark, or punitive result, and it
does not decide the still-separate Angela-attended Group presentation.
The bounded source and collision repair are recorded in the
[Angela-absent pair explosion reconciliation](../docs/design/2026-09-06-angela-absent-pair-explosion-visibility-reconciliation.md).

The writer supplies the local dramatic meaning; the
[runtime behavior record](../docs/design/2026-09-02-relationship-progression-runtime-behavior-record.md)
owns total deterministic mapping, freezing, persistence, progression timing,
and replay behaviour. Do not duplicate those mechanisms in prose notes or let
Dialogic presentation calculate them.

A scene normally uses two to four primary response families, with three as a
soft target, but only when that many materially distinct continuations exist.
This is a plot-complexity ceiling, not a quota or a required enum.

Response-family names describe observable conduct—such as refusal, direct
request, verification, withdrawal, delegation, or reinforced control—not a
certified inner feeling. A family name does not by itself assign relationship
readiness, a persistent outward stance, concrete carryover, character
knowledge, or Observer evidence; author each only when the plot actually uses
it. Several board causes may converge only when the
authored action and response, consent and material facts, downstream
consequence, character knowledge, and audience evidence remain the same.
Warmer wording, a different expression, or an unused board-history detail does
not justify a branch. If later fiction or eligibility reacts to a difference,
the continuations have not fully converged.

For a visible Group board, Angela is the counterfactual subject: the result
selects an authored Angela action, intervention, refusal, alignment,
withdrawal, or deliberate non-action, after which Priscilla and Lavinia respond
autonomously. When a visible Angela-absent pair continuation supplies fictional
conduct, its registered subject is Priscilla, Lavinia, or their joint outward
choreography; it selects conduct rather than certifying a private thought. The
approved `Exploded` visibility cutoff itself selects no character conduct. A
Private-offscreen event has no simulated board result, and a Prevented event
has no meeting or residue.

Any durable pair residue is concrete rather than numeric: an action, promise,
refusal, debt, object handling, exposed fact, or boundary the participants can
lawfully remember. It does not mutate their run-frozen relationship form or
manufacture desire, forgiveness, or consent.

### 9.1 Frozen Impulse Atom

Most challenged scenes need no Frozen Impulse Atom. Where a spontaneous local
burst materially improves the scene, use at most one window containing two or
three fully authored, character-plausible words, gestures, interruptions,
omissions, or small actions. The runtime record owns one-time selection,
freezing, and replay stability; this manual owns only the writing burden.

The alternatives remain surface microvariation. If one changes consent,
knowledge, an object, relationship progression, carried consequence, Observer
evidence, or future eligibility, write it as a material consequence variant
rather than disguising it as an atom.

Every shown burst receives an immediate aftermath: another person responds, or
the originator retracts, disguises, corrects, repeats, exploits, or knowingly
leaves it unanswered. Chance selects among actions already plausible for this
person; it does not create desire, erase agency, prove causelessness, or excuse
harm.

## 10. Ending closure, variant fairness, and mystery

### 10.1 Practically conclusive, interpretively open

Every core ending settles represented physical action and immediate material or
relational consequence while declining to certify one complete account of
motive, romance, or the week's total pattern.

An ending makes clear:

- what each materially involved character does;
- where relevant people and objects end the represented action;
- which access, custody, departure, permission, refusal, injury, obligation, or
  boundary changes; and
- what cannot be undone the next morning without another choice, cost,
  confrontation, or violation.

Do not manufacture openness by obscuring basic action, replacing causality with
atmosphere, or withholding a fact the anchor would plainly know. Openness comes
from plural, revisable motives attached to authenticated conduct. No final
confession, label, twist, or monologue solves the whole week.

An Observer coda adds, contrasts, or rearranges evidence. It does not certify a
correct route, replace the preceding core, reveal it as imaginary, or moralize
it retroactively. A later independent layer or coda must make its own new
action and evidence legible; it cannot repair missing closure in the core or
smuggle in an unseen history that contradicts the route already shown.

### 10.2 Variant non-dominance

When Group and Two-friends presentations share one encounter window, one may
not be the complete private version with bonus Angela material attached. They
may share a causal hinge and durable consequence, but each visible presentation
must own lawful evidence, interaction, and relational pressure unavailable in
the other. Neither is the complete or uniquely truthful edition. Equality does
not require equal word count, duration, or number of clues.

### 10.3 Mystery fairness

Some mysteries have a fixed production-side causal history that remains
unrevealed; others may deliberately have no single private answer. The
documentation must say which condition applies. In neither case may a private
authorial explanation rescue contradictory released evidence. Where the work
promises causal reconstruction, the released surfaces must let attentive
players reach a substantive supported account at the intended degree of
resolution without receiving final certification. A fixed history may instead
serve continuity while selected facts remain intentionally unrecoverable.
Where no single answer exists, authenticated facts and the limits of each live
account must still remain coherent rather than becoming arbitrary possibility.

- A materially incriminating clue needs a released counter-facet that supports
  or constrains its lawful provenance.
- When a fixed causal history exists, the complete clue constellation must fit
  it rather than several individually plausible but jointly impossible
  excuses. When no single answer exists, every deliberately live account must
  fit the authenticated physical and temporal facts and diverge only at
  genuinely unresolved steps.
- A fallible report must be recognizable as a report or record; authenticated
  physical fact cannot later be demoted merely to save a theory.
- Omission may preserve uncertainty, but it may not hide every necessary causal
  step.
- Each route must remain physically and causally coherent on its own.
  Cross-route synthesis may add facets or change the weight of an inference;
  it cannot repair a local contradiction or supply every missing causal step.

Characters may lie, misremember, misreport, or alter a nonclinical record when
the carrier presents that content as their act. The work may not use forged
clinical facts or actionable medical misinformation to create ambiguity.

## 11. Scene-construction card

This is a writer-facing planning aid, not a DTL schema, runtime record, or set
of compulsory beats. Complete it once at the smallest useful scene or ending
layer level. A brief message may answer each relevant item in a few words. Use
the core card for every proposed message, dating scene, or ending; attach only
the conditional modules whose risks are actually present.

### 11.1 Core card

Identify:

- the authorized narrative surface and the unit's practical entry and exit;
- the practical activity and immediate want of each materially active
  character, including a self-sufficient want unrelated to Angela where that
  independence matters;
- the exact fact, report, record, or inference each active character can
  lawfully possess;
- the dramatic burden: a recognizable change in action, evidence,
  opportunity, pressure, expectation, access, or consent—or a needed baseline
  or costly failure to change;
- any choice, refusal, partial acceptance, or exit that the situation genuinely
  offers;
- the concrete consequence, including which later authorized carrier must
  preserve it if the plot uses it again; and
- only when perceptual prose occurs, the eligible writer-side anchor and the
  physical field available to her. Record a foreground relational lens only
  when one is actually useful.

Do not manufacture an individual motivation ledger for incidental workers who
perform no materially distinct action. Do not expand the card merely because a
scene is long; expand it when the scene carries more causal risk.

### 11.2 Challenge and board module

For a challenged scene, add:

- the board class, its counterfactual subject, and every reachable terminal
  cause;
- the small set of authored response families and the cause-to-family map;
- the exact reason for any convergence;
- any scene-authored relationship-readiness effect or persistent outward
  stance, and the conduct that earns it;
- the conduct, material or consent fact, carried consequence, character
  knowledge, and audience evidence that each family changes or preserves; and
- any optional impulse-atom candidates and the immediate aftermath of each.

The writer records only readiness effects and consequences that the approved
plot actually consumes; she does not calculate tiers or endings. Technical
freezing, commit order, storage, validation, and progression evaluation belong
to the runtime record.

### 11.3 Mystery and evidence module

When a scene carries mystery evidence, add:

- whether production has a fixed causal truth, a supported but uncertified
  account, or deliberately no single private answer;
- the status of each important carrier as authenticated physical fact,
  received report or record, character inference, audience evidence, or
  audience inference;
- the lawful provenance and any released counter-facet required by an
  incriminating clue;
- what the scene supports, what it weakens, and what it leaves unproved; and
- any plot-bearing physical-world assertion that must be verified before the
  premise can be approved.

### 11.4 Ending module

For a core, prelude, later layer, or coda, add:

- the layer's one dramatic question and its role in the ordered experience;
- the exact action, final represented positions of people and objects, and any
  change in access, custody, departure, permission, refusal, injury,
  obligation, or boundary;
- the consequence that cannot be casually undone the next morning;
- the interpretive remainder after practical closure; and
- for a later layer or Observer coda, the new lawful action or evidence it owns
  without replacing, correcting, or moralizing the preceding core.

Eligibility, evaluation order, persistence, and Gallery identity remain in the
mechanical design and runtime record. This module does not invent them.

### 11.5 Hospital and credible-danger module

When Hospital, incapacity, injury, or credible danger is present, add:

- the established condition or visible symptom that makes the response
  proportionate;
- Angela's exact consciousness, capacity, knowledge, and consent at each
  relevant action;
- each helper's lawful role, access, and limits, including the route to
  qualified help;
- which necessary safety action interrupts ordinary banter, flirtation,
  conflict, or uncanniness; and
- which causal facts are authenticated and which suspicions remain inference.

Apparent professionalism never creates clinical authority. Do not use a false
medical claim, forged clinical fact, or actionable harm method as mystery.

### 11.6 Group, pair, and form-variation module

When Group, Two-friends, pair residue, or Sweet/Dark variation is present, add:

- each continuous passage, its lawful anchor, and any explicit anchor handoff;
- the evidence and pressure owned uniquely by Group and by the Angela-absent
  presentation, so neither becomes the complete edition;
- the exact separation among audience evidence, Angela's knowledge, and the
  pair's private knowledge;
- any concrete pair residue and its later carrier; and
- for Sweet/Dark or another substantive form distinction, the acting
  character, chosen conduct, affected protected interest, and concrete
  consequence—not merely warmer diction, a harsher image, or a darker mood.

A Private-offscreen encounter supplies no transcript, anchor, board, or
audience witness merely because a visible counterpart exists elsewhere.

### 11.7 Removal and pressure tests

Use only the questions that match the scene's real risk; answering every test
is not a mark of quality:

- **Character-local attention:** Would this person care about the selected
  detail if the route label, clue ledger, and audience disappeared?
- **Lens necessity:** Does the optional lens sharpen this relationship's exact
  pressure, and can the passage keep one anchor if attention relays?
- **Earned turn:** If the established want, pressure, opportunity, or cost were
  removed, would the turn become arbitrary?
- **Effect economy:** Does each extended movement change leverage, knowledge,
  expectation, consent, access, or felt rhythm, or establish a baseline needed
  for a later difference?
- **Decorative mystery:** Does withholding the information change any choice,
  evidence relation, consequence, or later interpretation?
- **Socially exact absurdity:** Does the bizarre act follow a local personal or
  institutional incentive closely enough that others can respond, and does
  credible danger still receive the response it requires?
- **Anchor integrity:** Could the registered consciousness perceive every
  narrated fact from the shown position?
- **Mystery fairness:** Can the supported account be reconstructed without a
  private authorial explanation?
- **Variant necessity:** If two branches differ only in warmth, wording, or
  spectacle, should they collapse?
- **Nonmoral darkness:** Who chooses which darker instrument, whose identifiable
  protected interest becomes expendable, and what concrete consequence follows
  without being framed as the greatest proof of love?
- **Directness:** Is ambiguity coming from plural motives, or merely from making
  a character speak unnaturally vaguely?

## 13. Production use and bounded review cadence

### 13.1 Completed manual gates

The four bounded records are the
[Sections 1–4 audit](../docs/design/2026-09-06-narrative-style-manual-sections-1-4-audit.md),
[Sections 5–8 audit](../docs/design/2026-09-06-narrative-style-manual-sections-5-8-audit.md),
[Sections 9–11 audit](../docs/design/2026-09-06-narrative-style-manual-sections-9-11-audit.md),
and
[Sections 12–13 audit](../docs/design/2026-09-06-narrative-style-manual-sections-12-13-audit.md).
Their completion makes this manual usable as a writer-facing style gate. It does
not convert examples into canon, close the open plot queue, authorize code, or
replace later owner review. The owner declined subagent and cross-model review,
so all four records disclose their same-context limitation.

### 13.2 Plot-to-dialogue use sequence

Use the manual at the smallest layer that can fail:

1. establish the essential causal plot and each participant's independent want
   before decorating the scene;
2. complete the small core scene card in Section 11.1;
3. invoke only the conditional modules whose actual risks are present;
4. audition complete ending families against route reachability, core closure,
   layer composition, agency, evidence, and substantive Sweet/Dark conduct;
5. audit each completed day under Section 13.4, retaining the complete Day 1
   plot gate before expanding its dialogue;
6. audit the complete Day 1–7 architecture before detailed DTL production; and
7. during dialogue and staging, reopen only the smallest law or plot layer that
   a concrete failure challenges.

An ending audit may precede the full seven-day plot because the project uses an
end-first hybrid, but an ending remains a target and constraint until the week
earns a lawful route to it. The manual governs how approved material is written;
it never supplies the missing event, desire, clue, gate, or outcome.

### 13.3 Review trigger and stop rule

Audit is a fault-finding tool, not ceremonial approval. A bounded pass looks for
concrete collision, needless law, duplicated doctrine, character flattening,
unfair mystery, decorative strangeness, lost agency, physical implausibility,
and any instruction whose removal improves the work. Stop when the named claim
survives its recorded checks and no further high-confidence in-scope defect
remains. Preserve unresolved questions and superseded provenance instead of
stretching the pass until it appears comprehensive.

### 13.4 Plot audits at successive levels

**Owner-confirmed 2026-09-06.** Decision provenance is recorded in the
[working ledger, Section 14.2](../docs/design/2026-09-02-narrative-constitution-working-decision-ledger.md#142-plot-audit-cadence-at-successive-levels).
Review completed units while their detail remains manageable, then inspect
relationships that become visible only when those units are assembled.

| Completed unit | Main question and checks |
|---|---|
| One variant, such as Sweet–Ambiguous | Does this particular form work? Check character voice, independent wants, the specific relationship form, perceptual and knowledge limits, causal continuity, applicable choices and consequences, and the intended pleasure or tension. Use the Section 11 core card and only relevant conditional modules. |
| An assembled scene family | Do its forms work together? Compare meaningful Ambiguous/Love differences, consequential Sweet/Dark conduct, shared facts, and distinct Group/Two-friends evidence and interactions where those forms apply. A local variant pass cannot establish these comparisons alone. |
| One completed day | Can these encounters coexist or exclude one another as intended? Check invitations, scheduling, attendance and absence paths, Angela's available knowledge, emotional pacing, and assumptions about other encounters that may not have been attended. |
| A complete event across days | Does the entire causal chain hold? Check setup, prevention and absence paths, aftermath, first and repeated discoveries, carried consequences, and ending dependencies. Preserve the special raw-conversation evidence requirement in Section 12.2 for the complete P–L bar/residue audit. |

Scene-family and day reviews occur in whichever order drafting reaches them;
an event spanning several days receives its wider review once that event is
assembled. Apply only forms the scene actually supports. A shared Sweet beat
does not constitute a completed Sweet–Ambiguous or Sweet–Love variant merely
because its short dramatic arc works. Open dependencies remain explicit.

Carry forward earlier findings with references to the reviewed material and
sources. A later change reopens the affected passages and dependencies; it does
not restart every unchanged check. A broader pass examines the newly assembled
relationships. Where scopes overlap, one record may cover both if it names each
scope and its findings explicitly.

Keep each review record small: identify the unit and version or source passages
reviewed, applicable authoritative documents and later owner corrections,
concrete strengths, defects with recommended repairs, and unresolved questions
or evidence gaps. State what was checked and what remains outside the pass.
Retain Section 13.3's stop rule and the ledger's existing ablation-cycle limit.

This cadence supplements the immediate material-change, ending-family,
cross-ending ensemble, and full-week checks already required. A local pass does
not approve canon, close a broader event audit, authorize code, or lift a
candidate hold. Promotion still follows Section 12.1 and the relevant plot
approval boundary.

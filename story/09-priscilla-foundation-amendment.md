## 1. Cast, viewpoint and preserved continuity

Angela no longer exists as an in-world character. Her personality survives only as notification-wording style: restrained emotional expression, precise questions, deadpan rhythm and occasional suitable absurdity. Her scenes, studies, romances, attendance, messages and obligations are not forward plot. Notifications do not become her surviving consciousness, a fourth narrator, or a source of otherwise unavailable private truth.

Priscilla is the sole viewpoint. Self-talk includes her private thoughts, observations and hesitation; it is not restricted to audible muttering. Other women do not hear a thought just because the audience can read it. No alternate Lavinia or Sylvia viewpoint is licensed by a selected relationship storyline. Observed detail, interpretation, imagination and deliberate address remain distinct for the writer.

Sylvia is Priscilla's childhood friend, not her sister. A Priscilla-Sylvia romantic storyline is permitted. Former Angela-related functions may be reconsidered, but no entire personality, dossier, rescue debt or family access transfers by name replacement. The exact replacement childhood incident remains OPEN. In particular, the assistant's suggestion that Sylvia discovers fabricated evidence later has not been selected.

Priscilla and Lavinia can be accomplice-like and understand each other's darker capacities. This approves no specific conspiracy, injury, coercion, permission or unseen act. Their history does not make P-L the compulsory dominant attachment in a P-S storyline. The existing scoped disclosure-intervention clarification, including Priscilla's lack of moral remorse for that intervention, is preserved.

Lavinia-Sylvia interaction can remain subtle and partly unknown through Priscilla. No independent third romance route, secret affair, omniscient explanation or agreed three-person relationship is fixed.

Unaffected adult ages, backgrounds, languages and the fictional university setting remain with the Bible and Handbook. Lavinia's ordinary Day 2 return survives. Existing residence, admission, bar/residue and full chronological-audit holds remain open. This amendment supplies no shared home, key, surname, diagnosis, new sexual history or replacement backstory. The retained no-confirmed-death boundary is not repealed by horror reference examples.

## 2. Chapter flow and the relationship choice

The narrative covers seven days in a flowing chapter form, without dating/scheduling gameplay or a required visible day-advancement interface. Play begins directly in Day 1 DTL. The characters do not acquire knowledge of a seven-playable-day deadline. Useful old encounters can be adapted as plot events; the old twelve-solo/two-pair calendar is not inherited as a mandatory chapter framework.

Begin with shared interactions and no partner selected at run creation. One consequential optional self-talk choice later selects the sustained relationship storyline:

- The active **Yes** alternative leads to Priscilla-Sylvia.
- Passing the opportunity without selecting Yes defaults to Priscilla-Lavinia.

After the fork, follow the selected relationship storyline. The other woman can still appear, act independently, help, disagree or have concerns of her own. She is not erased or made a compulsory antagonist. The choice opens a causal sequence; it does not manufacture love, reciprocity or consent.

Ambiguous and Love are no longer mandatory separate run variants or relationship-eligibility tiers for these storylines. Useful writing about attraction, uncertainty, acknowledgment and intimacy can coexist. Mutually exclusive old histories cannot simply be combined: for example, the Day 2 departure and accepted-extra-stay candidates require a coherent selection before reuse.

The fork's exact action, wording, placement, chapter and labels are OPEN. Examples such as `Go with Sylvia.` are auditions, not shipped text. A middle-of-story placement is a recommendation, not a selected day.

## 3. Ending form is separate from partner and local conduct

Retain start-of-run selection for the larger **Sweet/Dark ending form**, not for the partner. It provides a coherent authored direction and necessary preparation; it does not justify an ending that contradicts what happened. Exact identifiers, draw/witnessing policy, ending catalogue and storage representation remain OPEN.

Sweet endings should deliver affection, comfort, relief and a healing conclusion. They are not merely weaker horror. Dark capacities can remain without a compulsory ominous sting retracting the ending's warmth. The women need not become harmless, morally cured or perfectly agreeable.

Dark endings may be cruel, thrilling and horrifying through character-specific conduct and concrete consequences. Dark is not automatically the character's truest self; Sweet is not automatically a disguise. Neither classification measures love or replaces a personality.

Local Challenge results do not reroll the ending form. A local loss can bring more ruthless conduct into a Sweet-form run, and a win can create a softer encounter in a Dark-form run. Later affection does not erase an earlier act. The eventual ending needs compatible preparation and must acknowledge the particular consequential facts that survive.

Exact ending scenes and counts are not selected. Imprisonment, shared ruin, fatal scenarios, Assimilation and specific Deicide scenes discussed earlier remain reference material to challenge, not a compulsory catalogue.

## 4. Local choices and Challenge meaning

Ordinary optional self-talk presents one proposed thought/action and only one **Yes** button. There is no required displayed No option. Ignoring it follows the authored default when the opportunity passes, and the button disappears. These options express possibilities Priscilla is already considering; they do not count as a Challenge or reveal the observer. The route fork uses this same presentation.

Challenges are optional, bounded by authored DTL start and end labels. Opening the panel pauses DTL; closing it resumes DTL. A started but unfinished board can be reopened and resumed within the opportunity. The result affects the plot after the end label, not merely when the panel closes.

| State at the opportunity end | Authored local meaning |
|---|---|
| Never started | Priscilla's ordinary course in her actual circumstances. |
| Started, unfinished | Priscilla chooses not to do the relevant action; she need not have physically begun it, become frightened or leave. |
| Lost | A scene-specific Dark continuation: a more extreme or ruthless pursuit that makes a concrete limit expendable. |
| Won, not Perfect | A scene-specific Sweet continuation: softer conduct leads the encounter. |
| Perfect | A successful continuation plus first observer recognition or further development of awareness. |

The board changes an authored opportunity or circumstance, not the whole personality. Sweet need not be innocent, and Dark is not merely a bad mood. A withdrawal cannot erase an act already completed in the fiction. A never-started opportunity after earlier interference does not reset the run's history.

Preserve only particular facts later passages actually use. Small variants may return to shared progression when conduct, knowledge and consequences permit. Do not create a different week for every outcome, force incompatible histories to converge, or add a continuously changing global Sweet/Dark personality meter. Neither woman becomes the final partner through an affection leaderboard that overrides the selected route.

## 5. Observer knowledge and possible epilogues

The audience is fictional Observer Pressure, also described as divine. Accepted influence is limited to available circumstances and coincidences, not unrestricted bodily override or manufacture of desire and consent. Ordinary impulses and human schemes also occur without it. This is fictional metaphor, not an empirical explanation of real psychology. Exact limits needed by an ending remain OPEN.

Only the first Perfect enables Priscilla to identify the observer. Once aware, she can recognize later completed Challenge effects, including ordinary wins and losses. An unfinished attempt adds no newly recognized interference and does not remove existing awareness. Further Perfects can deepen her understanding. Exact numerical levels and their passages remain unselected.

Priscilla's attitude emphasizes curiosity and hostility rather than obligatory romance with the observer. Recognition does not give her knowledge of every route, every private fact or unlimited power. Intentionally performing self-talk for a recognized listener requires established awareness; earlier private thoughts do not automatically become deliberate lies addressed to that listener.

Lavinia and Sylvia do not automatically inherit her knowledge. Any participation needs events, communication or inference accessible through Priscilla's viewpoint. No independently randomized observer-personality system is selected for the three women.

All Perfect does **not** compel Deicide, a confrontation, a forced exit, a partner switch, an observer romance or an unhappy ending. Priscilla can remain with Lavinia or Sylvia. A human relationship ending is complete on its own. A possible observer epilogue is a later layer only where selected and earned; its presence, response and triggers remain OPEN. It must not erase or retroactively invalidate the human resolution.

For a selected special exit, record the required facts/results before deliberately exiting the application. No save/load-horror, reload-punishment or cross-save awakening subsystem is selected. Ordinary existing save responsibilities remain unaffected.

## 6. Writing range and approval boundaries

Characters follow their own thinking and mixed motives. Do not divide every ordinary act into genuine versus deceptive, explain every impulse with a trauma or diagnosis, or make all three women successive omniscient masterminds. An impulse may remain unexplained while its actual consequences remain binding.

Preserve humor, ordinary company, chosen activity, attraction, irritation and adequate scene duration beside danger. A pleasurable beat need not change a variable. Extreme character lists and horror examples are creative references, not exhaustive personality laws or approvals of concrete atrocities.

The selection lifecycle, bounded approval, causal/knowledge review, source discipline and whole-week plotting gate remain. A foundation amendment does not approve a route-fork scene, whole chapter, complete week, ending line, asset or runtime change. Provisional dialogue and bounded auditions remain useful before full DTL production.

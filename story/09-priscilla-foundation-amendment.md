## 1. Cast, viewpoint and preserved continuity

Angela no longer exists as an in-world character. Her personality survives only as notification-wording style: restrained emotional expression, precise questions, deadpan rhythm and occasional suitable absurdity. Her scenes, studies, romances, attendance, messages and obligations are not forward plot. Notifications do not become her surviving consciousness, a fourth narrator, or a source of otherwise unavailable private truth.

Priscilla is the sole viewpoint. Self-talk includes her private thoughts, observations and hesitation; it is not restricted to audible muttering. Other women do not hear a thought just because the audience can read it. No alternate Lavinia or Sylvia viewpoint is licensed by a selected relationship storyline. Observed detail, interpretation, imagination and deliberate address remain distinct for the writer. The owner's 2026-10-19 formal preference reduces explicit grammatical person and may withhold referents in perceptual prose across both story and self-talk fields; writer-only orientation retains the actual anchor and referents. Follow the [current style application](08-narrative-style-manual.md#42-unmarked-focal-presence).

Sylvia is Priscilla's childhood friend, not her sister. A Priscilla-Sylvia romantic storyline is permitted. Former Angela-related functions may be reconsidered, but no entire personality, dossier, rescue debt or family access transfers by name replacement. The exact replacement childhood incident remains OPEN. In particular, the assistant's suggestion that Sylvia discovers fabricated evidence later has not been selected.

Priscilla and Lavinia can be accomplice-like and understand each other's darker capacities. This approves no specific conspiracy, injury, coercion, permission or unseen act. Their history does not make P-L the compulsory dominant attachment in a P-S storyline. The disclosure-intervention history and lack of moral remorse remain. Apply the later [Bible judgment refinement](01-core-story-bible.md#current-pl-judgment-2026-10-18), which broadens Priscilla's preference-based accommodation and self-justification for current P–L conduct.

**Sylvia clarification, 2026-10-19:** the [Bible's current Sylvia direction](01-core-story-bible.md#current-sylvia-triangle-2026-10-19) now makes her stronger romantic attachment to Lavinia explicit and records her attempt to obstruct P–L, including through the Sylvia-focused week. The [earlier-acquaintance adaptation](01-core-story-bible.md#current-sylvia-history-2026-10-19) is the bounded exception to automatic biography transfer. The old blanket non-romantic S–L rule cannot override this direction. Actual interaction can remain subtle and partly unknown through Priscilla. Lavinia's reciprocation, an independent third playable route, secret affair, omniscient explanation or agreed three-person relationship is not fixed.

Unaffected adult ages, backgrounds, languages and the fictional university setting remain with the Bible and Handbook. Lavinia's ordinary Day 2 return survives. Existing residence, admission, bar/residue and full chronological-audit holds remain open. This amendment supplies no shared home, key, surname, diagnosis, new sexual history or replacement backstory. The retained no-confirmed-death boundary is not repealed by horror reference examples.

## 2. Chapter flow and the relationship choice

The narrative covers seven days in a flowing chapter form, without dating/scheduling gameplay or a required visible day-advancement interface. Play begins directly in Day 1 DTL. The characters do not acquire knowledge of a seven-playable-day deadline. Useful old encounters can be adapted as plot events; the old twelve-solo/two-pair calendar is not inherited as a mandatory chapter framework.

Angela attendance, old solo/pair activation and Angela-specific ending invitations must not gate the new story. Ordinary device functions are not deleted merely because their former narrative obligations retire.

Begin with shared interactions and no partner selected at run creation. One consequential optional self-talk choice later selects the sustained relationship storyline:

- The active **Yes** alternative leads to Priscilla-Sylvia.
- Passing the opportunity without selecting Yes defaults to Priscilla-Lavinia.

After the fork, follow the selected relationship storyline. The other woman can still appear, act independently, help, disagree or have concerns of her own. She is not erased or made a compulsory antagonist. The choice opens a causal sequence; it does not manufacture love, reciprocity or consent.

**Current P–S reading, 2026-10-19:** Yes selects the sustained Priscilla–Sylvia
storyline, not a guarantee that Sylvia prefers Priscilla romantically. Her
Lavinia-directed plan can organize this storyline while the P–S bond undergoes
real changes. Exact resolution and compatibility with retained P–S Sweet/Dark
endpoint guidance remain open; this clarification does not import the single
P–L ending into P–S or silently select a replacement ending.

Ambiguous and Love are no longer mandatory separate run variants or relationship-eligibility tiers for these storylines. Useful writing about attraction, uncertainty, acknowledgment and intimacy can coexist. Any retained numerical relationship state has only its explicitly approved purpose; it does not manufacture desire, consent, truth, observer knowledge or an ending circumstance. Mutually exclusive old histories cannot simply be combined: for example, the Day 2 departure and accepted-extra-stay candidates require a coherent selection before reuse.

The fork's exact action, wording, placement, chapter and labels are OPEN. Examples such as `Go with Sylvia.` are auditions, not shipped text. A middle-of-story placement is a recommendation, not a selected day.

## 4. Local choices and Challenge meaning

### 4.1 Optional thoughts

Ordinary optional self-talk presents one proposed thought/action and only one **Yes** button. There is no required displayed No option or confirmation menu. Ignoring it follows the authored default when the opportunity passes, and the button disappears. These options express possibilities Priscilla is already considering; they do not count as a Challenge, award a Perfect or reveal the observer. The route fork uses this same presentation.

RECOMMENDATION: tie expiry to script progress rather than elapsed reading time. Make the important fork's immediate action intelligible, without presenting an affection leaderboard or promising a selected chapter/line. Auto, keyboard ordering, replay and stale-input handling require bounded review against the actual presentation owners; [Section 9](#9-implementation-handoff-and-inspection-limits) preserves that handoff.

### 4.2 Challenge opportunities

Challenges are optional. The authored DTL start label makes a Challenge available; the end label closes its opportunity. The story has a valid continuation when the player never starts it. Opening the panel pauses DTL; closing it resumes DTL and preserves an unfinished board so it can be reopened and resumed within the same opportunity. Closing alone does not resolve an unfinished outcome. Resolve the result at the end label and select its subsequent authored continuation.

### 4.3 Local result meanings

The current auditions use A–E as prose comparison labels for these five meanings. They are not a displayed five-button menu or new runtime/DTL identifiers. Read the selected result, not every alternative in succession.

| Audition label | State at the opportunity end | Authored local meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A | Never started | Priscilla's ordinary course in her actual circumstances. |
| B | Started, unfinished | Priscilla chooses not to do the relevant action; she need not have physically begun it, become frightened or leave. |
| C | Won, not Perfect | A scene-specific Sweet continuation: softer conduct leads the encounter. |
| D | Lost | A scene-specific Dark continuation: a more extreme or ruthless pursuit that makes a concrete limit expendable; not an automatic visibility cutoff. |
| E | Perfect | A successful continuation plus first observer recognition or further development of awareness. |

The board changes an authored opportunity or circumstance, not the whole personality. Sweet need not be innocent, and Dark is not merely a bad mood. Perfect and ordinary success may share appropriate action text without sharing awareness consequences. A withdrawal cannot erase an act already completed in the fiction. A never-started opportunity after earlier interference does not reset the run's history. [Terminal identities and scoring](#91-terminal-identities-and-unaffected-technical-ownership) retain their separate technical status.

Preserve only particular facts later passages actually use. Small variants may return to shared progression when conduct, knowledge and consequences permit. Do not create a different week for every outcome, force incompatible histories to converge, or add a continuously changing global Sweet/Dark personality meter. Neither woman becomes the final partner through an affection leaderboard that overrides the selected route.

## 5. Observer knowledge and possible epilogues

The audience is fictional Observer Pressure, also described as divine. Accepted influence is limited to available circumstances and coincidences, not unrestricted bodily override or manufacture of desire and consent. Ordinary impulses and human schemes also occur without it. This is fictional metaphor, not an empirical explanation of real psychology. Exact limits needed by an ending remain OPEN.

Only the first Perfect enables Priscilla to identify the observer, at the opportunity's authored resolution and not before its cause. Once aware, she can recognize later completed Challenge effects, including ordinary wins and losses. An unfinished attempt adds no newly recognized interference and does not remove existing awareness. Further Perfects can deepen her understanding. Exact counts, opportunity identities and awareness passages remain OPEN; earlier illustrative thresholds are not constants to implement.

Knowledge, attitude and capability are separate. Priscilla's attitude emphasizes curiosity and hostility rather than obligatory romance with the observer. Recognition does not give her knowledge of every route, every private fact or unlimited power. Intentionally performing self-talk for a recognized listener requires established awareness; earlier private thoughts do not automatically become deliberate lies addressed to that listener.

Lavinia and Sylvia do not automatically inherit her knowledge. Any participation needs events, communication or inference accessible through Priscilla's viewpoint. No independently randomized observer-personality system is selected for the three women.

All Perfect does **not** compel Deicide, a confrontation, a forced exit, a partner switch, an observer romance or an unhappy ending. Priscilla can remain with Lavinia or Sylvia; all Perfect does not automatically cancel a compatible Sweet closure. A human relationship ending is complete on its own. A possible observer epilogue follows that completed ending only where selected and earned, with an authored predicate and established fictional means. Its presence, response and triggers remain OPEN. It must not erase or retroactively invalidate the human resolution.

For a selected special exit, record the required facts/results before deliberately exiting the application. This is a deliberate authored exit, not a requirement for a real fault. No save/load-horror, reload-punishment or cross-save awakening subsystem is selected. Ordinary existing save responsibilities remain unaffected.

## 6. Writing range and approval boundaries

Characters follow their own thinking and mixed motives. Do not divide every ordinary act into genuine versus deceptive, explain every impulse with a trauma or diagnosis, or make all three women successive omniscient masterminds. An impulse may remain unexplained while its actual consequences remain binding.

Preserve humor, ordinary company, chosen activity, attraction, irritation and adequate scene duration beside danger. A pleasurable beat need not change a variable. Extreme character lists and horror examples are creative references, not exhaustive personality laws or approvals of concrete atrocities.

The selection lifecycle, bounded approval, causal/knowledge review, source discipline and whole-week plotting gate remain. A foundation amendment does not approve a route-fork scene, whole chapter, complete week, ending line, asset or runtime change. Provisional dialogue and bounded auditions remain useful before full DTL production.

### 9.2 Recommended implementation invariants

- One logical resolution owner per opportunity; dependent facts are applied once, not again on panel closure, resumption or redisplay.
- A completed result survives panel closure. Reopening the same attempt is not a new attempt. Confirm the already intended one-attempt policy against the active runtime before implementation.
- Distinguish panel inspection from actually starting play. First meaningful board input is the recommended boundary, not a newly approved rule.
- Thought-choice expiry follows the authored opportunity. Replay/history displays cannot activate live choices. Review Auto and keyboard/input ordering explicitly.
- Both relationship storylines remain reachable with zero Challenges. The fork does not require a hidden affection quota or a Perfect.
- Loading preserves the selected ending form and the route/state of that save, subject to the unresolved P–L form handling in [Section 3](#3-ending-form-is-separate-from-partner-and-local-conduct). Returning to a pre-fork save can allow the other branch; presentation alone does not award a fresh Perfect.
- Count distinct resolved opportunities for awareness. For any future all-Perfect predicate, define the required set explicitly: shared plus selected-route opportunities, not inaccessible alternate-route boards or merely the boards the player happened to finish.
- No premature finale because all boards encountered so far are Perfect. A special ending still has an authored ending condition.
- Reuse existing DTL, state, persistence and panel owners. This file selects no service, class, variable schema, save migration or automatic repair architecture.

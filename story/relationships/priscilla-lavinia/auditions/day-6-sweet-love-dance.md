Lavinia sets the teapot on the table.

Priscilla takes her cup. Her bag is beside the chair.

Music is playing. Lavinia leaves her phone where it is instead of looking for another track.

They listen for a while.

Priscilla puts her cup down.

“Shall we?”

Lavinia turns toward her.

Priscilla tips her head toward the space beside the table.

“Not to this. Wait.”

Lavinia picks up the phone and looks through the music. Priscilla remains seated.

She starts another track, listens to its opening, and sets the phone down.

“This one.”

Priscilla gets up and offers her hand.

Lavinia takes it. They move into the space beside the table. Lavinia places her other hand on Priscilla’s shoulder; Priscilla comes nearer and rests a hand at her back.

Priscilla looks down as they begin.

Lavinia makes her next step smaller.

“You’re still watching your feet.”

“This is an improvement.”

Lavinia laughs.

They do not move far.

Lavinia begins a turn and feels Priscilla follow. On the next one, Priscilla changes direction first. Lavinia adjusts without saying anything.

The table comes into view beside them again, then passes out of Lavinia’s direct sight.

When the song finishes, Priscilla glances toward the phone.

“Another?”

“Not yet.”

Lavinia keeps her hand in Priscilla’s. She takes another step, and Priscilla moves with her.

They continue for a little while without the music.

Eventually, Lavinia stops.

“I want my tea.”

Priscilla releases her hand.

They return to their chairs. Lavinia picks up her cup and drinks.

“Could we hear the first one again?” Priscilla asks.

Lavinia starts it.

For a while, she listens with her cup resting against her knee.

“There’s another recording,” she says.

“Shall we try that?”

“After this. I want to hear the end.”

Priscilla takes a drink.

Lavinia leaves the phone on the table.

## 2. Inherited foundation

The candidate inherits only the following shared Sweet foundation:

1. Lavinia makes a fresh invitation. Priscilla chooses to accept it on this
   occasion, and Lavinia admits her into the flat. Priscilla arrives as a
   visitor; prior acceptance supplies no key, domestic standing, automatic
   access, or promise of another visit.
2. The late tea is taskless. Neither woman invents an errand, injury, work
   obligation, or rescue to justify wanting the other's company.
3. Music is already playing while they converse. Both may genuinely enjoy it;
   the song is not a lyrical cipher for their relationship.
4. A track changes. Priscilla asks for the previous track again because their
   conversation covered part of it. Lavinia restarts it; they hear this replay
   without the original obstruction.
5. Priscilla then asks once more in an unusually bare form. The first replay has
   already repaired what she missed, so this second request is not another
   repair. It makes her present desire for another hearing observable without
   explaining that desire. `Again` is the current audition shorthand, not
   approved final DTL. Lavinia restarts the track a second time.
6. The pair Minesweeper challenge occurs after that second restart.

## 3. Essential Sweet–Love plot

After a visible clear:

1. A different track and a locally intelligible mundane exchange are already in
   progress. The audience need not recover an offscreen anecdote to understand
   that the women have continued talking during the unwitnessed interval.
2. Lavinia says, without dressing it as praise or confession, that she preferred
   the initial hearing—the one their conversation interrupted.
3. Priscilla identifies the apparent defect: they talked through part of it.
4. Lavinia confirms her preference. She does not explain it with `because` or
   translate it into a relationship claim.
5. Priscilla returns to the negligible matter already under discussion. While
   she is speaking, Lavinia returns the original track—not to its beginning, but
   to the passage their first conversation covered. This third playback neither
   repairs nor completes a hearing. It places that passage beneath company
   already being freely given.
6. Priscilla's sentence does not break. Lavinia answers its ordinary content,
   and their exchange covers the same musical passage again.
7. At least one further ordinary turn begins after that passage has passed.
   Music, conversation and the visit continue beyond the excerpt.

The passage stops there. It does not add a meaningful smile, held look, touch,
seat change, farewell, future invitation, overnight implication, or explanatory
observation to prove that the exchange mattered. It does not time the dialogue
with theatrical precision, add a verbal cue solely to explain the playback, or
turn the reconstruction into a polished ritual.

## 5. Why the interrupted hearing works

The first replay removes the original obstruction and supplies a more complete
hearing. The second is no longer needed for that practical purpose. Lavinia then
discloses that completion was not what she preferred. What she selects is one
particular occurrence in which the music and Priscilla's ordinary speech
occupied the same time.

After the return cut has first established an unwitnessed interval through a
different track, Lavinia goes back only to the passage their speech covered. The
third playback therefore changes kind: it is neither repair nor another request
for musical completion. Lavinia does not manufacture speech, request a
performance, or translate the desire into `I wanted to hear you`. She places
the passage beneath conversation already being freely given.

Priscilla's uninterrupted sentence and continued participation are observable.
Because her speech was already in motion, they do not authenticate whether she
understood, accommodated, or simply continued through Lavinia's action. Lavinia
answers the ordinary content rather than explaining the playback. Her deliberate
selection is legible; each woman's complete private account remains unavailable.
Their accumulated familiarity permits the conduct to precede explanation.

This is reciprocal legibility through a strange practical act, not a larger
romantic reward or author-certified relationship label. It does not establish:

- a relationship label or confession;
- safety, innocence, pardon or moral repair;
- exclusivity or a future together;
- permanent domestic access;
- certainty about why Lavinia preferred that hearing;
- certainty that either woman calls the exchange love.

### 7.1 Entry and settling

Show that Priscilla has been freshly admitted on this occasion. A doorway,
coat, umbrella, shoes, cup, or other ordinary handling may do this, but no prop
may imply a key, stored belongings, cohabitation, bedroom access, routine
overnight stay, or permission that survives the visit.

The entry should permit a slight mismatch of anticipation. The mismatch should
create texture, not a miniature test of devotion.

**Owner-retained action grammar — 2026-09-07: The Completed Knock**

1. One physically intelligible arrival cue gives Lavinia reason to open when she
   does. Its exact source remains open; it cannot imply clairvoyance.
2. With an inward-opening or otherwise physically tested door configuration,
   Lavinia opens just before Priscilla's raised knuckles reach the panel.
3. Priscilla's hand visibly stops. She deliberately redirects it to a reachable
   jamb and completes one light knock. The action is not explained as momentum.
4. Lavinia's response remains externally rendered: she lets the knock finish
   before stepping back, without explanation or teasing. Priscilla crosses the
   threshold as a freshly invited and presently accepted visitor.
5. Lavinia closes the door. The already-playing music becomes distinct while
   they settle rather than announcing the hinge at the threshold.

The practical purpose of the action vanishes one beat before Priscilla completes
it. That wrong-order remainder is physically plausible, faintly absurd, and
unresolved: habit, insistence, caution, amusement, or something else may be read
from it, but none is authenticated. The knock does not establish fresh
admission; Lavinia's opening, permission, and movement do. Its only work is the
character-specific survival of a social form after its use has vanished. If the
tested staging does not deepen attention or uncertainty, cut the knock rather
than inventing another justification for it.

Only this action grammar is retained. Exact prose, landing sound, frame
composition, gaze duration, outside-item handling and dialogue remain open.
`Come in` is disposable audition wording; silence may be stronger. Do not stack
an offered hook, retained tray, witty exchange, repeated knock, or later doorway

### 7.2 Company before the song

Let them already be talking about something neither needs. This protects the
taskless premise and makes Priscilla's first replay request arise from genuine
divided pleasure rather than a scripted music demonstration.

Their exchange may show unequal sensory certainty, precision, irritation, or
amusement, but it should not smuggle in a confession, clue, prior quarrel, or
future plan.

Pleasure cannot be certified only in writer-facing explanation. Merely answering
one another is insufficient: politeness, competition, or irritation could do
that. The passage instead retains a genuine off-ramp followed by voluntary
surplus attention.

**Owner-retained interaction grammar — 2026-09-07: the genuine off-ramp**

## 10. Line and staging restraints

Avoid using `perfect`, `mistake`, `ours`, `with you`, or an explicit equivalent
of “I liked hearing you.” Do not let a lyric explain the preference. Do not use
the song as “their song.”

Lavinia may be plain, exact, faintly unreasonable, or irritated by the need to
clarify, but she should not deliver an analysis of her own attachment.
Priscilla may identify the practical contradiction and return to the ordinary
thread, but neither narration nor dialogue may certify whether she notices or
understands the purpose of Lavinia's playback action. She should not win the
scene by naming its meaning more elegantly than Lavinia can.

The richest absurdity is causal: one replay repairs an interrupted hearing; a
second, no longer practically required, is nevertheless requested; Lavinia then
selects the interrupted occurrence and uses a third playback to reproduce its
obstruction. Replay is used without pursuit of clearer hearing and is placed
beneath speech that required no manufacture. The category error exposes desire
through conduct. Additional oddity must deepen attention, desire, or uncertainty;
random ornament is not enough.

The scene may take advantage of *the weird* through the wrong presence of a
social form: a knock survives after the door has opened and its ordinary use has
ended. It may take advantage of *the eerie* through evidence of an unwitnessed
interval: after a clear-result return, ordinary playback shows that time and the
visit continued outside audience access. The already-in-motion re-interruption
adds a local absurd burst rather than a new paranormal claim: an accidental
obstruction is rebuilt beneath conversation already alive. Neither charge
authorizes supernatural agency, board-caused time, Angela's absence as evidence,
or another ornamental anomaly. The immediate effects remain local and visible.

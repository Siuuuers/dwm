## 5. Why the interrupted hearing works

The first replay removes the original obstruction and supplies a more complete
hearing. The second is no longer needed for that practical purpose. Lavinia then
discloses that completion was not what she preferred. What she selects is one
particular occurrence in which the music and Priscilla's ordinary speech
occupied the same time.

After the return cut has first established an unwitnessed interval through a
different track, Lavinia goes back only to the passage their speech covered. The
third playback therefore changes kind: it is neither repair nor another request
for musical completion. Lavinia does not manufacture speech, request a
performance, or translate the desire into `I wanted to hear you`. She places

Priscilla's uninterrupted sentence and continued participation are observable.

answers the ordinary content rather than explaining the playback. Her deliberate

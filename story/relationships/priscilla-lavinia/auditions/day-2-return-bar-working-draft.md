## 6. Latest substantial aftermath fragment

Verbatim passage from the visible assistant audition. This is a staging experiment after a strike; it does not supply the missing lead-in or approve its facts. This version excludes the room-extension ending above.

Priscilla lowered her hand from her cheek.

Lavinia had taken her coat from the chair.

“Sit down,” Priscilla said.

“No.”

“We can stop arguing.”

“I’m going.”

Priscilla stood when Lavinia moved toward the door. She reached it first, then turned back toward her.

Lavinia stopped.

“Move.”

“Just give me a minute.”

“I have.”

Priscilla looked at the coat over her arm.

There had been time for another drink. Lavinia had asked about the end of the chapter. Nothing on the table looked finished.

“I’m not angry with you.”

“That isn’t what I asked.”

Priscilla remained beside the door.

Lavinia waited.

Eventually, Priscilla stepped away.

Lavinia did not immediately pass her. She came closer instead and lifted her hand.

Priscilla drew back a little.

The hand stopped.

For a moment, they looked at one another.

Then Priscilla remained where she was.

Lavinia smoothed her hair back from her forehead.

“There.”

Priscilla had been preparing another argument. She could not remember its first sentence.

“Sit with me.”

Lavinia’s hand moved once more through her hair.

Then it fell away.

“I’m still leaving.”

Priscilla looked at the hand.

“Then why did you do that?”

Lavinia did not answer immediately.

Priscilla wished she had not asked. She wanted the hand back, which was a different and much less useful thing to want.

Lavinia put on her coat.

“You should finish your chapter.”

“I was showing it to you.”

“I know.”

Priscilla moved toward the table, expecting for an instant that Lavinia would follow.

The door opened behind her.

She turned.

“Lavinia.”

Lavinia remained in the doorway.

Priscilla could have asked again.

Instead, she said, “Tell me when you get home.”

Lavinia looked at her for a moment.

“All right.”

Then she left.

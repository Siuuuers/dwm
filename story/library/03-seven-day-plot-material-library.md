# Seven-Day Production Map

This Map is a derived production view; the Core Story Bible is the sole narrative authority and wins any narrative conflict, while existing code retains its owned boundaries.

## How to Use This Map

This is a private, spoiler-complete scene-production reference. Read the seven-day spine in order to preserve calendar causality, then select only the event premises that existing code makes available. The card library is deliberately separate from the calendar: a card supplies material for an available encounter, not a new date assignment.

Every counted event uses one compact construction:

`fixed spine + short relationship insert + one tone action + contextual echo`

Production descriptions are staging instructions, not narration. Released material must communicate through dialogue, audible self-talk, messages or documents, visible action and body language, interface behavior, and diegetic sound. Do not expand a card into exhaustive branch permutations.

## Code-Owned Scheduling Boundary

Existing code is the only authority for exact daily availability, time-window ownership, health conditions, relationship and tone storage, line identity, replay behavior, localization, and save/load behavior. This document does not assign any of the fourteen premises to an exact calendar window.

The fixed narrative boundary is:

- Priscilla is present from Day 1; Lavinia returns to Hong Kong on Day 2.
- On Days 1–6, Angela may attend up to two dates or scheduled actions with two different women whom code makes available.
- Days 1, 2, 4, and 6 carry their fixed mystery anchors regardless of which eligible encounter supplies the route facet.
- The Day 2 umbrella cutaway has its own fixed trigger and is not a counted event.
- A Priscilla–Lavinia counted window exists only where code offers it. If Angela separately occupies either woman during such a window, the pair does not meet and the counter does not advance.
- Day 7 has one decisive schedule action during the public Information Day.
- A personal Day 7 action appears only at ambiguous or love. An ineligible action is completely absent: no lock, silhouette, disabled label, question mark, or substitute hint.
- Alone requires Angela to select no Day 7 date and instead complete the final observation. It is an intentional action, not a failed route.

## Audience-Visible Scene Boundary

Before endings and postscripts, show plot only through Angela-attended solo dates or scheduled actions, Angela-attended group dates, and the authorized Priscilla–Lavinia private counterparts. Do not create standalone friend-without-Angela scenes for Priscilla, Lavinia, or Sylvia. The Day 2 umbrella pickup is the sole separately authorized micro-scene; it may be visible only under its fixed trigger.

Off-screen autonomy remains real but enters visible material only as residue inside an authorized scene. Private causality needs no camera, dialogue, or staging directions. Retain a hidden action only when it changes visible behavior, supplies necessary causality, or prevents a contradiction.

## Playthrough Pacing Budget

- Target approximately two hours for a first clear and approximately five hours for full completion: roughly three additional replay hours after the first clear.
- Keep ordinary study, rehearsal, volunteer, and Open Week work visible around relationship material. “Open Week” is a seven-day production period culminating in one major public Saturday Information Day, not seven full public days.
- Give an ordinary scene zero or one peripheral abnormality; give a relationship spotlight at most one relational intrusion. Reserve brief intensification for Observer or pressure material.
- Keep every extraordinary local effect physically possible. Each clue needs at least one ordinary explanation, while no explanation resolves the complete pattern.
- On Days 1–6, preserve room for up to two attended actions with different code-available women, off-screen autonomy, and a closing handoff. Never fill unused time merely to force route completeness.
- Across a full playthrough, show at most one eligible past fragment; a playthrough may show none.
- Let the emotional rhythm move from administrative uncertainty through return and competing care toward Day 6 implication, then stop expanding before Day 7’s decisive action.

## Global Event-Card Template

Every `Event` card contains exactly these seven production fields:

1. **Ordinary activity and setting** — plausible campus, conservatory, civic, or transit work.
2. **Characters present and absence version** — who can witness the premise and what continues if Angela does not.
3. **Fixed dialogue/action spine** — the irreducible beat in every experienced version.
4. **Relationship pressure** — the dramatic question pressed without assigning anyone’s desire.
5. **State-dependent insert** — a short hate/friend/ambiguous/love variation, or the four-state Priscilla–Lavinia insert.
6. **Tone-dependent visible action** — render one selected Sweet or Totally Dark action, never both.
7. **Mystery clue, fragment eligibility, and later echo** — route facet, optional fragment trigger, and explicit experienced/not-experienced residue.

Fragments never assign or advance state, select tone, or gate an ending. A leveling opportunity can advance only one tier. Intimate leveling is unavailable from hate; a non-intimate qualifying variation may advance hate to friend. Friend receives a restrained variation, ambiguous a fuller mutual variation, and love a deeper variation without another tier.

## Sisterhood Production Boundary

Priscilla and Sylvia are sisters, and Angela has known both since childhood while remaining only a long-standing occasional acquaintance of Sylvia. Establish the family relation incidentally rather than as a late twist. Family context can explain Sylvia's early awareness of Angela's name and limited old habits; it cannot explain Sylvia's exact present timing, reveal Angela's current preferences, private messages, or medical information, grant Sylvia access to private records or rooms, or supply Angela's consent.

Across family video calls during Lavinia's England attachment, Sylvia recognizes Priscilla constructing evidence of domestic continuity and correctly infers a rupture with Lavinia. She does not learn its cause, the disclosure-form conflict, either woman's private interpretation, or a relationship label, and she does not tell their parents. Whether Priscilla knows that Sylvia made this inference remains unresolved. The sisters' separately motivated actions may converge in one record or arrangement without giving them a shared hidden motive; never presume that Priscilla knows or shares Sylvia's fixed harmful intent toward Angela.

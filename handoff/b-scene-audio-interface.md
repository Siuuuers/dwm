## Observable outcome

At an admitted TEST scene boundary, the existing AudioManager activates the
registered bed. Opening and closing the computer does not suspend or restart
it. Ordinary Save retains the selected semantic plan; fresh-process Load starts
that same plan at its registered authored anchor, without replaying a narrative
action or changing Challenge/Profile/history facts.

## Verified source gap

- `scripts/data/AudioManifest.gd` has legacy music/ambience contexts and cues,
  but no scene-plan/anchor registry. It is unchanged relative to master.
- `autoload/AudioManager.gd` validates exactly four persisted context keys.
  `apply_restore_silent` force-restarts those contexts. Its newer physical
  compensation capsule is transient rollback state, not a saved authored plan.
- `scripts/audio/AudioPlaybackPort.gd` exposes `play(player_id)` and starts at
  the stream beginning. It is unchanged relative to master.
- `scripts/narrative/DialogicEntryManifest.gd` admits exact scene registration
  envelopes (versions 1 and 2); neither contains audio definitions/bindings.
- `scripts/application/restore/AudioRestoreParticipant.gd` delegates the
  existing context to AudioManager. A new fixture alone cannot supply the
  missing canonical admission and persistence path.

Independent read-only review confirmed these findings. No engine was run.
The acoustic-memory-atlas design sections 7.4 and 9 already settle the essential
behavior: neutral immutable plans, deterministic selection, authored Load
anchors, and separate transient physical compensation. No new user interview
or production soundtrack choice is needed.

## Smallest proposed TEST fixture

Use a single synthetic looping ambience bed, one variant (`base`), and one
neutral plan (`TEST.audio.01`). Propose a four-second PCM WAV generated by the
fixture with an authored entrance at zero and a distinct Load anchor at one
second. A nonzero anchor makes an accidental restart-at-zero detectable.
Exact waveform and asset digest belong to the fixture; no licensed production
asset, story evidence or listening-quality claim is involved.

Bind the plan to admission of existing `scene.test.b`, before its first visible
caption, through A's canonical scene admission path. The binding must not be
an ad hoc call from a test after scene startup. Keep the same bed through the
computer interaction used in this slice. Declare it authorial and nonessential;
do not invent an unseen physical source. Other layers/cues are explicitly absent.

Proposed semantic identity is `(plan_id, plan_revision, variant_id, anchor_id)`
plus the existing canonical registration/content identity. Store these facts
with the admitted scene checkpoint. File paths, arbitrary current playheads,
fades, AudioStream objects and decorative one-shot tails are not durable facts.
The catalogue resolves the anchor to playback time; the save must not authorize
an arbitrary seek. Selected variant is fixed by registration, not RNG or hidden
relationship values. Read APIs return detached values.

rare-failure matrix. Computer-open must not revive removed Pause behavior.

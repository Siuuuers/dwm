## Latest user requirement

History preserves the complete passage read in the current authored day and clears
when the day changes. Caption scrolling uses that same History. The earlier
three-caption limit is superseded. Reconstruct from current position, choices and
Challenge outcomes; exclude Contacts. Global read/Gallery facts remain independent.

## Implemented boundary

SoloReadingSession owns projection of full live schema 5 to player schema 6:
stable entry/line or control position, registration fingerprint, current occurrence,
session token, publication high-water and one genuine caption reference. Loading
expands only the current live frame and restores full native text or the authored
held-control predecessor without executing earlier effects. Full live capture,
transaction custody, rollback and native readiness/input fences remain.

SceneReadingHistory is display-only. It follows the registered day graph using
saved choices/outcomes, stopping at the current position without executing controls
or inserting reconstructed rows into the authentic publication ledger. Bridge
History and caption scrolling share it. Production has 91 scene entries, unique
choice IDs and no authored loops. A future repeatable choice or loop needs an
explicit reconstruction policy; do not silently guess a past route.

Top-level genuine story receipts remain: GameState still derives and checks choices
and outcomes against them. Historical caption anchors are no longer required for
Load. Ending-source reading is compacted but retained; audio/context/app metadata
remains. This does not complete all approved compact-save removals, and makes no
performance claim. Old development Scene save shapes are rejected, not migrated.

Make current choices/outcomes authoritative facts with valid IDs, values and active
state before removing top-level story receipts. Audit Challenge occurrences, return

old validator. Preserve failure/retry custody in live transactions separately.

Then derive audio and required app surface from the installed programme/mechanics
before deleting the corresponding saved fields or ending-source checkpoint. Measure

does not prove less lag. New choice rules, narrative content and Day7 endpoint

Each process clones the complete saves directory. Preparation, commit and continuous public-operation envelope are reported separately. Only clocks, token/reference bookkeeping, a counter and a shallow cache-array copy intervene between the two public calls; canonicalization, deep-copy proofs and hash oracles occur afterward. The nested timing scopes must not be added.

The measured envelope includes real public prepare/commit and physical disk I/O with a fixed clock and immutable capture provider. It excludes live UI/capture owners, issuer flushing, subscribers, rendering, human confirmation and physical input-to-paint. Process-fresh does not mean cold OS caches. Four pairs per cache mode cannot establish statistical significance or a general gameplay speedup.

Witness-specific fault/restart, refusal, consent, Quick Save and ordered physical-operation equivalence are still prerequisites for any production adoption. Passing this success-path measurement is not that acceptance.

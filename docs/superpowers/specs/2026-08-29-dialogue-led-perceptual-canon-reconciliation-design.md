# Dialogue-Led Perceptual Canon Reconciliation Design

## 1. Status and objective

This specification records the owner-approved correction from an absolute
dialogue-only surface to a **dialogue-led surface with sparse, character-bound
perceptual prose**. It also records the small set of reusable story laws approved
in the creative discussion but not yet stated adequately in the Core Story Bible
and its derived Character & Relationship Handbook.

The correction is architectural because the current August design, Bible,
Handbook, Beatbook, and Causal Matrix do not describe perceptual prose with one
consistent authority boundary. It changes story-delivery guidance only. It does
not select a premise, write final dialogue, place Room 2.17, change a relationship
valve, modify runtime code, or alter any calendar or message mechanic.

This work is a separately approved post-reconciliation amendment anchored at
`ec6b4cbcd843856d4c8475bfe294aed5ec59ebad`. It is not retroactively part of the
earlier Tasks 0 through 9 authorization.

## 2. Owner decisions

The owner approved all of the following on 2026-08-29:

1. sparse perceptual prose may appear to the audience rather than existing only
   as private staging guidance;
2. its desired discipline is *La Jalousie*-like material exactness tethered to
   the characters' own perception, not imitation of one novel or an omniscient
   narrator;
3. examples must remain scarce so they do not become compulsory templates;
4. the previously approved “why these seven days matter” and relationship-state
   amendment remains accepted; and
5. the wider conversation audit should promote only reusable, explicitly
   approved laws, while scene candidates and admired wording remain outside the
   Bible until their own approval path is complete.

## 3. Chosen architecture and rejected alternatives

### 3.1 Chosen: authority-wide dialogue-led reconciliation

The Bible owns one exact perceptual-prose law. The Handbook translates it into
performance tests. The August design, Beatbook, and Matrix receive only the
minimum terminology corrections required to stop contradicting that law.

This is preferred because it gives future scene work one inspectable contract
without creating a new narrator system or allowing an exception to hide in a
lower authority.

### 3.2 Rejected: a Bible-only carve-out

Changing only the Bible would leave the August design and Beatbook saying that
such prose cannot be released. Future authors and agents could follow either
instruction and remain locally defensible. The conflict would therefore survive.

### 3.3 Rejected: a full narration subsystem

Named narrators, selectable internal-monologue modes, viewpoint UI, narration
settings, new runtime schemas, and accessibility-specific prose variants are not
needed to establish the story law. They would prematurely turn a restrained
surface technique into a feature system.

## 4. Audience-visible perceptual-prose contract

### 4.1 Dialogue remains the dramatic engine

Dialogue carries the relationship turn. Visible action, diegetic text, objects,
and sound remain primary evidence. Perceptual prose is sparse support: it may
register an exact object, bodily sensation, position, line of sight, repetition,
replacement, omission, or immediate judgment when that fact becomes salient to
an on-scene consciousness.

The prose may be subjective without announcing itself as an interior-monologue
box. An immediate judgment is not permission to transcribe unrestricted private
thought. The prose may withhold the reason an observation matters. It may not
diagnose an emotion, state another person's private motive, reveal a fact the
declared anchor did not perceive, explain symbolism, identify an unresolved
cause, or tell the audience which interpretation is correct.

### 4.2 Perceptual anchors

- An Angela-attended solo or group scene, Angela-owned ending or postscript, or
  Hospital passage may use Angela-bound prose only while Angela is conscious and
  can physically perceive the stated fact.
- An authorized audience-visible Priscilla–Lavinia surface—including a private
  counterpart, pair ending or postscript, or the Day 2 umbrella cutaway—may
  declare Priscilla or Lavinia as its anchor. Group presentation with Angela
  present defaults to Angela.
- An unowned past fragment has no character-bound prose because assigning an
  anchor would assign memory ownership. It retains dialogue, action, objects,
  sound, and other already authorized external presentation.
- Gallery and Rehearsal replay inherit the source passage's anchor and cannot add
  one. No UI or archive surface becomes a new consciousness.
- When no eligible character is conscious and perceiving, there is no
  character-bound perceptual prose. This includes an Angela-owned passage while
  Angela is unresponsive unless another viewpoint has been separately approved.
- Separately presented visual or audio evidence may exceed what the anchor
  notices only through its already authorized external channel. It cannot be
  restated as perceptual prose or become character knowledge.
- The active anchor remains stable through a continuous passage. Unmarked
  head-hopping is forbidden.
- A private-offscreen event supplies no prose, scene, board, or private knowledge
  merely because a private-visible counterpart exists elsewhere.
- No perceptual anchor creates a new audience-visible surface or makes another
  character playable.

### 4.3 One contrast, not an example library

The written guidance may retain one compact contrast:

- lawful reaction-test surface: `The chair was comfortable. This was not a useful
  answer.`
- unlawful explanation: `Priscilla moved the folder because she feared Lavinia
  would leave.`

The first is immediate, bounded evaluation. The second dictates another person's
private motive. Neither sentence becomes final DTL merely by appearing in this
specification. The Bible itself contains no illustrative prose, and future scenes
must discover their own material grammar rather than repeat chairs, folders, or
elliptical corrections.

### 4.4 Omission and accessibility boundary

Not every visual or audio fact receives prose. Some evidence may remain subtle or
missable. Missing, unseen, or unreceived evidence proves only the absence of that
carrier for the relevant audience or character unless an authorized system
separately records more. It does not prove that an event did or did not occur,
identify authorship, or settle an unresolved cause.

The base plot-design surface is not required to restate every visual fact for
TTS. Any later optional description or accessibility implementation receives its
own design and may not force the base prose to explain the mystery.

## 5. Reusable canon package

### 5.1 Why these seven days matter

Open Week does not manufacture attraction in order to justify a seven-day
progression. Neutral institutional duties place existing familiarity, desire,
curiosity, investment, receptivity, care, and control methods inside one shared
timetable. Experience may expose, test, deepen, or reciprocate them. Records,
expectations, attendance, absence, and practical consequences make previously
deniable or postponable conduct harder to isolate.

Observer Pressure may amplify clustering or urgency but cannot originate desire,
consent, obligation, or a decision. Day 7 culminates the accumulated pressure; it
is not the first consequential act, a sudden discovery of love, a compulsory
confession, an official label, or the end of the characters' lives beyond the
week.

### 5.2 Relationship state and Day 1 temperatures

`Friend` names the starting degree of reciprocal enactment available in Angela's
route. It does not mean the absence of attraction, flirtation, long familiarity,
dangerous comfort, or one-sided investment. Promotion changes what may become
mutually legible and consequential; it does not manufacture desire or replace a
personality.

The three Angela pairings remain deliberately unequal on Day 1:

- **Angela–Priscilla:** correction, verification, and selective delegation form
  a familiar, relationally charged rhythm. Long practice supplies neither an
  agreed romance nor blanket permission and does not contradict Angela's lack of
  prior romantic experience.
- **Angela–Lavinia:** an existing astronomy friendship already permits natural,
  knowingly understood teasing and flirtation. Lavinia does not habitually build
  a prepared “I was only joking” escape into a charged exchange. Angela may
  recognize the register and preserve neutrality without knowing Lavinia's whole
  private desire or owing a reciprocal response.
- **Angela–Sylvia:** the baseline is asymmetric. Sylvia is already personally and
  erotically invested. Angela brings childhood recognition, occasional
  familiarity, and possible receptivity to useful care, not established
  reciprocal romantic intent.

### 5.3 Recognition is not classification

Angela's uncertainty is not simple obliviousness. She may accurately notice an
abnormality, flirtation, controlling gesture, or prepared arrangement and still
decline to classify, investigate, or answer it when doing so would create an
obligation she prefers to postpone. Normalization is selective priority, not
perceptual failure. Ignoring what she noticed remains a consequential character
choice.

A person, task, threatened result, or loss of inspectability may become more
troublesome to Angela than the impossible-looking detail itself. `Troublesome`
remains a writer-facing threshold, not a required catchphrase or supernatural
alarm.

### 5.4 Lavinia's independent return

Lavinia returns to Hong Kong on Day 2 because the agreed period of her sponsored
company attachment has ordinarily concluded. She is not recalled early by injury,
Priscilla, Angela, remorse, romance, or Open Week. Her ordinary return makes the
Day 1 roster inclusion current without explaining why the name appeared early.

### 5.5 Encounter-window semantics

`Date` is production shorthand for an Angela-attended solo encounter, not a
diegetic promise that either woman considers it romantic. A selected premise may
feel deliberate, practical, obligatory, or incidental. Apparent coincidence may
shape its surface but cannot bypass the August-owned invitation, scheduling,
attendance, Hospital, or absence law.

`Group` remains the authorized Angela–Priscilla–Lavinia presentation of a
Priscilla–Lavinia pair window. Its private counterparts remain Priscilla and
Lavinia without Angela. This clarification creates no triad route or additional
surface.

### 5.6 Incidental-cast economy

Do not invent an individuated speaking character merely to transport exposition,
logistics, causality, or emotional interpretation through the seven-day surface.
Routine institutional labor may remain ambient or become known through a record,
object, queue, room state, schedule, message, or changed circumstance.

This is an economy rule, not a denial that a university contains other people.
Already established parents, the childhood culprit, the bar pursuer, visitors,
and functionally necessary qualified help remain bounded to their existing or
physically required roles. None receives a new subplot, name, or interpretive
authority by convenience.

### 5.7 Character-agency and author-greed gate

Before a premise or beat may survive, its author must be able to answer:

1. Would this character initiate or continue the action without the author's need
   for a clue, symbol, romance beat, or shock?
2. Could she physically know every fact she uses?
3. Is she using her own established control signature rather than borrowing
   another woman's method?
4. What may she refuse, redirect, accept partially, ignore, or leave?
5. What consequence makes continued non-action costly?
6. Does each woman retain a task and motive not organized around Angela?
7. Is a beautiful idea making the character perform for the author?

A dossier constrains a performance but never claims to exhaustively define a
person or reduce every act to one wound. A failed gate requires mutation or
rejection; elegance does not excuse character betrayal.

### 5.8 Interpretive, mechanical, and physical restraint

Material facts remain exact while their emotional or global causal meaning may
stay unsettled. Show behavior and consequence before explanation. Keep sweetness
genuinely attractive and danger genuinely nearby. Prefer normalized procedure to
cinematic threat signals. Avoid graphic brutality used only to raise intensity,
sentimental reconciliation, ornate symbolic decoding, coy feyness, melodramatic
declarations, and dialogue written as a solution key. Randomness never removes
the need for causality.

McKee, Robbe-Grillet, Barthes, Fisher, and other named references are diagnostic
lenses, not recipes or styles the characters must imitate. State, tone, valves,
Observer rules, and other mechanics organize availability and consequence;
characters neither explain nor praise that machinery, and mechanics never settle
desire or interpretation.

A plot-bearing medical, astronomical, geographic, technological, scheduling, or
institutional claim must be verified before premise approval. Until verified, it
remains a candidate or fallible character inference rather than private world
fact. Failed verification reopens or revises the premise. Knowledge required for
a character-faithful action needs an established lawful channel and explicit
approval; it cannot be smuggled into dialogue.

## 7. Explicit exclusions

This amendment does not promote or place:

- the Day 1 proofing-room premise, its wording/authorship challenge, weather
  fallback, choices, outcomes, or Priscilla-refines-Angela mirror;
- `The Bright Point`, the sky photograph, location deduction, permission error,
  alt text, `Behind glass`, Angela's exact reply or silence, or attachment-card
  behavior;
- Room 2.17's weekday, final dialogue, Angela-present adaptation, Missed
  adaptation, or Sylvia occupancy-mark payoff;
- `Literal, Absurd, Evidential` as a compulsory slogan;
- a new scene, supporting-character subplot, relationship label, romance route,
  triad route, Observer language, or ending;
- final DTL, camera grammar, TTS description behavior, image storage, photo
  rendering, or any other Godot implementation.

Room 2.17 remains `APPROVED CAUSAL CORE — PLACEMENT UNSELECTED`. Its current prose
remains `REACTION TEST` material even though the reconciled contract now permits
that kind of prose to become audience-visible after separate approval.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

The design is complete when:

- dialogue remains primary while rare character-bound perceptual prose is
  unambiguously legal;
- no prose can grant unlawful knowledge or dictate emotional, symbolic, or
  mystery interpretation;
- the week, Friend tier, unequal relationship baselines, Angela's selective
  recognition, Lavinia's return, encounter labels, cast economy, and authoring
  gates are stated in their proper authorities;
- no attractive candidate has been promoted by proximity;
- prior Pass One mechanics and archive provenance remain unchanged; and
- a new independent reviewer declares the combined branch ready or reports exact
  blocking findings before integration.

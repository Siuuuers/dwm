**Status:** Approved after fresh-audit repair. The accepted design still records `implementation_authorized: false`; approving or committing this document does not authorize runtime execution.

**Goal:** Compose the registry-backed committed Schedule from `dwm-p2r.7` and the exact desktop-board lifecycle from `dwm-p2r.9` into one saved Schedule view, finite warning queue, atomic Done transaction, and real Days 1–7 production flow.

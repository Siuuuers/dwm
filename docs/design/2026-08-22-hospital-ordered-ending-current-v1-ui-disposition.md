# Hospital and Ordered-Ending Current-v1 UI Disposition

## 1. Accepted correction and objective

The project owner selected every recommended **A** in the current-v1 Hospital
and ordered-ending UI batch on 2026-08-22, then clarified one exact Hospital
occupancy law:

> Hospital may show zero or one friend, and the only possible friend is Sylvia.

This disposition records the resulting simple current-v1 projection. Hospital
and ordinary ordered-ending steps consume the same Room-Owned Aperture, caption
material, and six-control rail as other witnessed scenes. They add no UI that
explains why an event happened, identifies a speaker, evaluates a relationship,
announces an ending, or interprets observed evidence.

The artifact narrows player-facing Hospital and ordered-ending presentation
only. It does not change story content, Hospital or ending qualification, route
order, relationship consequences, Minesweeper results, Gallery discovery,
durability, Backup transactions, or title behavior outside the exact seams
restated below. It authorizes no implementation.

## 2. Shared current-v1 laws

### 2.1 One ordinary witnessed host

Hospital and each ordinary ordered-ending step use the current Room-Owned
Aperture above the Registered Material Field and the exact rail:

`History | Skip | Auto | Save | Load | Next`

They create no dedicated Hospital dialogue widget, ending template, portrait
register, speaker banner, participant roster, interpretation pane, Continue
button, or compatibility host.

The aperture is pointer-inert and nonfocusable. Normal activation belongs to the
current caption. The rail retains its accepted labelled order and functional
state grammar.

### 2.2 No Angela projection or public speaker identity

Angela receives no scene art, portrait, hand, reflection, shadow, silhouette,
slot, gap, missing icon, or assistive art node. Her nonprojection is identical
in Hospital and every ordinary ending step.

No visible label, caption ornament, portrait emphasis, History prefix, TTS
prefix, or assistive announcement publicly discloses speaker identity. Internal
speaker ownership may remain available to validated story playback, but the
player-facing UI never projects it.

### 2.3 Evidence without equivalent scene description

The UI does not convert aperture art into participant lists, room summaries,
medical summaries, object descriptions, action prose, narrator text, or
alternative evidence. Captions, History, functional controls, and their
operational accessibility remain available; the aperture's visual fineness is
not translated into an explanatory substitute.

No current surface introduces a scene icon, anomaly symbol, route colour,
relationship status, ending class, or other graphical sign that assigns
meaning to the composition.

### 2.4 Atomic publication

A scene or ending-step state becomes visible only when its complete approved
aperture composition, caption state, and rail state are ready. The aperture
composition includes the background, every character layer both permitted and
required by the consumer-specific occupancy law, and every consumed public
object/action overlay. A state requiring a character layer forbidden by that
consumer's occupancy law is blocked before publication rather than partially
projected. The host never exposes an empty loading composition, `Preparing`,
half-populated body arrangement, placeholder silhouette, or layer-by-layer
arrival.

Ordinary composition changes use atomic static replacement. The complete
background, allowed character, and consumed object/action layers publish as one
settled aperture; no layer independently fades, slides, scales, or ghosts
across states.

## 3. Hospital current-v1 projection

### 3.1 Exact friend occupancy

Hospital has exactly two lawful friend-art states:

| Hospital state | Visible friend art |
| --- | --- |
| Sylvia absent | none |
| Sylvia present | Sylvia's one finished artwork |

No other friend appears in Hospital. Sylvia-present Hospital places her artwork
at the accepted single-friend centre placement. Sylvia-absent Hospital shows the
authored treatment-room background and any already-approved public object state
without reserving Sylvia-shaped or Angela-shaped space.

Hospital does not create a generic clinician illustration, uniformed silhouette,
speaker icon, or stock medical figure. A current-v1 shot that requires a
non-friend clinician to speak or perform an essential visible action cannot
publish under this art contract. It requires owner-approved content restaging
that obeys the current host, or a later architecture that explicitly supersedes
this disposition. The UI may not improvise, omit, or nameplate a substitute.

### 3.2 No Hospital chrome

The treatment-room background, Sylvia's presence when applicable, public
objects, and published captions are the complete audience-facing evidence.
There is no:

- `Hospital`, `Treatment`, `Recovery`, or location heading;
- diagnosis, cause, danger, pressure, health, relationship, or discharge panel;
- empty or disabled Minesweeper frame, countdown, result, or challenge control;
- Hospital-specific Continue button, completion notice, or route preview; or
- explanation of a missed date, Sylvia consequence, deferred pair scene, or day
  transition supplied by UI.

### 3.3 Fresh caption and History session

Hospital begins one fresh semantic caption and History session. Neither the
visible caption leaves nor its History import captions from the desktop, a
prior date, an invitation, or another scene.

History contains only captions actually published in Hospital, in publication
order. It contains no generated room description, character roster, speaker
identity, clinical summary, route label, completion record, receipt, or private
presentation-atom prose. Inspecting History remains non-mutating.

When Hospital completes, a later Dating or Priscilla-Lavinia presentation begins
a fresh semantic session under the same visual host. No Hospital caption leaf or
History row crosses that boundary, and no seam heading explains the change.

### 3.4 Captionless silent visual beats

A genuine authored room-only or silent visual hold creates no blank caption,
zero-width text, invisible activation target, or clickable aperture. Its
authored timing is the real presentation fact. The host progresses when that
timing completes; the first ordinary player-operable narrative state begins
when a real caption is published and owns focus.

Pause or other already-authoritative suspension freezes a live hold rather than
letting it expire underneath the suspended surface. A silent hold is never
invented to conceal loading, persistence, routing, or failure time.

### 3.5 Hospital completion frontier

Ordinary activation past the final stable caption, or a lawful `Next` reaching
the already-witnessed completion boundary, submits Hospital's existing physical
completion operation. It does not first reveal a Continue control or completion
message.

If settlement is immediate, the next lawful surface replaces Hospital with no
visible intermediate state. If a genuine multi-frame transaction remains in
flight, the host preserves the exact settled treatment-room background, Sylvia
art if present, caption leaves, and rail geometry. The final caption remains the
readable semantic anchor. The rail uses its ordinary truthful unavailable
morphology and leaves focus traversal; accepted inputs are consumed rather than
queued.

The hold displays no spinner, percentage, `Saving…`, `Discharging…`, success
message, route label, or authored minimum duration.

### 3.6 Hospital technical recovery

Failure uses the trusted nonfiction recovery owner above the last truthful
surface:

- before Hospital publication, Retry may rebuild the complete candidate or the
  safe return action may restore the intact prior surface;
- after Hospital publication, Retry reconstructs the exact last settled
  Hospital state or the governing safe-return action preserves already-witnessed
  material; and
- during an admitted completion, recovery reconciles the factual transaction
  frontier before deciding whether Hospital or the next lawful surface owns
  presentation.

Recovery never fabricates a generic treatment room, guesses completion, rolls
back witnessed captions, exposes internal IDs, or offers a fictional Continue.

## 4. Ordered-ending current-v1 projection

### 4.1 Step composition

Each ordinary ordered-ending step consumes its exact authored background and
the finished artwork of only the friends physically present in that step. The
current supported layouts are background-only, one friend at the accepted
centre placement, or two friends at the accepted fair left/right placements.
Angela receives no art or reserved geometry.

No ending step displays a public ending title, step title, sequence number,
classification, route label, participant roster, or completion percentage.

### 4.2 One visible caption memory across steps

The ordered chain is one caption session. In single-language presentation, an
ordered-step boundary does not clear retained caption leaves. When the next
step's first current caption publishes at the lowest position, eligible prior
lines remain in their existing receding order and fall away only through the
ordinary visible-depth limit.

This persistence is chronology, not a label asserting explanation or causation.
The room may change while prior words remain materially present.

Dual Language retains its accepted current-caption-only topology. It does not
add retained bilingual leaves merely to imitate the single-language visual
effect.

### 4.3 One uninterrupted ending History

History remains one chronological ledger across every step actually presented
in the current ordered chain. It contains only published captions and exposes
no step headings, internal roles, classifications, public ending titles,
prerequisites, participant descriptions, or speaker identities.

History outlives the visible caption depth but does not rewind playback, change
an ending, grant evidence by inspection, or create absent lines from completion
receipts alone.

### 4.4 Atomic ordered-step seam

After the existing full nonfinal durability gate passes—including every
applicable completion, Gallery/profile fact, prerequisite, and cursor receipt—the
next complete approved aperture composition publishes through one atomic static
swap. The Registered Material Field and rail remain stationary and do not fade.
Retained single-language caption leaves remain in place; the next first caption
enters through the ordinary bottom-up law.

There is no whole-host crossfade, blended ghost frame, blackout, neutral matte,
paper interlude, Continue action, chapter card, title card, count, or seam copy
between nonfinal steps. Held pointer, touch, keyboard, and controller activation
must release before the new first caption accepts input.

### 4.5 Transport state at the seam

If Auto is visibly On at a durable nonfinal step boundary, it remains visibly
On. The prior timer and callbacks are discarded; the next first caption receives
one complete new reveal and delay cycle.

Skip is Off before the next step begins. `Next` remains a one-shot operation
bound to the prior step, and the activation that reaches completion is consumed.
It cannot reveal, focus, speak, or complete any beat of the next step.

### 4.6 Completion holds and rail truth

During every genuinely admitted ending-step completion hold, the exact settled
background, friend art, caption leaves, material field, and rail geometry remain
visible. The rail uses the ordinary unavailable morphology and is absent from
focus traversal; controls never look actionable while silently ignoring input.
The final caption remains the readable semantic anchor.

The hold has no spinner, percentage, `Preparing`, `Saving…`, ending title,
Gallery notice, achievement, or authored dwell. If durability is immediate, no
hold is perceptible.

A nonfinal step publishes the next step under Section 4.4 only after its
inherited full durability gate passes. A final step proceeds to the neutral
matte under Section 4.7 only after its inherited full final durability gate
passes. This disposition does not redefine either gate.

### 4.7 Neutral matte and title landing

After the existing full final durability gate passes, the complete ending host
withdraws to one textless neutral material matte. The matte owns no prose, logo,
quote, art, colour classification, progress, focus, or interaction. It accepts
and queues no input. This disposition inherits that gate without narrowing or
redefining its completion, profile, cursor, journal, and boundary-save facts.

The matte has no dramatic fixed dwell and is not user-skippable. It remains only
for the real host-to-title publication interval. Reduced Motion uses the same
static handoff without a fade.

The ordinary title then publishes atomically and owns its ordinary `New Acc`
focus. There is no credits presentation, ending acknowledgement, summary,
completion badge, Gallery notice, automatic Gallery opening, or Return button.

### 4.8 Save and failure feedback ownership

Hospital and Ending add no local save toast, banner, icon, or status line. Save
and Load invoke the ordinary trusted Backup owner. An immediate operation never
shows `Saving…`; a genuinely pending multi-frame operation may report its real
pending state only inside the owning Backup or trusted recovery surface.

Failures remain nonfiction and appear above the last trustworthy Hospital or
ending composition. No ending-specific fictional error, glitch, celebratory
failure, generic fallback art, or raw internal identifier appears.

## 5. Explicit deferrals and exclusions

The following do not belong to current-v1 Hospital or ordinary ordered-ending
presentation:

- Observer `CAPTURE`/`COMPARE` History grammar;
- the Observer false-cursor restraint mechanism;
- a visual anomaly or unseen-first random variant generator;
- special-ending flashes or compositions that have not yet passed the separate
  no-Angela-art feasibility audit;
- future avatar or photo-frame treatment;
- richer cameras, close plates, pose libraries, ambient motion, or irregular
  aperture frames; and
- all audio design.

Those future UI directions remain governed only by the approved Deferred UI
Feature Register. Their absence creates no placeholder, locked icon, hidden
branch, setting, save field, asset requirement, or compatibility scaffold here.

## 6. Narrow supersession and retained law

### 6.1 Superseded within this scope

For current Hospital and ordinary ordered-ending UI, this disposition
supersedes older directions requiring or permitting:

- portrait registers, visible Angela art, speaker emphasis, or public speaker
  tokens;
- Hospital art occupancy beyond zero friends or Sylvia alone;
- generic clinical figures or Hospital-specific UI chrome;
- History-generated scene, participant, medical, action, or system prose;
- narrative choices, choice state, or result selectors;
- clearing visible caption memory at every ordered-step boundary;
- whole-host or per-character inter-step fades;
- apparently actionable controls during a completion hold; or
- ending-specific save, completion, credits, or title-return feedback.

The general Room-Owned Aperture capacity does not authorize Hospital to consume
additional friend bodies. The Hospital-specific zero-or-one-Sylvia law in this
disposition is the narrower consumer rule.

### 6.2 Retained without redesign

This disposition retains:

- Hospital trigger, consequence, receipt, ordering, and resolver ownership;
- ordered-ending plan, step identity, qualification, durability, persistence,
  Gallery receipts, and route ownership;
- exact six-control rail order and current caption activation law;
- ordinary Backup, Pause, Settings, focus, localization, and recovery owners;
- bottom-up caption publication and Registered Material Field geometry;
- no credits and ordinary title return;
- no player-selectable narrative choices; and
- the complete separation between witnessed UI and story interpretation.

## 7. Verification requirements for later implementation

Any later implementation plan must prove at minimum:

- Hospital projects exactly zero friend layers or Sylvia's one finished layer,
  with no other friend, Angela layer, gap, icon, or generic clinician art;
- Sylvia uses the accepted single-friend centre placement and no-Sylvia Hospital
  reserves no person-shaped geometry;
- Hospital History contains only actually published captions from that semantic
  session;
- silent visual holds create no blank focusable caption or aperture input path;
- immediate completion publishes no intermediate status, while a real pending
  frontier preserves the exact composition and truthful unavailable rail;
- pre-publication, post-publication, and in-flight completion failures expose
  only actions valid at their factual frontiers;
- retained single-language caption leaves survive every nonfinal ordered-step
  seam and obey the ordinary depth limit;
- Dual Language remains current-caption-only;
- every step swap is atomic and static, with no ghost composition or whole-host
  fade;
- Auto, Skip, and Next reach the next step in exactly their Section 4.5 states;
- completion holds remove rail actions from focus and display unavailable rather
  than apparently actionable controls;
- the neutral matte owns no dwell, focus, queued input, copy, art, or status;
- immediate saves never show `Saving…`, and Ending creates no save-feedback
  owner beside Backup; and
- no deferred or excluded feature leaves a current runtime scaffold.

# Minesweeper Maintained Survey Worksheet Standard Palette and State Disposition

## 1. Accepted decision and objective

The project owner approved every recommended Minesweeper Standard design batch
and the complete consolidated design on 2026-08-23.

Minesweeper becomes one **Maintained Survey Worksheet**: a pale calculation
sheet seated inside cheap but correctly maintained university equipment. The
instrument is exact, quiet, and familiar before its consequences become
impossible. It records a covered cell, a negative estimate, a clear, an
explosion, and a post-clear marked mine with the same administrative sincerity.
It assigns none of those facts a meaning.

The private material shorthand is:

> Night contains the instrument; the instrument contains the worksheet; the
> worksheet exposes only the evidence produced by play.

The selected direction translates the earlier Calibrated Survey Worksheet and
After-hours Archive studies through the Haunted Instrumentarium and current
Standard grammar. It rejects a cream-dominant whole application, a sealed
horror device, a soft generic card interface, and nostalgic Windows
Minesweeper imitation.

This disposition is a finishing visual-system layer. It does not redesign the
board catalog, generator, commands, metrics, paid desktop session, replacement
law, Assignments, Rules copy, challenge consequences, Rehearsal isolation,
persistence, recovery, or audience agency. Minesweeper results remain the sole
choice-like narrative agency; this document changes no result or consequence.

It closes only:

- the exact Standard native host, band, register, dock, board-well, scrollbar,
  cell-target, terminal, and local-sheet geometry;
- the After-Hours Standard and Midnight Standard material mappings consumed by
  Minesweeper;
- the application-specific cell alphabet and its relationship to the shared
  functional alphabet;
- the visual composition of Minesweeper-owned facts, legal overlaps, and
  owner-level custody;
- the desktop and challenge terminal projections, including the non-Perfect
  two-target state;
- the in-band Rules and Assignments material and focus anatomy; and
- the first Minesweeper Standard native-proof family.

It authorizes no Godot implementation, asset production, localization writing,
audio work, save migration, schema or registry mutation, Beads work, plan
execution, High Contrast or colour-differentiation tuple, accessibility
certification, or release claim.

### 2.3 Narrow clarifications and supersessions

The following owner-approved Standard clarifications are exact:

1. **Full-rect application.** Full rect means the shell-provided Minesweeper
   content rect, not the whole 640-by-360 host. Minesweeper adds no app-local
   outer padding, window, title bar, title, X, or second host frame.
2. **Exact 100-percent bands.** The former logical minimum bands become the
   exact Standard 100-percent native bands in section 3.2. At 125 or 150
   percent, real copy may grow the register or dock and yield worksheet height.
3. **Fixed register bays.** Desktop uses the seven exact native bays in section
   3.3. Challenge and Rehearsal omit difficulty and Rounds without reflowing
   substitute content into their absence.
4. **Terminal currencies.** This disposition narrowly supersedes the earlier
   requirement for app-local terminal Money and Minesweeper Coins. Their exact
   current values remain visibly and accessibly present through Angela's
   persistent shell HUD. Minesweeper does not duplicate them. This changes no
   currency value, reward, transaction, timing, announcement, or ownership.
5. **Desktop terminal register.** Cleared or Exploded occupies the three former
   difficulty bays. Rounds and Mine estimate become honest blank capacity.
   Frozen Foresight and No flag remain in their established bays.
6. **Local sheet anatomy.** Rules and Assignments replace only the worksheet
   band with an opaque Worn-Cream sheet. They are not floating whole-board or
   whole-app dialogs. The status register and dock remain visible, truthful,
   inert, and outside the active foreground subtree.
7. **Scrollbar capacity.** The worksheet always reserves bottom and trailing
   gutter capacity for the current target size. It instantiates a scrollbar,
   thumb, hit target, focus target, and assistive node only when that exact axis
   overflows. An empty gutter is inert structural habitat.

Native notation in this disposition is evidence-space notation. One native
pixel remains two logical pixels. It does not replace the retained logical
canvas contract.

  fictional technical fault.

If a visual token and the retained product authority disagree about a fact,
the visual token is invalid.

### 3.5 Worksheet well and scrollbar capacity

The worksheet band reserves one trailing and one bottom gutter equal to the
current target size:

| Target mode | Content well | Trailing gutter | Bottom gutter |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ordinary desktop | 376 by 222 | 24 | 24 |
| Ordinary challenge | 456 by 222 | 24 | 24 |
| Large desktop | 368 by 200 | 32 | 32 |
| Large challenge | 448 by 200 | 32 | 32 |

The bottom-right gutter corner is inert. A scrollbar surface appears only on an
axis whose exact mounted grid exceeds the corresponding content-well extent.
Where content fits, the empty gutter remains habitat with no track, thumb,
button, target, focus, Hover, Press, or accessibility node.

An instantiated dark scrollbar uses controlled instrument material, Fog Blue
structural registration, and a Bone-Text thumb separated from the track by at
least one continuous native pixel of controlled well. Its thumb truthfully
reflects viewport and extent. A sheet-owned paper scrollbar instead uses
Worn-Cream well, contextual-dark structure, and contextual-dark thumb with the
same separation.

The target allocation, not merely the visible thumb, meets the current 24- or
32-pixel target contract. Scrollbars do not introduce arrows, labels, a
minimap, coordinates, zoom, or explanatory affordance.

For reproducible board scroll geometry, let W and H be the worksheet-band width
and height, T be 24 in ordinary mode or 32 in Large mode, A_w = W - T, and
A_h = H - T. The reserved content well is (0,0,A_w,A_h). Vertical overflow is
`mount_height > A_h`; its rail is (A_w,0,T,A_h). Horizontal overflow is
`mount_width > A_w`; its rail is (0,A_h,A_w,T). The reserved corner
(A_w,A_h,T,T) is always inert. A rail exists only when its overflow predicate
is true; the corresponding reserved gutter otherwise remains empty habitat.

For either instantiated axis, let L be rail length, A be viewport extent, M be
mount extent, and s be the integer scroll offset in [0,M-A]. Its thumb length
is `max(T,floor(L*A/M))`; its leading offset is
`floor((L-thumb_length)*s/(M-A))`. These expressions use native integers and
apply independently to both axes. Focus-driven scrolling clamps s before the
thumb is derived; it never rounds the board origin fractionally.

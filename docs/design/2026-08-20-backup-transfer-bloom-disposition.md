# Backup Transfer Bloom Disposition

## Current direction

The **Unclassified Transfer Bloom** remains the current approved Backup visual
direction. Use the refined companion study
`.superpowers/brainstorm/1476-1786711376/content/backup-unclassified-transfer-bloom-final-direction-v3.html`
as the present aesthetic evidence.

This disposition preserves the already approved save lifecycle and truth law.
It does not authorize production implementation or supersede persistence,
accessibility, failure, recovery, or operational-copy requirements.

## Deferred future refinement candidate

The **Distributed Transfer Field** is retained only as a future refinement
candidate. It is not part of the current visual direction and must not silently
replace the Unclassified Transfer Bloom during manual reconciliation, asset
production, or implementation.

If deliberately reopened later, the candidate may explore:

- more than one independent contact in a saved record;
- compositions without a required large or dominant contact;
- off-centre, edge-weighted, split, hollow, or scattered fields; and
- explicit exclusion masks that keep generated material outside functional
  record-name and metadata text bands.

The reviewed companion evidence is
`.superpowers/brainstorm/1476-1786711376/content/backup-distributed-transfer-field-refinement-v3.html`.

## Reopening rule

Reopening this candidate requires a new explicit owner decision. Until then,
the current Unclassified Transfer Bloom wins every conflict between the two
companion studies.

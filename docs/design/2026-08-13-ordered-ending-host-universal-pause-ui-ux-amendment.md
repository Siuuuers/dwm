# Ordered Ending Host, Universal Pause, and No-Credits UI/UX Amendment

## 1. Status and objective

This proposed written amendment records the conversation-approved design for
canonical ordered ending playback, the shared canonical Pause surface, and the
final return to title.

The conversation approved the product direction. The exact written artifact is
not accepted authority until the user reviews and approves these bytes after
self-review. Approval will establish bounded design authority only. It will not
authorize implementation, Beads mutation, plan execution, schema migration,
scene replacement, content production, localization, art, audio, tests, or a
commit.

The chosen ending experience is an **unbroken witnessed conclusion**:

- each of the accepted one through four ordered ending steps uses an adaptive
  version of the established third-person three-band proscenium;
- the host shows no ending title, semantic classification, layer count,
  achievement notice, or progress copy;
- matching physical completion and durable Gallery publication remain the only
  way to advance a step;
- a nonfinal step enters the next authored step automatically after the current
  transaction is durable;
- the final authored shot remains visible until the entire ending boundary is
  durably settled;
- no credits screen, roll, folio, route, or replay exists; and
- the completed run passes through a neutral matte directly to the ordinary
  empty title workfield.

The chosen Pause experience is one **universal glass Computer surface** wherever
canonical Pause already exists:

- a translucent left action pane contains `Continue`, `Backup`, `Settings`, and
  `Return to Title`;
- the right workfield hosts the real shared Backup, Settings, or trusted return
  confirmation;
- the surface borrows the computer's trusted material language without becoming
  Angela's desktop, launcher, or a saved app state; and
- `Return to Title` deliberately makes no new save, while the separate desktop
  `Log out` action remains the safe Autosaving exit.

### 1.1 Approved derived closures

The conversation fixed the experience. The following narrow closures make it
deterministic and testable:

- “Universal Pause” means one component and one behavior wherever canonical
  Pause already exists. It does not invent Pause in desktop Minesweeper,
  Gallery, Rehearsal, title, ordinary desktop apps, or technical recovery.
- Desktop Minesweeper retains Home/Back suspension to the launcher and has no
  Pause surface. Rehearsal retains its archive-specific Continue/Leave law.
- Canonical Dating, Hospital, canonical narrative challenges, and canonical
  ending playback consume the universal Pause component.
- The Pause Computer is presentation, not a semantic route. It owns no
  `active_app_id`, launcher, Home control, audience clock, notification lane,
  same-day desktop-app cache, game state, or save state.
- `Return to Title` is not an abbreviated label for Logout. It does not call a
  Logout or save operation and does not alter Autosave, Quick, or any numbered
  record.
- The no-save confirmation is intentionally concise and truthful. Minimal
  explanation never permits silent data loss.
- Real desktop `Log out` remains distinct: its accepted Yes path writes the
  exact live run to Autosave and routes only after durability is proven.
- An ordinary lifecycle-owned day or ending-boundary Autosave remains distinct
  from Pause. Opening or confirming Pause never creates such a trigger.
- Exact ending restore uses stable semantic presentation boundaries, not raw
  physical timeline positions. This narrowly supersedes the older rule that
  every mid-ending restore restarts the whole current unfinished ending label.
- A partial text reveal is not persisted as a glyph index. Deliberate Save
  completes the current visible line without advancing before snapshot capture.
- One ending-session History may span the ordered chain without exposing step
  headings, identities, roles, or counts.
- `Next` may seek only within the current ending step. The activation that
  reaches one step's legal completion can never enter or act on a later step.
- Continuous Skip also stops at the current step boundary and returns Off. It
  cannot carry into a newly begun semantic ending step.
- Auto may remain enabled as the profile-owned preference, but the next step's
  first beat receives its ordinary full reveal/TTS/delay behavior rather than
  inheriting a consumed timer or held input.
- No credits is product law, not an omitted asset or deferred placeholder.
- Normal final completion retains the lifecycle's silent ending-boundary
  Autosave. Pause -> Return to Title never creates or substitutes for it.
- Final title projection uses the current pending next-run Dark selector and
  title palette, never the departed run palette or the loaded ending's palette.
- Gallery-authored public ending titles debut only inside Gallery after durable
  discovery. Canonical playback itself has no title reveal.

## 4. Chosen design and rejected alternatives

### 4.1 Chosen: unbroken witnessed ending

The ending chain feels like one continuous witnessed conclusion even when the
domain plan contains several semantic identities. Familiar controls and
physical staging remain stable. Mechanical layers remain concealed.

The final shot, rather than an achievement card or credit roll, is the game's
closing image. Durability happens behind that image. The title appears only
when the ending owner proves that the complete chain and normal ending boundary
are safe.

### 4.2 Chosen: universal glass Computer Pause

Pause freezes the exact source scene and places one translucent trusted
Computer composition over it. The 480/800 split echoes the in-run workstation
without pretending the audience returned to Angela's desktop.

The left pane is a stable action spine. The right pane is a trusted workfield
for the real Backup and Settings components or the no-save return confirmation.
This provides one learned Pause behavior across every canonical host that owns
Pause.

### 4.3 Rejected alternatives

The initial release rejects:

- a bespoke ending-only cinematic player unrelated to the narrative host;
- a fixed achievement title before or after each semantic step;
- Gallery cards or public titles between canonical steps;
- `Scene X of Y`, chapter numbers, postscript labels, or ending-progress copy;
- a required Continue action between ordered ending steps;
- a direct Return button inside the canonical Ending scene;
- a vertical credits roll, static credits page, credits folio, credits menu row,
  or credits replay;
- a Pause sheet unique to each narrative route;
- a Pause Computer that instantiates the launcher or restores desktop apps;
- a hidden Autosave behind the shorter label `Return to Title`;
- destructive return without explicit consent;
- Save/Load shortcut-only access with no visible Backup path;
- a miniature narrative-only save-slot implementation; and
- silent or fictional treatment of save, load, route, or recovery failure.

## 5. Vocabulary and ownership

### 5.1 Ordered ending plan

The immutable one-through-four-step semantic plan frozen by the ending owner
after validating the exact `TERMINAL_PENDING` handoff. It is domain truth, not a
playlist assembled by the presentation host.

### 5.2 Ending step

One plan element with stable `step_id`, semantic ending identity, callable
entry identity, role, frozen context, playback form, prerequisite receipts, and
transaction token. Its state remains:

`pending -> playing -> completed`

### 5.3 Callable ending entry

One manifest-declared semantic presentation entry with closed frozen-context,
participant, shot, line, presentation-atom, completion-signal, and stable-
boundary capabilities. It is not the same thing as the semantic Gallery/save
identity it presents.

### 5.4 Physical completion

The matching token-bound completion emitted only after the current entry's
registered physical end is actually presented. Starting an entry, reaching its
last line, showing its final shot, opening History, seeking with Next, or
closing Pause is not completion by itself.

### 5.5 Stable ending beat

A registered semantic boundary reconstructible from an entry ID, frozen
context, shot ID, presentation-atom/line ID, and committed receipts. It is never
a raw DTL index, line number, file position, glyph count, tween time, or audio
sample position.

### 5.6 Final-shot hold

The locked projection of a step's last authored shot while matching completion,
Gallery, milestone, cursor, or ending-boundary work is reconciled. It is not a
new story beat, dialog choice, loading screen, or save-visible modal.

### 5.7 Universal canonical Pause

One orthogonal presentation component consumed only by live canonical hosts
that already own Pause: canonical narrative/Dating, Hospital, canonical
challenge, and canonical ending.

### 5.8 Pause Computer

The visual composition of universal Pause: a glass action pane plus a trusted
right workfield. It is not the canonical desktop shell and owns no game state.

### 5.9 No-save Return to Title

A confirmed abandonment of the mounted unsaved continuation followed by a
normal title route. It writes no player save and is intentionally distinct from
desktop Logout.

### 5.10 Normal ending-boundary Autosave

The existing lifecycle-owned automatic save after normal terminal completion.
It is neither caused by Pause nor controlled by the ending scene presenter.

## 6. Terminal admission and ordered-plan boundary

### 6.1 Exact admission

The ending owner accepts only the exact saved Day-7 handoff already admitted in
lifecycle state `TERMINAL_PENDING`.

The handoff contains, at minimum:

- one issuer-valid terminal-intent root;
- source kind `schedule_done` or `condition_action`;
- the saved terminal-intent handoff;
- a byte-equal unpublished destination outbox; and
- every prerequisite upstream receipt required by the governing cause.

Missing, already-published, changed, mismatched, or non-`TERMINAL_PENDING`
input fails before an ending identity, form, entry, plan, audio context, or
route is published.

### 6.2 Sole resolver

The ending owner alone maps the typed cause plus validated run/profile context,
freezes the accepted one-through-four-step plan, and atomically transitions the
run to ending playback.

Schedule, desktop consequence handling, relationship owners, Dialogic, and the
presentation host cannot duplicate or revise that mapping.

Identical replay returns the already-frozen plan. Changed ancestry, bytes,
identity, form, order, prerequisite, or context conflicts and starts nothing.

### 6.3 Detached presentation projection

The host receives only the current step's validated detached presentation
projection and semantic command capabilities. It receives no permission to:

- recompute ending eligibility;
- inspect hidden classifications for display;
- replace one semantic identity with another;
- change Full versus residue playback;
- skip a prerequisite receipt;
- advance the ending cursor directly;
- commit Gallery or milestone facts; or
- construct title routing on its own.

## 7. Adaptive ending proscenium

### 7.1 Shared witnessed grammar

Canonical ending playback uses the established witnessed three-band
proscenium, not an audience-view camera, Gallery card, unlock screen, or
separate cinematic player.

Angela remains a physically staged third-person character. Every speaker is
physically present and visible. No ending may introduce an offscreen voice,
fallback portrait, silhouette standing in for an absent speaker, floating
speaker-name card, or retained portrait after physical departure.

### 7.2 Exact logical geometry

The ending host uses the accepted 1280-by-720 logical canvas and inert aspect-
preserving matte.

At 100% text its functional regions remain:

| Region | Logical rect | Height |
|---|---|---:|
| Portrait register | `(0, 0, 1280, 104)` | 104 |
| Tableau | `(0, 104, 1280, 344)` | 344 |
| Caption deck and controls | `(0, 448, 1280, 272)` | 272 |

The pinned control rail owns the lowest 64 logical pixels of the caption deck.

At 125% and 150%, the caption deck computes its intrinsic functional height and
may expand upward to 392 logical pixels. The tableau yields the same amount and
retains at least 224 logical pixels. Authored focal crop/reframe protects
physical action; text never shrinks to save the artwork.

If complete functional text still exceeds the deck, caption content scrolls
vertically inside the deck while the current card and pinned rail remain
reachable. There is no horizontal text scrolling, clipping, ellipsis, overlap,
or silent font reduction.

### 7.3 Adaptive shots, stable topology

“Adaptive” means that each validated authored shot may choose its registered:

- portrait layout;
- participant blocking;
- tableau focal crop;
- lighting and restrained tint;
- visible hand/object/body close-in; and
- sparse high-value CG or presentation atom.

It does not permit arbitrary runtime reflow, speaker-driven portrait reorder,
camera-as-Angela framing, first-person Angela hands, participant omission,
offscreen speech, or decorative takeover of the caption safe region.

### 7.4 Portrait and body parity

The portrait register contains exactly the characters physically present in
the current shot, in authored left-to-right tableau order.

Body entry and portrait entry occur at one registered presentation boundary.
Body exit and portrait exit do the same. A speaker change never reorders the
register. A speaking participant receives the accepted non-colour frame/tab cue
plus restrained luminance; dimming alone is never the sole speaker signal.

### 7.5 Ending layer order

The ending host layer order is:

1. environment and authored depth;
2. physically present bodies and registered objects;
3. restrained presentation atoms and effects;
4. portrait register;
5. caption deck and inline story controls;
6. universal Pause, History, confirmation, Backup, or Settings owner; and
7. trusted technical recovery.

No effect may cover caption text, focus rings, trusted operations, or recovery.

## 8. Captions, controls, History, and transport

### 8.1 Exact control rail

Canonical ending playback retains the exact labelled rail:

`History | Skip | Auto | Save | Load | Next`

There is no `Advance` control. Normal caption activation retains the accepted
visual-novel law: one activation completes a revealing line; only a later
activation advances.

Every rail action remains reachable by pointer, touch, keyboard, controller,
and assistive activation without relying on F5/F9.

### 8.2 Caption language and history depth

Single-language presentation retains the current beat plus the previous two
available semantic beats at the selected text size. Dual Language presents
only the current semantic beat with Primary then Secondary in one card.

The ending-session History is one chronological record across all steps that
have actually been presented in the current chain. It contains no step
headings, internal roles, classifications, public ending titles, step counts,
or hidden prerequisites.

Exact restore reconstructs the selected snapshot's stored ending-session
History sequence exactly. Receipt reconciliation never synthesizes History
from completion, Gallery, milestone, or cursor receipts alone.

When a newer durable receipt causes reconciliation to skip a step that is
absent from the selected snapshot, that step's lines merge only when the same
durable authority proves the exact ordered witnessed-line/atom sequence bound
to that step token. The sequence merges once in plan order. Without that proof,
the skipped step remains absent from History. Conflicting ordered sequences
enter truthful recovery before presentation.

Restored or merged prior-step History never replays content, emits audio, or
recommits any fact.

### 8.3 Skip

Skip retains the accepted Settings choice:

- `Read only` traverses exact witnessed variants and stops at the first exact
  unseen variant; and
- `All text` may rapidly present unseen ordinary prose while preserving normal
  receipts and boundaries.

Skip never crosses an unresolved choice, acknowledged marker, effect, route,
physical completion command, or semantic ending-step boundary. Reaching a step
boundary turns Skip Off before any later step begins.

### 8.4 Auto

Auto retains its profile-owned preference, full reveal/TTS/delay order, and
stop boundaries. It never acknowledges a completion while a required utterance
or delay remains.

Auto may remain enabled across a durable inter-step transition, but the next
step begins with a new full eligible reveal/TTS/delay cycle. No remaining timer,
held Accept, or prior-step callback can advance it.

### 8.5 Next

`Next` remains the stronger one-shot seek command. It is bound to the current
step token and may traverse only exact witnessed material inside that step.

It stops at:

- the first exact unseen presentation variant;
- an unresolved choice;
- an acknowledged marker or effect command;
- an unrelated route boundary;
- the current step's physical-completion boundary; or
- any failure/recovery boundary.

When the remainder of the current exact step variant has already been
witnessed, Next may reach and submit that step's ordinary matching physical
completion. The same activation is consumed at that boundary and cannot enter,
reveal, speak, focus, or complete any beat of the next step.

### 8.6 Save and Load

Save and Load invoke the accepted Backup component in the requested mode over
the paused ending host. There is no ending-only slot system.

If Save is deliberately activated while a line is revealing, the host
completes that line's visual reveal only, does not advance, then opens Backup at
the stable semantic boundary. Successful manual Save remains inside Backup with
truthful refreshed metadata. Successful Load replaces the branch and routes to
the selected canonical state.

F5/F9 retain their accepted scope and confirmation law at eligible stable ending
boundaries. They may act without closing Pause only at the admissible Pause-root,
nonmodal hosted-Backup, or nonmodal Settings states defined in section 13.4.
They cannot bypass a trusted confirmation, binding capture, completion hold,
route replacement, restore, incompatible record mutation, or technical
recovery.

## 9. Physical completion and inter-step transition

### 9.1 Presentation never implies completion

Starting an entry never completes a step. Showing the last line or final shot
never completes it. Only a physical completion carrying the current matching
step token may prepare completion.

A mismatched, stale, duplicated-conflict, or out-of-band completion cannot
advance the cursor, unlock Gallery, commit a milestone, return to title, or
start another step. An exact duplicate matching completion is idempotent.

### 9.2 Completion transaction

The matching completion transaction reconciles, as applicable:

- current-step completion;
- the exact reached presentation signature;
- Gallery discovery and first-discovery chronology for that physical identity;
- the first-ending milestone;
- exceptional and Dark derived profile facts;
- Priscilla-Lavinia combination witness;
- following-step prerequisite receipts; and
- the run cursor.

The presentation host never applies these fields locally.

### 9.3 Final-shot hold

After the physical completion is accepted, the current final authored shot
remains visible while the completion transaction settles. Canonical content
controls, Pause, F5/F9, and conflicting input are inert during an admitted
completion operation and are never queued for later surprise execution.

The hold shows no spinner, percentage, `Preparing`, `Saving`, public ending
title, chapter number, `Ending achieved`, Gallery notice, or success sound that
could claim durability before proof.

Technical failure places the trusted recovery surface above the last
trustworthy shot. Recovery copy remains nonfiction and exposes no internal IDs.

### 9.4 Automatic next step

After the current completion, required Gallery/profile facts, and cursor are
durable, a nonfinal next step begins automatically through one restrained
static swap or short crossfade. Reduced Motion uses an immediate static swap.

There is no Continue action, paper interlude, chapter card, title card, count,
or mandatory blackout between steps.

The input that completed the prior beat is consumed. All held pointer, touch,
keyboard, and controller activation must release before the next step can
receive input. The next step's first beat then follows ordinary reveal, TTS,
Auto, and focus behavior.

## 10. Final completion and no-credits title return

### 10.1 Final durability gate

For the final planned step, the host retains its final shot until all of the
following that apply are proven durable:

- every planned step completion;
- every matching Gallery discovery/chronology receipt;
- first-ending milestone and derived profile facts;
- final ending cursor;
- completed-run fact;
- recoverable cross-store journal completion; and
- the retained normal ending-boundary Autosave.

The title cannot appear while any required result is ambiguous or pending.

### 10.2 Deliberate absence of credits

The game has no credits presentation.

There is no:

- credit roll, static credit page, credit folio, or post-ending credit scene;
- Credits title-ledger row;
- Credits Gallery, Rehearsal, or Settings destination;
- credit Skip, Auto, scroll, TTS, resume, or saved cursor;
- credit manifest, route, checkpoint, completion receipt, audio context, or
  profile fact; or
- credits playback after canonical, Gallery, Scene, or Rehearsal sessions.

This is accepted product intent pending exact-artifact approval, not missing-
content fallback, implementation omission, or deferred UI.

### 10.3 Neutral matte transition

After the final durability gate passes, the ending bands withdraw through one
neutral material matte. Reduced Motion uses a static replacement with no fade.

The matte carries no prose, title, logo, progress, unlock, quote, participant
portrait, route colour, or interaction. It masks the atomic change from the
departed run palette/audio context to the ordinary title projection.

### 10.4 Exact title landing

The completed chain returns directly to:

- the ordinary logged-out title shell;
- the current pending next-run palette rather than the departed run palette;
- the truthful audience-local title clock;
- an empty title workfield; and
- `New Acc` focus.

There is no discovery badge, newly unlocked mark, automatic Gallery opening,
ending title, ending summary, completion percentage, or Return button.

## 11. Universal canonical Pause scope

### 11.1 Included hosts

The universal canonical Pause component is consumed by:

- canonical narrative and Dating scenes;
- boardless Hospital;
- active canonical solo or Priscilla-Lavinia challenge boards; and
- canonical ordered ending playback.

### 11.2 Explicit exclusions

Universal does not invent Pause elsewhere:

- desktop Minesweeper uses Home/Back to suspend and return to launcher;
- ordinary desktop apps use their accepted Home/Back and modal laws;
- Gallery and Rehearsal use their archive-session exit law;
- title uses title navigation;
- Contacts/Shop/Schedule/Backup/Settings use their app-local roots;
- technical recovery owns only its registered recovery actions; and
- a final-shot completion hold or other admitted indeterminate transaction may
  temporarily make Pause unavailable until one stable frontier exists.

### 11.3 Orthogonal presentation state

Pause-open, Backup-open-inside-Pause, Settings-open-inside-Pause, and return-
confirmation-open are orthogonal presentation modes. They never replace the
underlying narrative, Hospital, board, or ending phase.

Pause state never enters a run save, profile save, active app identity, ending
plan, board snapshot, History, visited-line ledger, or Gallery receipt.

## 12. Pause Computer composition

### 12.1 Exact geometry

Pause covers the accepted 1280-by-720 logical canvas and preserves this exact
split:

| Region | Logical rect |
|---|---|
| Glass action pane | `(0, 0, 480, 720)` |
| Computer pane | `(480, 0, 800, 720)` |
| Computer context strip | `(480, 0, 800, 64)` |
| Computer workfield | `(480, 64, 800, 656)` |

The left pane uses a 32-pixel safe inset. Its four rows are each at least 64
logical pixels high so ordinary and Large Targets fit without topology change.

At 100%, 125%, and 150%, the 480/800 split and 64/656 right division remain
fixed. Long labels wrap vertically. If a supported locale plus selected text
size exceeds a pane, that pane scrolls vertically while focused content and
pinned actions remain visible. There is no horizontal text scroll, clipping,
ellipsis, overlap, or silent font reduction.

contrast-safe backing. No meaning depends on transparency, blur, tint, or

## 13. Hosted Backup and Settings

### 13.1 Preview versus entry

Moving keyboard/controller focus onto Backup, Settings, or Return to Title may
prepare and visibly project its detached right-pane surface. Focus alone
performs no save, load, preference mutation, confirmation, abandonment, or
route command.

Keyboard/controller Right or Accept enters the visible right surface. Pointer
or touch activation selects and enters it once. Hover never selects, focuses,
mounts, or acts.

While a right surface owns focus, the visible left pane is inert until the
surface's legal retreat returns to its exact source row.

### 13.2 Backup

Backup is the authoritative in-run nine-record component with its accepted
Save/Load modes, fixed cabinet, inspector, confirmations, fallbacks, statuses,
timestamps, transactions, and accessibility semantics.

The Pause host creates no miniature slot list or parallel cache. A successful
manual Save remains in Backup with refreshed durable metadata and truthful
`Saved`. Deliberate root retreat returns focus to the left Backup row.
Successful Load replaces the branch and routes to the loaded canonical state;
it never returns to the abandoned Pause.

If Backup is requested while a narrative line is partially revealing, the host
first completes that visible line without advancing so Backup receives one
stable semantic save boundary. A stable active board remains exact and is not
revealed, reset, or forfeited merely to open Backup.

### 13.3 Settings

Settings is the authoritative shared in-run Settings component. It retains its
seven-category order, immediate preference commits, rollback, samples, binding
capture, Records restrictions, and absence of Dark Mode.

Preference changes reproject the same frozen semantic source without advancing
or duplicating a beat. Leaving Settings stops previews/tests according to its
accepted law and returns focus to the left Settings row.

### 13.4 Save projection boundary

Neither hosted component may set desktop `active_app_id`, serialize Pause
presentation, route through the launcher, or create a second state owner.

A Save legally admitted through hosted Backup, or an F5 Quick Save legally
admitted while Pause root, an admissible nonmodal hosted-Backup state, or an
admissible nonmodal Settings state owns input, stores the underlying canonical
scene/board/ending boundary, never the glass, selected Pause row, focus object,
scroll pixels, mounted surface, preview, sample, pointer state, or animation.

Save and F5/F9 are unavailable while Return to Title or any other trusted
confirmation, binding capture, route replacement, restore, or technical
recovery owns input. They queue nothing and cannot wake after that owner
closes.

Load reconstructs the canonical destination with Pause closed.

## 14. Pause focus, input, and Back

### 14.1 Admission

Pause opens only at a safe stable frontier. If a bounded command is already
admitted, it reaches its governing stable frontier first. Opening Pause never
cancels, duplicates, or partially publishes it.

A request that cannot legally act is rejected rather than queued for surprise
execution after a modal, transaction, route, restore, or completion hold.

### 14.2 Left navigation

Pause opens with Continue focused. Up/Down moves among the four left actions
without wrapping. Tab/Shift-Tab follow the same semantic order and enter the
active right surface only when it has legal focus targets.

Focus, selected row, hosted surface, busy state, disabled state, and
destructive confirmation are distinct through text, shape, outline, and
accessible state. Colour and luminance alone never distinguish them.

### 14.3 Exact Back precedence

Back performs one legal retreat per activation:

1. technical recovery owns only its explicit actions;
2. the first applicable active confirmation inside Backup or Settings, or the
   Return confirmation, cancels under its own law and consumes Back;
3. inside Settings, exactly the first applicable Settings stage acts and
   consumes Back: cancel binding capture; otherwise stop a preview/sample;
   otherwise return sheet focus to its rail; otherwise close Settings to the
   exact left Settings row;
4. root Backup returns to the exact left Backup row; and
5. root Pause performs Continue.

No Back press both closes a child surface and resumes the scene. At Pause root,
Back never invokes Return to Title.

### 14.4 Continue

Continue or Back at Pause root restores the exact source scene, semantic focus,
board focus, and scroll required by that host. It does not:

- advance or complete a line;
- choose a reply;
- acknowledge a marker/effect;
- reveal, flag, chord, reset, or pan a cell;
- complete or forfeit a board;
- advance an ending cursor; or
- create any save or receipt.

The triggering event is consumed. Held pointer, touch, keyboard, and controller
input must release before resumed content can act.

## 15. Return to Title without saving

### 15.1 Trusted confirmation

Focusing Return to Title projects one trusted confirmation in the right
workfield. Entering it gives initial focus to Cancel.

The localized English-source meaning is exactly:

> Return to title?  
> No new save will be made. Unsaved progress will be left behind.

The actions are `Return to Title` and `Cancel`. Back means Cancel and restores
focus to the left Return to Title row.

### 15.2 No save side effect

Confirming Return to Title:

- creates no Autosave;
- creates no Quick or numbered save;
- creates no fallback checkpoint or saved-time change;
- calls no Logout or `save_for_logout` path;
- leaves all nine Backup records and their validated fallbacks unchanged;
- creates no board result, forfeit, reset, reward, refund, relationship effect,
  date completion, Hospital completion, ending completion, or Gallery receipt;
- preserves every run/profile fact that was already durably committed under its
  own owner;
- abandons only the mounted unsaved continuation;
- invalidates its live route/session/input tokens; and
- routes to the ordinary title only after the abandonment transition is proven.

An internal idempotent route/abandonment receipt may prove whether the mounted
session was discarded. It is not a player save, contains no resumable progress
snapshot, creates no Backup record, and cannot be loaded.

### 15.3 Host-specific consequences

For a narrative/date scene, Return fabricates no choice, scene/date completion,
relationship consequence, or day advance.

For Hospital, it fabricates no recovery completion, day advance, deferred pair
scene, or terminal handoff.

For a canonical challenge, the board is neither reset nor completed. This
title retreat is not the accepted causal-day departure/forfeit path. Any
profile-owned anti-reroll or attempt receipt that was already durable remains
durable.

For an ending, an unfinished step receives no completion, Gallery discovery,
or cursor advance. Earlier physically completed steps and milestones remain
durable. The run is not marked Completed merely because the audience returned
to title.

### 15.4 Safe route transaction

The title destination is prepared before the live session is discarded. A
pending or indeterminate incompatible transaction makes Return unavailable
until recovery resolves it; the command is never queued.

If title preparation fails, the current canonical scene and Pause remain
intact beneath truthful Retry/Cancel recovery. A failure cannot strand the
audience at title with a still-mounted live continuation or destroy the
continuation while leaving Pause interactive.

Successful Return clears transient host, Pause, focus, timer, TTS, audio,
preview, and presentation caches, then projects the normal empty title with
`New Acc` focused.

### 15.5 Deliberate separation from Logout

Desktop `Log out` remains the safe Autosaving exit. Its accepted Yes action
writes the exact live run to Autosave and routes only after persistence is
proven. No/Cancel does neither. Failure stays in-run with truthful recovery.

Pause Return does not rename, invoke, weaken, or replace Logout. Backup remains
available from Pause if the audience deliberately wants to save before
returning.

## 16. Ending persistence and exact restore

### 16.1 Canonical snapshot facts

An exact ending snapshot stores, when applicable:

- ordered-plan identity and fingerprint;
- cursor and current `step_id`;
- semantic ending identity and callable entry identity;
- step role, token, playback mode, and frozen context;
- prerequisite receipt IDs;
- step state and host phase;
- current stable-boundary, presentation-atom, line, and shot IDs;
- registered participant/body/portrait projection identities;
- acknowledged presentation receipts;
- seamless ending-session History identity sequence;
- completion intent, cross-store journal identity, and exact receipt stage;
- final-shot hold and ending-boundary transition stage; and
- route/session revision and idempotency records.

It never stores:

- a Node, Control, Resource instance, or focus object;
- raw DTL index, path, label position, or line number;
- glyph reveal index;
- tween, opacity, shader, particle, or animation time;
- audio stream/playhead object;
- live TTS utterance;
- pointer press, held key, controller repeat, or queued input;
- Pause row, glass state, right-pane surface, or confirmation; or
- localized prose or physical art paths as canonical truth.

### 16.2 Stable-beat Save

A deliberate Save while a line is revealing first completes that line's visual
reveal without advancing. The snapshot stores the stable semantic line/atom.
Restore shows it fully visible and never replays TTS or live announcement.

Save at any other stable ending beat captures that exact semantic beat, not the
beginning of the whole current entry.

### 16.3 Reconciliation before projection

Before reconstructing presentation, Load reconciles every incomplete
completion journal and merges monotonic run/profile receipts. It then chooses
exactly one lawful projection:

- a pending step begins at its registered first stable beat;
- an unfinished playing step resumes its saved exact stable beat;
- an uncommitted or partially committed completion reconstructs the final-shot
  hold with audience input locked and resumes from the first uncommitted receipt
  stage;
- a durably completed nonfinal step is never replayed and routes to the first
  unfinished planned step;
- a durably completed final plan resumes any unfinished ending-boundary
  Autosave/title-route stage; or
- an already completed route reconstructs title directly.

A stale run snapshot cannot resurrect a step whose matching completion is
already durable in the monotonic profile/cross-store receipts.

### 16.4 Presentation reset after Load

Load reconstructs with Pause, Backup, Settings, History, and confirmation
closed. Hover, scroll momentum, selection animation, modal transition, TTS,
held input, and samples are absent.

Auto retains its profile preference but receives one new full eligible delay.
Skip resumes Off. Next has no persistent active mode. Restore/reflow never
announces or speaks the current beat again automatically.

## 17. Ending and Pause recovery

### 17.1 Completion-journal recovery

A process loss during step completion reconciles the retained cross-store
intent before choosing presentation. Recovery may:

- resume the locked final-shot hold;
- begin the next unfinished step after durable nonfinal completion;
- complete the normal ending-boundary Autosave; or
- route to title after durable final completion.

It may never infer completion merely from a visible final shot or restarted
timeline label.

### 17.2 Final boundary recovery

A process loss after final completion but before the normal ending-boundary
Autosave reconstructs the final-shot hold and completes or truthfully recovers
that write. A loss after the boundary Autosave but before visible title
publication routes to title without replaying the final step.

### 17.3 Technical failure surface

An ending or Pause transaction failure keeps the last trustworthy source image
beneath a cleaner nonfiction recovery surface. Retry reuses the same semantic
operation and idempotency key. Cancel returns only when the operation can be
cancelled without ambiguity.

Recovery Return to Title follows its own trusted recovery contract and may act
only when every durable journal can remain safe or later reconcile. It cannot
erase an ambiguous cross-store intent under the ordinary Pause no-save path.

No public recovery surface exposes ending identity, step role, token, hash,
path, raw exception, checkpoint ID, journal filename, or schema internals.

### 17.4 Missing presentation resources

A missing optional decorative ending atom degrades through an authored neutral
fallback that changes no participant, line, physical action, completion, or
meaning. A required participant, line, shot, completion signal, manifest entry,
or accessible name failure blocks before presentation and enters technical
recovery.

The host never silently substitutes Alone, another ending, a generic portrait,
an offscreen speaker, or another route's art/audio.

right-pane swaps, and a static final matte. No essential fact relies on motion.

### 18.6 TTS

Ending Read Aloud speaks Primary only through the compatible system voice. The
public speaker token is announced only when the disclosed physically present
speaker changes, never on every line and never from a hidden actor ID.

Opening Pause stops the current utterance. TTS does not restart automatically
on Continue, pane retreat, uncover, focus restoration, text-size change, locale
change, or Load reconstruction. Deliberate Read Aloud may speak the currently
focused stable content under its accepted law.

### 18.7 Audio

Opening canonical Pause pauses the current authored music and ambience at exact
registered playheads without a pause sting. Continue resumes them exactly once.
No story SFX or voice continues beneath Pause.

Settings audio changes apply to the stored paused semantic context. A Settings
sample/test owns audio only while active and stops or restores its prior context
under accepted Settings law.

Step audio may carry across a durable inter-step boundary or change at that
boundary according to the next entry's authored audio context. It never changes
early merely because the visual final shot appeared.

Normal final completion hands audio ownership through the neutral matte to the
title context. No credits audio context exists. Essential audio facts always
have a visible physical/textual equivalent, and environmental audio cannot
introduce an offscreen speaker.

# Gallery and Rehearsal Archive UI/UX Amendment

## 1. Status and objective

This accepted written amendment records the approved Gallery and Rehearsal
archive design.

The user approved this exact self-reviewed artifact on 2026-08-12. That
approval establishes bounded design authority but does not authorize
implementation. Requirement packets, authority registries, Beads, hash-bound
plans, profile schemas, manifests, scenes, localization, tests, art, audio, and
runtime code remain unchanged until separately reconciled and explicitly
authorized.

The chosen experience is one **maintained university archive with two truthful
registers**:

- **Endings** contains only semantic endings that physically completed and
  became durable Gallery discoveries.
- **Rehearsal** contains only canonically reached starting situations and exact
  interpersonal scenes. It lets the audience revisit what Angela experienced
  and explore what could legally follow from a reached date context without
  rewriting the run.

The design has six objectives:

- make discovery feel personal rather than checklist-like;
- preserve absolute absence for every undiscovered or erased thing;
- separate exact witnessed replay from hypothetical Full Date exploration;
- let production Minesweeper play reveal hypothetical post-date dialogue while
  preventing every canonical consequence;
- fit the accepted title workstation at 100%, 125%, and 150% text without macro
  reflow; and
- keep profile history, resets, replay, interruption, and technical failure
  exact and truthful.

### 1.1 Accepted derived closures

The conversation fixed the product direction. The following derived closures
are also part of this amendment so the result is deterministic and testable:

- Canonical eligible entries are recorded from the beginning of a profile. The
  first completed ending milestone gates Rehearsal projection; it does not begin
  recording and therefore cannot erase the first run from the archive.
- A title-hosted Full Date never copies a currently loaded run or save. Each
  eligible reached pre-challenge context owns a validated, primitive,
  self-contained `rehearsal_start_seed` sufficient to reconstruct that exact
  starting situation under production rules.
- Preparing a seed does not make it visible. Its record becomes reached only at
  the registered canonical presentation boundary proving Angela experienced the
  pre-challenge situation.
- The Full Date board is the exploratory input. Starting relationship context
  is never adjustable through sliders, debug fields, or outcome selectors.
- A hypothetical board result may change the sandbox's local relationship
  values and therefore reveal its legal post-date dialogue. Those values cease
  to exist when the session ends.
- Exact repeated signatures deduplicate by stable entry identity plus signature
  hash. Repeating the same experience never creates another visible version or
  changes its original order.
- Gallery chronology uses durable first-discovery sequence, not array order,
  alphabetical sorting, timestamps, or the most recent replay.
- Observer and Special Gallery replay exposes Full signatures only. Residue is
  never offered as an archive version.
- Alone defaults to its latest physically completed Normal or Dark-mode form,
  but an older exactly reached form may be chosen under Other witnessed
  versions.
- Clear Gallery removes visible ending discoveries and their personal
  chronology but preserves the first-ending milestone, reached signatures,
  Rehearsal availability, Dark entitlement, visited History, attempt ledgers,
  saves, and live or frozen run state.
- Entire Profile Reset clears the first-ending milestone, Gallery chronology,
  reached signatures, Rehearsal seeds, visited History, and other profile facts
  while leaving Backup-owned save records untouched.
- A clean Rehearsal exit may merge only manifest-validated line identities that
  were actually presented. No other sandbox value can cross the boundary.
- Archive playback is transient. It creates no Autosave, Quick Save, numbered
  save, run branch, resumable replay checkpoint, or attempt record.

## 4. Chosen design and rejected alternatives

### 4.1 Chosen: one split registrar with two truthful registers

Gallery opens inside the accepted title workfield as one maintained campus
registrar. Endings and Rehearsal share material, focus, and navigation grammar
but remain semantically separate.

The selected view uses a fixed split:

- a left discovered-only index; and
- a right record desk containing the selected record, optional media, exact
  version choice, and one explicit action.

This approach is chosen because it:

- prevents hypothetical Rehearsal material from appearing canonical;
- keeps exact versions understandable without exposing internal signatures;
- fits long localized titles and multiple versions at 150% text;
- preserves direct comparison between index and detail;
- supports keyboard, controller, pointer, and assistive navigation without a
  deep page stack; and
- makes negative space feel intentional rather than incomplete.

### 4.2 Rejected: separate menu destinations

Separate Gallery and Rehearsal title destinations were rejected. They duplicate
navigation, visual language, replay plumbing, and failure handling while making
the relationship between reached experiences and ending discoveries harder to
understand.

### 4.3 Rejected: one blended feed

A mixed collection was rejected. It would make hypothetical sandbox lines look
like completed endings or canonical history and would weaken the visited-only
isolation boundary.

### 4.4 Rejected: full-width drilldown

A single-column ledger with pushed detail pages is operable but adds unnecessary
Back layers and makes variant comparison slower inside the available workfield.

### 4.5 Rejected: packed card catalogue

A three- or four-column card grid was rejected. It is brittle under long Chinese
and English authored titles, multiple exact versions, 150% text, and 64-pixel
targets. It also encourages hidden-slot and checklist expectations.

### 4.6 Rejected interaction and metadata models

The following are rejected:

- latest-first Gallery reordering;
- fixed character-family sections that imply missing siblings;
- immediate, double-click, or double-tap replay;
- route, tone, tier, attitude, evidence, result, ending-class, or stat dossiers;
- line totals, ending totals, percentages, locked records, silhouettes, and
  question marks;
- searching Endings or search-first Rehearsal navigation;
- mechanical signature labels;
- outcome selectors, debug buttons, stat sliders, and editable start contexts;
- fake filenames, folders, corruption, deleted-record ghosts, or fabricated
  timestamps.

## 5. Vocabulary and ownership

### 5.1 Archive

The title-hosted projection containing Endings and, after its milestone,
Rehearsal. The archive is presentation. It owns no canonical run.

### 5.2 Endings

The archive view projecting only durably discovered semantic ending identities.
There is exactly one visible record per currently discovered identity.

### 5.3 Rehearsal

The profile-unlocked consequence-free archive view containing Scenes and Full
Dates. It projects canonically reached records but executes replay inside an
isolated transient session.

### 5.4 Scene

A manifest-registered, canonically completed, physically staged interpersonal
entry with at least two registered present participants. Direct Scene replay
uses one exact reached signature and does not reopen its choices, actions, or
outcome.

### 5.5 Full Date

A manifest-registered solo or visible-pair challenge encounter whose exact
pre-challenge starting context was reached canonically. It recreates that start,
runs one fresh production challenge in isolation, applies only local sandbox
effects, presents the legal post-challenge entry, and then stops.

### 5.6 Exact witnessed version

One unique, canonically completed presentation signature identified internally
by stable entry identity and signature hash. Its public label is an authored cue
or familiar excerpt, never a hash or hidden field list.

### 5.7 Gallery discovery record

A profile-owned durable record proving one semantic ending physically completed.
It includes an idempotent discovery receipt and monotonically assigned first-
discovery sequence. The sequence is not displayed.

### 5.8 Reached presentation record

A profile-owned append-only record containing a validated entry identity,
optional ending identity, manifest/content fingerprint, exact frozen signature,
signature hash, canonical completion receipt, and monotonic completion sequence.
Rendered prose and mutable objects are forbidden.

### 5.9 Rehearsal start seed

A validated primitive record associated with an eligible reached pre-challenge
context. It contains the complete closed input required to reconstruct that
starting situation and run the production challenge/post-challenge rule family
without consulting a live run, selected save, current branch, or ambient RNG.

The seed may contain hidden mechanical facts needed by the production rules. The
archive never displays them and the sandbox may not publish them.

### 5.10 Archive record metadata

Manifest-owned authored presentation data:

- stable public record ID;
- localized 2-to-6-word title;
- one localized non-mechanical sentence;
- localized exact-version cues;
- participant and story-day facets when applicable;
- optional still or object-mark asset ID;
- Scene or Full Date eligibility; and
- the closed signature/seed schema owned by the entry family.

Metadata never grants discovery or reached status.

### 5.11 Archive view cache

Noncanonical current view, subsection, filters, selection, scroll positions, and
meaningful focus. It exists only while Gallery remains the active title
destination, including one replay round trip. It is never saved or profile-owned.

### 5.12 Visited-line merge

One atomic union of manifest-validated line IDs and required immutable History
presentation receipts that were actually presented by the current archive
session. It is the only allowed persistent output from replay.

## 6. State partition and canonical recording

### 6.1 Profile-owned monotonic facts

The profile owns:

- `first_ending_step_completed`;
- Gallery discovery receipts and first-discovery sequence;
- exact reached presentation records and completion sequence;
- validated Rehearsal start seeds bound to reached records;
- visited-line and skip-seen History under its existing owner;
- existing attempt, evidence, witness, Dark, and exceptional-replay ledgers under
  their existing owners; and
- recoverable cross-store transaction facts.

Archive UI state is not part of this partition.

### 6.2 Recording begins before unlock

The canonical recorder operates from Day 1. It records eligible completed
signatures even when Rehearsal is not yet visible.

The first-ending milestone changes only the audience projection. It does not:

- initialize a fresh reached ledger;
- erase earlier records;
- rescan prose;
- infer signatures from History;
- copy a run save; or
- fabricate encounters that were never completed.

### 6.3 Recording boundary

An entry's candidate context and any eligible Full Date seed are prepared and
validated before presentation. Neither becomes reached merely because playback
started.

At the entry family's registered physical completion or pre-challenge boundary,
the canonical recorder atomically and idempotently records:

- the exact presentation signature;
- its completion receipt and sequence;
- the matching Rehearsal seed when the reached boundary is Full-Date eligible;
  and
- any already-authoritative canonical continuation receipt.

A crash cannot publish a reached record without its validating boundary, or
advance canon while losing the corresponding record.

Ending completion additionally reconciles ending-step completion, exact reached
signature, Gallery discovery, first-ending milestone, exceptional/Dark derived
profile facts when applicable, and ending-plan cursor under the existing
cross-store transaction law.

### 6.4 Deduplication and chronology

Reached versions deduplicate by exact `{entry_id, signature_hash}`.

- An identical completion receipt and signature is an idempotent no-op.
- A reused receipt with another signature is a conflict.
- A later canonical completion of an identical signature may update an internal
  latest-completion reference but creates no second public version and does not
  reorder its first appearance.
- A genuinely different signature becomes another version with its own
  completion sequence.

Gallery order is the ascending first-discovery sequence of currently discovered
identities. Rediscovery and replay do not reorder it.

### 6.5 Self-contained Full Date seed

Every Full-Date-eligible entry family declares an exact seed schema. The seed
contains only validated primitives and finite semantic IDs. It must include all
inputs the production rules need from the reached start through the end of its
post-challenge entry, including as applicable:

- entry and encounter-family identity;
- day/window and registered participants;
- exact pre-challenge frozen presentation context;
- the affected participants' required relationship-rule state;
- pair mode/deck facts when applicable;
- due authored context required by the entry;
- the production challenge input owned by the challenge registry;
- compatible manifest, content, seed-schema, and rule versions; and
- the receipt binding the seed to the reached canonical boundary.

The schema rejects undeclared values, mutable objects, Nodes, Resources,
Callables, physical paths, Dialogic positions, arbitrary text, global RNG state,
or unrelated run sections.

The seed is not a save snapshot. It cannot resume the surrounding day or grant
the sandbox authority over Contacts, Schedule, Hospital, Shop, endings, or
another encounter.

## 7. Unlock, absence, and empty states

### 7.1 Endings is always the default archive view

Opening Gallery selects Endings. Endings exists even when no ending is currently
discovered.

### 7.2 Rehearsal availability

Rehearsal is wholly absent until `first_ending_step_completed` is true.

Before that milestone it contributes no:

- mode control or disabled gap;
- explanation or unlock hint;
- focus target or assistive node;
- index extent;
- tooltip, sound, icon, or animation; or
- inference that another feature exists.

Once the milestone is true, Rehearsal remains available for that profile until
Entire Profile Reset. Clear Gallery does not hide it.

### 7.3 Absolute absence for unseen endings

An undiscovered ending contributes no record, reserved position, category,
silhouette, lock, question mark, percentage, denominator, count, filter value,
focus node, accessible description, scroll extent, or decorative proxy.

The archive may contain ordinary negative space. That space cannot encode the
number, type, family, or position of absent identities.

### 7.4 Empty Endings

When Endings contains no discoveries, the workfield shows one quiet factual,
noninteractive empty record such as `No records are filed here yet.` Final copy
is localized content.

It shows no call to action, unlock explanation, `0/13`, progress, hidden frame,
or tutorial. The shell Return remains the escape.

If Rehearsal is available, its mode control remains available without changing
the honest empty Endings state.

### 7.5 Empty Rehearsal

If the milestone exists but no public Scene or Full Date record is currently
eligible, Rehearsal shows one quiet factual empty record. It does not expose
which entry families could someday appear.

Within Rehearsal, Scenes and Full Dates subsection controls exist only when that
subsection contains at least one visible record. No empty subsection heading or
disabled control appears.

### 7.6 Failure is not emptiness

A missing Profile owner, invalid reached ledger, missing required manifest,
incompatible signature/seed, or replay-owner failure must not project as an empty
archive. It enters the trusted technical failure boundary in section 21.

## 8. Fixed title-workfield composition

### 8.1 Outer ownership

The accepted title shell provides:

- a 960-by-656 logical workfield below its own 64-pixel strip;
- one shell-owned Return target;
- the localized Gallery host title;
- the truthful noninteractive title clock; and
- the persistent 320-pixel title ledger outside the workfield.

This amendment adds no duplicate title, clock, Return, or full-scene menu route.

### 8.2 Archive inner rect

Gallery uses a 16-pixel inset on every side. The resulting inner rect is
928-by-624 logical pixels.

When Rehearsal is available:

- the Endings/Rehearsal mode bar occupies the inner rect's top 64 pixels;
- a 16-pixel vertical gap follows it; and
- the registrar body is 928-by-544 pixels.

When Rehearsal is absent:

- no mode bar or gap is reserved; and
- the registrar body uses the full 928-by-624 inner rect.

Capability appearance may add the mode bar when the archive is rebuilt after
the milestone. Text size, language, target mode, palette, and content count may
not add or remove it.

### 8.3 Registrar body

The body uses:

- left index width: 344 pixels;
- seam/gutter width: 16 pixels; and
- right record desk width: 568 pixels.

The split remains fixed in both Endings and Rehearsal and at 100%, 125%, and
150% text.

The index and record desk are sibling vertical scroll regions. Neither nests a
second free-scrolling region inside itself. A pinned action dock may remain
outside the record-detail scroll body.

### 8.4 Mode bar

When present, the bar contains exactly Endings and Rehearsal in that order.
Each is at least 64 pixels high. Selected state, focus, and unread/new status are
not conflated. The bar contains no discovery count.

### 8.5 Rehearsal subsection and facets

Inside Rehearsal's left index:

- the first visible control is the available Scenes/Full Dates subsection
  selector;
- Scenes appears before Full Dates when both exist;
- the first nonempty subsection is selected, preferring Scenes;
- Day and participant facet controls follow only when meaningful; and
- the remaining height belongs to the discovered-only record list.

Facet controls may open one bounded selection sheet rather than expanding a
permanent chip cloud. They never show result counts.

### 8.6 Record desk and action dock

The record desk contains, in reading order:

1. optional authored media;
2. audience-facing title;
3. one authored sentence;
4. Other witnessed versions, only when more than one compatible version exists;
5. an unavailable/status line only when operationally necessary; and
6. one pinned explicit `Replay` or `Begin Rehearsal` action.

The pinned action remains reachable at every supported text size. Detail text
scrolls vertically when needed. There is no horizontal scrolling.

When the detail text overflows, its scroll body becomes one semantic,
focus-visible `Record details` region before the version and action controls.
When it does not overflow, that extra focus stop is absent. The readable text
remains present in the assistive tree in either state.

## 9. Endings index and record detail

### 9.1 One record per discovered identity

Endings projects one record per currently discovered semantic ending identity.
Micro-variation, Full/Residue entry, Alone form, route cause, and repeated
completion never multiply the top-level identity.

### 9.2 Personal order

Records appear in ascending durable first-discovery sequence. The UI never sorts
them by:

- friend or pair;
- semantic class;
- internal ID;
- localized title;
- latest replay or rediscovery;
- clock time; or
- current language.

Changing locale cannot reorder records.

### 9.3 Public record language

Each record uses:

- one authored localized title of approximately 2 to 6 words;
- one restrained authored non-mechanical sentence; and
- optional authored media.

It never displays `Sweet`, `Dark`, `Observer`, `Special`, relationship values,
route predicates, board results, evidence, ending IDs, entry IDs, signature
fields, completion time, or discovery sequence.

Final titles may be poetic, physical, spatial, or action-based. They cannot
function as a mechanical classification or completion checklist.

### 9.4 Optional media

Media may be a restrained still, contact-sheet fragment, or ordinary symbolic
object mark that became public with the record.

A missing optional asset removes its media block and lets the authored text use
the space. It does not show a broken-image icon, generate a substitute clue,
disable Replay, or change record order.

Character/background pixels are not recoloured by UI palette, contrast, or
colour-differentiation settings. Functional frame, focus, and text tokens are.

### 9.5 No Endings retrieval controls

Endings has no search, sort, family heading, filter, date facet, completion
counter, or favorites system. Thirteen possible identities do not justify those
features, and unseen identities must not influence the interface.

## 10. Gallery exact-version and replay law

### 10.1 Default version

Selecting an ending record resolves a default exact witnessed version:

- ordinary solo and Priscilla-Lavinia Sweet/Dark identities use the latest
  unique compatible canonically completed signature;
- Observer and Special use the latest unique compatible canonically completed
  Full signature;
- Alone uses the latest physically completed Normal or Dark-mode form; and
- no identity may fabricate a signature or form that was not reached.

### 10.2 Other witnessed versions

The control appears only when at least two compatible exact reached signatures
exist for the selected identity.

Versions are ordered latest unique canonical completion first. Each is labelled
by a manifest-authored familiar scene cue or short excerpt. Labels cannot expose
hidden tone, tier, attitude, evidence, route predicate, signature hash, result
formula, or internal form enum.

Selecting a version updates the record desk but starts nothing.

### 10.3 Exceptional identities

Observer and Special Gallery replay is always Full.

- Residue entries never appear under Other witnessed versions.
- The Settings exceptional-replay preference does not change Gallery.
- A Full signature must have been physically completed canonically.
- Missing Full context makes Replay unavailable and enters truthful recovery; it
  never substitutes Residue or another identity.

### 10.4 Alone

Alone remains one semantic Gallery identity. Normal and Dark-mode are exact
witnessed forms, not separate records.

The latest physically completed reached form is default. If both forms were
reached, the older exact form may be selected under Other witnessed versions.
Hospital-cause inserts remain internal presentation context and do not create a
new identity or public mechanical label.

### 10.5 Explicit Replay

One activation on an index row selects it. One activation on a version selects
it. Neither starts playback.

Only the pinned `Replay` action starts the selected exact version. Double-click,
double-tap, rapid Accept, and row activation never replay.

Before starting, the replay owner revalidates:

- current profile revision;
- Gallery discovery;
- exact reached-signature receipt;
- ending/entry/form compatibility;
- Full-only exceptional policy;
- manifest/content compatibility;
- current locale locator; and
- no active archive session or blocking operation.

Stale or invalid input starts nothing and refreshes the record truthfully.

### 10.6 Consequence-free Gallery playback

Gallery replay runs through the isolated archive-session capability boundary.
It may present the selected exact entry and merge newly visited lines under
section 16. It may not:

- complete an ending step;
- add or reorder Gallery discovery;
- set the first-ending milestone;
- grant exceptional or Dark entitlement;
- record another reached signature;
- grant evidence or pair witness;
- mutate a run, relationship, ending plan, attempt, branch, save, or RNG; or
- emit a canonical completion receipt.

Completion or deliberate Back returns to the exact ending record, selected
version, scroll, and Replay focus.

## 11. Rehearsal index and retrieval

### 11.1 Separate Scenes and Full Dates

Rehearsal contains two possible subsections:

- **Scenes** for exact replay of witnessed physically staged interpersonal
  moments; and
- **Full Dates** for sandbox exploration from reached pre-challenge starts.

They are never mixed into one list. A record may have related Scene and Full
Date projections only when the manifest explicitly owns both public record IDs;
one does not imply the other.

### 11.2 Grouping

Each subsection groups exact signatures or seeds under one manifest-authored
public record ID. The top-level index therefore shows one familiar scene/date
record rather than one row per mechanical variation.

Other witnessed versions appears in the record desk only when at least two exact
eligible versions exist.

### 11.3 Rehearsal record order

Within each subsection, public records use the first canonical completion
sequence of their first visible exact version. A later new version does not move
the record. Versions inside the record use latest unique completion first.

### 11.4 Day and participant facets

Rehearsal may expose:

- Day; and
- participant.

A facet control appears only when the current visible subsection contains at
least two distinct meaningful values. Its options derive only from currently
visible reached records.

The facet UI shows no count, disabled empty value, undiscovered participant,
unreached day, or result total. Clearing facets restores all records in the
subsection and preserves their canonical archive order.

There is no free-text search.

## 12. Scene eligibility and exact replay

### 12.1 Closed manifest capability

A Scene is public only when all of these are true:

- its manifest record declares a stable archive Scene record ID;
- its archive eligibility is `interpersonal_scene`;
- its exact signature completed canonically;
- its presentation declares at least two registered physically present
  participants; and
- its signature and current content contract validate.

Eligibility is never inferred from speaker count, name scanning, line text,
background art, route name, or a generic callable-entry list.

### 12.2 Included scene families

Eligible authored families may include:

- Hospital encounters;
- solo date scenes;
- visible Priscilla-Lavinia encounters; and
- another physically staged interpersonal scene explicitly registered by the
  manifest.

Angela may be silent, passive, incapacitated, or unconscious. Her lack of
speech or agency does not disqualify Hospital or another physically shared
scene.

### 12.3 Excluded material

Scenes always excludes:

- ending entries, which belong to Endings;
- Contacts correspondence, drafts, offers, follow-ups, and message history;
- desktop, app, notification, system, and technical events;
- Angela self-talk;
- archive-authored narrator identity, unrestricted private-thought transcription, viewpoint,
  interpretation, new prose, consciousness, or knowledge; replayed source
  material retains its already-approved perceptual prose and source anchor;
- erased, expired, no-trace, superseded, unread, or unseen entries;
- offscreen encounters and Private-offscreen pair meetings;
- History-only records not backed by a completed exact scene signature; and
- hypothetical results created inside Rehearsal.

An exclusion contributes no row, facet, count, inaccessible metadata, or assistive
trace.

### 12.4 Exact playback

Direct Scene replay restores the selected exact reached signature and its frozen
presentation context.

It does not reopen or alter:

- prior A/B/C selections;
- intervention/withholding opportunities;
- invitation acceptance;
- relationship or attitude outcomes;
- board results;
- evidence, echoes, or witnesses; or
- another canonical choice/action.

Only ordinary narrative presentation controls such as advance, Auto, Skip,
History, and Read Aloud remain available under their accepted law.

Scene completion or Back exits immediately, performs the visited-line merge if
needed, and restores the exact originating Scene record/version/action.

## 13. Full Date eligibility and starting context

### 13.1 Closed eligibility

A Full Date is public only when:

- its manifest declares a Full Date public record ID and closed seed schema;
- it is a solo or visible Priscilla-Lavinia challenge encounter;
- Angela canonically reached the registered pre-challenge presentation boundary;
- the exact start seed was validated and durably bound to that reach receipt;
- its challenge and post-challenge entry family remain compatible; and
- the current profile still contains the reached record.

### 13.2 Excluded encounters

Full Dates excludes:

- Hospital;
- missed, Prevented, superseded, or Private-offscreen encounters;
- Contacts and Schedule offers;
- boardless Day-7 destinations and endings;
- a post-challenge signature without its reached pre-challenge seed;
- a challenge inferred only from attempt history; and
- any debug/test encounter.

### 13.3 Starting contexts are witnessed versions

One reached start seed is the default latest compatible version. Other reached
start contexts appear only under Other witnessed versions.

The audience cannot edit the starting:

- tier, tone, attitude, affection, or dark values;
- relationship outcome;
- pair mode, deck state, or participants;
- day/window, due context, or prior choices;
- difficulty/rule facts owned by the reached seed; or
- hidden manifest fields.

Canonically reaching another genuinely different starting context adds another
exact selectable version. Rehearsal itself can never add one.

## 14. Full Date sandbox lifecycle

### 14.1 Start transaction

Only the explicit `Begin Rehearsal` action may start.

The owner revalidates the selected public record, seed receipt, profile revision,
manifest/content/rule versions, production challenge capability, current locale,
and absence of another session. It then creates one isolated transient session.

Rapid or duplicate activation yields one session. A stale selection starts
nothing and refreshes the archive.

### 14.2 Isolated reconstruction

The session reconstructs only the selected seed's exact starting context. It
owns detached copies of every state needed by that encounter, including its
relationship-rule state, Dialogic variables, production board state, sandbox
RNG, nonce allocator, receipts, command capabilities, and presented-line ledger.

It never copies an unrelated current save or reads mutable live run state after
start.

`execution_mode = rehearsal` is a capability boundary, not a presentation field
that can be authored or saved as if it were canon.

### 14.3 Full Date sequence

One Full Date executes exactly:

1. reconstruct the reached starting seed;
2. present the matching exact pre-challenge entry;
3. create a fresh unknown board under production challenge rules;
4. accept ordinary player Minesweeper input;
5. resolve one legal board truth and local relationship outcome;
6. apply its effects only to the sandbox's detached state;
7. present the matching legal hypothetical post-challenge entry; and
8. stop at the end-of-date boundary.

It never continues into another date, day resolution, Hospital check, Contacts
follow-up, Schedule mutation, ending, or canonical route.

### 14.4 Production play, not a result selector

The audience explores through the real production board rules.

There are no:

- Perfect/Solved/Exploded buttons;
- relationship-outcome buttons;
- stat or attitude sliders;
- seed entry fields;
- board reveals or debug hints unavailable in canonical play;
- lists of undiscovered outcomes; or
- completion counters.

Every start and Rehearse Again creates a new Rehearsal-local board, nonce, and
RNG namespace from the original starting seed. It never consumes or appends a
canonical board nonce or attempt.

### 14.5 Hypothetical local effects

A legal board result may locally change the sandbox's affected relationship
state exactly as the production rule family would at that point. That local
state selects the corresponding hypothetical post-challenge presentation.

The audience may therefore witness dialogue that did not occur in the canonical
run. Those line identities may enter visited History only after actual
presentation and validated merge. The local relationship state itself never
persists.

### 14.6 Forbidden persistent outputs

A Full Date cannot persist:

- affection, dark, tier, tone, attitude, promotion, or relationship outcome;
- health, pressure, motivation, money, coins, inventory, or Shop facts;
- Contacts, invitation, Schedule, Hospital, echo, or condition facts;
- board nonce, layout, result, mastery, attempt, residue, or branch facts;
- Observer evidence, pair witness, withholding/intervention evidence, or
  combination credit;
- ending plan, milestone, entitlement, Gallery discovery, or chronology;
- reached signatures or Rehearsal start seeds;
- canonical RNG, global RNG, save records, or transaction receipts; or
- character knowledge beyond the presented sandbox lines.

Every denied canonical command fails inside the sandbox capability boundary and
cannot partially mutate a real owner.

## 15. Playback exit, completion, restart, and return

### 15.1 Direct Gallery and Scene playback

Gallery and Scene playback has no sandbox board or unresolved hypothetical
outcome. Back may leave immediately at a safe presentation boundary. Completion
returns automatically after the visited-line merge boundary.

No confirmation is shown.

### 15.2 Unfinished Full Date

Back or shell Return during an unfinished Full Date opens one trusted
confirmation with exactly:

- `Continue`; and
- `Leave Rehearsal`.

Continue is initially focused. Back equals Continue. Continue closes the
confirmation and returns to the exact session focus. Leave Rehearsal ends the
transient session after attempting the visited-line merge for lines already
presented.

No canonical progress-loss warning, board forfeit language, or save language is
shown because the session owns no canonical run.

### 15.3 Completed Full Date

After the post-challenge entry completes and the visited-line merge resolves,
the completion plate offers:

- `Rehearse Again`; and
- `Return`.

Return is initially focused. Rehearse Again rebuilds the original selected seed
and generates a fresh sandbox board. It does not carry forward the prior
sandbox's relationship values, result, board, RNG, or presented-line delta.

Return restores the exact Full Date record, selected version, filters, scroll,
and Begin Rehearsal focus.

### 15.4 Process loss and suspension

Focus loss applies the accepted quiet-suspension law: presentation, Auto, TTS,
timers, board input, and nonessential processing pause; an admitted bounded
operation reaches its stable frontier first.

The archive session is not saved. Process termination, application crash before
merge, or title-session destruction discards unmerged sandbox-only progress.
Canonical saves and profile consequences remain unchanged.

If a visited-line merge intent was already durably admitted, recovery completes
or rolls back that one idempotent merge before claiming an outcome.

### 15.5 Switching title-ledger destinations

The accepted title ledger remains the owner of destination switching. A request
to open another title destination while Gallery or archive playback is active
first invokes the same local retreat law as Back or shell Return:

- the safe archive root closes immediately;
- direct Gallery or Scene playback exits at its next safe presentation boundary
  and completes any visited-line merge before switching;
- an unfinished Full Date opens the same Continue/Leave Rehearsal confirmation;
  choosing Leave completes or truthfully resolves its visited-line merge before
  switching, while Continue cancels the requested destination change;
- a completed Full Date Return closes cleanly before switching; and
- an admitted merge, indeterminate commit, or technical recovery keeps the
  ledger inert until the result is proven.

The requested destination opens only after clean closure. No ledger activation
can abandon a sandbox behind the new host, bypass consent, or lose a durable
History merge.

## 16. Visited-line and History merge

### 16.1 Collection inside a session

The archive playback bridge may collect only stable line IDs and immutable
presentation receipts that:

- belong to the validated active semantic entry;
- are declared collectable by its manifest;
- were actually presented through the registered visual or assistive boundary;
- match the active session token; and
- are valid under the current History schema.

Raw strings, caller-provided IDs, hidden branch labels, line indexes, and
unpresented alternatives are rejected.

### 16.2 Atomic allowlisted merge

At a clean exit or completion, the session owner builds a detached candidate
that unions the validated new line records into profile visited History.

The merge is idempotent and atomic. It cannot write any other profile field. A
deep comparison must prove every nonallowlisted run/profile owner remains
equivalent.

### 16.3 Quiet success status

If the durable merge added at least one previously unvisited line, the restored
archive shows one quiet localized status:

`New dialogue added to History`

It includes no count, percentage, title list, completion claim, sound, toast,
badge, or focus change. Replaying only previously visited lines is silent.

The status is transient archive feedback, not a History entry or saved fact.

### 16.4 Failure

Before merge commit, failure leaves profile History unchanged and retains the
session when possible for Retry or Leave Without New History under the trusted
technical recovery boundary. It never claims the lines were saved.

After a durable pending intent, recovery reuses the exact candidate and
idempotency key. It never rescans currently visible prose or merges twice.

## 17. Focus, pointer, keyboard, controller, and Back

### 17.1 Initial focus

Gallery always opens on Endings.

- If Endings contains records, focus the first record in personal chronology.
- If Endings is empty and Rehearsal is available, focus Rehearsal in the mode
  bar while leaving Endings selected.
- If Endings is empty and Rehearsal is absent, focus the shell Return control.

Opening Gallery does not auto-select Rehearsal or auto-start playback.

### 17.2 Selection and focus

Focus, selection, active view, and playback are distinct.

- Index focus follows deliberate keyboard/controller navigation.
- Moving focus onto an index record selects it and refreshes the record desk but
  starts nothing.
- Pointer hover changes neither focus nor selection.
- One click/tap selects a record or control.
- Only one click/tap/Accept on Replay or Begin Rehearsal starts.
- Double activation is ignored as an additional command.

### 17.3 Spatial navigation

Explicit semantic neighbors prevent engine-geometric guesses.

- Left index Up/Down moves through visible records without wrapping.
- The focused row is scrolled completely into view with a stable inset.
- Right from an index record enters the overflowing `Record details` region
  when present, otherwise it prefers Other witnessed versions when present and
  then the action.
- Left from a record-desk control returns to the selected index record.
- Right or Tab from the `Record details` region advances to Other witnessed
  versions when present, otherwise the action; Left returns to the selected
  index record.
- Mode-bar Left/Right moves Endings/Rehearsal without wrapping.
- Rehearsal subsection Left/Right moves Scenes/Full Dates when both exist.
- Tab follows mode bar, subsection/facets, index, an overflowing Record-details
  region, versions, and action in logical reading order; Shift-Tab reverses it.
- Focus never enters absent modes, empty facets, optional missing art, decorative
  paper, the title clock, or negative space.

### 17.4 Scroll ownership

Wheel, trackpad, stick scroll, and keyboard scrolling apply only to the focused
index or record desk. While the semantic `Record details` region owns focus,
Up/Down, Page Up/Page Down, Home/End, stick, and wheel input scroll that detail
without changing the selected record or activating an action. Changing the
selected record scrolls the new detail to its top unless a replay round trip is
restoring its exact cached position.

No control steals focus merely because scrolling reveals it.

### 17.5 Back and Return precedence

Within the archive:

1. trusted recovery owns only its explicit controls;
2. a confirmation or facet/version selection sheet consumes Back as its safe
   cancel action;
3. an active Full Date follows section 15.2;
4. an active direct replay follows section 15.1;
5. otherwise Back closes Gallery exactly like shell Return; and
6. the shell restores the title-ledger Gallery row.

Selecting a record in the fixed split is not a Back layer. One Back from the
safe archive root leaves the archive.

Shell Return is inert while a blocking confirmation, admitted merge, or
technical recovery owns input. It never silently cancels admitted work.

### 17.6 Focus restoration

Replay return restores the exact originating semantic action when it remains
valid. If a profile reset or content change removed it, the archive rebuilds and
focuses:

1. the same record's remaining default action;
2. otherwise the nearest surviving record in archive order;
3. otherwise the applicable mode control; or
4. otherwise shell Return.

It never guesses another ending or starts a fallback replay.

## 18. Localization, text, TTS, motion, colour, and access

### 18.1 Archive language

Archive chrome, titles, descriptions, cues, facets, status, and errors use the
current Primary UI language and its accepted per-key fallback. They are system
archive UI, not dual-language narrative cards.

Playback uses the accepted narrative language behavior:

- current catalogs at replay start;
- Primary/Secondary and Dual Language presentation;
- Primary-only system Read Aloud;
- stable line/speaker disclosure identity;
- Auto and Skip under existing visited rules; and
- exact same-entry English fallback only when permitted.

Changing locale inside another title host rebuilds Gallery copy on return but
does not change order, selection identity, discovery, signatures, or visited
state.

### 18.2 Text size and topology

The archive supports exactly 100%, 125%, and 150% in the initial release.

At all three sizes:

- mode bar, registrar split, subsection order, and action-dock position remain;
- long titles, descriptions, cues, facets, and errors wrap vertically;
- index rows grow vertically when needed;
- the index and detail scroll independently;
- no text clips, ellipsizes, overlaps, or silently shrinks;
- no horizontal text scrolling appears; and
- the focused control remains completely reachable.

An inability to render a supported locale at 150% is a layout failure, not a
reason to reduce text or hide content.

### 18.3 Targets

Every archive mode, subsection, facet, index record, version, action, modal
button, and recovery control is at least 64 pixels high in both ordinary and
Large Targets modes. This deliberate archive rhythm exceeds the ordinary
48-pixel minimum and avoids topology change when Large Targets is enabled.

Text growth may make a target taller. It may not make it narrower than its safe
interactive region or create overlap.

### 18.4 Assistive semantics

Visible and assistive information are equivalent.

Each visible record exposes:

- its authored title;
- its one-sentence description;
- selected/unselected state;
- selected witnessed-version cue when applicable;
- Replay or Begin Rehearsal availability; and
- truthful unavailable status when applicable.

Assistive output never adds internal class, hidden counts, absent identities,
signature fields, relationship values, or undisclosed participants.

The semantic tree order matches visible order. Empty copy and quiet History
status are announced politely once. Routine focus restoration, locale change,
scroll, or return does not reannounce them as new discoveries.

An overflowing detail body exposes its `Record details` region, scrollable
state, and current scroll position semantically. The region is not announced as
a button and activation on it performs no replay.

### 18.5 Colour, contrast, and motion

Focus, selection, active mode, active subsection, unavailable state, and errors
use text/shape/boundary evidence and never colour alone.

Both run-independent title base palettes, High Contrast, and Standard/Protan/
Deutan/Tritan token mappings preserve all functional distinctions. Decorative
stills are not colour-filtered.

Reduced Motion replaces record/media transitions with immediate swaps. No film
flicker, projector strobe, auto-scrolling contact sheet, pulsing lock, or moving
paper is required or allowed.

### 18.6 Audio and suspension

Archive selection and empty states require no sound. If restrained UI SFX are
later authored, SFX settings own them and no sound may be the only evidence of
state.

Focus loss stops Read Aloud, pauses Auto and public timers, parks a Full Date
board after the accepted stable frontier, and throttles nonessential archive
processing. Refocus restores exact visible state without replaying speech or
announcements.

## 19. Archive cache, reset, and profile changes

### 19.1 Cache lifetime

Gallery preserves current view, subsection, facets, selection, scroll, and
meaningful focus only:

- while it remains the active title destination; and
- across a Gallery/Scene/Full Date playback round trip launched from it.

Shell Return, switching to another title-ledger destination, Shut down, New Acc,
successful Load, logout publication, or Entire Profile Reset destroys the cache.
The next open starts at Endings under section 17.1.

The cache is never serialized into profile or save data.

### 19.2 Clear Visited History

Clear Visited History removes visited/skip-seen line facts under Settings law.
It preserves:

- Gallery discoveries and order;
- first-ending milestone;
- reached signatures and Rehearsal seeds;
- Rehearsal availability and records;
- ending and relationship mechanics; and
- saves.

Later archive playback may add actually presented lines again.

### 19.3 Clear Gallery

Clear Gallery removes:

- visible semantic ending discoveries;
- Gallery first-discovery chronology;
- exceptional-replay availability; and
- exceptional-replay selection, returning it Off.

It preserves:

- first-ending milestone;
- Rehearsal availability;
- reached Scene records and Full Date seeds;
- visited History;
- Dark entitlement and pending next-run choice;
- attempt/evidence/witness ledgers;
- saves; and
- any mounted/frozen run.

Reopening Gallery therefore may show empty Endings beside a populated Rehearsal.
Reached ending signatures retained for ledger integrity do not repopulate
Endings without a new durable Gallery discovery.

### 19.4 Entire Profile Reset

The separately confirmed title-only Entire Profile Reset clears:

- Gallery discoveries and chronology;
- first-ending milestone;
- reached presentation records;
- Rehearsal seeds and availability;
- visited History;
- exceptional-replay and Dark entitlement/intent; and
- every other player-facing profile ledger under Settings law.

It does not delete Autosave, Quick, or numbered saves. A later Load may restore
the saved run exactly but cannot restore profile ledgers wholesale. Canonical
presentation reached again after reset may build new records under new profile
receipts.

### 19.5 External profile revision while open

The archive snapshot binds one profile revision. Before any action it
revalidates that revision and the selected receipt.

When a reset or other valid publication changes available records, the archive
rebuilds discovered-only projection, removes vanished controls from focus, and
applies section 17.6. It never starts a record that disappeared or treats an
unavailable record as empty unseen content.

## 20. Save, Load, run, and migration boundary

### 20.1 No live-run ownership

Gallery is title-hosted and has no mounted canonical run. Archive playback uses
profile-owned reached records plus isolated transient state.

It cannot:

- inspect or copy the current Autosave merely because it is newest;
- choose a numbered save as a starting context;
- write a save during replay;
- restore a run route after replay; or
- merge sandbox values into a later Load.

### 20.2 Save shortcuts

F5/F9 and every live-run save/load shortcut are unavailable inside Gallery and
archive playback. Backup remains the sole save-record owner.

### 20.3 Current unshipped development data

The project has not shipped. The obsolete current profile shape cannot preserve
personal discovery chronology, exact thirteen-ID identity, reached signatures,
or Full Date seeds truthfully.

Therefore target implementation must not fabricate those facts from the stale
eleven-ID array:

- an empty legacy Gallery may migrate to an empty target archive;
- a nonempty obsolete development Gallery/profile must be regenerated as a
  target fixture or rejected as unsupported without overwriting its source; and
- no generic Priscilla-Lavinia ID, sorted array position, ending ID alone,
  History line, or save route may be converted into a guessed new identity,
  signature, discovery sequence, or seed.

This is an unshipped development-artifact disposition, not a promise to discard
future released player data. Any later shipped migration requires its own exact
mapping and compatibility proof.

## 21. Failure, rollback, and technical recovery

### 21.1 Optional media failure

Missing optional media is harmless. The archive omits the media block and keeps
the record, copy, versions, order, focus, and action unchanged.

### 21.2 Required metadata failure

A discovered/reached record with missing required title metadata, incompatible
schema, invalid signature, invalid seed, or missing required English locator
must not disappear, expose an internal ID, or replay another entry.

It retains its truthful position as one nonplayable `Unavailable record` using
localized technical UI copy. Replay/Begin Rehearsal is disabled. The rest of the
archive remains usable when isolation is proven.

### 21.3 Start failure

If preparation fails before a session starts:

- no session, line merge, run, profile, save, RNG, or focus mutation occurs;
- the archive stays on the initiating record;
- exact cached context remains;
- one concise localized nonfiction status is shown; and
- Retry revalidates the same semantic record/version rather than choosing
  another.

### 21.4 In-session failure

An in-session presentation or board failure parks the sandbox at its last proven
stable boundary and opens trusted Retry/Leave Rehearsal recovery. It does not
turn corruption into horror, attribute failure to a character, expose raw IDs,
or claim canonical progress loss.

Leave discards sandbox-only state after resolving any already-admitted visited
merge. Canonical state remains unchanged.

### 21.5 Indeterminate merge

An indeterminate visited-line merge blocks archive return until recovery proves
whether the exact idempotent intent committed. It never guesses, merges a newly
rescanned line set, or reports success early.

### 21.6 Diagnostic boundary

Release UI shows concise trusted facts only. Internal IDs, hashes, signatures,
seed fields, paths, stack traces, manifests, and raw failure codes remain in
developer diagnostics.

Technical recovery uses the accepted high-priority neutral surface. It never
uses glitch wipes, horror red, fake missing records, character voice, or archive
fiction.

### 23.8 History merge owner

Validates the session token and presented-line receipts, prepares one atomic
visited-only profile candidate, rejects every nonallowlisted delta, and publishes
quiet success only after durability is proven.

### 23.9 Semantic ports

The public archive boundary exposes semantic operations equivalent to:

- obtain one immutable archive snapshot for a profile revision and locale;
- select a public record/version locally;
- prepare Gallery replay from ending ID plus opaque reached-record receipt;
- prepare Scene replay from public record plus opaque reached-record receipt;
- prepare Full Date from public record plus opaque seed receipt;
- stop/finish/restart one bound archive session; and
- reconcile one visited-only merge.

Concrete API names remain an implementation-plan concern. The ownership and
data-direction boundaries above are normative.

# Settings University Calibration Registry Standard Palette and State Disposition

## 1. Accepted decision and objective

The project owner approved the complete recommended Settings Standard-master
batch on 2026-08-22. This disposition does not redesign Settings. It translates
the already accepted Settings behavior, fixed University Calibration Registry
anatomy, and shared functional-state alphabet into one closed visual mapping for
After-Hours Standard and Midnight Standard.

The governing law is:

> The registry calibrates the instrument; it never interprets the world.

Settings therefore publishes only ordinary operating facts: the selected
category, current committed values, truthful capability and actionability,
active focus, physical contact, real custody, exact conflicts, and factual
failure or recovery. It never turns material age, a colour, a bloom, a seam, a
control specimen, or a technical condition into narrative evidence.

This is the finishing visual-system layer, not a new feature layer. It closes:

- the two Standard primitive-to-semantic mappings used by Settings;
- component-token bindings for the fixed directory, spine, folio, controls,
  scrollbars, Control Sample, confirmations, and recovery handoff;
- the coexistence of Selected, Focus, Hover, Press, Disabled, Unavailable,
  Warning, Danger, Destructive action, custody, and exact local status;
- title, in-run desktop, and in-run Pause mounting parity; and
- the first native-pixel proof family for Settings.

It creates no preference, category, command, explanation, story clue, setting
value, save field, input binding, host, or implementation authority.

## 6. Directory, spine, and folio material law

### 6.1 Night is the room; paper is the visitor

The plate habitat, directory, and filing spine remain nocturnal. The folio is
the only pale material field. It never expands into the title ledger, desktop
strip, Angela field, Pause pane, modal background, or full canvas.

The directory is one continuous controlled-dark register. It does not become
seven floating cards. The spine is one `settings.spine.fill` channel with one
inset `settings.spine.registration` rule; required separation from the folio is
carried by the spine fill itself, not by its registration rule against paper.

The folio is one `settings.folio.fill` document with
`settings.folio.text.primary` and `settings.folio.text.secondary`. Required
rules on paper use the named folio rule tokens. Fog Text, Bone Text, Fog Blue,
Filed Verdigris, and warm risk colours cannot serve as meaningful paper text or
unassisted paper boundary evidence.

### 6.2 Maintained wear

The first Standard functional masters use flat fills and hard raster rules.
Optional paper grain, soot variation, registration wear, or age is excluded
from measured text, controls, focus, state marks, target edges, scrollbars, and
the Control Sample.

Later final material art may add accepted stationary wear only to inactive
planes. It remains identical across values, categories, operations, results,
runs, routes, and story states. Wear never thickens, moves, reveals, stains a
control, or reacts to a preference.

## 7. Categories and ordinary controls

### 7.1 Category directory

2. Reading;
3. Audio;
4. Display;
5. Controls;
6. Accessibility; and
7. Records.

Default rows remain controlled dark. The selected row receives Filed

When Selected and Focus coincide, the Filed plane and seam remain intact while

### 7.2 Literal option grammar

Binary preferences use literal localized values equivalent to `Off` and `On`.
They do not depend on an unlabeled switch glyph. Multi-value preferences use
literal segmented keys or the retained registered selection control. Exactly
one committed value owns Selected.

An option key does not claim a draft. Pointer or touch activation submits its
accepted semantic value command. Keyboard, controller, and assistive activation
use the same command. A proven new value replaces Selected atomically; failure
retains or reconstructs the prior Selected key.

### 7.3 Sliders

The slider row contains a literal label, exact visible value, ruled track, and
one square thumb. Its committed segment is the registered nontext Selected
projection of that exact committed numeric range and instantiates
`settings.slider.committed_fill` only inside the complete
`settings.slider.track` enclosure. Neither fill length nor colour replaces the
literal value. The transient pointer preview never owns Selected.

Pointer dragging previews the value locally. While contact remains held, the
value plate instantiates `settings.slider.preview_value_fill`,
`settings.slider.preview_value_ink`, and `settings.slider.preview_value_rule`
with the localized operational label `Preview`. That plate has no Selected
seam, Success mark, durable-status copy, or profile announcement. One release
submits one command. Keyboard and controller steps remain discrete commands.
Commit replaces the preview plate atomically with the ordinary committed value
and thumb position; failure restores both prior facts.

Focus belongs to the semantic slider row, not a free-floating thumb. The row's
paper Focus stays visible while the thumb moves. Text-size or target changes do
not alter the preference value.

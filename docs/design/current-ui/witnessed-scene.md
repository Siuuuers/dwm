## 2. Product character, interpretive law, scope, and exclusions

The witnessed scene is a **fixed room-owned aperture above a registered
reading apparatus**. Rooms and bodies present facts. Captions accumulate from
the bottom. The apparatus records chronology and permits operation without
naming the speaker, evaluating the scene, interpreting a result, or explaining
why any visible fact matters.

The private governing law is:

> The room shows what is there; the register preserves what was said; neither
> tells the audience what the evidence means.

Robert McKee, Alain Robbe-Grillet, Roland Gérard Barthes, and Mark Fisher
remain private calibration lenses, never player-facing attribution, tutorial,
copy, symbol legend, or explanation. Story pressure may change literal
content, staging, sound, and consequence, but it cannot cause the apparatus to
become more expressive or interpretive.

## 3. Vocabulary, ownership, and state partition

- A **semantic session** is one token-bound narrative playback context with
  immutable frozen inputs, a presentation stream, History identity, command
  receipts, and one governing completion owner.
- A **Room-Owned Aperture** is the inert rectangular composition of one room
  surface, present non-Angela friend bodies, and required public object/action
  overlays.
- A **staging atom** is one immutable, validated composition identity within an
  authored shot.
- A **projection variant** is the exact public text, private ownership,
  staging, tone, result, pair, residue, and other signature identity used by
  witnessed-state law.
- The **Registered Material Field** is the opaque UI-owned reading substrate
  between aperture and rail.
- A **caption leaf** is one visible semantic beat projection. In Dual Language,
  both language rows belong to one leaf.
- **current**, **previous**, and **oldest retained** are chronological
  positions, not evaluation, commitment, speaker, or importance states.
- **Normal Accept** is the one command produced by activating the current
  caption: complete reveal only, or on a later input advance one beat or
  dispatch an acknowledged boundary.
- **Auto** is a profile-owned preference projection; **Skip** is a
  session-owned mode; **Next** is a one-shot exact-witness seek.
- **History** is the current semantic session's inspect-only caption ledger,
  never rewind, evidence interpretation, or story ownership.
- A **stable frontier** is a semantic point at which one complete authoritative
  state may be saved, paused, replaced, or handed to another owner.
- **challenge custody** is the durable interval in which the Minesweeper board

> Skip witnessed content to the next unseen beat or challenge.

### 8.5 Transport arbitration

- Auto, Skip, and Next never operate simultaneously.
- Enabling Auto stops Skip.
- Enabling Skip first commits Auto Off.
- Next first commits Auto Off and stops Skip.

  beat arms exactly one fresh full delay.
- Every transport stops current TTS before taking custody.
- If a required cross-owner preference commit fails, the new transport does
  not begin and the old state remains truthful.

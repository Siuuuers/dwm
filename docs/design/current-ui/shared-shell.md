## 2. Product character, interpretive law, scope, and non-goals

The shared shell is one continuous maintained campus workstation. Logged-out
title, in-run desktop, app hosting, Angela's persistent alcove, Pause, and
truthful recovery use one learned institutional grammar. Its causal procedures
are exact even when the accumulated evidence feels impossible.

The project's interpretive lineage remains active here:

- McKee supplies consequence and causal clarity: accepted actions have exact
  outcomes and no decorative state pretends to be consequence.
- Robbe-Grillet supplies witnessed surfaces and absence: the interface presents
  objects, order, repetition, time, and gaps without explaining them.
- Barthes leaves interpretation with the audience: the shell supplies evidence
  but no decoding key, diagnosis, or privileged meaning.
- Fisher keeps the institution competent and ordinary while its familiar
  continuity becomes quietly uncanny; horror is never simulated software
  failure.

The shell therefore changes only when a public fact changes. It never styles an
anomaly as an anomaly, explains a route, names a hidden cause, supplies a
relationship meter, or turns ambience into a mechanic. It never lies about
focus, availability, time, saves, durability, routes, app state, or failure.
There are no selectable spoken-scene narrative choices; Minesweeper results
remain the game's sole choice-like narrative agency.

Four alternatives remain explicitly rejected: a visually unrelated cinematic
title world; a fluid art-first dream desktop that reflows topology or makes
cosmetic motion look mechanical; a simulated window manager with overlapping,
draggable, resizable, minimized, or taskbar-managed windows; and any standalone
Opening, blocking tutorial, hidden tutorial alias, or mandatory orientation
dialogue.

In scope are the canvas, host partitions, title ledger and optional presenter,
New Acc projection, Desktop Host, launcher, Home/Back/focus, shell cache
lifetime, Angela HUD/tableau/keepsakes/self-talk projection, audience clocks,
global surface arbitration, imported Pause host, Standard material/state
mapping, localization/accessibility projection, shell-level recovery, and
acceptance evidence.

Out of scope are app internals; story prose and deeper canon; relationship,
Schedule, Minesweeper, Hospital, ending, Gallery, Observer, and economy
mechanics; Backup storage I/O; Settings preference ownership; final art,
typography, motion, music, audio, and TTS production; mobile portrait layout,
200% text, exclusive fullscreen, and a resolution selector; draggable windows,
desktop files, taskbars, character customization, or decoration controls; and
runtime, task, plan, schema, manifest, requirement, or migration execution.

The archived Lawful-Universe Atlas and Brainstorm Companion artifacts are
prototype evidence only. No Atlas label, explanation, icon system, presence
rule, or hidden analytical UI returns through this dossier.

## 4. Coordinate systems, host geometry, and imported functional grammar

### 4.1 Logical and native canvases

The product geometry is authored on one fixed 1280×720 logical, 16:9 canvas.
Standard proof uses a 640×360 native capture at an exact 2:1 mapping: one native
pixel equals two logical pixels on both axes. Native coordinates are evidence
coordinates, never a competing layout.

Operating-system display scaling is outside this logical coordinate system.

Windowed mode uses the logical baseline. Borderless mode uniformly fits it to
the display's usable bounds while preserving aspect. Other aspect ratios center
the full canvas inside inert current-palette matte bars. Matte contains no
content, hover, focus, status, toast, anomaly, hit target, or remapped edge
input. Nothing crops, rearranges, or expands into it.

The supported text masters are 100%, 125%, and 150%. They alter typography,
wrapping, and local vertical scrolling, not macro partitions, order, safe-region
anchors, app identities, or input semantics. Ordinary targets are at least
48×48 logical (24×24 native); Large Targets are at least 64×64 logical
(32×32 native). Text never uses a narrower font, silently shrinks, clips,
ellipsizes, becomes an image, overlaps, or requires horizontal scrolling.

### 4.3 Functional-state grammar

The shell conditionally consumes the shared roles and morphology described in
Section 15.5; it does not manufacture a fact because a token exists.

- Focus is a detached double outline and is never Selected.
- Hover is lawful pointer inspection. Press is brief physical contact and
  replaces Hover on their shared contact carrier for the duration of a lawful
  hold. Neither activates or selects by itself.
- Selected means committed selection owned by a component; Current means the
  already-active destination or mode. They are not synonyms for Focus.
- Disabled is a presently nonactionable action; Unavailable is a missing
  capability. They use distinct text, shape, outline, and accessible state.
- State evidence belongs to the smallest truthful semantic target.
  Unavailable attaches only to the exact missing capability. Authorized child
  actions remain independently present, enabled, Disabled, or absent according
  to their own facts; the grammar fabricates no child merely to echo the missing
  capability. If one visible action cannot presently act, that child owns
  Disabled. A parent state never washes an independently lawful child, and a
  child state never falsifies its parent.
- A higher custody owner may make a control inert without assigning it a new
  canonical Disabled or Unavailable identity. Only the foreground owner accepts
  input, and no activation queues behind that owner.
- A target that becomes absent, hidden, Disabled, unavailable for that exact
  command, command-inadmissible, or outside foreground/custody cancels any Press
  that can no longer be admitted and clears held or queued activation input.
  Later command readiness requires fresh activation. Disabled or Unavailable
  does not by itself erase an independently authorized inspection/help Hover.
- Semantic Hover clears only when the exact target loses its lawful inspection
  role—for example because it becomes absent, hidden, inert, modal-covered,
  recovery-covered, or otherwise noninspectable. Raw pointer overlap cannot
  preserve or recreate Hover after that loss; later inspection must be fresh.
  No ghost Focus, Hover, Press, or assistive actionability survives its owning
  fact or custody.

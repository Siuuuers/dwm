## What the archive proves

The current Profile supplies the ordered discovery snapshot. Its order is kept;
no private ending classification becomes a public title. Failed acquisition and
an empty archive have distinct localized messages. Clear Gallery and restored
profiles refresh the visible list. A cached hidden Gallery waits until reopening
to refresh, preserving the active screen's focus and navigation.

The current lineage has no public record metadata projection or production
replay owner. Discovered rows therefore say ?Unavailable record,? and Replay
remains disabled and outside keyboard focus. Discovery alone is not evidence of
a witnessed version or replay entitlement. This is useful read-only archive
presentation, not complete Gallery content or replay acceptance.

The existing licensed Source Sans and regional Source Han fonts are reused.
English, Simplified Chinese and Hong Kong Traditional Chinese keep the current
24/30/36 logical font sizes. Row captions wrap rather than shrink. The index
scrolls independently, and directional navigation stops at its first and last
records. Tab reaches the shared Return control.

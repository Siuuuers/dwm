## 2. Product character, interpretive law, scope, and non-goals

resting on the nocturnal desktop, followed by one fixed controlled-dark dock.

uncommitted current-day order. The apparatus is administratively competent and
emotionally silent.

The private governing law is:

> The desk records availability and order; it never explains consequence.

The audience sees exact evidence and decides what it means. The apparatus does
not interpret absence, repetition, a person, a warning, an unavailable source,
bare paper, or the one Day-7 entry. No theorist name, token name, design-system

## 3. Vocabulary, ownership, and state partition

### 3.1 Exact Schedule terms

- An **Available source** is a registry-backed action or accepted invitation
  that the domain projection currently permits Schedule to present.
- A **ScheduleView** is the saved day-local draft and warning state. It is
  canonical run data for continuation, but it is not a committed Schedule.
- A **draft occurrence** is one identity-bearing appearance of a source in the
  Docket. Repeatable ordinary sources may create distinct occurrences.
- A **Docket position** is internal zero-based order. Days 1–6 may announce
  one-based order; Day 7 never announces an index.
- The **inspected occurrence** is the one presentation-only occurrence whose
  large folio and edit controls are projected.
- A **committed Schedule** is the validated, charged, immutable day-resolution
  input created only by successful Done.
- An **insertion move** removes one occurrence from its old position and
  inserts it at a boundary, shifting the intervening occurrences. It is never
  a pairwise Swap.
- A **warning-state fingerprint**, **pending warning activation**, and
  **consumed warning receipt** retain M's exact distinct meanings.
- **Selected** names the inspected Days 1–6 occurrence or chosen Day-7 source.
  **Focus** names the one active input target. They are independent.
- **Unavailable** is a visible source whose underlying identity remains real
  but cannot currently be appended. It is not Disabled, hidden, or an
  explanation of why.
- **Disabled** belongs to a visible boundary command such as Earlier or Later
  that cannot execute in the current ordering.
- **Custody** exists only while a real admitted operation owns input or
  transition across visible frames. Foreground warning ownership is the
  separate Warning fact and never creates a Custody seam.
- The **presentation cache** contains only Schedule-local view position and
  Focus/inspection anchors. It never owns domain facts.

## 4. Exact native mounting, sheet anatomy, and target law

### 4.1 Coordinate contract and mounted regions

All rectangles are 100%-text native pixels in Schedule-local coordinates,
half-open `(x, y, width, height)`. One native pixel equals two logical pixels.
Edges are integer-aligned; fractional placement, transforms, scaling, and
resampling are forbidden.

The shell supplies a 400×328 native app body below its strip. Schedule retains
the eight-native-pixel inset, producing the 384×312 working rect from `(8,8)`
through `(392,320)`.

`274 + 6 + 32 = 312`. There is no outer Schedule window, title bar, title,
close button, footer, shadow, floating card, or app-local frame.

### 4.2 Sheet insets and inner rectangles

Each paper sheet has an exact eight-pixel inset:

- Available inner rectangle: `(16,16,109,258)` globally Schedule-local;
- Docket inner rectangle: `(147,16,237,258)` globally Schedule-local; and
- Docket inner width: `96 order lane + 6 unprinted-paper gutter + 135 focused
  folio = 237`.

The six-pixel macro gap is exposed outer mat. The six-pixel Docket gutter is
unprinted Worn Cream. They never share tokens, hit regions, assistive nodes, or
meaning.

### 4.3 Scaling and target contract

Sheet edges, macro gaps, dock, column order, and Day-7 topology remain fixed at
100%, 125%, and 150% text and under ordinary or Large Targets. Growth uses real
wrapping, row-height expansion, exactly two vertical scroll owners, and
reserved clearance. It never causes horizontal scrolling, ellipsis, shrinking,
app scaling, dock overlap, folio collapse, or topology change.

- ordinary target minimum: 24 native pixels / 48 logical pixels;
- Large Target minimum: 32 native pixels / 64 logical pixels; and
- every focusable semantic body reserves a four-native-pixel detached Focus
  expansion on every side.

Visible grip geometry is a nonsequential pointer/touch subregion of its owning
occurrence, not another Focus target.

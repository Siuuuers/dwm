inspector, transaction dock, Supportz presentation, Shop-local focus and input,
view cache, accessibility, failure projection, visual system, and proof

## 2. Product character, interpretive law, scope, and non-goals

Shop is one **Maintained Campus Stores** counter: a competent dark service
instrument holding pale laminate specimen cards, literal object apertures, one
inspector, and a restrained transaction dock. Coffee, wine, bandages, a plush,
a Debug Key, keepsakes, and one blank capacity receive the same sincere
administrative treatment. The interface never comments on their coexistence.

The private covenant is:

> The counter records the object, the price, and the present transaction fact;
> it does nothing else to them.

The four interpretive lenses remain private design discipline:

- McKee requires every action to have exact causal consequence rather than
  decorative eventfulness.
- Robbe-Grillet requires objects, placement, repetition, and absence to remain
  evidence without explanatory psychology.
- Barthes forbids the interface from closing interpretation with a privileged
  meaning, rarity, provenance, or authorial hint.
- Fisher allows ordinary institutional competence itself to become strange;
  technical failure must therefore remain technical and trustworthy.

None of those names or explanations appears to the audience. Shop shows literal
identity, exact price and currency, current operational availability, selected
quantity, exact total, command custody when real, refusal when true, and the
committed projection afterward. It does not expose effects, hidden stock,
branch caps, relationship meaning, Supportz identity or purpose, future route,
stat headroom, board-generation law, or why any object matters.

Shop contains no selectable spoken-story choice. Card selection, quantity,
Buy, modal consent, and paging are ordinary application operations.
Minesweeper results remain the game's sole choice-like narrative agency.

Out of scope are gifting; final product prose; final art, fonts, texture,
animation, music, or sound; 200-percent text; mobile layouts; balancing;
relationship or ending mechanics; save-file I/O; shared shell arbitration;
authoring High Contrast and colour-vision tuples; runtime implementation; task
mutation; and release claims. Accepted High Contrast and Protan, Deutan, and
Tritan tuples plus their proof remain mandatory cutover inputs even though
this dossier does not invent them.

### 11.3 Confirmation Focus and no-scroll law

An open confirmation is the exclusive foreground owner. The background remains
visible but inert and absent from active Focus and assistive-action traversal.
Interactive traversal is No then Yes, with No initially focused. Back or Close
means No.

Confirmation factual copy never scrolls and never becomes a Focus target at
rest. During a genuinely unresolved Yes command, the factual body is the one
read-only Focus anchor described in section 8.5, not an interactive scroll
region.

Supported locale and text-master fixtures must prove all permitted factual
copy plus the reserved refusal line fits within the fixed body. Overflow is a
rejected fixture, not permission to clip, shrink, ellipsize, scroll, move
actions, or create another sheet.

### 11.4 Input parity and shell boundaries

Pointer, touchscreen, keyboard, controller, and supported assistive activation
all have a complete visible path through selection, page navigation, quantity,
direct Buy, modal No or Yes, Back, and Home. Supportz never depends on Hover.
Double-click is the only intentionally pointer-exclusive accelerator and adds
no exclusive function.

While a real purchase or required condition handoff is unresolved, Shop asks
the shared host to hold Home and other route-changing controls for that exact
custody lifetime. Ownership of those controls remains with the host. The word
shortcut in Shop custody means Shop-local purchase accelerators; it does not
silently redefine F5 Quick Save, F9 Quick Load, or another global binding.

When no Shop foreground or custody owner exists, Back and Home follow the
Shared Shell contract. Shop does not invent a local close button, second app
strip, navigation history, or Logout behavior.

A suspended desktop Minesweeper board does not lock Shop. Its external owner
preserves that board while Shop remains usable. A later Shop purchase cannot
mutate an already frozen board candidate, and a later board completion cannot
overwrite a valid committed purchase.

## 12. Purchase intent, external transaction, and consequence handoff

### 12.1 UI-owned intent

A normal Shop request identifies at least:

- immutable item ID;
- positive integer quantity;
- quote identity and registry-version fingerprint;
- quoted unit price, exact total, and currency;
- run, branch, logical-day, and relevant revision identity; and
- one external transaction identity for idempotent retry.

The UI freezes those displayed facts when intent is accepted. It does not
validate a catalog row, spend currency, apply an effect, grant a keepsake,
increment a count, issue a receipt, evaluate a condition, or choose a route.

### 12.2 External validation and atomic result

A's transaction owner revalidates the item, quantity policy, complete quantity,
quote, total, currency, context, result IDs, transaction identity, and
idempotency state. A stale or conflicting request cannot be auto-clamped,
substituted, partially bought, or spent against a revised quote.

The UI receives one detached result:

- **committed** — the complete authoritative projection and any route handoff;
- **expected refusal** — Insufficient funds or Unavailable with no mutation;
- **technical recovery** — truthful safe, pending, or committed state with the
  same transaction identity; or
- **condition route custody** — the purchase is already committed and the
  external destination now owns presentation.

For batch quantity q, atomic application, purchase-count change, spend,
receipt, and one final condition check remain domain law in A. Shop projects
only the resulting facts.

### 12.3 Ordering visible to Shop

Every successful ordinary, batch, capability, Supportz, or keepsake purchase
obeys this visible ordering:

1. Shop intent is accepted and the full request is externally validated.
2. Spend, count, registered result or grant, and receipt commit atomically.
3. The one authoritative post-Shop condition check runs.
4. A required Hospital or Day-7 destination takes custody with the entire
   purchase retained.
5. Otherwise Shop unlocks and publishes the refreshed projection.

No success copy, animation, toast, modal dismissal flourish, cache publication,
or Focus fallback may delay or outrank a condition route. A later board
completion cannot overwrite a valid purchase. Fatal recovery cannot rewind a
purchase already known committed.

### 12.4 Duplicate, stale, and crash behavior

Rapid duplicate delivery of one request returns the same external receipt and
cannot double-spend. Reuse of one transaction identity for different intent
fails without mutation.

A crash after durable commit but before condition resolution resumes the same
forward transaction. It does not refund, repeat the grant, or present the
purchase as uncommitted. Shop never reconstructs truth from its last visible
card state.

## 13. Feedback, technical failure, and trusted recovery

### 13.1 Operational truth table

| Situation | Player-facing Shop result | Forbidden substitution |
|---|---|---|
| immediate accepted purchase | refreshed truthful projection, with no Busy frame | invented Processing, Purchased, Saved, or celebration |
| genuine visible-duration command | silent static custody for its real lifetime | fake delay, spinner, progress, heartbeat, animation |
| affordability refusal | origin-bound Insufficient funds | recommendation, cap explanation, Error skin |
| other expected rejection | origin-bound Unavailable | hidden-reason disclosure, horror fault |
| stale quote or projection | no partial mutation; refresh authoritative facts and publish lawful refusal where returned | auto-clamp, substitute, partial purchase |
| committed purchase followed by route | destination takes custody; purchase remains | Shop success message delaying route |
| missing lawful item art | same-item manifest fallback or trusted recovery | blank ordinary card, Supportz-like absence, wrong object |
| technical transaction failure | shared trusted recovery with truthful safe, pending, or committed state | fictional corruption, anomaly, fake OS error |
| damaged presentation cache | discard cache and rebuild from canonical state | alter eligibility, counts, balances, or branch truth |
| missing essential localized copy after registered fallback | shared technical fallback or recovery | effect-revealing prose or raw invented meaning |

### 13.2 Trusted recovery

Trusted recovery replaces the current Shop custody owner and receives Focus.
It accurately distinguishes whether no durable mutation occurred, a transaction
is pending reconciliation, or a commit is proven. Retry resumes or replays the
same idempotent transaction. It cannot create a new request, double-spend,
refund a committed purchase, suppress a required route, or infer truth from UI.

Development evidence may contain machine-readable diagnostics. Audience-facing
copy remains concise, technical, and nonfictional. Shop never converts a
technical fault into a narrative clue, secret product, haunted receipt,
glitch, fake crash, corrupted price, or changing hitbox.

## 14. Same-day cache, persistence projection, Load, and reset

### 14.1 Same-day presentation cache

One same-day cached Shop instance may retain only:

- current page;
- each page's remembered selected ordinary card;
- current selected ordinary card;
- current selected card's quantity;
- lawful inspector information offset when real overflow exists; and
- last meaningful Focus target.

There is no catalog scroll field. Refusal, modal, Hover, Press, custody,
Shop-local balance copy, Supportz eligibility, Supportz component state, track
or thumb geometry, and detached functional marks are not cached.

The last-Focus key may name page-one slot nine only when that target was
eligible and lawfully focused. The key preserves no eligibility or component
state. Reopen recomputes Supportz first. If the target still exists, Focus may
return; otherwise it moves to Spa Coupon without changing ordinary selection.

Presentation cache cannot affect purchase eligibility, canonical run state,
currency, cap, or transaction outcome. Invalid cache is discarded as one unit.

### 14.2 Canonical persistence remains external

Canonical persistence owns balances, stats, purchase counts, capabilities,
keepsake ownership IDs, Supportz day and branch facts, and any durable
transaction or condition handoff.

It does not save Shop page, selection, quantity, inspector offset, Focus,
Hover, Press, refusal, open confirmation, custody seam, or any component node.
Environment keepsakes persist semantic ownership IDs, never Shop or tableau
Nodes, asset paths, transforms, sockets, visibility, or z-order.

### 14.3 Reconstruction

Logical-day change discards the Shop view cache. Explicit Load and New Run also
reconstruct rather than resume presentation. The result is page one, Coffee
selected, quantity one, Coffee focused, inspector offset zero, and no
confirmation or refusal.

Ordinary availability and Supportz eligibility are recomputed from validated
canonical state. Loading an earlier snapshot may truthfully rewind balances,
counts, keepsakes, capabilities, and availability under the external
continuation law. No Saving, Loading, or other duration copy appears unless a
real operation spans visible frames and its shared owner publishes truthful
custody.

## 15. Typography, localization, accessibility, targets, and motion

### 15.1 Typography and literal currency

Fusion Pixel proportional masters serve names, descriptions, availability,

status icon. Price remains literal text.

### 15.2 Text and target masters

Initial Windows support is 100%, 125%, and 150% text. The same macro topology
survives all three plus ordinary and Large Targets:

- catalog stays left and inspector right;
- each page stays a fixed 3-by-3 grid;
- page and slot order stay identical;
- transaction controls stay pinned;
- Supportz stays page one slot nine; and
- only inspector information may gain vertical overflow.

Text wraps vertically. It never clips, ellipsizes, scrolls horizontally,
becomes art, or shrinks below the active master. Confirmation copy must fit.

Final complete English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese catalog
names, descriptions, action strings, and regional font subsets remain

support remains future-only and creates no current scaffold.

### 15.3 Assistive projection

An ordinary card exposes only generic name, exact price and currency, Available
or Sold out, Selected where true, and control identity. The inspector exposes
the same operational facts, selected quantity, exact total, and current
refusal.

Assistive output never exposes effect, cap, remaining stock, relationship,
keepsake mapping, route, hidden reason, Supportz identity or effect,
Lucky/Debug mechanics, object provenance, or anomaly interpretation. Object
art receives no invented equivalent description and no separate traversal
node.

Supportz uses Blank shop card only while eligible. Ineligible and exhausted
absence remains absent from the assistive tree. Its confirmation exposes only
visible $45, actions, and current refusal.

Ordinary and Supportz confirmations have a neutral dialog role and localized
neutral identity without a visible title. The ordinary sheet may expose only
visible item, quantity, total, actions, and current refusal.

The conditional inspector scroll region follows section 11.2. Confirmation
never exposes scrolling semantics. The design defines semantic and evidence
obligations but claims no certification for a particular Windows assistive
product before that exact combination passes.

### 15.4 Input and motion parity

Focus, Selected, Hover, Press, availability, Disabled, refusal, and custody
remain distinguishable without colour, opacity, animation, or sound. Reduced
Motion removes no evidence because Shop contains no required motion.

Pointer, touch, keyboard, controller, and supported assistive paths all reach
the complete ordinary transaction. Large Targets, text size, theme, locale,
and input method do not change catalog order, product law, price, availability,
or Supportz resting pixels.

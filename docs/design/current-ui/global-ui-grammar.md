## 1. Status, authority, precedence, and migration effect

This dossier is intended to become the single cross-family authority for the
game's functional visual grammar. It owns the shared language by which an
already-owned fact becomes visibly and assistively distinguishable. It does
not own the fact, decide whether it exists, admit its command, or perform its
transaction.

This is not a tenth screen, application, menu, overlay, fictional ledger, or
player-facing explanation. The audience encounters only the ordinary results
of the grammar inside the nine current-v1 UI families.

Until a valid cutover, precedence remains:

1. the project owner's latest explicit correction within its declared scope;
2. M for its approved exact fact-family, morphology, overlap, Focus, contact,
   custody, and accessibility wording;

   art-recolouring boundary, subject to M's three narrow supersessions;

   and still-current mixed Foundation direction;

   persistence, recovery, art, audio, or deferred-feature authority for facts
   and behavior inside its own scope; and
6. prototypes, companion HTML, plans, runtime scaffolds, and historical notes
   only as nonbinding evidence where they do not conflict with accepted design.

When this accepted dossier is validly cut over, it supersedes A and M only
inside the exact cross-family scope restated here. F remains living because it
contains mixed scopes that this dossier deliberately does not own. Acceptance
does not transfer any app command, screen topology, routing, input admission,
save-file operation, failure recovery, story fact, mechanic, or asset-production

## 2. Product character, interpretive law, scope, and exclusions

The grammar serves a romantic psychological and supernatural horror visual
novel built as authored pixel art on a fixed native raster. It must feel like a
pixel-art love letter inside a maintained, haunted instrumentarium: tender
enough to trust, exact enough to use, and wrong in ways that invite
interpretation without obstructing action.

The governing reliability sentence is:

> **Trust the hand. Question the trace.**

Story evidence may disagree. A registered anomaly may alter a trace. Core
software operations may not lie. Reading, advancing, opening, closing,
selecting, saving, loading, deleting, buying, changing settings, focusing,
navigating, quitting, and recovering remain literal under their owning
authorities. The grammar never makes a player infer whether a command worked.

### 2.1 Four-Lens theory-to-form covenant

The approved private covenant inherited by every current-v1 family is:

> **Change composition only when represented reality changes. Let object,
> position, repetition, replacement, and absence carry the evidence. Maintain
> ordinary and impossible facts with the same competent materials. Supply no
> privileged decoding key.**

Its four internal duties are:

- **Robert McKee — pressure at the boundary.** Visual pressure or reversal
  follows a genuine causal boundary; unchanged facts do not receive fabricated
  drama.
- **Alain Robbe-Grillet — exact objects.** Objects, surfaces, positions,
  distances, repetitions, replacements, and absences perform observation
  without an explanatory ornament.
- **Roland Barthes — open traces.** Recurring marks may rhyme but never become
  a conclusive symbol dictionary, private annotation key, or diagnosis.
- **Mark Fisher — administered impossibility.** Functionally identical routine
  and impossible facts receive the same competent material, typography,
  geometry, interaction treatment, and assistive parity.

These names and this calibration language remain private. They never appear as
UI copy, graphical signs, lore labels, tooltips, or audience instruction.

The six inherited styling laws are:

1. establish exact repetition before one charged surface changes one measurable
   property;
2. let distance, alignment, enclosure, density, axes, seams, or one lawful
   boundary trespass carry pressure before colour supports it;
3. keep one institution while allowing each application its own instrument and
   material dialect;
4. allow lawful blankness—genuine absence leaves no placeholder, scar,
   silhouette, mystery symbol, reassurance, hit target, Focus target, or
   assistive residue;
5. keep the project-wide mass restrained, with nocturnal habitat dominant and
   pale or warm material a bounded visitor; and
6. keep wear sparse, deterministic, stationary, and clear of required evidence,
   while motion remains brief punctuation owned by a real boundary.

### 2.2 Anomaly restraint and external ownership

F records an anomaly occurrence/scheduling budget, but that budget remains with
the mixed Foundation and the Deferred UI Feature Register. It is not imported
as global functional-presentation authority here.

This dossier imports only the grammar boundary: a separately authored anomaly

route, relationship, or hidden-state meaning, or explain itself through a
horror badge, fake diagnostic, interpretive tutorial, or special assistive
disclosure. Constant glitching and random visual noise remain rejected visual

This dossier does not create, count, generate, schedule, persist, or interpret
an anomaly. Those facts and features remain external or deferred.

## 3. Grammar architecture, normative strength, and custody

### 3.1 Normative-strength labels

Because the sources have different review states, this dossier uses literal
prose rather than visual badges to preserve their strength:

| Label used below | Meaning in this pending dossier |
|---|---|
| **Approved exact source law** | inherited from M's owner-approved exact written bytes; still nonbinding here until this dossier is accepted |
| **Approved direction** | the owner selected the design, but F or A exact successor wording and/or production values remain pending |
| **Current mixed-source direction** | useful surviving F detail outside F's exact approved-writing boundary; preserved without retroactive approval |
| **Open proof or production gate** | required later work, not a permission to infer a value or claim parity |
| **External owner** | fact or behavior remains with another authority and is linked rather than duplicated |

These labels are documentation only and never project to the audience.

### 3.2 One-way dependency

The approved dependency is:

> **registered primitive → semantic role → component token**

A primitive is a raw, registered material/value root. A semantic role expresses
a stable public functional meaning. A component token binds one semantic role
to one specific visual zone of an application component.

Consequences:

- components never introduce undeclared raw colour values;
- components never choose primitives directly;
- one semantic role may map to different registered primitives in different
  complete tuples;
- the role's public meaning and required non-colour morphology do not change
  with that mapping;
- app-specific material dialects may change substrate and composition but not
  redefine shared functional meaning; and
- final art pixels remain outside this dependency except for a separate
  functional overlay drawn above them.

### 3.3 Fact ownership versus evidence ownership

The application or domain authority owns:

- whether a fact exists and which semantic target owns it;
- visible localized copy and assistive name;
- focusability, inspection, action legality, and command admission;
- transaction and execution custody;
- status lifetime and replacement boundary;
- persistence, recovery, and domain consequences; and
- focus fallback and restoration.

The global grammar owns only the invariant visible and assistive evidence for a
fact after its owner publishes it. A token cannot create its fact. An overlap
entry cannot legalize a combination that the application forbids. A missing
global token cannot justify hiding an owned public fact.

### 3.4 Cross-application custody

Settings calibrates and lets the player select accepted tuples, but it is not
the alphabet's sole owner. Shared chrome, Contacts, Schedule, Shop,
Minesweeper, Backup, Gallery, and witnessed scenes consume the same meanings.

Domain marks remain local. Examples include friend-identity rails, Schedule
ordering marks, Minesweeper digits and cell evidence, Backup record states, and
Gallery admission marks. They may coexist with Focus, Selected, Unavailable,
or other global evidence but cannot redefine those global roles.

## 4. Native grid, typography, material, and interaction roots

The values in this section preserve current F direction. Only values separately
accepted by a family authority are exact authorities today. This dossier's
later owner approval would approve these successor bytes within global-grammar
scope; it would not transfer shell mounting or app geometry.

### 4.1 Native raster roots

- Authored component coordinate space: **640 × 360** native raster.
- Default presentation reference: **1280 × 720** at 2× integer scale.
- Additional exact integer references: 1920 × 1080 at 3× and 2560 × 1440 at
  4×.
- Authored pixel assets use nearest-neighbour filtering.
- The logical canvas never crops, stretches non-uniformly, or reflows its macro
  topology merely because a landscape display has a different aspect ratio.
- Any physical matte is inert, noninteractive, and contains no story evidence.

The Shared Shell remains the owner of supported display modes, physical window,
centering, matte dimensions, canvas mounting, and host rectangles. Each family
owns its local geometry inside its assigned host.

### 4.2 Target and text roots

- Ordinary minimum target: 24 × 24 native.
- Large Targets: 32 × 32 native.
- Text presets: 100%, 125%, and 150%.
- Text growth uses wrapping, protected taller regions, or local vertical
  scrolling under the family authority.
- Text does not shrink to fit, acquire horizontal scrolling, or replace macro
  topology.

Exact family allocations, target exceptions, focus neighbours, overflow, and
scroll behavior remain with the corresponding dossier.

### 4.3 Typography roots

The current mixed-source direction uses two type voices:

- Fusion Pixel proportional builds for prose and controls, with its monospaced
  builds limited to calculations, timestamps, tables, and numeric registers;
  and
- Source Han Serif for rare title, record-heading, and literary display raster
  at exact supported native sizes.

Current functional native masters are 8, 10, and 12 pixels. One master is not
scaled to imitate another. Simplified and Traditional Chinese require their
correct regional glyph forms. Dual Language is a composed layout, not two
strings squeezed into one row.

No essential fact depends on italics, handwriting, decorative corruption, tiny
capitals, or display serif. No localized string is baked into authored art.
Final packages, regional subsets, licenses, raster proof, and locale parity are
open production gates.

### 4.4 Material and component roots

The current mixed-source material grammar is:

- square corners and one-pixel seams by default;
- a two-pixel seam and one corner notch may support Selected where accepted;
- paper receives at most one offset registration shadow;
- fibres, soot, wear, and registration drift remain deterministic, static,
  sparse, and absent beneath glyphs and required evidence;
- instrument glass uses two or three flat value bands and a restrained double
  inset;
- icons use 12 × 12 or 16 × 16 native grids with one-pixel strokes;
- core operations retain literal text labels rather than a private pictogram
  language;
- Hover never moves geometry;
- Focus remains a separated outer instrument ring; and
- Press remains an inward/reversed seam without scale, bounce, or layout shift.

Rounded cards, blur, glow, continuous gradients, neon cyberpunk, CRT filters,
scanlines, animated grain, large dot fields, star wallpaper, decorative hearts,
skulls, occult shorthand, and emoji icons remain rejected global directions.

### 4.5 Interaction-feedback timing

The mixed Foundation principle is:

> **The hand answers now. The world answers after.**

Semantic state, Focus, readiness, and assistive publication never wait for
decorative motion. Current motion roots are:

| Root | Current duration | Current purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Now | 0 ms | state publication, Focus, Reduced Motion, and technical truth |
| Touch | 60 ms | pressed inset and release acknowledgement |
| Settle | 120 ms | local line, notch, or value settlement |
| Shift | 180 ms | local content arrival or accepted spatial shift |
| Veil | 240 ms | rare art transition or wordless seam |
| Authored hold | 0.4–1.6 s | explicit story rhythm only, never disguised loading |

These durations remain current Foundation direction rather than production
proof. Each use requires an owning family or story boundary. Reduced Motion
publishes the same final state immediately. This dossier does not own input
admission, transport arbitration, challenge boundaries, or story timing.

## 6. Orthogonal fact model and smallest truthful target

**Orthogonal Fact Ledger** is a private design-system term. It is not a literal
ledger, screen, menu, statistic, diagnostic overlay, explanation, or story
fact. The audience sees only truthful ordinary UI.

M's approved exact central law is:

> Type every fact by its exact semantic target and fact family; compose every
> compatible fact; then apply the owning application's focus-order and input-
> admission law separately.

One projected semantic target may carry facts from these independent families:

| Fact family | Private values or owner | Composition law |
|---|---|---|
| Projection | present or absent | Presence gates every projected fact. |
| Content / occupancy | app-owned values such as Empty, readable, filled, or fallback-loadable | At most one value for the same content owner. |
| Availability / capability | app-owned values such as available, unavailable, sold out, or incompatible | At most one value for each exact capability; separate from action input. |
| Actionability | enabled or disabled | One value for each exact action target. |
| Commitment / location | Selected, Current, or neither | One value for each exact target under the app's commitment model. |
| Focus ownership | Focused or not Focused | Independent from pointer inspection and physical contact. |
| Pointer inspection | Hovered or not Hovered | Exists only for an app-authorized inspection/help role; publishes no new unowned information. |
| Physical contact | Pressed or not Pressed | Temporary contact only, never transaction success. |
| Risk / action scope | Warning, Danger, Destructive action, or none | At most one risk value for each exact target. |
| Execution custody | idle, Busy, temporarily inert, recovering, or terminal-locked | One value for each custody owner. |
| Operational status | none, Information, Success, or Error | One current status for each exact local status owner. |
| Public metadata | identity, Unread, and other disclosed app facts | Cumulative only when the app owns compatible facts. |

This fact model is private. Names such as `execution custody` or `commitment
plane` never become public copy unless an application independently owns the
same literal words.

### 6.1 Availability is not actionability

`Unavailable` describes one public content or capability fact. Disabled means
one real action does not currently accept input. They are not synonyms.

An unavailable record, voice, clock, source, or item may remain present,
labelled, Selected, and inspectable while each related action is independently
Enabled, Disabled, or absent. Conversely, an available target may have a
temporarily Disabled action for an app-owned reason.

The grammar cannot infer a reason, add explanatory copy, reveal withheld
mechanics, or make an unavailable capability actionable.

### 6.2 Smallest truthful semantic target

Every fact attaches to the smallest semantic target that owns it. A compound
surface does not inherit one dominant child state, and a child does not inherit
all parent states.

For example, one Backup record may be present, occupied, Unavailable, Selected,
and inspectable at its record/container targets while its Load action is a
separate Disabled target. The drawer does not become one opaque disabled no-op,
and selection does not make Load legal.

A parent transaction may own Busy or Recovering while only conflicting child
actions become inert. Readable or scrollable children remain available only
where the app authority permits them. No child receives Busy styling merely
because an ancestor owns the transaction.

Physical pointer intersection is not automatically Hover. Hover exists only
when the exact target retains an app-authorized inspection role in the current
custody state.

### 6.3 Projection and lawful absence

An absent component has no hit target, Focus, Hover, Press, Selected, Current,
status, metadata, or assistive node. Quiet ruled capacity, negative space, and
decorative geometry are not hidden components and receive no component-state
tokens.

Genuine absence therefore leaves no placeholder, mystery icon, gap marker,
ghost Focus, tooltip, announcement, silhouette, or accessible residue unless a
family authority explicitly owns a visible capacity rather than an absent
item.

## 7. Shared functional-state morphology

The treatments below are approved conceptual functional evidence. Final pixel
dimensions, colours, line weights, hatch density, and complete tuple bindings
remain open calibration work.

### 7.1 Selected and Current

Selected uses a committed fill plus a stable filing seam. It denotes only the
owning application's committed selection or location. It never means Focus,
Hover, Press, Unread, identity, relationship progress, route, recommendation,
or success.

Current is an app-owned location fact. Where an application distinguishes
Current from Selected, their component bindings must remain separately
legible. The global grammar does not decide which target becomes Selected or
Current.

### 7.2 Focus

Focus uses a detached double outline, separated from Selected fill/seam,
outermost in the registered zone order, and never clipped by the component,
host, scroll viewport, or neighbouring geometry.

Focus identifies the current input destination only. It does not commit,
inspect, activate, confirm, advance, purchase, or publish a story fact. Exact
focus order, neighbours, initial target, fallbacks, and restoration remain
family-owned.

### 7.3 Warning, Danger, and Destructive action

The three risk meanings remain distinct:

- Warning uses the literal warning glyph plus one governing edge rule on the
  exact warning owner; app-owned copy remains additional public evidence where
  its authority requires it.
- Danger uses its distinct danger glyph plus a double rule; app-owned copy
  remains additional public evidence where its authority requires it.
- A Destructive action uses an explicit destructive verb and filled action key
  on the exact action. Its application authority owns the safe confirmation
  flow, initial focus, binding, cancellation, execution, and recovery.

Only one risk value may exist on one exact target. A dialog's Warning and its
child action's Destructive scope are separate typed targets when the
application owns both.

### 7.4 Hover

Hover keeps shape and position unchanged and uses only the owning application's
quiet local surface response. It receives no detached Focus outline, badge,
lasting mark, or new semantic label.

Hover confirms lawful pointer inspection only. It never selects, Focuses,
reads, confirms, advances, purchases, admits a command, clears Unread, or
creates canonical, narrative, or transactional evidence. It may expose only
already-authorized operational help. Touch, keyboard, controller, and
assistive operation never depend on Hover.

### 7.5 Press

Press uses one brief inward or reversed contact seam inside the exact target.
It does not borrow Selected fill, Focus morphology, Success evidence, scale,
bounce, or layout movement.

Press reports physical contact, not committed outcome. A committed state
appears only after the application admits and proves the command. When Hover
and Press coexist, Press owns the contact plane during the hold; Hover may
resume after release only if the owner still publishes it.

An action cannot be semantically Pressed when it is Disabled, lacks its
required available capability, or is Busy-locked, temporarily inert,
Recovering, or terminal-locked. When the external input/custody owner withdraws
Pressed under one of those conditions, the grammar removes the Press morphology
without prescribing input cancellation, held-event handling, or fresh
activation.

### 7.6 Disabled

Disabled remains fully readable in its ordinary component boundary. It uses a
consistent blocked-action bar or closed action-edge treatment and never relies
on tint or opacity alone.

Disabled never accepts Press or command input. An app-owned literal reason
appears only where that authority already requires it. The app decides whether
the exact Disabled action leaves focus order or remains Focusable solely for
approved help/inspection. Focus never makes it actionable, and a separately
inspectable parent remains a different target.

### 7.7 Unavailable

Where generic localized `Unavailable` is itself the application-owned public
fact, it uses that literal label, a dashed boundary, and a stable hatch or
equivalent pattern.

A more precise accepted value—such as `Sold out`, incompatible, or an
app-specific no-compatible-voice fact—retains its own literal copy and
registered redundant morphology. The grammar does not add a duplicate generic
label, dash, or hatch.

The treatment attaches only to the unavailable content/capability. It does not
reclassify a compound surface. The hatch never obscures copy, Focus, risk,
identity, metadata, or other compatible facts.

### 7.8 Busy

Busy exists only while a real accepted operation remains in progress across
visible frames. An immediate operation publishes its result directly and
shows no Busy interstitial.

Where an application already owns pending copy, it uses the exact present-tense
operation, such as `Saving…` or `Loading…`, plus one static custody seam. An
application may instead own a silent truthful hold. The grammar invents no
copy, spinner, fake percentage, animated disk, staged progress, or minimum
duration.

Selected/Current, visible content, public metadata, and applicable risk remain
legible. Only actions that conflict with the real operation become inert. The
whole application is not dimmed or frozen when its authority retains lawful
inspection.

### 7.9 Error

Error binds literal localized nonfiction copy and one clean status boundary or
edge to the exact failed item or operation. The last trustworthy value, record,
or surface remains visible unless its recovery owner proves it cannot be shown
safely.

Error never corrupts geometry, impersonates a narrative anomaly, attributes a
technical failure to a character, borrows Danger/Destructive morphology, or
replaces the entire app with a generic failure page unless the trusted recovery
authority owns the whole surface.

### 7.10 Success

The proven changed value, metadata, destination, or committed state is primary
Success evidence. An existing app-owned status surface may use a restrained
settled seam or literal result copy only under that authority's lifetime.

The grammar creates no global toast, checkmark, celebration, reward motion,
sound, banner, or timer. A durable result that is already self-evident need not
manufacture a Success status.

### 7.11 Information

Information styles only an existing app-owned fact and surface. It is quiet,
locally attached, and ordinarily single-boundary. It creates no plane, copy,
fact, lifetime, or global banner and borrows no Focus, risk, Error, or Busy
mark.

Information never explains a narrative event, names an undisclosed speaker,
interprets evidence, hints at a route, or tells the audience what an anomaly
means.

### 7.12 Unread

Unread or `new` is app-owned public metadata. Its exact copy, morphology,
lifetime, and read/witness boundary remain with its application. The global law
keeps it distinct from Focus, Selected, identity, risk, relationship, route,
mood, and hidden story evidence.

Contacts retains sole authority over its message-Unread morphology, avatarless
topology, and read/witness clearing boundary. Its composite row is referenced
later only as an external family-owned collision fixture. The global obligation
is limited to keeping any app-published Unread fact distinct from identity,
Selected, Focus, risk, and story meaning.

Assistive projection names only the same localized public fact the application
publishes; it never exposes a count or internal watermark the visual UI
withholds.

## 8. Registered visual zones and stacking order

Compatible facts occupy stable semantic zones instead of competing for one
dominant skin:

1. **Semantic body:** app-owned content, literal labels, identity, and domain
   marks.
2. **Commitment plane:** Selected or Current fill and filing seam.
3. **Metadata gutter:** Unread and other explicitly disclosed metadata.
4. **Availability/action edge:** the exact app-owned capability treatment or
   Disabled action treatment.
5. **Risk edge:** one Warning, Danger, or Destructive treatment on its exact
   owner.
6. **Custody/status attachment:** an existing app-owned Busy or operational-
   status surface and its authorized copy.
7. **Contact plane:** quiet Hover response or inward Press treatment inside the
   target.
8. **Focus plane:** detached double outline, outermost and unclipped.

These are semantic zones, not a mandated scene tree, shader stack, node family,
or requirement to create eight children. They prevent one fact from occluding
another.

Adding, removing, or composing a mark never moves component geometry, changes
hit bounds, rewrites focus neighbours, or fabricates a state. App-owned state
may still truthfully change whether a target participates.

## 9. Legal overlap and contradiction matrix

Compatibility is evaluated first on one exact semantic target, then across the
typed targets of a compound component. Parent facts do not leak into children;
child facts do not reclassify parents.

### 9.1 Structurally compatible when the owner publishes both

On one exact target, these combinations remain simultaneously legible when the
application owns both facts:

- Empty or another content value with Selected or Current;
- Disabled with Selected, Current, or a truthful Risk scope;
- Selected or Current with Focus, Hover, one operational status, or public
  metadata;
- Selected or Current with Press only when the contacted action is available,
  Enabled, and outside conflicting custody;
- Focus with Hover or Press;
- Focus with Warning, Danger, or Destructive scope;
- identity and Unread with Selected, Focus, lawful inspection, or custody; and
- one operational status with commitment, Focus, content, or metadata.

Across typed parent/child targets, a Selected or Current item may remain visible
inside a Busy, inert, modal, or Recovering owner without inheriting its custody
mark.

### 9.2 Application-owned combinations

Each application decides:

- actions available for Empty, fallback-loadable, Unavailable, sold-out,
  incompatible, or other content/capability states;
- whether a visible container retains lawful Focus or inspection;
- whether Busy retains read-only Focus or transfers it to a custody owner;
- which descendants conflict with a transaction and become inert;
- whether Risk and public metadata coexist on a component;
- whether a status projects, its literal copy, and its replacement boundary;
  and
- whether terminal content remains inspectable after mutation actions end.

The matrix records those decisions. It never creates them.

### 9.3 Contradictions on one exact target

These combinations are never legal:

- Absent plus any projected content, hit target, component state, or assistive
  node;
- two mutually exclusive values in one closed fact family;
- several Information/Success/Error values under one status owner;
- several Warning/Danger/Destructive values on one target;
- Press on an action that is Disabled, lacks its required capability, or is
  Busy-locked, inert, Recovering, or terminal-locked;
- Hover where no lawful inspection role exists;
- active Focus on an exact Disabled action without an app-authorized help role,
  on an inert/Busy-locked action, or in an inert background subtree;
- a lower surface retaining active interaction while a modal/recovery owner
  owns the boundary; or
- identity/Unread reinterpreted as risk, relationship, route, mood, anomaly,
  or story evidence.

An Unavailable container may still accept a separately enabled inspection
command. Capability and inspection are different typed targets.

### 9.4 Operational-status composition

For one exact status/custody owner, the grammar never renders Busy together
with Success or Error, and never renders Success together with Error. Pending
and result copy such as `Saving…` and `Saved` therefore never coexist after the
application publishes one current status for that owner.

Focusing or merely offering Retry does not itself change Error morphology. The
application owns whether and when a new command replaces, clears, or sequences
status facts. Error and Recovering may be rendered together only when separate
exact targets explicitly own both published facts. Information may coexist
with custody only under the same smallest-truthful-target typing rule.

## 14. Technical truth, failure restraint, and rejected global patterns

### 14.1 Technical truth

The grammar must never:

- fabricate a successful save, load, delete, purchase, setting change, replay,
  or navigation result;
- misreport a price, quantity, balance, preference, record, selected target,
  public time, compatibility fact, or technical failure;
- show `Saving…`, `Loading…`, another Busy label, or progress when the

- present Press as Success or Focus as commitment;
- hide an Error by corrupting geometry or replacing trustworthy content;
- erase an offered operation without a truthful public state change;
- make a player interpret a glitch to continue; or
- turn a real software failure into fictional character behavior.

The owning recovery authority preserves or reconstructs the last trustworthy
state. This dossier governs only the public evidence and non-fiction boundary.

### 14.2 Accessibility and horror prohibitions

The UI must not:

- move targets away from the player or exchange affirmative/negative actions;
- steal Focus or leave ghost Focus in invisible geometry;
- hide essential horror from non-pointer, non-audio, or Reduced Motion users;
- simulate an operating-system crash, corrupt installation, or fake diagnostic;
- use unreadability, clipped CJK glyphs, tiny text, latency, or disabled
  accessibility as atmosphere;
- punish an accessibility setting or provide it with inferior facts; or
- give assistive output a solved interpretation unavailable in the visible
  public evidence.

### 14.3 Rejected state-system alternatives

The following remain rejected for current v1:

- Press feedback on an already blocked action;
- treating raw pointer overlap as semantic inspection;

- folding Information, Success, and Error into Warning or Busy;
- stacking several risk glyphs/edges on one target;
- changing underlying facts merely because a modal/Pause treatment obscures
  them;
- universal spinners, success toasts, checkmarks, celebration, or timers;
- component-local raw hex values or direct component-to-primitive binding;

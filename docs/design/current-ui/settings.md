## 2. Product character, interpretive law, scope, and non-goals

Settings is one maintained **University Calibration Registry**: a nocturnal
category directory joined through a filing spine to one visiting cream folio.
The private direction name never appears to the audience; the registered
localized application label remains `Settings`.

Its governing law is:

> The registry calibrates the instrument; it never interprets the world.

It states only operating facts: selected category, committed value, truthful
capability, actionability, Focus, physical contact, real custody, exact
conflict, factual failure, and recovery. Material age, colour, bloom, seams,
specimens, and technical conditions never become narrative evidence.

The theory remains private and formal:

- repeated objective geometry and literal values carry Robbe-Grillet's
  discipline;
- operational words never become a Barthesian key for decoding story signs;
- commit, rollback, and consequence retain McKee's causal discipline without
  creating narrative choice; and
- the maintained old institution carries Fisher's temporal unease without fake
  corruption, occult shorthand, or explanatory horror.

No theorist name, design-system term, token, state-zone diagram, or calibration
rationale appears player-facing.

## 3. Vocabulary, state partition, and ownership

### 3.1 Exact Settings terms

- A **profile preference** is global player-facing presentation or input state.
  Ordinary preferences apply immediately, survive run replacement, and are
  never rewound by Load.
- **Dark availability** is a monotonic profile entitlement earned by its
  external story predicate.
- **Pending Dark** is the title-owned durable Boolean for the next successful
  New Run, not a live preference.
- **Captured Dark** is the immutable run Boolean stored by every run save. It is
  the only Settings-originating fact restored by Load.
- The **effective presentation tuple** is a derived complete record: base
  palette, contrast, colour differentiation, text size, targets, Reduced
  Motion, and remembered shake. It is not a second mutable bag.
- **Selection** names the committed category or option. **Focus** names the one
  active input target. They are independent.
- **Disabled** means a visible action currently cannot execute while its value
  or capability remains real. **Unavailable** means a required capability does
  not exist. They never attach to the same exact semantic target.
- A **pointer preview** is transient slider contact state. It is neither a
  committed value nor a durable draft.
- **Custody** exists only while a real admitted operation owns visible duration
  or a foreground sheet owns the active subtree.
- The **Control Sample** is one static descriptive group of four production
  state specimens. It is not interactive and does not mirror live state.
- The **transfer bloom** is one inert authored material exemplar. It is not a
  state, record, save mark, result, warning, anomaly, blood, or clue.
- The **presentation cache** contains category, two offsets, and semantic Focus.
  It is never profile or run data.

Every published fact attaches to the smallest semantic target that truthfully
owns it. An Unavailable capability group may retain a separately owned
inspection role only where an accepted owner already authorizes one, while one
child mutation action is Disabled. Parent capability, child action admission,
and inspection role never collapse merely because they share a compound control.

## 4. Coordinate systems, host mounts, plate anatomy, and targets

### 4.1 Native coordinate contract

All exact Settings geometry uses the 640×360 native master. The 1280×720
logical baseline is an exact 2× presentation. Resampling, fractional placement,
local zoom, shrink-to-fit, and target-size substitution are forbidden.

The same logical 400×328 Settings plate mounts in three contexts:

| Context | Host fact | Global native Settings plate |
|---|---|---|
| Logged-out title | centered in shell workfield `(160,32,480,328)` with 40-pixel margins | `(200,32,400,328)` |
| In-run desktop | below the 32-pixel app strip, beside the 240-pixel Angela field | `(240,32,400,328)` |
| In-run Pause | opaque content in the 400×328 right workfield | `(240,32,400,328)` |

Title keeps the 160-pixel ledger and hides optional title art. Desktop leaves
Angela's authored field untouched. Pause keeps its left 240-pixel action pane;
preview activates no right-subtree target, entry activates Settings, and exit
returns to the exact left Settings row. Pause is a mount, not a third Settings
implementation.

Settings adds no title, clock, Home, launcher, toast, save status, host badge,
logged-out mark, in-run mark, Pause mark, or movable faux window.

### 4.2 Fixed local plate anatomy

At 100% text and ordinary targets:

| Region | Local native rect |
|---|---|
| Plate | `(0,0,400,328)` |
| Content rectangle | `(8,8,384,312)` |
| Category directory | `(8,8,104,312)` |
| Filing spine | `(112,8,8,312)` |
| Folio | `(120,8,272,312)` |
| Folio heading | `(120,8,272,40)` |
| Independently scrolling control sheet | `(120,48,272,200)` |
| Pinned Accessibility footer | `(120,248,272,72)` |

The arithmetic is exact: 104+8+272=384 and 40+200+72=312. Heading, sheet, and
footer are bands of one folio, not cards, panes, or separate documents.

At 125% or 150%, Large Targets, or longer reviewed localization, internal
vertical allocation may change and real local overflow may expose fewer rows.
The plate, inset, directory, spine, folio, category order, independent vertical
owners, and absence of horizontal scrolling never change. Exact non-baseline
band allocation remains an open production proof dependency; this dossier does
not invent it.

### 4.3 Target and Focus clearance

Ordinary targets are at least 24×24 native, equivalent to 48×48 logical. Large
Targets raises the minimum to 32×32 native, equivalent to 64×64 logical. Large
Targets may increase vertical scrolling but cannot change gameplay geometry or
hide an action.

The detached Focus perimeter extends four native pixels and never counts toward
the hit-target minimum. Scroll owners keep the complete target and its Focus
perimeter visible with a stable inset.

the prior context and makes that sample capability Unavailable; no story audio

### 10.3 Quiet suspension lifecycle

Window deactivation and minimization follow:

> Active → Draining → Suspended → Active

On focus loss:

1. input admission becomes inert and held inputs clear;
2. no new narrative, timer, generator, notification, or gameplay command may
   begin;
3. admitted bounded transactions drain to their registered stable
   commit-or-rollback frontier;
4. music and ambience follow Mute When Inactive;
5. TTS, Settings samples, and one-shot SFX/UI stop without restart;
6. Auto, notifications, confirmations, and user-facing timers preserve
   remaining foreground time;
7. generators and workers park; and
8. nonessential redraw and processing park.

When Mute When Inactive is On, music and ambience pause at exact playheads,
including both crossfade legs, gains, and remaining transition. When Off, only
music and ambience may continue. Narrative, gameplay, input, notifications,
Auto, generators, and foreground time still suspend. This is not a general
Pause command and makes no unmeasured energy-saving claim.

Resume occurs once from the last stable frontier, is idempotent under rapid
focus changes, and requires fresh activation. A result completed while inactive is
presented only after foreground return.

## 11. Display, Dark entitlement, New Run, Save, and Load projection

### 11.1 Window Mode

Display contains only `Window Mode` with literal `Windowed` and `Borderless`.
Windowed defaults and uses the 1280×720 logical baseline. Borderless uses the
current display's usable bounds.

The window coordinator prepares, applies, verifies, persists, and publishes

then shows concise factual Error. There is no half-selected mode, exclusive
fullscreen, resolution picker, fake restart requirement, or silent fallback.

### 11.2 Dark is absent from Settings

Dark Mode is never a Settings row, footer mark, category, gap, or help node.
Its durable entitlement and pending selector live in the title New Run area.
The selector changes only the title palette and next-run intent; it never
changes a live or saved run. Restore Preferences preserves both entitlement and
pending choice.

Before entitlement, that title selector is absent with zero geometry and
semantics. Once entitlement exists, it is visible and enabled and initially

### 11.3 Atomic New Run capture

A successful New Run or New Acc transaction:

1. resolves replacement confirmation;
2. freezes old-run revisions and pending Dark;
3. prepares a new run with captured Dark equal to that frozen value;
4. prepares the initial run save and pending selector Off;
5. commits run, save, and selector consumption idempotently; and
6. publishes route only after every owner agrees.

Cancel or failure changes neither old run nor pending selector. Retry uses the
frozen intent. Recovery permits only old run plus unchanged selector, or new
run plus selector Off. Entitlement remains available.

### 11.4 Save, Load, and palette truth

Every run save stores captured Dark; no save stores pending intent. Load never
reads, clears, consumes, or writes pending intent. Successful Load atomically
publishes the save's captured palette. Failure preserves current state and
palette.

A dark save remains dark after profile reset without restoring entitlement or
arming the next run. A light save remains light while pending Dark is On.
Return to title uses current pending intent, not the departed run palette.

## 12. Controls, capture, conflicts, Swap, and restoration

### 12.1 Binding inventory and conflict truth

Every rebindable semantic action owns exactly one keyboard and one controller
binding. Mouse-button rebinding is absent. Fundamental UI navigation, Accept,

Two actions conflict only when the same-device binding and declared live

labelled shared. Controller records describe semantic positions, not one

### 12.2 Capture custody

Capture ignores the activating input, rejects unsupported events truthfully,
and has no timeout or countdown. It is neutral custody, not Busy, Warning, or
Error.

Back, category or Settings departure, focus loss, or active capture-device
disconnect cancels with zero mutation and returns Focus to the initiating row

### 12.3 Conflict sheets

One conflict names both actions and offers `Swap` or `Cancel`. Several
conflicts list every affected action and the complete before/after same-device
table. A legal Swap proposal owns Warning because it proposes displacement.
Cancel initially owns Focus and changes nothing.

every registered context intersection. A multi-conflict fan-out is legal only
when displaced actions are mutually exclusive with one another. Otherwise the
Swap capability is Unavailable with its precise fact, its child action is
separately Disabled, and Cancel remains available. Failure restores the entire
prior binding set.

### 12.4 Restore Controls

`Restore Controls` is a separate confirmed profile command. It resets every
rebindable keyboard/controller slot atomically to registered defaults and

Cancel initially owns Focus; failure changes nothing.

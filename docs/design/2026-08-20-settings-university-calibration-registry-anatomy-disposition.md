# Settings University Calibration Registry Anatomy Disposition

## 1. Accepted direction

The project owner selected **A: Lock the registry anatomy** on 2026-08-20.
Settings uses the **University Calibration Registry** direction: one maintained
nocturnal directory joined to one visiting calibration folio. Its unease comes
from sincere institutional procedure, never simulated malfunction.

University Calibration Registry is the private manual/material-direction name.
The registered player-facing application label remains the localized
`Settings`; this disposition does not rename the app or bake English into art.

This disposition closes the Settings screen anatomy and material relationship.
It does not replace the accepted Settings behavior, persistence, navigation,
input, accessibility, or failure law. It authorizes no Godot implementation.

The approved visual evidence is:

- `.superpowers/brainstorm/1476-1786711376/content/settings-university-calibration-registry-anatomy-v5.html`; and
- the rendered-equivalent restart recovery at
  `.superpowers/brainstorm/581-1787233443/content/settings-university-calibration-registry-anatomy-recovered-v6.html`.

Because companion files are temporary design evidence, every binding decision
is restated below.

## 2. Locked host composition

Settings owns one fixed **400 × 328 native-pixel plate** in both hosts.

### 2.1 In-run host

- The native 640 × 360 composition remains a 240-pixel Angela/presence field
  beside the 400-pixel computer workspace.
- The established 32-pixel app strip remains above the 400 × 328 Settings
  plate.
- Settings never overlaps, replaces, narrates, or causes a procedural reaction
  in Angela's presence field.
- Settings never becomes a cinematic cutaway, a second title bar, or a movable
  faux window.

### 2.2 Logged-out title host

- The same 400 × 328 plate is centred in the accepted 480-pixel title
  workfield.
- The remaining 40 pixels on each side stay nocturnal margin.
- The accepted 160-pixel title ledger remains separate and operable.
- Extra title width does not create a second Settings composition or additional
  features.

## 3. Locked plate anatomy

At 100% text with ordinary targets, the 400 × 328 baseline uses:

- an 8-pixel outer inset;
- a 104-pixel continuous dark category directory;
- an 8-pixel filing spine; and
- a 272-pixel bounded cream folio.

The baseline folio is composed as a 40-pixel heading, a 200-pixel independently
scrolling control sheet, and a 72-pixel pinned Accessibility footer.

The category order remains:

1. Language;
2. Reading;
3. Audio;
4. Display;
5. Controls;
6. Accessibility; and
7. Records.

The directory rail and control sheet own independent vertical scrolling and
never own horizontal scrolling. At 125% or 150% text, with Large Targets, or
with longer reviewed localization, they may show fewer rows and adjust local
vertical allocation. They never shrink text, clip controls, or macro-reflow the
host composition.

All dimensions in this disposition are native 640 × 360 units. Ordinary and
Large Targets remain 24 × 24 and 32 × 32 native respectively (48 × 48 and
64 × 64 at the 2× presentation scale).

The selected directory tab appears to file into the cream folio. Its extension
across the spine is inert decoration: the tab's semantic bounds, hit target,
focus rectangle, and place in the rail never move. Selection and input focus
remain independently visible.

## 4. Locked material and state laws

The governing relationship remains **night is the room; paper is the visitor**.
The Settings folio is a bounded, application-specific document inside the
nocturnal host, not a new pale global habitat.

- Roughness and registration wear belong only to inactive material planes.
- Labels, values, seams, focus, warnings, failures, targets, and scrolling stay
  geometrically clean.
- Functional meaning never depends on texture, tint, motion, or opacity alone.
- Any visible scroll indicator must reflect real overflow and position or be
  absent. The decorative thumb in the companion is not runtime authority.

The Accessibility category alone owns a pinned, noninteractive Control Sample.
Its semantic set contains exactly:

- Selected;
- Focus;
- Warning; and
- Unavailable.

These specimens use the active production semantic roles and redundant
non-colour evidence. They do not accept focus, announce live changes, or create
a second preference draft.

The adjacent transfer bloom is a fifth **material exemplar**, not a fifth
semantic state. It is fixed and inert inside Settings. It is never generated
from a profile or record, never borrows a persisted save impression, and never
communicates selection, compatibility, chronology, importance, success,
warning, or availability. Its exact SVG contour, pigment, softness, and export
are not locked by this disposition, and it does not authorize literal blood as
Settings system truth.

## 5. Locked interaction settlement

- Ordinary discrete preferences publish immediately under the accepted
  Settings transaction law.
- Pointer slider dragging is preview-only and commits once on release;
  keyboard and controller steps retain their accepted immediate behavior.
- Binding capture, reset confirmation, platform failure, rollback, and
  indeterminate durability retain their existing truthful exceptions.
- Therefore `APPLIES AT ONCE` is specimen- or category-scoped copy, not a
  universal promise for every Settings operation.
- After a proven commit, only an internal seam or value plate may perform the
  accepted local settlement. Its box, hit target, focus outline, neighbours,
  and scroll position do not move.
- Reduced Motion, including the action that enables it, publishes the same end
  state statically.

## 6. Deliberately unlocked details

Approval of the anatomy does not lock:

- any raw hexadecimal colour or any of the sixteen functional
  theme/accessibility tuples;
- the companion's Trebuchet, Courier, microtype, negative tracking, or exact
  localized copy;
- internal row or footer heights outside the 100% ordinary-target baseline;
- the exact transfer-bloom artwork or compositor;
- final Angela, room, equipment, or title art;
- the host Home glyph, clock presentation, or scrollbar thumb position;
- sound, ambience, or scene-authored anomalies; or
- Godot scenes, resources, scripts, tests, assets, import settings, or runtime
  compatibility behavior.

Production typography remains the approved Fusion Pixel family at its exact
8-, 10-, and 12-pixel native masters. Local fit is solved by wrapping and
vertical scrolling without changing the locked macro topology.

The next visual gate is the complete functional calibration of:

> 2 base palettes × 2 contrast states × 4 colour-differentiation presets = 16
> authored tuples.

That calibration may remap semantic roles and non-colour morphology. It may not
reopen the Settings plate, host split, directory/folio relationship, or
Accessibility-only Control Sample.

## 7. Reopening rule

The rejected alternative—keeping the concept while revising its proportions,
density, control shapes, or Control Sample before tuple work—does not remain an
equal candidate. Reopening the locked anatomy requires a new explicit owner
decision. Until then, this disposition wins conflicts with exploratory
companion geometry while all retained product and accessibility authorities
continue to govern behavior.

# Contacts Maintained Correspondence Register Standard Palette and State Disposition

## 1. Accepted decision and objective

The project owner approved the complete recommended Contacts visual batch on
2026-08-22. Contacts becomes one **Maintained Correspondence Register**: a dark
institutional registrar surrounding one Worn Cream correspondence sheet.

This disposition calibrates the provisional After-Hours Standard and Midnight
Standard material and state projection of the already-authorized Contacts app.
It does not alter correspondence, ordering, read boundaries, replies,
invitations, timestamps, expiry, erasure, notification behavior, persistence,
accessibility semantics, or recovery.

The governing visual law is:

> Correspondence, order, time, direction, and absence are the evidence. The
> register never explains why the evidence looks wrong.

The app therefore remains competent, sparse, and exact. It never performs a
fictional malfunction or marks a mystery as a mystery. This disposition
authorizes no implementation.

## 2. Authority and narrow visual supersession

The Contacts Messaging, UI/UX, and Canon amendment retains sole ownership of:

- the fixed three-contact order and 31/69 master-detail topology;
- focus, activation, bulk-read, witnessed-presentation, and cache boundaries;
- canonical sequence, ordinary correspondence, operational responses, and
  invitation law;
- notification content, timing, routing, and Schedule-warning entry;
- authored timestamps, shared-minute groups, clock bleed, and erasure;
- localization, TTS, assistive output, persistence, and technical recovery; and
- public copy and domain commands.

The shared shell retains the app strip, host bounds, safe regions, Contacts
toast plate, clock arbitration, and route/focus restoration. The global
functional alphabet and state-morphology disposition retain the meaning and
invariant evidence of Selected, Focus, Hover, Press, Unread, Information,
custody, Error, and absence.

This disposition closes only the provisional Standard-master material mapping,
friend-identity calibration, component state zones, functional portrait
protection/backing, and native proof family. It narrowly supersedes older
statements that Contacts palette and slip material remain wholly unspecified.
Portrait art, crop production, identity-safe fallback, and final asset approval
remain owned by the Visual-Art Placement and Asset-Production Guide.

It also records one owner-approved redundant identity repetition: the thread
header may repeat the exact same identity slit already visible on the selected
contact row. This is styling of the same public friend identity, not a fourth
row fact, new metadata field, badge, status, or interactive component. It
cannot appear anywhere else.

The shell cohort's statement that it requires no seventh shared functional
derivative remains true within that shell scope. This Contacts cohort separately
provisionally registers two identity-family derivatives for Standard proof only.
It does not complete High Contrast, Protan, Deutan, or Tritan values.

## 3. Geometry and material hierarchy

### 3.1 Retained host geometry

The retained shell expresses geometry on a 1280-by-720 logical canvas and its
exact 640-by-360 native proof mapping. Contacts occupies the 800-by-656 logical
app-content region, equivalent to a 400-by-328 native plate beneath the shared
32-native-pixel app strip.

The accepted 31/69 split resolves to:

- contact rail: 248 logical pixels, or 124 native pixels; and
- conversation pane: 552 logical pixels, or 276 native pixels.

The split remains fixed at 100%, 125%, and 150% text. Local vertical wrapping
and scrolling absorb growth. There is no phone-page transformation, horizontal
text scroll, ellipsis, clipping, overlap, or silent type reduction.

### 3.2 Standard material roles

| Semantic role | After-Hours Standard | Midnight Standard |
|---|---|---|
| app habitat | Void | Green-Black Glass |
| registrar instrument | Deep Navy | Blackened Verdigris |
| documentary paper | Worn Cream | Worn Cream |
| outgoing diluted-ink plane | Bruised Plum | Bruised Plum |
| commitment plane | Filed Verdigris | Filed Verdigris |
| primary ink on controlled dark | Bone Text | Bone Text |
| secondary ink on controlled dark | Fog Text | Fog Text |
| contextual ink on paper or Filed | Deep Navy | Blackened Verdigris |
| invariant identity backing | Void | Void |
| paper Focus inner | Deep Brass | Deep Brass |
| dark Focus pair | Bone Text + Tarnished Gold | Bone Text + Tarnished Gold |

Components consume these semantic roles. They never reach directly to raw
hexadecimal values.

The app chassis, fixed rail, master-detail divider, and thread header use the
current registrar instrument. The entire conversation pane is one uninterrupted
documentary-paper plane. The initial Standard masters use flat opaque fields,
hard raster rules, and negative space. Texture, dither, glow, transparency, and
generated paper noise do not cross text, identity, Unread, controls, focus, or
measured boundaries.

### 3.3 Empty conversation pane

With no thread open, the conversation pane is bare Worn Cream. It contains no
inset card, heading, prompt, tutorial, icon, instruction, placeholder, fake
thread, focus target, or accessibility node.

First ordinary launcher entry must Focus Priscilla while no row is Selected.
Schedule-warning entry focuses the accepted neutral `Contacts list` app-root
stop and selects no friend. Blankness never means Unavailable or Error.

These values are not character-named primitives, text colours, general status
colours, or production-ready tuple values. The provisional registrations apply
only to the two Standard-master Contacts proofs. Failed proof returns the value
to calibration without changing the friend identity or morphology.

### 4.2 Identity morphology and invariant backing

The already-approved redundant identity morphologies remain:

- Priscilla: solid slit using semantic Priscilla identity;
- Lavinia: notched slit using semantic Lavinia identity; and
- Sylvia: twin slit using semantic Sylvia identity.

Every identity slit sits on one opaque Void backing in both Standard masters.
Midnight's Void use is a deliberate component semantic mapping, not accidental
reuse of the After-Hours habitat. It is required because Weathered Verdigris
fails required nontext contrast against Green-Black Glass, Deep Navy, and
Blackened Verdigris.

The slit is tiny, stable, and noninteractive. Exact native length and thickness
remain part of native-pixel proof; the component cannot animate, pulse, vary by
relationship, grow with Unread count, or change with route, mood, anomaly,
invitation, or message type.

Identity belongs only to the three rail rows and the owner-approved repetition
in the current thread header. It never colours names, portraits, correspondence
slips, operational responses, timestamps, linked sender labels, Unread,
notifications, Schedule, risk, or the whole thread.

## 5. Contact-row anatomy and state composition

### 5.1 One semantic target, four visual zones

Each contact row is one semantic and focus target with four internal visual
zones:

1. left Void-backed identity slit;
2. central semantic and commitment body;
3. fixed right Void-backed Unread gutter; and
4. detached outer Focus plane.

The identity slit and Unread punch are nonfocusable component evidence
represented through the owning row. They do not create child controls or extra
accessibility nodes.

The row still exposes only the retained public facts: protected portrait,
literal localized first name, and binary Unread when present. The identity slit
is redundant styling of the literal friend identity, not another public fact.

### 5.2 Default and Selected rows

A default row uses registrar-instrument body, Bone Text name, protected portrait
aperture, and the two Void gutters. Activating a friend opens that thread and
makes its row Selected only after the accepted open transaction commits.

Selected uses Filed Verdigris on the central commitment body, contextual dark
name ink, and the accepted dark filing seam. The identity and Unread gutters
remain Void and are never covered by the Selected plane. Selected means the
currently open thread; it never means keyboard Focus, recent activity,
relationship, invitation acceptance, or preferred friend.

Portrait pixels remain authored and unaltered. If a requested Contacts portrait
is unavailable, only a declared identity-safe neutral portrait of the same
woman's validated model may substitute. Otherwise Contacts enters trusted
technical recovery. An anonymous or generic silhouette is forbidden and a
missing portrait never becomes a broken-image badge or story anomaly.

### 5.3 Focus, Hover, and Press

An unselected dark row uses the Bone Text and Tarnished Gold detached Focus
pair. A Selected row uses the accepted contextual-dark Filed Focus rails and
gap. Focus remains outside the row and never clips against the rail or app edge.

Hover is the quiet inner inspection response. Press is the brief inward contact
seam. Neither borrows Selected fill, opens the thread, reads correspondence, or
predicts a consequence. Only explicit activation reaches the Contacts open
command.

### 5.4 Binary Unread

Unread is one Worn Cream square registration punch on the fixed right Void
gutter. One unread entry and several unread entries produce the identical punch
pixels, semantic state, and localized binary `Unread` description.

The punch has no count, pulse, envelope, invitation silhouette, message-type
variation, friend colour, preview, timestamp, route hint, or relationship
meaning. Focus, Hover, Press, Selected, visual exposure, toast dismissal, toast
replacement, and toast timeout do not clear it. Only the accepted friend-open
read boundary may do so.

Identity, Unread, Selected, and Focus may coexist on one row. Each remains
legible in its own zone. A later delivery to an already-open thread may
truthfully create Selected plus Unread without projecting that new entry.

## 6. Thread header, correspondence, and direction

### 6.1 Thread header

The narrow registrar-instrument header contains the protected current friend
portrait, literal first name in Bone Text, and the owner-approved repetition of
the same identity slit. It contains no unread total, online state, last-seen
time, message preview, relationship ornament, invitation category, status,
route, or anomaly mark.

The repeated slit remains noninteractive and represented through the header's
existing friend identity. It never produces another announcement or
accessibility node.

### 6.2 Incoming correspondence

Incoming slips remain Worn Cream on the Worn Cream correspondence sheet. Their
rectangle is proven by left alignment, stable margins, and one contextual-dark
one-pixel ink rule rather than a second weak paper fill. Primary and secondary
language copy both use contextual dark ink.

Dual-language hierarchy on Worn Cream comes from registered typography,
spacing, and Primary-then-Secondary order. Fog Text is prohibited on Worn Cream
because it does not meet meaningful-text contrast.

Ordinary one-to-one incoming slips repeat no portrait or visible sender label;
the selected thread header already establishes the participant.

### 6.3 Angela's outgoing correspondence

Outgoing slips use Bruised Plum, are slightly narrower, align right, and retain
the accepted structural edge notch. Primary copy uses Bone Text. Secondary
language copy may use Fog Text because Fog Text passes on Bruised Plum.

Direction remains redundant through alignment, width, edge notch, and the same
accessible sender fact. It never depends on colour alone. Outgoing slips carry
no audience-authored-text implication, delivery check, `Sent` status, or
relationship result.

### 6.4 Linked Priscilla-Lavinia sender labels

Only linked Priscilla-Lavinia incoming slips carry a small literal `Priscilla`
or `Lavinia` sender label at one stable position. The label uses contextual dark
text on Worn Cream. It receives no identity colour, identity slit, portrait,
group badge, reply arrow, or special card.

The words disambiguate operational source because two incoming participants
share one thread projection. They do not explain inviter meaning, relationship,
causality, or internal group-action identity.

## 7. Operational correspondence responses and invitations

### 7.1 Three equal response stamps

An unresolved ordinary source owns exactly three compact operational response
rows directly beneath its slip. They are Contacts message operations, not
spoken-scene narrative choices, moral cards, route selectors, result grades,
Minesweeper-result selectors, or a persistent composer.

Each row uses Worn Cream with contextual-dark text and stamped boundary. The
authored response text is its action label; no extra category badge is added.
Availability does not use Filed Selected fill. Focus uses the paper Focus
recipe; Hover and Press use the global contact morphologies. Inspection reveals
no relationship effect, tone grade, “best” answer, danger, or future echo.

### 7.2 Atomic commitment

Only activation invokes the exact response command. On successful atomic
commitment:

1. all three response controls become absent with zero geometry and no
   accessibility nodes;
2. Angela's authored outgoing slip appears;
3. the scripted incoming response appears; and
4. Focus resolves to the committed Angela response or accepted transcript
   anchor.

There is no Filed chosen-answer residue, `Sending…`, `Sent`, Busy interstitial,
Success mark, check, toast, celebration, or delayed visual promise. The new
correspondence is the complete result evidence. Failure preserves the previous
controls and trustworthy transcript and uses the accepted factual technical
boundary.

### 7.3 Solo and linked invitation boundaries

Opening a thread containing a solo invitation is already the acceptance action.
The app projects the scripted outgoing acceptance as ordinary correspondence
and adds no Accept dialog, decline menu, confirmation, badge, toast, check, or
special colour.

The Priscilla-Lavinia linked operation remains the sole invitation exception
with response controls. Its first committed response accepts the shared
invitation exactly once. A later second-participant response may add only its
registered authored variation; it cannot accept twice or create another
Schedule consequence. The visual grammar never predicts which operation owns
which consequence.

## 8. Session anchor and overflow evidence

### 8.1 `NEW` is not Unread

The temporary oldest-new anchor uses one thin bilateral contextual-dark rule
with the localized literal equivalent of `NEW` centred in the same ink. It owns
the semantic role `session_new_boundary`, not `metadata.unread`.

By the time this divider appears, the admitted stack is already read. It never
uses the Unread punch, Void gutter, identity colour, Information accent,
Selected fill, count, pulse, or live alert. It is static and nonfocusable,
participates in transcript reading order, creates no witnessed receipt, and
disappears under the existing reopen/cache law.

### 8.2 Overflow notches

Top and bottom continuation use one quiet contextual-dark cut-notch at the
correspondence-sheet edge only while content is genuinely offscreen. They
convey overflow, not Unread, count, entry type, importance, or instruction.

The notches have no separate accessibility nodes. The owning transcript scroll
region exposes ordinary scroll position and commands. There is no arrow, fade,
percentage, pulse, or decorative animation.

## 9. Time, shared minutes, and ordinary anomaly

### 9.1 Routine timestamp component

Every routine authored timestamp remains beneath its owning slip on the Worn
Cream paper plane and uses contextual-dark tabular text. No timestamp moves
inside a Bruised Plum outgoing slip. Day separators and registered minute-group
rules use contextual dark on paper.

Canonical sequence alone orders correspondence. Timestamp text, sender,
localized content, screen position, clock bleed, and minute grouping never
reorder entries or alter read, invitation, expiry, Schedule, route,
relationship, echo, ending, or command facts.

### 9.2 Day-2 shared-minute pair

The Day-2 `I know` then `Lavinia is back` pair remains two ordinary slips in the
exact canonical order with one ordinary shared `HH:MM` marker immediately
before the first slip. The duplicate per-slip timestamps alone are replaced by
that one group-boundary marker.

The pair receives no group card, connector, reply arrow, anomaly border,
special colour, animation, sound, causal label, character explanation, or
assistive interpretation. Assistive traversal announces the minute once at the
group boundary and presents both entries in visual order.

### 9.3 Rare clock bleed

An allocated Contacts clock-bleed value consumes the exact routine timestamp
component. Only its frozen displayed digit glyphs differ. Typography, material,
weight, spacing, rule, label grammar, assistive sentence, focus behavior, and
component identity remain identical.

It receives no warning, status, icon, glitch, tooltip, sound, identity change,
or provenance. It cannot control sorting, grouping, read state, actionability,
deadline, mechanics, or command identity. Same-run restoration reproduces the
frozen value exactly without resampling.

## 10. Withheld delivery and absolute player-facing erasure

### 10.1 Late held delivery

When a new entry arrives for an already-open thread, the selected row acquires
binary Unread. The entry's slip, response controls, sender/time/text nodes, TTS,
live announcement, witnessed receipt, and layout allocation remain absent.

Scrolling, Focus movement, Hover, resize, language change, or window refocus
cannot reveal or read it. A later explicit accepted open/reopen activation
admits the new watermark, clears only the admitted Unread fact, publishes the
entry at the oldest-new anchor, and moves Focus under the retained transcript
law.

### 10.2 Unanswered ordinary erasure

At its accepted logical-day boundary, an unanswered ordinary entry has no
player-facing erasure state. Its source slip, three response rows, timestamp or
minute-group ownership, temporary `NEW` divider, toast trace, gap, ruled
vacancy, status, History projection, accessibility nodes, and layout allocation
are all absent. Remaining correspondence reflows without a scar.

There is no deletion animation, dust, fade, blank rule, missing-time mark,
`Unanswered` label, disabled late-reply action, ghost Focus, echo, or pre-expiry
target styling. Erasure occurs inside logical-day resolution; it invents no
intermediate Contacts focus transfer. The retained day-transition and shell
law clears the view cache and governs the next launcher/Minesweeper Focus.

The private deterministic tombstone remains solely for generation and
idempotency. It cannot reconstruct or expose text. Loading an earlier valid
pre-expiry snapshot may truthfully restore that earlier correspondence with the
same frozen target. A post-erasure snapshot cannot resurrect it.

### 10.3 Rare unread solo erasure

The rare selected solo target obeys the same total player-facing absence. The
offer, its Unread contribution, toast binding, `nevermind`, gap, History entry,
accessibility trace, and replacement target are absent after lawful resolution.
The private seed, selected identity, tombstone, and suppressed follow-up remain
private and receive no palette treatment.

## 11. Shell notification and recovery boundary

The Contacts toast remains the shell-owned controlled-dark Information plate
defined by the shared-shell disposition. It is outside Contacts component/state
ownership even when the shell places it over a compatible computer-content safe
region while Contacts is visible. It receives no friend identity slit or
colour, Unread punch or gutter, portrait, preview, timestamp, count, invitation
type, timer bar, or response action.

Toast `Go` invokes the ordinary friend-open boundary. `X`, timeout, replacement,
and suppression never clear Unread. The toast remains outside the Contacts
plate and obeys the shell safe-region and overlay priority laws.

Known ineligibility uses its existing truthful state. A real user-impacting
failure preserves the last trustworthy correspondence and uses factual trusted
recovery. Failure never creates, removes, reorders, reads, accepts, or replies
to correspondence; impersonates erasure or clock bleed; attributes itself to a
character; consumes anomaly allocation; or exposes raw diagnostics.

## 12. Accessibility, language, and artwork boundary

Assistive output exposes exactly the same public facts:

- friend name and binary Unread through the owning row;
- disclosed sender only where visibly disclosed;
- displayed authored or frozen time;
- message text in canonical visual order;
- available operational response controls; and
- ordinary control role, state, and action label.

Portraits, identity slits, and Unread punches are represented through their
owning row rather than extra nodes. Overflow belongs to the transcript scroll
region. Linked sender labels and sender/time/text remain in semantic reading
order. Assistive traversal or TTS may satisfy the same witnessed-presentation
boundary as visual viewport entry, but bulk read alone does not witness an
offscreen line.

Assistive output never exposes Unread count, invitation category, erasure
target, tombstone, clock source, linked internal action identity, relationship,
route, Observer metadata, or an explanation unavailable visually. Erased and
withheld entries have no assistive substitute or extra clue.

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and supported dual-language
orders wrap vertically within the fixed topology. Both Worn-paper language
lines use contextual dark ink; the secondary line remains quieter through
typography and spacing while retaining meaningful-text contrast.

Portrait pixels and every authored decorative asset remain unaltered across
the tuple. Identity colour never recolours portrait art. Functional contrast is
proved on controlled backing and never sampled from artwork.

## 14. Contacts Truth-Trace Standard Deck

The proof family reuses the shared six-sheet Standard schema:

> After-Hours and Midnight × 100%, 125%, and 150% text masters

Each sheet renders every fixture as one complete 640-by-360 native capture in a
24-by-24 target column and again in a 32-by-32 target column. Contacts occupies
the real 400-by-328 native plate inside the real shell. Each capture has its own
manifest record and exact target mode.

The closed fixture family contains:

1. blank ordinary list entry with Priscilla Focused, no Selected row, and bare
   Worn pane;
2. Schedule-warning list entry with neutral app-root Focus and no Selected row;
3. all three row identity morphologies on invariant Void backing in both modes,
   plus exact row/header identity agreement with no extra node or announcement
   and no propagation into sender labels, slips, or toast;
4. one row carrying identity plus binary Unread plus Selected plus Focus, with
   Hover and Press collision captures that occlude nothing;
5. one-Unread and several-Unread sources producing pixel-identical punches and
   the same public binary state;
6. late held delivery to a selected open thread, then explicit reactivation and
   lawful `NEW` anchoring;
7. incoming and outgoing slips, unresolved response stamps, Focus/Hover/Press,
   and atomic committed replacement without Busy or Success;
8. solo opening with acceptance but no response controls or confirmation;
9. linked Priscilla-Lavinia correspondence with literal sender labels, exactly
   three rail rows, and participant-specific Unread clearing;
10. top and bottom overflow notches with no separate semantic nodes;
11. the Day-2 shared-minute pair in exact visual and assistive order;
12. routine, clock-bleed, and same-run-restored timestamps whose styled pixels
    are identical after displayed digit glyphs are masked;
13. pre/post unanswered-ordinary erasure with geometry, Focus, History, and
    accessibility-tree diffs; and
14. pre/post rare unread solo erasure whose post-projection is indistinguishable
    from lawful never-projected absence apart from unrelated correspondence.

Every fixture uses registered longest-wrap English, Simplified Chinese, and
Traditional Chinese strings plus supported dual-language orders. The manifest
records semantic fixture IDs, canonical sequence, read watermark, Selected and
Focus owners, hit rectangles, scroll anchors, status zones, public timestamp,
private test provenance, asset IDs and hashes, `artwork_unaltered`, resolved
token lineage, accessibility snapshot, transaction trace, and final-pixel
contrast.

A whole Standard master fails if any required collision, locale, target mode,
or paired trace fails. Rejection includes:

- identity, Unread, Selected, or Focus covering another fact;
- one/many Unread producing different punches, counts, or semantics;
- Focus, Hover, inspection, or toast dismissal reading correspondence;
- withheld content appearing in transcript, TTS, layout, or accessibility;
- response rows styled as Selected, route, moral, or result choices;
- fabricated `Sending…`, `Sent`, Busy, Success, check, or acceptance toast;
- linked sender identity relying on colour or creating a fourth contact;
- `NEW` reusing Unread morphology or surviving its accepted boundary;
- ordinary and clock-bleed timestamp styling differing beyond digits;
- erased content leaving geometry, copy, animation, History, focus, or
  accessibility residue;
- clipped text, ellipsis, shrink-to-fit, horizontal text scroll, focus clipping,
  opacity-only evidence, resampling, or native-coordinate drift;
- portrait recolouring or art-dependent functional contrast; and
- any Observer, avatar, anomaly-generator, special-ending, or post-v1 aperture
  scaffold.

## 15. Explicit exclusions and completion boundary

This disposition does not:

- alter Contacts mechanics, content, chronology, commands, persistence, or
  notification law;
- remove legitimate Contacts operational responses or turn them into spoken-
  scene choices;
- complete High Contrast or colour-differentiation tuples;
- approve Worn Mauve or Weathered Verdigris outside provisional Contacts
  Standard proof;
- add texture, animation, new portraits, new messages, UI copy, audio, or
  interpretation;
- reopen Observer UI, the visual anomaly generator, avatar frames, post-v1
  aperture refinements, or special-ending reconciliation; or
- authorize a Godot plan, scene, resource, script, token file, asset, test,
  migration, import, task, or runtime change.

The written artifact is complete for owner review when it contains no
placeholder, unresolved alternative, hidden behavior change, or production
claim and keeps every operational fact attached to its existing owner. Owner
approval of these written bytes would close only this Contacts Standard visual
disposition and would still authorize no implementation.

# Ordinary Witnessed-Scene Reading Apparatus Standard Palette and State Disposition

## 1. Accepted decision and objective

The project owner approved the complete recommended ordinary witnessed-scene
reading-apparatus batch on 2026-08-22. The Room-Owned Aperture, Registered
Material Field, bottom-up caption leaves, canonical six-control rail, ordinary
History, and Minesweeper handoff become one competent institutional witnessing
instrument.

This disposition closes the provisional After-Hours Standard and Midnight
Standard material and state projection for that instrument. It does not alter
dialogue, scene order, art staging, caption publication, transport behavior,
Minesweeper mechanics, result mapping, consequences, persistence, Backup,
localization, TTS, accessibility facts, recovery, or route logic.

The governing law is:

> The witnessing apparatus never interprets what it records; chronology and
> custody are shown by topology alone.

The apparatus therefore records literal words, visible surfaces, chronological
replacement, and real operational ownership. It never marks a line as strange,
solves a speaker, grades a board result, forecasts a consequence, or performs a
fictional malfunction. This disposition authorizes no implementation.

## 2. Authority and narrow visual supersession

The Room-Owned Aperture v1 design retains ownership of:

- the authored room surface, body, object, action, crop, and staging contract;
- Angela's invariant nonprojection inside canonical witnessed scenes;
- visible non-Angela participant truth and public scene-description atoms;
- the aperture's pointer-inert, nonfocusable, noninteractive boundary;
- asset approval, required-art failure, and trusted recovery; and
- the future atomic migration from the current host.

The Narrative Scene Host amendment, as corrected by the Canonical Spoken-Scene
Agency disposition, retains ownership of:

- semantic beats, reveal, Normal Accept, Auto, Skip, Next, and completion;
- exact control capabilities in canonical run, Gallery, and Rehearsal hosts;
- challenge admission, durable receipts, consequence order, and terminal
  return;
- History contents, focus trap, source restoration, TTS, and language
  projection;
- Backup invocation, save/load truth, pause, modal precedence, and recovery;
  and
- input normalization, release gates, localization, target modes, and
  assistive semantics.

The Bottom-Up Scene Caption Rhythm amendment retains publication movement,
visible retention depth, language-mode depth, chronological visual order, and
current-first assistive order. The Room-Owned Aperture Caption Material
disposition retains the Registered Material Field, one aperture seam, nearly
flush leaf family, opaque History family, and nonsemantic-wear boundary.

The global functional alphabet and state-morphology disposition retain the
meaning and invariant evidence of Focus, Hover, Press, Selected or Current,
Disabled, Unavailable, Busy, operational status, custody, and absence. This
cohort cannot manufacture one of those facts merely because a token exists.

This disposition closes only:

- the two provisional Standard material mappings for the caption field, leaves,
  rail, and History;
- the exact noninterpretive use of those materials;
- the rail's Standard state composition and native visual inset;
- complete caption-and-rail withdrawal during board custody;
- atomic visual reconstruction after durable consequence; and
- the first Standard native proof family.

It narrowly supersedes the Caption Material disposition only where that
artifact left final Standard leaf value steps, backing, registration, and
History anatomy unlocked. It does not settle High Contrast, Protan, Deutan, or
Tritan values, and it does not make either Standard master production-ready.

The later production family remains sixteen explicit tuples:

> {After-Hours, Midnight} × {Standard, High Contrast} ×
> {Standard, Protan, Deutan, Tritan}

Each tuple must be authored and validated explicitly. A runtime filter, shader,
generated colour transformation, or component-local fallback cannot stand in
for one of them.

Older inline-choice clauses remain retired. Dating, visible
Priscilla-Lavinia, Hospital, ordered-ending playback, and faithful Gallery or
Rehearsal projection contain no narrative choice surface, state, geometry, or
assistive residue.

The later owner correction that speaker identity is never publicly disclosed
also controls this complete cohort. Internal speaker ownership may remain
available to validated story playback, but no visible label, leaf ornament,
History prefix, TTS prefix, assistive announcement, or operational help names
the speaker. This narrowly supersedes the Room-Owned Aperture draft's optional
named `public_speaker_disclosure` projection for these hosts.

The Hospital and Ordered-Ending Current-v1 UI disposition is the narrower
consumer authority for those hosts. It retains Hospital's zero-or-one-Sylvia
occupancy, captionless silent holds, and complete prohibition on public speaker
identity or equivalent participant, room, object, action, or medical
description. It also retains ordered-ending caption/History continuity,
completion holds, step seams, final neutral matte, and title landing. This
cohort supplies their ordinary material skin only.

## 3. Native geometry and material ownership

### 3.1 Fixed witnessed-scene bands

The fixed native canvas remains 640 by 360. The default 1280-by-720 physical
window is its exact 2x integer presentation. Every coordinate in this
disposition is native unless explicitly labelled as a physical presentation.

| Text preset | Room-Owned Aperture | Registered Material Field | Control rail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100% | `(0,0,640,224)` | `(0,224,640,104)` | `(0,328,640,32)` |
| 125% | `(0,0,640,196)` | `(0,196,640,132)` | `(0,328,640,32)` |
| 150% | `(0,0,640,164)` | `(0,164,640,164)` | `(0,328,640,32)` |

The aperture and complete authored composite end at the applicable field
boundary. No art pixel, body, object, clue, shadow, or environmental texture
continues beneath caption glyphs. The field always ends at native `y = 328`,
and the rail remains pinned to `y = 328..359`.

Presentation at 2x, 3x, and 4x uses integer nearest-neighbour scaling with no
gap, overlap, resampling, or topology change.

### 3.2 Registered field and single seam

The field is one opaque low-value university-maintained material. It is
independent of room, character, speaker, relationship, route, board result,
Observer state, anomaly, health, pressure, and emotional intensity.

Exactly one one-native-pixel `witness.aperture_seam.rule` occupies the UI side
of the aperture boundary and resolves through `reading.registration`. The
authored aperture loses no row of pixels. The seam is stationary and identical
for ordinary prose, strange prose, pre-challenge thresholds, Hospital, visible
pair scenes, and post-result continuation.

The seam never doubles, glows, pulses, breaks, discolours, acquires a notch, or
changes thickness. There is no aperture mask, frame, crown, recording mark,
speaker edge, or relationship lip.

### 3.3 Initial Standard material discipline

The Standard masters use flat opaque fields, hard raster rules, and negative
space. Quiet maintained wear is omitted from this first calibration family.
If later admitted under its existing authority, it remains stationary,
nonsemantic, and confined to inactive planes; it can never cross copy,
registration, controls, focus, targets, or measured evidence.

The field is not paper, a transcript sheet, an evidence wall, a camera display,
a chat surface, or a continuation of room art.

### 4.3 Excluded colour implications

Worn Cream is not used by the caption field, leaves, rail, or History. Those
surfaces are maintained instruments, not visiting documents or paper
correspondence.

Oxidized Mint is not used as a current-caption edge, active scrollbar mark, or
post-result signal. Adding it would create an unnecessary settled, success, or
special-line implication. Rust Thread cannot carry required Midnight nontext
evidence and is absent. Fired Rust and Fired Rose Enamel appear only if an
existing owning recovery or risk surface truthfully requires them; the ordinary
reading apparatus invents no such state.

Bruised Plum is a material plane, not a warning, affection, speaker, route,
result, or interpretive status. Reuse of Bruised Plum by the current leaf and
the History reading plane creates material kinship only.

## 5. Bottom-up registered caption leaves

### 5.1 Single-language topology

The caption stack remains bottom-anchored immediately above the rail.

| Available semantic beats | Visual order from top to bottom | Material order |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | current | `caption.current.fill` |
| 2 | previous, current | `caption.previous.fill`; `caption.current.fill` |
| 3 | oldest retained, previous, current | `caption.oldest.fill`; `caption.previous.fill`; `caption.current.fill` |

Blank capacity remains above the stack. A newly published beat becomes the
lowest current leaf, the former current rises, and the fourth-oldest beat leaves
the visible stack while remaining in History.

Every visible leaf uses exactly one one-native-pixel
`caption.leaf.leading_rule` on its leading horizontal edge. The rules are
identical in colour, thickness, and morphology. A leaf receives no full
decorative frame, paper edge, shadow, glow, speaker notch, portrait tab, route
mark, or special-current glyph.
When the uppermost visible leaf begins exactly at the aperture boundary, its
leading rule coincides with and consumes the single aperture seam; it cannot
create a second adjacent rule.

The leaves remain nearly flush within the field. Their approved typography
metrics and safe text insets are consumed from the caption owner; this
disposition adds no decorative padding system. Final composited proof must keep
each registration edge separate, each line legible, and the current leaf
visibly lowest at every text preset and locale.

### 5.2 Chronology is not global commitment

The current leaf is current only in chronological caption terms. It does not
receive the global Filed Selected or Current commitment plane. Its lowest
position and `caption.current.fill` material are sufficient.

If the current caption activation target owns Focus, only the detached
`caption.current.focus` treatment appears. Focus does not recolour the leaf,
create selection, mark a speaker, or imply that the prose is actionable as a
narrative choice.

The first perimeter row remains untouched by Focus: at the leading edge it is
the mandatory `caption.leaf.leading_rule`, and at the other three edges it is
the owning Bruised Plum buffer. Inside that protected row,
`caption.current.focus` draws one outer Bone rail, one untouched Bruised Plum
gap, and one inner Tarnished Gold rail. The caption's protected text inset
begins inside this four-native-pixel perimeter. No Focus pixel enters the rail,
the preceding leaf, the aperture seam, the leading registration rule, or text
ink.

Retained leaves:

- keep the same selected text size as current;
- keep complete meaningful-text contrast;
- remain unconditionally nonfocusable in current v1;
- never fade, blur, shrink, become translucent, or lose glyph weight;
- never replay TTS or live announcement when they move; and
- never become evidence that one line is less true or important.

The three material levels are permanent upholstery of the three spatial
positions, not metadata attached to a semantic beat. A beat changes material
only because publication moves it into a different topological position.
Position and order remain the source of chronological meaning; colour cannot
identify chronology independently and is never exposed as chronology to
assistive output.

Publication may retain the accepted restrained upward material shift. Reduced
Motion, restore, locale change, text-size change, and uncover reconstruct the
correct topology immediately. Movement creates no beat, receipt, History entry,
state, or interpretation.

### 5.3 Reveal and language modes

Partial reveal occurs only within the lowest current leaf. Completing reveal
does not move the stack; only publication of a new semantic beat does.

When actual vertical overflow remains after the accepted text-preset geometry
is applied, one narrow UI-owned caption-scroll gutter appears at the field edge.
It uses `caption.scroll.gutter`, a Fog Blue outer track rule, one native pixel of
`caption.scroll.well`, and a Bone thumb. The thumb never directly touches the
Fog Blue rule. It reports only the real viewport extent and position, creates no
separate focus stop or assistive node, and disappears when overflow is absent.
Publication scrolls only enough to keep the complete current leaf visible.
Caption scrolling never changes chronology, witnessed state, or History and
never becomes progress, importance, or clue evidence.

Single-language mode may show current plus the previous two available beats.
Dual Language mode shows exactly one current semantic leaf at the bottom:
Primary first, Secondary second. Those language rows share one beat, receipt,
History entry, and advance boundary. Dual Language never creates retained
leaves.

Within the caption apparatus, assistive order begins with current, then the
available previous leaves, then the rail. Visual top-to-bottom chronology is
implemented independently and cannot force oldest-first assistive traversal.
Primary and Secondary remain separately identified language projections
without gaining different narrative status.

### 5.4 Hospital captionless silent hold

The narrower Hospital authority may publish a genuine authored room-only or
silent visual hold before a real caption. During that hold:

- the caption stack owns zero leaves, activation targets, Focus, reveal state,
  TTS, live announcement, History entry, or assistive-caption node;
- the aperture remains pointer-inert;
- the six-control rail keeps its exact stationary geometry, but its narrative
  commands project the truthful Disabled action edge under the temporary hold
  and leave focus and input traversal;
- the authored hold timing, not an invisible control, owns progression; and
- Back may still invoke an already-authorized suspension owner, which freezes
  rather than expires the hold.

The hold contains no blank string, zero-width label, hidden current caption,
spinner, status, minimum technical delay, or loading concealment. The first
ordinary player-operable narrative state begins only when a real caption is
published and owns Focus.

## 6. Canonical six-control rail

### 6.1 Exact order and native allocation

Canonical run scenes expose exactly:

`History | Skip | Auto | Save | Load | Next`

The rail is one 640-by-32-native-pixel `rail.chassis.fill` plane. Six contiguous
semantic hit rectangles divide the complete width through deterministic
proportional boundaries:

> `x(i) = floor(640 × i / 6)` for boundaries `i = 0..6`

The four remainder pixels are distributed one at a time by the proportional
hit boundaries and never appear as a special or privileged command. The
default visual action plate uses `rail.control.default_fill` and is inset two
native pixels on every side of its hit
rectangle. The full hit rectangle remains interactive. The inset reserves
native pixels for detached Focus at the canvas edge without reducing the
accepted 24-by-24 or 32-by-32 target contract.

The Focus plane begins at the visual plate boundary and draws inward rather
than outside the hit rectangle. On a default dark plate it is one outer Bone
rail, one untouched control-face gap, and one inner Tarnished Gold rail. On an
active Filed plate it is one outer current-theme habitat rail, one untouched
Filed gap, and one inner controlled-dark rail. The two-native-pixel exterior
inset remains clear between this outermost Focus plane and the canvas edge or
neighboring target. No Focus pixel clips, enters another hit rectangle, or
covers label ink.

Labels remain in the exact order in every locale. Registered localized labels
wrap within their target if needed; they never shrink, clip, ellipsize, scroll
horizontally, reorder, or change the macro topology.

Ordinary dialogue entry focuses the current caption activation target. Tab then
enters the rail in visual order; Shift-Tab reverses it. The aperture never enters
focus order.

### 6.2 Truthful component states

Each control owns only its existing operational facts:

- Hover is a quiet local face response and never moves or focuses the target.
- Press is one brief inner contact seam and never impersonates commitment or
  success.
- Focus on an ordinary dark plate is the detached
  Bone-and-Tarnished-Gold double outline. Focus on an active Filed transport
  uses the current theme's habitat and controlled-dark rails plus their
  separating gap.
- Disabled remains readable and uses the global blocked-action morphology only
  when the owning command is genuinely inadmissible.
- Unavailable appears only when an existing capability owner publishes that
  fact; it is not a synonym for Disabled.
- Busy or operational status appears only on the surface that actually owns a
  visible-duration operation or result.

No rail control acquires a dominant whole-row skin. Compatible facts compose in
their registered zones, and an absent control owns no geometry, target, focus,
hover, press, label, or assistive node.

### 6.3 Auto, Skip, and Next

Auto and Skip are labelled On/Off modes and are mutually exclusive under the
existing transport arbitration. Auto uses `rail.control.mode_on_fill` with
`rail.control.mode_on_ink` and `rail.control.mode_on_seam` when the
profile-owned Auto preference is On; that plate does not claim that an Auto
timer is currently eligible or running. Skip uses the same commitment grammar
only while the session-owned Skip mode is active. A temporarily suspended Auto
timer does not falsify the visible On preference. These two Filed states cannot
coexist and remain distinct from Focus, Hover, and Press.

Next is a one-shot seek, never an active mode, destination preview, result
selector, completion forecast, or relationship hint. Its accepted contextual
help remains:

> Skip witnessed content to the next unseen beat or challenge.

That help may appear only through the already-authorized focus, hover, or
assistive description. It is never persistent caption prose, a tutorial card,
or a narrative promise.

### 6.4 History, Save, and Load

History opens the ordinary full-canvas reading owner defined below.

Save and Load invoke the accepted Backup component in the corresponding mode.
The rail creates no local `Saving…`, `Saved`, `Loading…`, success mark, failure
mark, spinner, timer, minimum delay, or slot status. Any truthful
visible-duration or settled save state remains inside Backup or the accepted
shell-owned Quick surface.

Gallery and Rehearsal retain their existing capability laws. A control that
does not exist for an archive host is absent rather than displayed as a fake
record. This disposition does not manufacture archive capabilities.

## 7. Ordinary full-canvas History

### 7.1 Material anatomy

History replaces the visible scene with one opaque full-canvas institutional
reading ledger from the Registered Material Field family. It is not a
translucent overlay.

Its Standard anatomy is:

- full-canvas `history.canvas.fill`;
- one `history.header.fill` well containing only the existing History
  structure;
- one dense `history.reading_plane.fill` for admitted lines;
- `history.text.primary` and `history.text.secondary` text;
- `history.separator` rules and one `history.scroll.track` outer rule;
- one one-native-pixel `history.scroll.well` gutter inside that track; and
- one `history.scroll.thumb` inside the dark well.

The Bone thumb never directly touches the Fog Blue track. The dark well is the
final adjacency for both: Fog Blue proves against `reading.field`, and Bone
proves against the same controlled-dark well.

The History plane contains no Worn Cream, loose slips, evidence pins,
comparison columns, portraits, speaker colours, outcome categories, or
collectible marks. No scene pixel remains visible beneath it.

### 7.2 Content and interaction

History contains only stable lines and presentation atoms admitted to the
current scene's History stream, in canonical order. Dual Language reprojects
Primary then Secondary inside the same semantic entry. Read Aloud remains
Primary-only under its existing owner.

History is inspect-only. It cannot rewind, mutate, replay a decision, change a
shot, alter a board, grant evidence, recalculate a route, or present a line as
an answer. Ordinary lines are not Selected or Current components.

While open, History traps focus. Initial focus remains its accepted heading or
current-history anchor, never an invented default verdict. Back closes History
and restores the exact valid source focus. Opening, scrolling, or closing does
not duplicate live announcement or current-line TTS.

When the heading owns initial Focus, `history.heading.focus` draws the
Bone/substrate-gap/Tarnished-Gold perimeter inward on the header target. When
the accepted current-history anchor owns initial Focus, that anchor focuses the
semantic scroll container and `history.scroll.focus` surrounds the reading
plane; it never outlines an individual line. Both owners reserve their existing
registration row and text inset before drawing Focus. Neither treatment makes a
History line Selected, Current, editable, or actionable.

## 8. Minesweeper custody withdrawal and return

### 8.1 Durable entry

Before a challenge-entry command is admitted, the complete witnessed-scene
apparatus remains intact and truthful. Admission immediately consumes the
triggering input, release-gates held input, cancels Press and Hover, and makes
every narrative command inert. No apparently actionable `ENTERING_CHALLENGE`
frame may publish.

If entry durability completes before another presentable frame, the next
published frame is the durable board takeover below. If genuine preparation
spans one or more visible frames, one opaque
`challenge.entering_matte.fill` covers the complete frozen apparatus. That
matte owns no focus, copy, status, progress, minimum dwell, or input queue. It
is the sole lawful visible-duration entry projection; the underlying caption
and rail trees remain frozen and inactive until durability decides whether they
are removed or restored.

While retained privately for exact rollback, those covered trees are absent
from player-facing rendering, active focus, input, hover, tooltip, TTS,
announcement, and assistive traversal. In particular, visible Save is already
withdrawn throughout `ENTERING_CHALLENGE`.

No board becomes active until the entry receipt and exact board snapshot are
durable.

Only after durable entry:

1. the exact settled authored aperture freezes;
2. one static `challenge.active_veil.fill` is composited above the frozen art;
3. the Registered Material Field, all caption leaves, and the complete rail
   withdraw;
4. their rendered rectangles, layout allocation, hit targets, focus stops,
   hover regions, tooltips, TTS ownership, live announcements, and assistive
   nodes become absent; and
5. the durable board becomes the sole active input and focus subtree.

Withdrawal is complete replacement, not Disabled narrative chrome. There is no
ghost rail, dim caption residue, unavailable-looking control, hidden choice
row, or reserved caption gap beneath the board.

The veil uses one fixed opacity across After-Hours and Midnight and across every
board result, relationship state, route, participant, and line. Its exact
Standard alpha is a native-proof calibration parameter. One candidate value is
declared once at the Standard-deck root before capture and inherited unchanged
by every fixture in both themes, all text presets, both target modes, and all
content states. It cannot vary per capture. If native proof rejects it, the
entire six-sheet family is invalidated, one new candidate is registered, and
the complete family is regenerated. Production acceptance remains deferred
until continuity stays perceptible without weakening board legibility. The veil
never pulses, thickens, or grades pressure or outcome.

If preparation fails before durable entry, recovery returns to the complete
intact pre-entry apparatus, restores its exact valid Focus, and proves that no

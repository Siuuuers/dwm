# Sylvia Causation Immediate Ablation

## 1. Outcome

The owner fixed Sylvia's non-causation as production truth while deliberately
allowing the released evidence to support suspicion that she induced Angela's
collapse. Because the decision changes causality, Hospitalization, agency, and
ending interpretation, it received an immediate adversarial gate.

The single-model review is complete. The owner explicitly declined the offered
cross-model review, so reconciliation proceeds with single-model findings only.
Applicable repairs are incorporated in Section 8.5 of the working Narrative
Constitution ledger. No Bible, plot, Dialogic timeline, or runtime code is
changed by this record.

## 2. Removal and simplification test

- **Remove fixed non-causation:** a later writer could turn any suspicious prop
  into a concealed induction method. That would erase the audience-caused
  consequence, contradict the owner, and make prior ambiguity a withheld answer
  rather than a fair plurality of readings.
- **Remove audience suspicion:** Sylvia becomes only an obvious opportunist;
  preparedness loses its dangerous double image, and the audience has less
  reason to combine timing, custody, access, and testimony across routes.
- **Replace both with “cause unresolved”:** this is smaller in wording but not
  in maintenance. It restores the exact ambiguity the owner rejected and lets
  every scene silently invent a different causal truth.

The two-level rule is therefore the smallest form that performs both required
jobs: exact backstage causality and unresolved audience judgment.

## 3. Adversarial findings and reconciliation

### SC-01 — Private truth could become an unrecoverable retcon

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Requiring a private innocent
explanation for each clue is insufficient. The released surfaces must also let
attentive synthesis recover a substantive ordinary causal account. They need
not certify Sylvia's innocence, but they cannot make non-causation exist only in
an unpublished manual.

### SC-02 — Abstract meters were mistaken for physiology

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Pressure and health remain game
abstractions. Section 8.5 now requires a later, physically checked continuity
account of observable circumstances and qualified response without prematurely
inventing a diagnosis or actionable method.

### SC-03 — Indirect acts and omissions escaped the first wording

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The causal exclusion now covers direct
and indirect acts, psychological or logistical engineering, proxies, withheld
timely aid, and delayed escalation. Ordinary audience-directed costs attached
to a Sylvia encounter remain possible, but they cannot be rewritten as her
secret design.

### SC-04 — “Control the aftermath” implied authority she lacks

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The revision subordinates every
aftermath opportunity to the lawful-channel rule. Preparation and proximity do
not grant clinical, record, visitor, or treatment powers.

### SC-05 — Exploitation lacked local consent and progression firewalls

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The revision repeats the relevant
constraints at the point of risk: no affection, consent, romance, or progression
award; no conversion of incapacity into permission; and no intimate or erotic
contact while Angela is unresponsive.

### SC-06 — Causal authorship accidentally settled unresolved resolver details

**Classification: Contract misread caused by over-broad artifact wording.** The
owner has fixed the joint-boundary requirement but not comparison operators,
resolver timing, recovery, or sequela. The revised rule governs attribution only
after the eventual approved resolver returns Hospital and expressly leaves
those implementation details open.

### SC-07 — Individually plausible clues could be impossible together

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The fairness test now applies both to
each clue and to the complete constellation, including timing and institutional
access. One stable non-causal history must accommodate the whole set.

### SC-08 — Supersession had no bounded provenance

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Section 8.5 identifies the displaced
intent-and-means claims and the five files that currently repeat them. It also
protects unrelated trigger, order, object, and image decisions from accidental
retirement.

### SC-09 — “No false fact” could forbid character deception

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The revised rule protects exact
authorial physical facts and authenticated observations while continuing to
permit lies, mistakes, misreports, and altered records as fixed character acts.
It requires the carrier to distinguish a received claim from neutral fact.

## 4. Cycle 2 findings and reconciliation

The repaired artifact received a second fresh-context adversarial pass.
Cross-model review remained skipped under the owner's explicit standing choice.

### SC-10 — The joint Hospital gate was only cross-referenced

**Classification: Contract misread; defensive clarification applied.** Section
8.1 already makes pressure maximum and health minimum jointly necessary, and
the review contract repeated that law. Section 8.5 now restates both boundaries
to prevent an isolated excerpt from being read as a one-boundary route. Exact
resolver timing and comparison details remain open.

### SC-11 — Sylvia-scene costs conflicted with absolute non-causation

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The revision now permits an encounter
to locate an audience-directed burden only when that burden arises from
Angela's exertion, neglect, or action rather than Sylvia's conduct. A
continuation that cannot preserve this division may not join the qualifying
collapse chain.

### SC-12 — Character deception reopened medical misinformation

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The permission to lie, misremember, or
alter a carrier now excludes false medical claims, forged clinical material,
and actionable medical misinformation without exception.

### SC-13 — Audience action could merely correlate with the physical cause

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The private continuity sheet must
connect audience-directed, condition-affecting operations to the accumulated
physical burden and collapse. The hidden meters can trigger game resolution;
they cannot stand beside an unrelated physical cause and borrow its consequence.

### SC-14 — The ordinary solution could be fixed after its clues

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The causal continuity is now required
before any collapse-facing plot beat, Hospital passage, or suspicious Sylvia
clue is approved for the new spine—not merely before final Hospital prose.

### SC-15 — Fallible-record provenance could remain private

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** When a discrepancy affects causal
interpretation, an authorized released surface must provide a legible reason to
treat its carrier as fallible. Later writing may not reclassify a previously
authenticated fact to rescue a theory.

## 5. Cycle 3 findings and reconciliation

The twice-repaired artifact received the third and final bounded adversarial
cycle. Cross-model review remained skipped under the owner's explicit standing
choice. The findings below were repaired; the cadence forbids a fourth cycle on
the same artifact, so this record now stops and exposes the remaining owner
choice rather than manufacturing indefinite review.

### SC-16 — The complete causal account could still become a method

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Non-actionability now governs the
entire private chain and the aggregate of released clues, not merely its list of
observable circumstances. Physical plausibility may never become reproducible
instruction.

### SC-17 — Reasonable suspicion was grammatically optional

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The full released design must now make
Sylvia-induced collapse a reasonable but unproved hypothesis. Individual clues
remain selectable; the dramatic function does not.

### SC-18 — An invitation could launder Sylvia's causal contribution

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** A neutral-substitution test now asks
whether Angela's accumulated condition and the same audience-directed action
would still support the burden without Sylvia's special staging. If not, the
candidate conflicts with her non-causation and cannot join the collapse chain.

### SC-19 — Lawful access was only implied through prohibited powers

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Every access channel Sylvia actually
uses must now be ordinary, lawful, and approved under the adjacent-hospital
rule. Extraordinary or illicit access cannot hide behind suspicion.

### SC-20 — “Authorized” could conceal a fourth surface

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Every clue, causal exposition, and
reordered fragment must occur inside a message, dating encounter, or ending.
The causation mystery creates no additional cutaway, website, dossier, or
Hospital-only narrative class.

### SC-21 — Major suspicious clues could retain only private excuses

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Every materially inculpatory clue now
requires a released counter-facet supporting or constraining its non-causal
provenance. Complete explanations may remain unavailable, but decisive-looking
facts cannot be rescued solely by production notes.

### SC-22 — Legacy fragments could evade prospective safeguards

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The Special order, bag, slip, and
reordered images are not automatically retired, but none is grandfathered.
Reused material must pass the new causation, access, integrity, surface,
joint-clue, and non-actionability tests.

## 6. Surviving trade-off and owner resolution

The audience may finish without proving Sylvia's causal innocence. That is a
deliberate epistemic cost, not permission for production indecision. Cross-route
evidence must make the non-Sylvia chain genuinely reconstructible.

**OWNER-CONFIRMED 2026-09-03:** The evidence does not remain evenly balanced.
A first or incomplete reading makes Sylvia's inducement salient and reasonable;
attentive cross-route synthesis makes the audience-directed ordinary chain the
best-supported account without conclusively disproving an additional unseen
intervention. The audience therefore earns a real change in inference while
Sylvia retains a residual shadow of possible guilt.

This ruling adds no runtime statistic, branch, ending, or fourth narrative
surface. Its maintenance cost is a production-side causal continuity sheet and
a joint-clue audit when Hospital scenes and the Sylvia Special presentation are
authored.

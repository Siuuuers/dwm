# Narrative Style Manual Sections 5–8 Audit

## 1. Outcome and limitation

Sections 5–8 of the
[Narrative Style Manual](../../story/08-narrative-style-manual.md) survive one
bounded adversarial cycle after eleven actionable repairs and two explicit
trade-offs. The repairs make the approved lenses, weird/eerie/absurdity law,
dialogue and object discipline, love-signal grammar, and Sweet/Totally Dark
distinction more executable without approving exact scenes, dialogue, route
facts, romance, or implementation.

The owner has previously chosen not to use subagents or cross-model review for
this work. This is therefore a deliberately degraded same-context audit rather
than an independent fresh-context verdict. The claim and source contract were
fixed before the doubt pass, findings were classified against exact current
documents, and the stop condition is recorded below. Shared-context blind spots
remain possible.

## 2. Claim under test

**Claim:** Sections 5–8 produce varied, character-specific pressure without
turning literary references, absurdity, objects, love signals, or Sweet/Totally
Dark into formulas, moral scores, style quotas, or covert agency overrides.

**Why it matters:** These sections will shape almost every message, dating scene,
and ending. A loose instruction here can make every woman perform the same
literary mannerism, reward decorative randomness, transform background objects
into compulsory symbols, invent private evidence, or classify atmosphere as
character action.

## 4. Findings and reconciliation

### M58-01 — A home lens could attach to a woman or become mandatory

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The table was relationship-scoped, but
the prose did not expressly prevent writers from carrying one woman's apparent
“style” into every scene. “Use one foreground lens” could also require a lens
whenever several relationships were present. The repair assigns each lens to
the currently foregrounded relationship, permits no lens, allows at most one at
an instant, confines its facts to the anchor, and states that a lens relay does
not change the passage's anchor.

### M58-02 — Priscilla–Lavinia lens terms were too compressed

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** `Misaligned anticipation`, `unclaimed
continuity`, and `reciprocal proof-hunger` were evocative but underdefined. The
repair identifies their observable conduct and states that either woman may
initiate, none is compulsory, and treating an intervention as proof of
irreplaceability does not make that inference true.

### M58-03 — Paired-anchor counterpoint lacked its actual audition method

**Classification: Valid + actionable, with a retained trade-off.** The short
version could be read as assigning one anchor to each window before either
scene existed. The repair requires both causal plots first, an anchor-swap test
on each decisive beat, and selection by lawful uncertainty, active choice, and
window distinction. One Priscilla-bound and one Lavinia-bound blueprint remains
a strong default, but it can be overridden by documented ablation and may not
vary by hidden form or board result.

The retained cost is that even a “strong default” can become a subconscious
quota. Its value presently outweighs that risk because the pair needs two
different perceptual structures; the explicit override is therefore
load-bearing.

### M58-04 — Weirdness, eeriness, and absurd bursts shared one utility gate

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Requiring every abnormal element to
alter access or consequence was appropriate for a plot-bearing weird intrusion
but too instrumental for a spontaneous character burst. The repair gives each
construction its own burden: weirdness changes practical conditions, eeriness
uses exact evidence of presence/absence/agency, and a local absurd burst exposes
character pressure and changes immediate interaction without needing a clue,
variable, or persistent payoff.

### M58-05 — An eerie absence could be confused with missing production

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** An omitted asset, unimplemented scene,
failed interface element, or inaccessible route is not automatically authored
absence. The repair requires an authorized surface to make the absence itself
perceptible evidence before it can carry eerie meaning.

### M58-06 — “Normalized” could excuse nonreaction or unsafe continuation

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Normalized abnormality means that
characters address the local problem without announcing a horror category; it
does not mean they ignore what they can see. The repair requires proportionate
practical or qualified help under credible danger and forbids competent
continuation from preserving uncanniness by deleting safety conduct.

### M58-07 — The absurdity tests lacked contrasting failure examples

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The previous questions still required
writers to interpret terms such as functional and socially exact. Four brief,
noncanonical contrasts now distinguish a condition-changing weird intrusion,
an evidence-bearing eerie absence or agency problem, a character-revealing
absurd burst, and competent continuation from decorative or unsafe imitations.
They are expressly tests, not scene templates.

### M58-08 — Dialogue appeared to require a change in every scene

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** “Pressure and change” could make quiet
messages and early scenes overperform. The repair also permits a needed
baseline or a costly failure to change, while continuing to reject padding and
requiring the scene's actual dramatic function to become legible.

### M58-09 — Object economy threatened ordinary environmental life

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** “Objects do practical work” was useful
for foreground evidence but over-broad as a rule for every item in a lived-in
world. The repair applies the burden to foregrounded or recurring objects,
allows ordinary background texture, requires only the relevant access or
custody facts, and lets repetition establish a needed baseline before changed
recurrence carries pressure.

### M58-10 — Private love evidence could invite an illegal cutaway

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Asking whether tenderness continues
after its intended witness leaves is useful only where an authorized surface
can show it. The repair states that limit, forbids treating a missing carrier as
proof of private continuation, and requires every candidate signal to be tested
against a character-specific baseline in the Handbook. Generic kindness,
sacrifice, jealousy, or intensity remains insufficient.

### M58-11 — Atmospheric darkness could masquerade as Totally Dark conduct

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Psychological darkness can inhabit any
form; it does not by itself define the capitalized classification. The repair
requires the writer to record the acting character, chosen darker instrument,
owner of the expendable protected interest, and concrete consequence. It also
forbids inferring every participant's approval or complicity and requires
substantive difference in conduct, access, cost, or consequence rather than
palette, cruelty, diction, or insert length.

### M58-12 — Named lenses still risk becoming house styles

**Classification: Valid trade-off.** Attaching memorable theorist names to four
relationships can encourage imitation even with disclaimers. The owner wants
these references as working lenses, and their differentiating value presently
outweighs the risk. The safeguard survives only because every lens is optional,
relationship-local, anchor-bound, removable, and subordinate to character
voice. If later scenes become recognizable by technique before character, the
table should be reduced rather than defended.

## 5. Seven-question ablation result

| Test | Result after repair |
|---|---|
| Removal | Removing relationship-local lenses erases useful differentiation; removing their optionality creates four formulas. Both halves remain necessary. |
| Simplification | Weird intrusion, eerie evidence, and absurd burst now carry different minimum burdens; merging them recreates the over-broad utility rule. |
| Collision | No internal collision remains in Sections 5–8. The older numerical abnormality budget and romantic-asymmetry passages remain logged Bible/Handbook drift. |
| Variation | A scene may use no lens or no weird/eerie construction, direct speech is lawful, ordinary texture survives, and Sweet can remain psychologically dark. |
| Evidence | Private continuation, jealousy, love, and irreplaceability remain inferences unless an authorized carrier and character-specific pattern support them. |
| Maintenance | The repairs add no state, board family, route, variable, asset, or implementation requirement; local bursts need only an immediate authored aftermath. |
| Agency | Lenses cannot infer feelings, absurdity does not erase responsibility, and Totally Dark identifies the actor rather than assigning collective complicity. |

## 6. Rules that survived pressure

The pass found no sufficient reason to remove:

- four optional relationship-specific home lenses;
- the separation between anchor and lens;
- no numerical abnormality quota;
- the weird/eerie/competent-continuation distinction;
- socially exact local absurd bursts with immediate aftermath;
- direct declarations when character-consistent and consequential;
- practical custody before object interpretation;
- relationally scoped recurrence rather than a symbol dictionary;
- incidental-cast economy with local will;
- love signals as candidate modes rather than proof or a ladder; or
- Sweet and Totally Dark as nonmoral, substantively different conduct forms.

## 7. Stop condition

After reconciliation and a final distinction pass, no further high-confidence
defect whose repair belongs inside Sections 5–8 remained. Remaining
issues are exact scene applications, the known Bible/Handbook drift, manual
Sections 9–13, and the future raw-conversation audit of the complete
Priscilla–Lavinia bar-and-residue event. Under the bounded-ablation rule, this
gate stops after one cycle and does not make the whole manual final authority.

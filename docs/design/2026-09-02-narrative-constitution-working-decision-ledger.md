# Seven-Day Narrative Constitution: Working Decision Ledger

## 1. Status, purpose, and boundary

This is the private, spoiler-complete working ledger for the new Day 1–7 plot
and its [working **Narrative Style Manual**](../../story/08-narrative-style-manual.md).
Its purpose is to prevent owner-confirmed decisions from being lost during the
design interview.

The linked style manual is now a reconciled writer-facing reference. This
ledger remains the working record of mixed-status decisions and does not by
itself approve a Day 1–7 plot, dialogue script, exact event lattice, ending
catalogue, Godot implementation, website revision, or migration plan. It makes
no runtime change.

Individual entries carry one of four statuses:

- **OWNER-CONFIRMED** — explicitly selected or accepted by the project owner;
- **RETAINED FOUNDATION** — inherited under the selected selective reset unless
  a later owner decision reopens it;
- **RECOMMENDED** — current design advice, not canon until accepted; or
- **OPEN** — deliberately unresolved and not safe to infer.

For any conflict, the project owner's latest explicit declaration wins. During
this interview, an `OWNER-CONFIRMED` ruling in this ledger records that
declaration; it does not silently promote neighbouring recommendations.

## 2. Documentation authority during the redesign

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The existing exact Day 1–7 messages, dates, event premises, and material in
`docs/story-auditions/` are reference material rather than active plot canon.
The game has not shipped, so compatibility with obsolete development versions
or saves does not constrain the new plot.

The owner selected a **selective foundation reset**:

- retain useful character, setting, agency, consent, epistemic, and
  physical-reality foundations unless explicitly reopened;
- reopen exact Day 1–7 event premises and stale approvals;
- retain mechanics only where they genuinely serve or constrain the plot;
- permit recommendations for mechanic changes when they improve atmosphere,
  character agency, or causal meaning; and
- do not preserve a rule merely because an unshipped implementation already
  contains it.

“Useful” is not itself a canon status. Every inherited premise used by the new
plot must remain traceable to its source and be explicitly marked retained,
reopened, superseded, or newly owner-confirmed. Unlabelled material does not
survive the reset by convenience or implication.

The numerical one-abnormality-per-ordinary-scene rule is discarded. No new
numeric abnormality quota replaces it. The story still requires perceptual and
causal legibility: constant arbitrary noise must not make attention meaningless.

The former two-hour first-clear target applies, at most, as a soft target for
plot content. The plot may exceed it when earned; it is not a compatibility or
canon law.

## 3. Project identity and craft covenant

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The work is a romantic, chilling psychological-horror visual novel about
Angela, Priscilla, Lavinia, and Sylvia across seven days of Open Week at a
fictional Hong Kong university and affiliated conservatory.

The desired experience is:

- dreamlike, surreal, psychedelic, and capable of ridiculous turns;
- tense, noir-inflected, sweet, and quietly dangerous;
- psychologically dark beneath ordinary affection;
- normalized rather than announced in its abnormality;
- subtle rather than graphically brutal;
- dialogue- and interaction-led;
- committed to showing physical facts while withholding final motives;
- pleasurable to analyse without becoming a symbol dictionary;
- cruel or immoral where dramatically earned;
- free of coy feyness, cheese, stock romance, portentous affectation, and
  self-congratulatory drama; and
- concise at the level of effect, not starved of interaction or dialogue.

Robert McKee, Alain Robbe-Grillet, Roland Barthes, Mark Fisher, and Nathalie
Sarraute are private diagnostic lenses rather than names, quotations, imitation
targets, or visible theory lessons inside the game.

The provisional theory-to-story translation is:

- **McKee:** pressure and reversal arise from character decisions and causal
  boundaries, not decorative escalation;
- **Robbe-Grillet:** exact objects, positions, repetitions, and discrepancies
  provide evidence without explanatory motive narration;
- **Barthes:** signs remain plural and revisable rather than forming a single
  authorized decoding key;
- **Sarraute:** a scene may selectively magnify conversation, sub-conversation,
  and disproportionate attention when the richest action lies beneath an
  otherwise ordinary exchange;
- **Fisher:** institutional routine continues competently around events whose
  combined pattern should not quite exist; and
- **La Jalousie discipline:** attention may be exact while interpretation is
  jealous, selective, recursive, and possibly wrong. Patterned repetition may
  revisit an exact object or fact from altered positions; it does not license
  repeated filler, borrowed syntax, or ornamental object catalogues.

### 3.1 The weird, the eerie, and competent continuation

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

Mark Fisher's work supplies a private diagnostic distinction, not a vocabulary
lesson for characters or a compulsory checklist for every scene:

- **the weird** is a materially out-of-place presence, practice, or category
  that enters the represented situation and changes its practical conditions;
- **the eerie** arises around a conspicuous failure of expected presence or
  absence, or an unresolved question of agency; and
- **competent continuation** lets people and institutions respond accurately
  to literal conditions while an ordinary social ritual continues after its
  context has become disturbingly inappropriate.

The absence that produces an eerie effect must be perceptible through exact
facts. It cannot depend on missing content being mistaken for intention, a
software failure being mistaken for haunting, or every necessary causal step
being withheld. Likewise, a weird intrusion must change activity, constraint,
knowledge, leverage, or consequence; changing only the background is a visual
gimmick rather than a scene event.

These terms remain writer-facing. No character, interface, narrator, or ending
explains the theory to the audience. A scene need not demonstrate all three,
and repeated use must not become a numerical abnormality quota or house-style
tic.

The private distinction is grounded in Fisher's *The Weird and the Eerie* and
its account of things that do not belong, failures of presence or absence, and
questions of agency:

- [Penguin Random House: *The Weird and the Eerie*](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/546891/the-weird-and-the-eerie-by-mark-fisher/);
- [The Quietus: published book excerpt](https://thequietus.com/culture/books/mark-fisher-weird-eerie-kubrick-tarkovsky-nolan-review/).

### 3.2 Invisible architecture and character-local attention

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The essential-plot pass records the causal spine: each participant's local
want, the encounter, any operative choice, its concrete consequence, and the
residue or ending debt that must survive. It does not invent a conspicuous
foreground mechanism merely to demonstrate the author's thematic diagnosis.

The author keeps causality, eligibility, physical truth, and route composition
precise backstage. Visible attention belongs first to the characters. A line,
pause, gesture, object, or change of subject should arise because someone
notices, wants, avoids, protects, enjoys, or misreads something—not because the
audience needs to be shown a route label in disguise. A useful diagnostic is:

> Would this person still care about this detail if the route label, clue
> ledger, and observing audience were removed?

The scene as a whole must alter or clarify relational pressure, knowledge,
practical conditions, or carried residue. Individual lines and objects have no
such utility quota. A detail may be psychologically active without becoming a
clue, symbol, foreshadowing device, metric change, or later payoff; interaction
may linger where a character's attention lingers.

Disproportion therefore belongs to consciousness rather than automatically to
the physical world. A word, chair, cup, delay, correction, or silence may become
enormous because a character cares about it while remaining objectively
ordinary. Conversely, a procedure or object designed chiefly to announce the
author's theme should be reduced or removed even when its symbolism is clear.

Sarraute supplies an **optional scene-level attention lens**, not the game's
universal rhythm, a character voice, a prose template, or an excuse for explicit
homage. Use it when an otherwise comprehensible situation contains more
compelling relational movement beneath the spoken exchange, and when a small
detail exposes this particular participant's desire, shame, fear, pleasure, or
irritation. Removing the microscopic attention should materially weaken the
scene's intimacy or unease; merely making a scene slower or more literary is
not sufficient.

No scene quota applies. Urgent physical facts, essential choice information,
rapid absurd eruptions, and actions whose force is their bluntness may use no
Sarrautean preoccupation or sub-conversation at all. The game need not imitate
interior monologue, reproduce Sarraute's syntax, or turn its astronomy material
into a reference to *Le Planétarium*. The lens is grounded in her treatment of
fleeting movements near the edge of consciousness and the pressure between
conversation and sub-conversation:

- [INA: Nathalie Sarraute on *Tropismes*](https://www.ina.fr/ina-eclaire-actu/video/i00007759/nathalie-sarraute-a-propos-de-tropismes);
- [OpenEdition: study of speech in *Le Planétarium*](https://journals.openedition.org/amerika/1999).

### 3.3 Soft relational home lenses

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The three Angela pairings and the Priscilla–Lavinia pairing have different
**soft home lenses** so their scenes need not sound or attend alike. A lens
belongs to the relationship's current field of attention, not permanently to
either woman. It may influence what the scene selects, repeats, withholds, or
finds unsettling; it may not replace a character's established vocabulary,
sentence rhythm, agency, knowledge, desire, or decision process.

| Relationship | Optional home lens | What it may bring forward | Governing safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Angela–Priscilla | Sarrautean micropressure | Tiny corrections, unfinished wording, social embarrassment, and relational movement beneath a polite exchange | Priscilla remains governed by her own polished voice; no pause or object is automatically profound, and a scene may use none of this lens |
| Angela–Lavinia | Robbe-Grillet / *La Jalousie*–inflected **embodied parallax** | Exact distance, weight, placement, recurrent movement, changed position, and jealousy as selective or possibly mistaken observation | Lavinia authors her movement and proximity; the lens may not turn her into a body inventory, passive spectacle, dance symbol, or object beneath a supposedly superior gaze |
| Angela–Sylvia | Fisherian competent eeriness | Care and institutional routine continuing effectively while agency, preparation, expected presence, or absence feels subtly wrong | Sylvia remains human and physically plausible; efficacy is not supernatural proof, and procedural competence does not certify her motive or authority |
| Priscilla–Lavinia | **Misaligned anticipation** | A preparation made before a request, then accepted, altered, refused, left unused, or answered by counter-preparation; unclaimed continuity and reciprocal proof-hunger may appear as local accents | Neither woman is fixed as initiator or responder; familiarity grants no authority, consent, diagnosis, or relationship label; do not make every object or adjustment symbolic |

For Priscilla–Lavinia, familiarity is precise enough that either woman may act
before the other asks, while they remain divided over whether such knowledge
grants the right to act. The lens therefore follows the visible sequence of
anticipation and counter-adjustment rather than explaining "closeness" as a
theme. **Unclaimed continuity** may let a practical or domestic routine persist
without settling what their relationship is. **Reciprocal proof-hunger** may
let one woman receive the other's intervention or provocation as evidence of
irreplaceability without making that inference true. Both are optional local
accents, not additional house lenses.

The same home lens applies across all four Priscilla–Lavinia state/tone forms
and must not reveal the run's deck draw. Ambiguous may leave the significance of
their fluency less mutually acknowledged; Love may make its exceptional
importance more mutually legible without supplying a label. Sweet may interrupt
pre-emption through disclosure or a direct request; Totally Dark may knowingly
reward pre-emption as intimacy. These are possible character-level consequences
of the selected form, not four prose templates or mandatory beat patterns.

The **anchor** and the **lens** answer different questions. The declared anchor
governs what perceptual prose is allowed to know; the active relationship
governs which optional lens may shape what that prose selects, notices,
repeats, or withholds. A lens may foreground only facts available to the anchor
and may not infer its relational target's private feeling. It primarily shapes
the selection and arrangement of sparse perceptual prose and visible staging;
it neither rewrites dialogue voice nor causes a character's action. Each woman
continues to author what she does.

Anchor eligibility remains unchanged: Angela anchors Angela-attended or
Angela-owned surfaces while able to perceive; an authorized private
Priscilla–Lavinia surface may instead declare Priscilla or Lavinia; and a group
surface with Angela present defaults to Angela. Sylvia has no independent prose
anchor under the current surface law. Within one continuous passage the anchor
must remain stable: a soft lens may relay at a qualified turn, but the prose may
not head-hop with it.

Across the two authored conditional Priscilla–Lavinia window blueprints, use
**paired-anchor counterpoint** as a strong ensemble default: one pair-only
counterpart is Priscilla-bound and the other is Lavinia-bound. This is not an
unconditional quota. Draft both essential causal plots before assigning either
anchor, then perform a short **anchor-swap audition** on each decisive beat. The
preferred anchor is the one whose lawful perceptual limits produce richer
uncertainty, preserve a costly active choice, keep the counterpart's motive
unsettled, and distinguish the two windows—not simply the one with more secrets
or more decorative prose. Override the one-each distribution only when a
documented ablation shows that it materially weakens a scene.

The selected anchor belongs to the window's authored pair-only blueprint and
does not vary with Ambiguous/Love, Sweet/Totally Dark, pair-board result, or
contact flavor. It therefore cannot disclose the hidden deck draw or game-state
consequence. An attended Group presentation remains Angela-bound. A
Private-offscreen or Prevented window supplies no perceptual prose. Consequently,
one Priscilla-bound and one Lavinia-bound blueprint do not guarantee that a
single playthrough will display both anchors. This paired-window ruling does not
preassign the anchor of the umbrella micro-scene, pair ending, or postscript.

These are defaults for diagnosis, not uniforms, quotas, outcome generators, or
route signatures. Any scene may decline its home lens, use an ordinary direct
mode, or borrow a secondary lens when the local material earns it. Sweet and
Dark do not select a literary method, and the audience must not be able to
deduce a relationship state or board result from technique alone.

McKee remains a global backstage check on causal pressure and consequence.
Barthes remains a global check against one authorized interpretation. Neither
supplies a character voice.

When a scene contains more than one active relational field, it uses **one
foreground lens at a time**. The relationship carrying the immediate causal or
emotional pressure determines that foreground. A lens may relay to another only
when the pressure genuinely transfers at a discernible turn in the interaction;
mere co-presence does not add a lens. Two house styles are never blended
mechanically or run simultaneously. Every lens and relay remains subordinate to
the scene's existing anchor, knowledge, agency, and physical-truth rules.

### 3.4 Unmarked focal presence

**OWNER-CONFIRMED 2026-09-06**

The attended Day 6 Priscilla–Lavinia Group presentation uses **unmarked focal
presence**. Production registers Angela as its stable perceptual anchor while
the audience-facing text and UI never certify whose limited sight they occupy.
The passage therefore uses no `Angela looked back`, Angela-labelled perceptual
box, first-person viewpoint cue, or later explanatory reveal. An attentive
player may infer Angela from geometry and timing; inference is permitted, while
textual certification is withheld.

Every selected visual or audible fact must remain consciously and physically
available from Angela's exact position, and the passage ends when her sight and
hearing end. Spatial restriction may expose pressure of attention without
naming jealousy or motive. This is not an unknown writer-side anchor,
head-hopping, an invisible observer, or an omniscient camera. It clarifies that
anchor declaration is writer-facing and remains subject to every existing
knowledge, physical-truth, and passage-stability law.

The exact door, bag, occlusion, dialogue, and shot wording remain noncanonical
auditions. The approved method and its medium-specific risks are recorded in
[*La Jalousie*: Unmarked Focal Presence in a Visual Novel](../research/2026-09-06-la-jalousie-unmarked-focal-presence-vn-adaptation.md)
and distilled in the
[Narrative Style Manual](../../story/08-narrative-style-manual.md#42-unmarked-focal-presence).

## 4. Observable narrative surfaces

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The audience observes story through exactly three narrative surface classes:

1. **messages**;
2. **dating scenes**; and
3. **endings**.

“Ordinary” may describe tone or causality. It is not a fourth scene class.
There is no standalone cutaway, explanatory interstitial, object-only resolver
scene, or unrestricted viewpoint vignette in the current game.

Dating is production language for an authored encounter, not proof that the
participants call it a date or understand it as romantic.

Conditional Priscilla–Lavinia meetings may be audience-visible as dating scenes
without Angela. They remain bounded exceptions that do not transfer their
private facts into Angela's knowledge. All other offscreen actions may enter
the observable story only through evidence lawfully carried into a message,
dating scene, or ending.

Friends continue acting outside Angela's attention. Audience knowledge and
Angela knowledge remain separate. No absence of an observable scene proves
that an offscreen event did or did not occur.

**OWNER-CONFIRMED — HOSPITAL SURFACE OWNERSHIP**

Hospital is a runtime condition and possible setting, not a fourth narrative
surface:

- on Days 1–6, if no qualifying Sylvia solo encounter has been committed, the
  Hospital transaction restores Angela to fixed recovery levels and performs
  necessary lifecycle bookkeeping without opening a narrative scene;
- on Days 1–6, if a qualifying Sylvia solo encounter has been committed, that
  encounter is superseded by its Hospital-set **dating-scene variant** rather
  than supplemented by a bonus encounter; and
- on Day 7, Hospital consequences remain ending-owned: the approved conditions
  select either Hospital-flavoured Alone or `Sylvia Special -> Sylvia Dark`
  before any independently qualified later layers.

For a Days 1–6 Sylvia encounter, viewing the eligible invitation produces the
automatic reply and commitment already belonging to that invitation flow. That
committed encounter may become the Hospital variant. Mere calendar eligibility,
an unread invitation, or Sylvia's general availability cannot summon her.

A code-only Hospital transaction still consumes and settles the triggering
condition, affected schedule state, time/day transition, persistence, and the
ordinary non-narrative recovery receipt. “No scene” does not mean “leave the
run unresolved.” It creates no direct story, relationship, knowledge, or
evidence reward. A later message or ending may use the Hospital fact only under
its own separately approved carrier and eligibility law.

## 5. Umbrella history

### 5.1 Forward law

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The umbrella pickup becomes an **offscreen canonical event**, provisionally
called the **umbrella history**. It is not an in-game cutaway.

The production-side story must eventually fix what physically happened and
when, even though the released game withholds those answers. Audience
uncertainty must not become author uncertainty or permit contradictory travel,
knowledge, possession, or dialogue.

Inside the game:

- the event may be inferred only through a message, dating scene, or ending;
- a carrier may establish only its immediately visible or audible fact, such
  as the umbrella's presence, condition, possession, changed location,
  recognition, or familiar shorthand;
- no single carrier directly recaps or depicts the pickup;
- no carrier is allowed to confirm its date, exact time, location, initiator,
  route, baggage sequence, motive, emotional interpretation, reconciliation,
  or relationship label;
- Angela learns only what she witnesses or lawfully receives;
- the event grants no affection, relationship tier, dark value, pair count,
  board credit, route gate, witnessed combination, or ending eligibility; and
- the main plot remains causally intelligible even if a particular audience
  member overlooks the inference.

The preferred hint pattern uses different functions instead of repeated
explanation:

- one message may contain unexplained familiar shorthand;
- one dating scene may use the umbrella practically without an ominous
  close-up; and
- a later dating scene or ending may make the easiest earlier interpretation
  less stable without supplying the full event.

This pattern is a ceiling, not a requirement to repeat the prop three times.
An object remains an object; the prose never declares the umbrella a metaphor.

### 5.2 Relationally scoped mosaic

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

Umbrella discovery is relationally scoped. A playthrough that reaches an
approved Priscilla–Lavinia-relevant dating scene or ending receives at least
one natural umbrella facet. Stronger but still incomplete corroboration may
appear through other eligible paths or later playthroughs. A Sylvia base ending
or deliberate Alone base ending carries no umbrella reference by obligation;
if a separately qualified Priscilla–Lavinia ending follows either base layer,
that later pair layer may carry its own facet.

“Priscilla–Lavinia-relevant” is narrow. The mere presence of Priscilla or
Lavinia does not qualify a scene. A facet is eligible only when the approved
ordinary activity already makes the object, its present custody, a remembered
preference, or the exact wording locally natural. No scene may be constructed
merely to deliver umbrella evidence. One bounded facet is normally sufficient;
two naturally inseparable present facts may coexist only when neither
reconstructs the pickup or turns the scene into an evidence delivery mechanism.

Different facets expose different sides of truthful present evidence. The
audience may mentally combine them across routes and replays, but:

- the combination never becomes Angela's cross-run knowledge;
- no umbrella-specific state, counter, collection, Gallery identity, replay
  unlock, Observer evidence, route gate, or ending gate is created;
- replay repeats its source facet without adding explanation;
- a message facet creates no dedicated pickup message, fallback echo, expiry
  residue, or notification clue;
- state and tone may change present willingness or handling, but cannot change
  whether the private event occurred; and
- even the union of every approved facet must leave at least two plausible
  ordinary chronologies and must not prove the pickup's location, initiator,
  sequence, motive, or relationship meaning.

The full mosaic may make it increasingly persuasive that Priscilla and Lavinia
share private umbrella history. It does not reconstruct that history into a
distributed cutaway or final solution.

Every later facet candidate must record its source surface, perceptual anchor,
speaker's lawful knowledge, audience-visible fact, Angela knowledge delta,
invited inference, unproved facts, state/tone independence, replay behaviour,
and whole-mosaic compatibility.

### 5.3 Private Day 2 airport causal core

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The umbrella history is one event, not separate airport and post-return events.
“After Lavinia returns” means during her Day 2 arrival in Hong Kong, before she
reaches Lavinia's flat, campus, or any optional dating window.

The fixed private causal core is:

1. After landing, Lavinia privately asks Priscilla to bring the long umbrella
   while Lavinia is still completing arrival formalities or waiting for
   baggage. The broad interval is fixed so Priscilla has credible travel time;
   the exact sending moment is not.
2. Priscilla is not already waiting conveniently at the airport. She chooses to
   make the journey after the request.
3. They meet only after Lavinia has collected checked baggage and cleared
   customs, in the public arrivals or meeters-and-greeters area.
4. The umbrella remains closed inside the covered terminal, Airport Express
   platform, and train. Its practical use belongs to a later exposed station
   exit, transfer, walkway, or other onward segment if the final weather and
   route require it.
5. They travel onward together. Lavinia's practical request is genuine, and so
   is her deniable wish for Priscilla's presence.
6. Priscilla answers by coming and by exceeding the requested help. Genuine
   care and control coexist. The exact over-helping gesture remains open;
   taking the heavier bag is a candidate rather than a fixed fact.
7. Neither woman declares the meeting a reconciliation, and the event does not
   erase their unresolved conflict.

The event occurs before optional dating windows and therefore does not depend
on whom the audience later schedules. Priscilla and Lavinia know their own
actions and the facts they directly witnessed; neither receives authoritative
knowledge of the other's complete motive.

The released game may make the private umbrella history increasingly
persuasive, but its complete mosaic still does not confirm the airport, Day 2,
the request, Priscilla's journey, their onward travel, or either woman's motive.

**OPEN STAGING DETAILS**

- exact clock time and flight timing;
- exact weather and exposed onward threshold;
- exact private message wording;
- Priscilla's starting point and full route;
- exact waiting duration;
- exact over-helping and luggage gestures; and
- the later message, dating, and ending facets that carry bounded residue.

## 6. Nonbinding future-cutaway note

**OWNER-CONFIRMED AS A FUTURE-PRODUCTION NOTE; NOT CURRENT NARRATIVE LAW**

No cutaway system exists now, and the plot does not depend on adding one.
A future event deserves a cutaway only if all of the following are true:

1. an autonomous offscreen decision changes several later observable facts;
2. its essential dramatic information lives in action, timing, or gesture and
   would be flattened by dialogue recap;
3. audience knowledge without Angela creates useful dramatic irony;
4. seeing it does not solve a mystery that should remain open;
5. the game remains coherent if the cutaway is unavailable; and
6. the viewpoint exception is rare enough to remain meaningful.

Under the current umbrella law, any future direct version would be better
considered as separately approved post-completion supplemental material than as
part of the first-clear week. No candidate is selected yet.

This advice belongs in a future-ideas or production appendix when the finished
Narrative Constitution and Scene Manual are compiled. It must not appear there
as a fourth current narrative surface or an implied implementation commitment.

## 7. Audience, Angela, consequence, and Observer Pressure

### 7.1 Constrained co-authorship

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The audience–Angela relationship uses constrained co-authorship rather than
literal possession or passive observation:

- the audience may operate only registered possibilities and never supplies
  free-form personality, dialogue, desire, consent, diagnosis, or explanation;
- Angela owns every exact line, acceptance, refusal, action, and deliberate
  silence that becomes part of the current continuation;
- an audience operation may expose or propose an Angela-authored possibility,
  but it cannot require an action for which no character-consistent mapping
  exists;
- Minesweeper operations belong to the audience while the authoritative result
  and its derived consequences belong to the game state; neither lets Angela
  choose another woman's feelings;
- Hospitalization may be causally attributable to audience risk without making
  Angela's incapacity a choice or consent;
- Save, Load, Gallery, Rehearsal, and cross-run synthesis are audience-level
  continuation or observation operations and never become Angela's memory;
- Priscilla, Lavinia, and Sylvia retain independent motives, decisions, and
  offscreen causality; and
- Observer Pressure may disturb future opportunities and traces but cannot
  activate a control, counterfeit an admitted input, rewrite a committed fact,
  or force a character's decision.

The input covenant remains: **trust the hand; question the trace**. A physical
input must receive a truthful operational result even when later evidence or
interpretation becomes strange.

### 7.2 Consequence vocabulary

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The owner states that only message-option selection and Minesweeper challenge

### 7.4 Frozen pair form and relationship-field continuation

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

Priscilla and Lavinia begin each genuinely new playthrough in one frozen
two-axis relationship form:

1. **Ambiguous + Sweet**;
2. **Ambiguous + Dark**;
3. **Love + Sweet**; or
4. **Love + Dark**.

The state axis is Ambiguous or Love. The tone axis is Sweet or Dark. Neither
axis is a moral rank, and Dark is not a truer or stronger form of Love.

Tone answers **which character-specific facet becomes governing visible
conduct**, not whether the cast passes a moral, therapeutic, or relationship
health test. Sweet foregrounds tender, playful, ordinary, or less-dangerous
facets in the decisive conduct. The same person's darker capacities remain
part of her and may exert pressure through an impulse, implication, or
near-action; Sweet does not delete them. Dark lets a character-specific darker
facet become observable chosen conduct with a concrete consequence; the label
does not create that capacity or certify it as the person's truest self.

Sweet is not required to interrupt a named destructive pattern, honor a
boundary as a lesson, or pay a prescribed cost. Dark is not required to depict
mutual complicity or knowing reinforcement by every participant. Interruption,
restraint, complicity, and reinforcement remain available scene actions when
the characters and situation earn them, not definitions of the tone axis.
Both tones must remain psychologically and causally substantive rather than
cosmetic changes of mood, cruelty, color, or diction.

**Owner-confirmed deeper Dark diagnostic (2026-09-06).** Tone also asks how
desire reorganizes conduct. In Dark, desired love or attachment makes one
concrete, character-specific limit, value, truth, safety, freedom,
relationship, or other protected interest expendable as the darker facet
becomes the chosen means of pursuit. The plot must identify what is made
expendable and show the causal consequence; generic extremity, cruelty
inflation, or atmosphere alone does not qualify. Sweet may contain obsession,
danger, loss, or an ugly impulse, but the character's darkest instrument does
not become the decisive means by which she tries to secure the attachment.

`Stop at nothing` is permitted only as private emotional shorthand. It is not
a literal world law, an escalation quota, omnipotence, or permission to make
all four women pursue love through interchangeable tactics. Mutual complicity
may intensify a Dark expression but remains optional. The exact expendable
interest must be auditioned per character and event. The owner's spontaneous
examples of Priscilla gaslighting and Lavinia harming herself or another person
are explicitly noncanonical prompts, not approved acts. A later use of
`gaslighting` must mean a sustained attempt to destabilize another person's
trust in her own perception rather than a single lie or reframing; deliberate
self-harm would be a separate major premise with real consequence and no
procedural depiction.

This definition supersedes every earlier rule that treated one costly
interruption, one honored boundary, or mutual knowing reinforcement as the
mandatory diagnostic for tone. Those actions may survive as scene-specific
conduct, but they cannot derive the classification by themselves. Any existing
ending or encounter whose distinction depends only on that former test must be
re-auditioned before it is plot-ready. The bar fork remains compatible at the
current envelope level because Sweet foregrounds Lavinia's capacity to stop and
speak while Dark lets her violent facet become action; neither branch is ranked
as healthier, truer, or more loving. The post-confrontation exit remains
unresolved.

The combination is randomly selected from the eligible pool at run creation
and remains canonical for that entire playthrough. Save, Load, Rehearsal,
branch replacement, pair-board performance, Angela's presence or absence, and
all in-run conduct must not reroll or mutate it. Before the profile has actually
witnessed all four combinations, a witnessed combination is excluded from the
next new-run pool. This is a constrained unseen-first random draw, not four
independent unrestricted draws.

A combination becomes profile-witnessed only when the audience actually sees a
counted Priscilla–Lavinia event or their ending under that combination. A hidden
draw, Prevented window, Private-offscreen meeting, umbrella facet, or other
non-counting trace does not mark it seen. After all four combinations have first
been witnessed, each later new playthrough draws freely from all four. The
unseen-first guarantee is a one-time discovery cycle, not a recurring bag cycle.

The frozen combination is not the entirety of their relationship. Their own
choices may change present trust, distance, damage, repair, knowledge,
obligation, and future conduct while the run's categorical state and tone stay
fixed. Audience operations can select or catalyse an authored circumstance; they
cannot promote, demote, sweeten, darken, manufacture, or erase the pair form.

The inherited pair-board rule is too causally thin for the new plot: it changes
how much of Priscilla and Lavinia the audience may observe, but changes nothing
that the women themselves carry away. The forward rule should instead be:

> A visible pair board selects one complete, authored conduct continuation for
> that encounter. It may leave a durable relational residue, but it never
> manufactures a character's feeling, desire, consent, or private motive.

The board is the counterfactual **decision field of the encounter**, not a
literal object and not a scan of any character's mind. Its subject depends on
who is present:

- **Group / Angela present:** Angela is the counterfactual subject. The terminal
  result selects an authored Angela disposition expressed as an action,
  intervention, refusal, alignment, withdrawal, or deliberate non-action.
  Priscilla and Lavinia then respond autonomously. Angela can catalyse what
  happens between them, but she is not the source or fuel of their attachment.
- **Missed or Private-visible / Angela absent:** the scene freezes Priscilla,
  Lavinia, or their joint outward choreography as its registered subject and
  one of them as its perceptual anchor. The terminal result selects one
  fully authored outward-conduct continuation. It does not select an inner
  thought or command either woman to love, forgive, desire, or consent. Angela
  gains no knowledge of the private continuation merely because the audience
  saw it.
- **Private-offscreen:** no board and no simulated result. The meeting may still
  leave one coarse, author-fixed causal residue, but no exact unseen dialogue or
  cognition is invented for the audience.
- **Prevented:** no pair meeting, board, joint continuation, or pair residue.

Perfect, Solved, and Exploded remain trusted structural board facts, not global
psychological or dramatic meanings. Each pair scene maps every reachable fact
to its own authored continuation. Any result may be tender, dangerous, quiet,
direct, interrupted, or impulsive when that exact scene earns it; Exploded is
not a literal injury, a failure of cognition, or an automatic Dark result.

The selected continuation should leave a concrete, nonnumeric **pair residue**:
an action, promise, refusal, debt, object handling, exposed fact, or boundary
that both participating women can remember and that later lawful dialogue may
carry. Board result and residue must be recorded separately. No terminal result
directly mutates pair desire, consent, meeting count, or the frozen state/tone
combination. Accumulated conduct may determine whether later pair material is
eligible and which concrete facts it carries, but it does not derive or
overwrite the relationship form. Any qualifying pair ending reads the already
frozen combination.

Individual cells and operations remain counterfactual texture rather than
literal thoughts or motives. What fuels Priscilla and Lavinia is their own
history, wants, fear, jealousy, and choice. When Angela is present, her selected
conduct perturbs the situation; it does not author the other women's interior
lives.

### 7.5 Attention and chance

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

Minesweeper performance and Observer Pressure may evoke the mixture of pattern,
chance, and selective awareness found in daily life. Awareness can reveal
opportunities, improve navigation, preserve options, or avert some harms. It

The governing law is:

> Attention changes the available evidence and opportunities. It does not

More evidence does not automatically produce the correct interpretation.

### 7.6 Non-moralized outcomes and non-hierarchical evidence

**OWNER-CONFIRMED FOR FORWARD DIRECTION; EXACT OBSERVER GATE DEFERRED**

The game has no author-certified good route, bad route, true relationship, or
best ending. Perfect, Solved, Exploded, Sweet, Dark, Special, Observer, and any
successor terms describe operational facts, conduct patterns, presentation
forms, or evidence relations rather than a ladder of human worth.

This perspectival stance does not make all claims equivalent. The story keeps
separate:

- what physically happened;
- whether a person had capacity and gave, withheld, or withdrew consent;
- what harm, pleasure, loss, access, or dependency followed;
- what each character wanted or experienced; and
- how each character and the audience interpret, value, forgive, or condemn it.

Characters may disagree about motive, meaning, responsibility, forgiveness, or
whether a cost was acceptable. Perspective cannot turn an unconsented act into
consent, erase an injury, or make a false physical claim factually true.

The inherited `Perfect -> Sweet -> Observer` privilege is superseded. Observer
or privileged evidence must not certify technical mastery or Sweet conduct as

incompatibility, or recurrence across lawful continuations rather than from

### 7.7 Optional scene-authored response families

outcomes—Hatred, Upset, Amused, Loved, Foresight, and Dark—is retired for the

continuations its dramatic situation and characters can support.

“Optional” cannot mean that a reachable terminal board cause has no authored

deterministically to a valid continuation. Several terminal causes may converge
on one family when they produce the same conduct, effect, and residue; false
distinctions made only of warmer wording, facial expression, or legacy quota
must collapse.

The six inherited labels mix incompatible semantic kinds: Hatred, Upset,
Amused, and Loved purport to name an emotional response; Foresight names an
epistemic relation; Dark names a conduct pattern or ending tone. They therefore
cannot remain one authoritative domain enum without pre-interpreting motives
and restoring a global ladder.

The recommended replacement is a hybrid contract:

- the board emits one closed, nondiegetic operational terminal fact;
- each scene registers stable namespaced response-family IDs named for
  observable conduct, such as refusal, direct request, verification,

- a total, version-frozen scene map connects every reachable terminal cause to
  exactly one response family;

  companion's autonomous response, not the companion's inner feeling;
- effect profile, persistent attitude, knowledge change, concrete residue, and
  Observer evidence remain separate registered fields rather than being
  inferred from the family name; and
- optional cross-scene tags may describe participation, attention, boundary
  relation, or dangerous-pattern relation, but they do not generate prose,
  morality, desire, or consent.

Two alternatives remain available but are not recommended: retaining a
scene-selected subset of the old six preserves their category confusion, while
fully arbitrary scene outcomes without a stable operational/effect interface
weaken validation, save determinism, and ending reachability.

Each challenged scene should normally contain **two to four primary response
families**, with three as the soft target. This is a complexity ceiling and
authoring guide, not a quota: use only the distinct continuations the scene can
earn. An optional special-mine continuation counts inside this budget and
exists only when that scene offers a concrete, tempting act worth choosing
after the clear. It is never a compulsory fourth family. The exact family IDs,
scene allocations, persistent-stance vocabulary, and effect/ending derivation
remain subsequent decisions.

#### 7.7.1 Convergence and consequence projection

**OWNER-CONFIRMED 2026-09-03**

Convergence is semantic rather than cosmetic. Two terminal board causes may
share one response family only when, at the response-defining level, they
preserve the same:

- authored Angela conduct and companion response, apart from non-reused
  surface microvariation;
- material and consent facts;
- relationship transition or typed conduct receipts;
- carried stance;
- causal carryover, obligation, and future eligibility;
- knowledge acquisition for every character; and
- Observer-capable witnessed facts.

They may still retain different nondiegetic board histories: layout, terminal
cause, reveal path, Perfect reason, explosion site, action count, attempt ID,
and audiovisual terminal treatment. If later fiction or eligibility reacts to
one of those differences, the branches are not fully converged and require
separate consequence-plan variants even when they share a dialogue skeleton.

Every resolved continuation should select one immutable **Response Consequence
Plan** with two commit stages:

1. **Resolved-consequence stage:** after board truth and every materially
   distinct variant are fixed, commit every approved progression input,
   relationship transition, dangerous-pattern conduct receipt, and carried
   interaction stance. Progression may evaluate only after this stage.
2. **Presented-atom stage:** only when the exact line, action, silence, or
   visible record occurs, commit its causal carryover, message-echo
   satisfaction, character-knowledge acquisition, or audience-only Observer
   evidence opportunity. Unpresented prose cannot grant knowledge or evidence.

A merely presentational atom cannot be a progression input. If a variation
changes an input, it is a materially distinct consequence-plan variant and must
be fixed and committed in the first stage; presentation cannot retroactively
alter an evaluation that already occurred.

The working definitions are:

- **carried stance** replaces a compulsory emotional `attitude`; it records an
  observable posture relevant to a later interaction and changes only when the
  scene earns a persistent difference;
- **causal carryover** replaces the overloaded use of `residue`; it is a
  concrete physical, documentary, practical, or social fact with a lawful
  later carrier;
- **knowledge acquisition** identifies the knower, exact claim, source atom,
  and whether it was witnessed, reported, read from a record, or inferred;
- **Observer evidence** is an audience/profile receipt for a witnessed
  divergence, incompatibility, recurrence, or registered operation, never a
  truth point or character memory; and
- **conduct receipts** record actions such as a bounded delegation, honoured
  refusal, voluntary return, exposed dependence, or reinforced control without
  declaring love, virtue, motive, or future consent.

Relational momentum is therefore not a master key for hidden scenes or

#### 7.7.3 Maintainability ceiling and local board interpretation

**OWNER-CONFIRMED 2026-09-03**

The full typed-receipt model should not become the ordinary scene author's
interface. The smallest maintainable architecture is one deep behavioral
response-resolution boundary. It receives the frozen scene, run, board, and
approved variation context required for deterministic interpretation and
returns one frozen authored response plan. Exact function signatures,
parameters, data shapes, and storage are deferred to technical design.

Each challenged scene owns one small declarative map. The board supplies a
stable structural terminal fact, while the scene supplies all local dramatic
meaning. Exploded, Solved, and Perfect do not globally prescribe early,
bounded, or high-resolution conduct. Thus the same Perfect result may select
direct inquiry in one scene, overconfident interpretation in another, and quiet

The author-facing response record should contain only:

- a stable local response ID and dialogue continuation;
- an optional relationship-readiness change;
- an optional carried stance;
- only those scoped story facts or knowledge changes that a later surface
  actually consumes; and
- a rare Observer opportunity ID only in deliberately designated material.

Absent fields mean no change. Do not record every gesture, inferred mood, or

#### 7.7.4 Frozen Impulse Atom

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

A challenged scene may register zero or one optional **Frozen Impulse Atom
window**. There is no per-scene or per-day quota; the device should remain rare
enough that spontaneity does not become a visible machine.

After the terminal board fact and response family are fixed, frozen attempt
context selects once among two or three scene-local, fully authored words,
gestures, interruptions, omissions, or small acts. The selected atom ID is
frozen into the response plan before presentation. Reload, replay, and Dialogic
resumption reproduce it rather than drawing again. Prose is never generated
procedurally.

The atom may occur in any compatible response family. Binding it only to
Exploded would quietly turn Exploded into the irrational or bad result. Chance
selects which already-plausible manifestation becomes observable; it does not
create a character's want, erase agency, manufacture consent, establish
incapacity, or excuse responsibility.

A true impulse atom is surface microvariation. Its alternatives may differ in
exact wording, gesture, timing, self-correction, denial, or doubling down while
preserving response-defining conduct and all reusable canon. If an alternative
changes an object, consent fact, knowledge, relational momentum, relationship
progression, carried stance, causal carryover, Observer evidence, or later
eligibility, it is not merely an atom: it must become its own frozen and
persisted consequence-plan variant before progression is evaluated.

Every presented burst receives an immediate authored aftermath: another person
reacts, or the originating character retracts, disguises, corrects, repeats,
exploits, or deliberately leaves it unanswered. Use **before fully reflecting**
or **under-attended action** as the ordinary psychological model. Do not imply
that surprise to the character or audience proves literal causelessness,
dissociation, quantum origin, or loss of legal or moral agency.

### 7.8 Core-ending closure and interpretive openness

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

Every core ending is **practically conclusive and interpretively open**. It
settles the characters' represented physical action and its immediate material
or relational consequence while declining to certify one complete account of
motive, romantic meaning, or the week's total pattern.

Each core must make clear:

- what Angela and every materially involved character actually does;
- where relevant people and plot-bearing objects end the represented action;
- which access, custody, departure, permission, refusal, injury, obligation, or
  relationship boundary has concretely changed; and
- which consequence cannot simply be undone the next morning without a new
  choice, cost, confrontation, or violation.

Interpretive openness cannot come from concealing the basic action, replacing
causality with atmosphere, or withholding a fact the scene's own viewpoint
would plainly establish. It comes from plural, revisable motives attached to
the same authenticated conduct. A character may confess, name love, choose a
relationship label, accuse, forgive, or deny; that speech remains a consequential
character action rather than an author-certified diagnosis of every earlier
sign.

No final confession, label, twist, or explanatory monologue converts the entire
week into one authorized solution. Sweet and Dark forms may impose sharply
different actions and costs, but neither is globally good, bad, true, mature,
or more loving. An Observer coda adds or rearranges available evidence; it does
not replace, invalidate, reveal as imaginary, or retroactively moralize the
preceding core. The complete pattern may remain unresolved even though every
ending-specific physical fact is stable.

**End-first hybrid authoring sequence.** "Ending first" fixes a destination's
dramatic and material envelope before it fixes finished prose or turns every
earlier encounter into delivery for a predetermined image. For each ending
family or independent ending layer:

1. define the one dramatic question and the practical-closure obligations that
   every permitted form must answer;
2. audition a small set of terminal situations and physical or social carriers;
3. select the shared situation or carrier capable of supporting every required
   form without recolouring one conclusion;
4. specify each form's distinct action, boundary, cost, access distribution,
   and interpretive remainder;
5. backchain only the necessary debts and residues into earlier messages and
   dating scenes, while preserving each earlier scene's local want; and
6. return to the ending for full beat design and eventual dialogue.

This is neither a pure ending-first method that polishes a finale before testing
its routes nor free encounter-first exploration without a destination. The
possibility audition occurs inside the ending envelope, before exact earlier
plots are made to converge on it.

For the Priscilla–Lavinia layer, use **one shared ending situation and carrier
with four substantive quadrant resolutions**. Ambiguous versus Love governs how
mutually legible their exceptional importance becomes; Sweet versus Dark
governs which of their character-specific facets becomes decisive visible
conduct. It does not mechanically ask whether one named pattern was interrupted
or reinforced. Every quadrant must change character conduct and concrete
relational consequence, not merely atmosphere, diction, or insert length. The
same misaligned-anticipation home lens remains available across all four, so
technique does not disclose the frozen draw. Exact ending IDs, file division,
Gallery grouping, and storage representation remain technical decisions rather
than narrative ontology.

**Priscilla–Lavinia dramatic question — Proof After Necessity.** Every quadrant
answers:

> When no institutional role, unfinished task, or bodily crisis requires
> contact, what will each woman accept as sufficient proof that the other is
> choosing to remain?

The shared terminal situation must bring them to a point where no legitimate
current obligation requires continued contact and each retains a practicable
way to refuse, leave, or decline the next arrangement. "Remain" may concern
immediate physical presence or a concrete continuation of contact; it does not
pre-authorize an official relationship label. The ending must make the accepted
standard of proof operative through visible conduct and material consequence,
not settle it through a thematic conversation.

**Owner-confirmed constraint on the Packed Room audition—not carrier
approval.** If the Packed Room remains a candidate terminal situation,
Priscilla's move is genuine before Lavinia responds: Priscilla independently
intends to leave, has a credible usable destination, and has arranged matters so
the move can practically complete if Lavinia does nothing. Packing may not be a

The motive is not certified as moral repair. Ending the selective-truth

#### 7.8.3 Priscilla family: operative authorship

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

Both Priscilla forms answer one central ending question:

> When Angela's own imperfect account threatens the arrangement Priscilla has
> made safe and defensible, who gets to make Angela's choice operative?

The question concerns **operative authorship**, not which woman secretly
understands Angela best. Angela's account must concern a consequential choice
whose accepted version causes a concrete person, institution, object, access
arrangement, departure, commitment, relinquishment, or comparable part of the
physical and social world to change. A private statement that produces no
materially different conduct is not enough to discharge the ending.

The carrier is a coupled **durable-record plus physical-arrangement** sequence.
First, one authenticated version of Angela's choice is sent, submitted,
accepted, registered, or otherwise made durable enough that reversal would
require a new act, explanation, cost, or confrontation. That version then
immediately reorganizes visible conduct: somebody departs or remains, access or
custody changes, an alternative is relinquished, or a continuing commitment
begins. The causal connection between the accepted wording and the enacted
arrangement must be observable inside the ending.

A record without materially altered conduct is too abstract; movement without
an operative record is too generic to test Priscilla's authorship. Both parts
are therefore mandatory and neither is decorative corroboration of the other.
Eerie duplication, premature response, or other anomalous evidence may surround
the sequence, but the ending must leave no ambiguity about which version became
operative or what physical arrangement followed. The exact record, recipient,
arrangement, and relinquished alternative remain open.

object. The Sweet test is whether she can state her concern, leave Angela's
authorship usable, and refrain from covertly correcting the implemented result
despite a real cost. The Dark test is whether Angela, lucidly and knowingly,
permits Priscilla's refined or prearranged version to replace her own as the
version on which the world acts. Conscious delegation makes the authorship
transfer attributable to Angela under Section 7.8.2; it does not make the
cultivated dependency harmless, equal, or free of pressure.

##### 7.8.3.2 Procedural-ratchet suspense

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

Priscilla's ending uses **delayed functional legibility through a procedural
ratchet**. No interface, narrator, or character labels the decision as freedom,
control, Sweet, Dark, or the thematic test. Instead, three ordinary stages make
the consequence progressively undeniable:

1. Angela inspects one small but material functional difference between her
   account and Priscilla's version, such as who receives later amendments;
2. after Angela knowingly confirms one version, the campus process routes the
   coordination credential to the corresponding device; and
3. at the programme space, the credential holder receives the relevant update
   and performs the entry action, physically completing the recorded
   arrangement.

Each stage removes an alternative interpretation without stopping to explain
what the sequence symbolizes. Angela must understand the immediate functional
difference before confirmation; the audience may understand its full
relational weight later, when the routine process works. By the end, which
version became operative and who now controls coordination are facts rather
than interpretive puzzles.

Priscilla's tenderness may increase as the available alternatives narrow. A
calm correction, returned device, waiting hand, or ordinary instruction should
carry more pressure than a threat, villain confession, horror sting, romantic
starscape, or conspicuous change of visual palette. Sweet and Dark tension
arises from action order and relinquished authority, not from signalling which
ending the audience ought to fear.

An impossible timestamp, duplicated reminder, premature generic reply, or
already positioned object may sit beside the ratchet only if it does not
operate the decision in advance. In particular, Priscilla cannot receive or use
the valid coordination credential before Angela's confirmed act; doing so
would turn Angela's agency into theatre. The exact discrepancy, message copy,
local anomaly if any, and beat-level timing remain open.

## 8. Hospitalization and audience-caused condition

### 8.1 Trigger

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

Hospitalization is possible only when audience-caused play crosses both of the
following hidden condition boundaries:

- pressure crosses its maximum boundary; **and**
- health crosses its minimum boundary.

Both are necessary. At the plot layer this is an `AND` contract, not an `OR`
contract. Exact numeric values, inclusive/exclusive comparison operators,
resolver timing, and whether a carried sequela remains additionally required
are implementation details not yet decided here.

The August seven-day design currently defines danger with pressure **or**
health plus a carried sequela. That wording conflicts with the new owner ruling
and must not be used as plot law.

### 8.2 Physical and ethical boundary

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

Pressure and health are game abstractions, not real medical measurements or
diagnoses. In the fiction, characters encounter credible signs, limitations,
and qualified medical decisions. The story must not claim that hospitalization
in the physical world proves neglect, nor that affection can prevent illness.

Within this particular causal system, audience operations can drive both hidden
conditions across their limits. Call that conduct knowing only when the game
has first supplied legible warning evidence about the joint risk; hidden author
knowledge cannot be retroactively assigned to the audience. The resulting
Hospitalization is audience-caused whether or not the risk was understood.

“Non-compensatory” is mechanical, not a final moral verdict: Hospitalization
does not award relational momentum, valid consent, romance, or an automatic
offsetting reward merely because it grants Sylvia practical access.

### 8.3 Dramatic consequence and Sylvia

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The audience's punishment is primarily structural rather than the spectacle of
Angela's suffering:

- selected dating opportunities may be superseded;
- later messages may close, change, or arrive without the expected encounter;
- other relationships and duties continue beyond the audience's access; and
- Sylvia may gain practical access and knowingly take advantage of Angela's
  vulnerability.

Hospitalization may increase Sylvia's access, opportunity, or leverage. It must
not automatically increase Angela's affection or manufacture valid consent.
Any genuine affection change requires a later informed, lucid, character-authored
choice by Angela. Access, control, care debt, trust, and love are not synonyms.

If Angela cannot give informed consent, no romantic or sexual escalation can be
treated as mutual. Dark material may portray a boundary violation without
describing the violation as proof of love or medically necessary care.

### 8.4 Adjacent university-owned teaching hospital

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

East Harbour University owns a non-profit teaching hospital immediately beside
the lower edge of its fictional campus. The hospital is clinically and
administratively separate from ordinary university operations even though its
ownership, teaching work, and location connect it to the university.

The arrangement is a fictional stipulation with a verified Hong Kong precedent,
not a copy of a real institution. The Chinese University of Hong Kong describes
CUHK Medical Centre as a private teaching hospital developed within its campus,

right to remain with Angela. Every Hospital appearance or fact available to

After qualified staff determine that a visit is clinically and institutionally
permissible, they obtain Angela's clear permission offscreen and after
stabilization for Sylvia to remain as her chosen visitor. Staff permission and

The exact emergency service, transfer procedure, means by which Sylvia learns

### 8.5 Sylvia's causal innocence and apparent guilt

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

The production truth and the audience-facing mystery deliberately occupy two
different levels:

- **production truth:** Sylvia does not cause Angela's collapse; and
- **released hypothesis:** the available evidence must make secret physical
  inducement by Sylvia a reasonable but unproved suspicion.

When the eventually approved Hospital resolver returns a qualifying result,
the story attributes Angela's collapse to the accumulated, audience-directed
condition path in Section 8.1 and to an ordinary physical chain in the fiction.
A qualifying Hospital result must satisfy **both** necessary boundaries—the
pressure maximum and health minimum—under that resolver; neither boundary alone
may enter Hospital. The `pressure` and `health` values are abstractions, not
diegetic forces or medical diagnoses.

Before any collapse-facing plot beat, Hospital passage, or causally suspicious
Sylvia clue is approved for the new seven-day spine, a private causal continuity
sheet must fix a credible but non-actionable combination of observable
circumstances, signs, timing, and qualified response. It must identify how
audience-directed, condition-affecting operations produce the accumulated
physical burden that leads to the witnessed collapse; the meter result may
trigger resolution but cannot merely correlate with an unrelated cause. The
account as a whole must remain non-actionable: neither the private chain nor an
aggregation of released clues may encode a reproducible collapse method. It
must receive an appropriate physical-world plausibility check. This ruling does
not decide the comparison operators, resolver instant, recovery behaviour, or
possible sequela still left open in Section 8.1.

Sylvia does not intend Angela to collapse. She does not deliberately cause,
hasten, worsen, or increase the likelihood of the qualifying condition, either
directly, indirectly, openly, covertly, or through another person. No hidden
substance, device, technique, deprivation, false instruction, engineered
schedule, psychological campaign, manipulation toward the condition limits,
withheld timely warning or aid, or delayed emergency escalation belongs to her
causal history. She cannot perceive the meters, receive privileged system
knowledge, or know through Observer Pressure that collapse will occur.

An encounter involving Sylvia may still be the setting in which an
audience-directed choice taxes Angela's condition. Any burden contributing to
the Hospital gate must arise from Angela's exertion, neglect, or action as
selected through audience-authorized operations—not from Sylvia's conduct.
Sylvia's invitation creates an opportunity the audience may take; it is not by
itself a causal force. A candidate continuation that cannot preserve this
division may not contribute to the qualifying collapse chain. Apply a neutral
substitution test: replace Sylvia's invitation, chosen setting, and staging with
an ordinary opportunity of comparable effort. If Angela's already accumulated
condition plus the same audience-directed action no longer supports the burden,
the candidate has laundered Sylvia's conduct into audience causation and fails.

## 10. Love-signal grammar

### 10.1 Candidate modes, not a ladder

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

Love is not proved by generic kindness, one striking act, or a hidden point
total. Conduct becomes more legible through character-specific deviation,
context, recurrence under different pressures, and the recipient's actual
freedom. Useful candidate modes include:

1. **selective attention** — she notices what she normally disregards;
2. **voluntary return** — she comes back after practical usefulness ends;
3. **chosen cost** — she spends convenience, status, certainty, or control;
4. **exposure** — she states a preference without disguising it as usefulness;
5. **refusable approach** — she leaves the recipient genuinely able to refuse;
   this is an agency condition, not proof of love; and
6. **repair** — after harm, she changes conduct instead of merely performing
   remorse.

None of these authenticates an interior state. Cost may manipulate, apparent
repair may be strategic, and correct consent practice remains a baseline rather
than a romantic prize. Repetition under different circumstances may make an
attachment increasingly legible without making it certain. A scene uses only
the modes its situation earns; relationships do not march through one formula.

Conduct that persists without an intended witness rules out some performative
explanations, but privacy does not prove sincerity. Conduct designed for a third
person's observation supplies evidence of strategy without proving that care is
absent or present.

### 10.3 Sweet expressive range

**OWNER-CONFIRMED**

At the strongest Sweet expression, desire and consent may become explicit.
Characters may state that they wanted a particular person present, ask someone
to stay, initiate clearly consensual touch, use a relationship label, or say “I
love you” when the directness is character-consistent and consequential. Such a

future choice. Direct language never overrides another person's consent.

Interpretive openness therefore comes from plural motives and consequences,
not compulsory coyness or emotional evasiveness.

## 12. Scene-construction foundation

**RETAINED FOUNDATION, WITH OWNER-CONFIRMED EMPHASIS**

Every proposed message, dating scene, or ending must identify:

- each present character's practical activity;
- each character's self-sufficient want, including wants unrelated to Angela;
- the exact fact each character can lawfully know;
- the relevant established behaviour or the baseline this scene establishes;
- any meaningful deviation the audience can actually recognize;
- what choice, refusal, or exit remains available;
- what materially changes in situation, evidence, opportunity, pressure, or
  expectation—or which costly failure to change becomes newly legible; and
- for a nonterminal scene, which later surface carries any consequence the plot
  needs again; for an ending, which accumulated facts it resolves, transforms,
  or deliberately leaves open.

A challenged scene must additionally identify whether it contains an optional
impulse window, whose established want, habit, fear, jealousy, or distraction
the possible atoms express, what immediate aftermath each receives, and whether
each candidate is a surface atom or a materially distinct consequence variant
under Section 7.7.4.

Dialogue and interaction must be long enough to make the specific pressure and
change of that scene legible. Rhythm, resistance, misunderstanding, and changed
conduct are available diagnostics, not four compulsory beats in every exchange.
Length alone is not value: no exchange may pad itself with decorative mystery,
repeated flirtation, or identical branch templates.

Objects do practical work before they accumulate interpretation. Absurdity may
be socially exact, useful, funny, or normalized; it must not replace required
safety conduct or dissolve every causal distinction.

For scene review:

- **earned** means that removing the established want, pressure, opportunity,
  or cost would make the turn feel arbitrary;
- **concise at the level of effect** permits long interaction when each movement
  changes leverage, knowledge, expectation, or felt rhythm;
- **decorative mystery** withholds or distorts information without altering a
  choice, evidence relation, consequence, or later interpretation; and
- **socially exact absurdity** may be bizarre, but it follows a character or
  institution's local incentive closely enough that others can respond to it.

## 13. Physical-world and evidence discipline

**RETAINED FOUNDATION, WITH OWNER-CONFIRMED EPISTEMIC CLARIFICATION**

Plot-bearing medical, geographic, astronomical, technological, scheduling, and
institutional claims require verification before premise approval. Until
verified, a proposed claim remains either a candidate or a fallible character
belief.

Within the fiction, the manual and later plot must distinguish evidence
carriers:

- directly observed physical fact;
- received report or record;
- character inference;
- audience inference; and
- deliberately unresolved cause.

A metaphor is not empirical proof. Care can reduce risk without guaranteeing
health; attention can improve judgment without guaranteeing success; accurate
details can support a false relational theory; and a fictional mechanism may
organize coincidences without becoming a scientific claim about observation or
human desire.

Lack of proof does not by itself establish impossibility. Conversely, mere
conceivability or failure to disprove a claim is not evidence that the claim is
physically real, probable, or supported. Absence of evidence may also lower a
claim's plausibility when the proposed event should reliably have left
detectable evidence.

Production-side claims use two independent axes rather than one mixed ladder.

**Claim basis:**

- **external-world proposition** — a claim about medicine, geography, physics,
  technology, institutions, or another part of the physical world; or
- **fictional stipulation** — made true inside the game by declared world law
  without being presented as an external scientific discovery.

**Epistemic status:**

- **established** — adequately supported under its stated basis;
- **supported inference** — evidence favours it without proving it
  conclusively;
- **open possibility** — not established and not presently ruled out; or
- **contradicted under stated constraints** — incompatible with committed
  facts or rules unless those commitments are explicitly revised.

A fictional stipulation may therefore be established in canon while an
analogous external-world proposition remains unsupported or contradicted.
Mystery may move among epistemic categories in a character's or audience's
mind, but the production-side ledger must record both axes. Characters may
sincerely confuse them; authorial documentation may not.

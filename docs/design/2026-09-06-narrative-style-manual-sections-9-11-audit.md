# Narrative Style Manual Sections 9–11 Audit

## 1. Outcome and limitation

Sections 9–11 of the
[Narrative Style Manual](../../story/08-narrative-style-manual.md) survive one
bounded adversarial cycle after eleven manual repairs, two bounded
Bible/Handbook conflict repairs, and two retained trade-offs. The result separates
author-facing board craft from code-facing determinism, strengthens ending and
mystery fairness, and turns the scene card from a universal production schema
into a small core with risk-triggered modules.

The owner has chosen not to use subagents or cross-model review for this work.
This is therefore a deliberately degraded same-context audit rather than an
independent fresh-context verdict. The claim and source contract were fixed
before the doubt pass, each objection was checked against current project text,
and the stop condition is recorded below. Shared-context blind spots remain
possible.

This gate does not audit or approve exact Day 1–7 plots, the complete
Priscilla–Lavinia bar-and-residue event, dialogue, scene-local response-family
allocations, ending carriers, eligibility predicates, or implementation. No
exact plot claim was promoted from an automatic conversation summary.

## 2. Claim under test

**Claim:** Sections 9–11 can guide board-selected continuations, ending
closure, mystery fairness, and everyday scene construction without duplicating
runtime mechanics, turning every scene into paperwork, hiding agency inside
randomness, or requiring cross-route play to repair an incoherent route.

**Why it matters:** These sections sit at the handoff from concept to complete
plot. If the boundary is wrong, writers may treat Perfect or Exploded as a
global personality switch, let presentation mutate canon, overbranch every
date, mistake an optional atom for causal randomness, obscure ending action in
the name of ambiguity, or force a two-line message to satisfy an ending-sized
checklist.

## 3. Review contract

The audit compared the manual against:

- Sections 7.7 through 7.8, 12, 13, and 14.1 of the
  [working decision ledger](2026-09-02-narrative-constitution-working-decision-ledger.md),
  covering response families, convergence, local board interpretation,
  impulse atoms, ending closure, scene construction, evidence status, and

- the [runtime behavior record](2026-09-02-relationship-progression-runtime-behavior-record.md),
  especially its ownership of frozen result interpretation, progression,
  persistence, ending resolution, and playback order;
- the retained surface, physical-reality, agency, ending, perceptual, and
  mystery laws in the
  [Core Story Bible](../../story/01-core-story-bible.md);
- the voice, consent, knowledge, danger-response, and whole-scene tests in the
  [Character & Relationship Handbook](../../story/02-character-relationship-handbook.md);
- the pair-board and ending mechanics in the
  [August 7 design](2026-08-07-seven-day-dialogic-flow-design.md); and

The review used the manual's own authority rule: later explicit owner approval
wins; a stale Bible sentence is reported and deliberately reconciled only when
the owner has authorized Bible edits and the conflict directly corrupts this
scene-writing gate.

## 4. Findings and reconciliation

### M911-01 — Section 9 mixed story craft with runtime ownership

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The earlier text placed deterministic
mapping, replay stability, and generated-prose prohibition beside authoring
rules without a clear seam. That invited a style note to become a second
technical specification. The repair keeps local dramatic meaning, coverage,
agency, response-family budgeting, and consequence legibility in the manual;
it assigns total mapping, freezing, persistence, progression timing, and replay
behaviour explicitly to the runtime record. Relationship-readiness intent,
persistent outward stance, concrete carryover, knowledge, and Observer evidence
remain separately authored only when the approved plot consumes them; a family
name cannot assign them implicitly.

### M911-02 — Terminal labels could still acquire global moral meaning

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The manual already denied that
Exploded meant Dark or Perfect meant loving, but the denial followed the family
contract and could be read as an exception. The repaired opening makes local
scene interpretation the governing premise: terminal structure never supplies
character psychology or moral rank by itself.

### M911-03 — Convergence needed a future-use test

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The prior list correctly compared
conduct, consent, material fact, carryover, knowledge, and Observer evidence,
but did not state the easiest operational check. The repair adds: if later
fiction or eligibility reacts to a difference, the branches were not fully
converged. Cosmetic differences still collapse; reusable causal differences
do not.

### M911-04 — The impulse-atom paragraph duplicated storage behaviour

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** A writer needs to know that the device
is optional, rare, fully authored, small, and followed by an immediate
aftermath. The writer does not need the style manual to restate how attempt
context stores or redraws it. The repair preserves the authoring envelope and
links one-time selection, freezing, and replay stability to the runtime record.

### M911-05 — Core closure did not assign a burden to later layers

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The preceding rule prevented an
Observer coda from replacing the core, but it did not say what a pair layer,
prelude, or coda must accomplish positively. The repair requires every later
layer to make its own new action and evidence legible and forbids it from
repairing missing core closure or smuggling in a contradictory unseen history.

### M911-06 — Mystery fairness implied that every mystery has one hidden answer

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** “A production-side causal truth may
remain unrevealed” could silently turn deliberate non-resolution into an
author-held solution. The repair distinguishes fixed-but-unrevealed causal
history from an approved absence of one private answer. It also distinguishes
a mystery that promises reconstructible causality from a hidden history whose
exact chronology intentionally remains unrecoverable. All still require
coherent authenticated facts and constrained live accounts.

### M911-07 — Cross-route synthesis could become a patch for local failure

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** A real evidential shift across routes
is valuable, but a route cannot contain a physical contradiction and ask a
later playthrough to excuse it. The repair requires each route to remain
physically and causally coherent on its own; cross-route material may add
facets or reweight inference, not manufacture every missing causal step.

### M911-08 — The scene card was too large for its smallest surfaces

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Twelve mandatory fields treated a
short message, a full dating scene, and a layered ending as equally complex.
It also required motives for every present person, encouraging invented depth
for incidental workers. The replacement uses a seven-item core at the smallest
useful scene or ending-layer level and limits character fields to materially
active participants.

### M911-09 — Anchor and lens fields were mandatory even when unused

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** A pure message exchange may contain no
perceptual prose, and many scenes need no foreground literary lens. The repair
requires an anchor and physical field only when perceptual prose occurs and
records a lens only when it genuinely improves the scene.

### M911-10 — High-risk situations lacked their own focused checks

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** A generic “if challenged” list
combined board, Observer, Sweet/Dark, and physical verification while omitting
the exact failure modes of Hospital, incapacity, Group passage boundaries, and
pair non-dominance. Five conditional modules now cover challenge/board,
mystery/evidence, ending, Hospital/credible danger, and Group/pair/form
variation. Technical fields remain outside the writing card.

### M911-11 — Removal tests risked becoming another mandatory checklist

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Eleven useful questions placed after
one universal card could be interpreted as eleven further approvals for every
message. The repair marks them as targeted pressure tests selected by actual
risk. Their content remains available without rewarding paperwork.

### M911-12 — The Core Bible still authorized a fourth visible surface

**Classification: Valid + actionable cross-document conflict.** The Core Bible
called the Day 2 umbrella event a directly shown cutaway, while the later
owner-confirmed law makes it an offscreen history inferred only through
messages, dating scenes, and endings. Because the core card begins by checking
surface legality, leaving the Bible stale would invalidate the manual's first
question. The bounded repair removes cutaway authority, promotes the approved
private airport causal core, limits released facets, and removes the event from
perceptual-anchor eligibility. It does not choose final facet carriers or
staging.

### M911-13 — The Bible and Handbook retained a discarded anomaly quota

**Classification: Valid + actionable cross-document conflict.** The Core Bible
still required zero or one abnormality in an “ordinary scene,” and the Handbook
told writers to enforce that budget. The owner explicitly discarded the quota.
The bounded repair replaces it with functional weird/eerie/absurdity,
non-duplication, causal-legibility, and credible-danger tests. It creates no new
quota and does not require every scene to contain abnormality.

## 5. Retained trade-offs

### T911-01 — The two-to-four response-family budget can become a template

Keeping “two to four, with three as a soft target” risks making unrelated
scenes share one visible architecture. It survives because it is explicitly a
complexity alarm, not a quota or enum, and because one cause-to-family map is
still required for every challenged scene. If finished plots repeatedly force
three cosmetic branches, remove the soft target before defending the pattern.

### T911-02 — Conditional modules can be skipped too casually

A modular card lowers maintenance cost but permits a writer to avoid the exact
module that would expose a flaw. The safeguard is risk-based rather than
discretionary: board causality, mystery evidence, endings, credible danger,
Group/pair structure, and substantive form variation trigger their named
modules. Empty fields are preferable to invented facts, but an applicable
module cannot be omitted merely to preserve a favourite scene.

## 6. Seven-question ablation result

| Test | Result after repair |
|---|---|
| Removal | Removing scene-local board interpretation restores global result morality; removing the runtime boundary restores duplicate specifications. Both remain load-bearing. |
| Simplification | One small core plus five risk modules is materially simpler than one universal ending-sized card. Merging the modules would hide their distinct failure modes. |
| Collision | The umbrella cutaway and numerical abnormality quota were the two direct cross-document collisions affecting this gate; both are now explicitly reconciled. Romantic-geometry, exact progression-window, and residual anchor-authority drift remain open. |
| Variation | A message may be brief, a scene may use no lens or atom, several board causes may converge, and multiple abnormal elements may coexist when they perform distinct work. |
| Evidence | Each route now needs local coherence; cross-route facets can reweight inference without becoming Angela's knowledge or rescuing a contradiction. |
| Maintenance | Runtime freezing and evaluation stay in one behaviour record; the writing card stores only facts the authored plot needs. No variable, UI, DTL entry, or code change is introduced. |
| Agency | Boards select authored conduct rather than desire; atoms do not erase responsibility; Hospital checks preserve capacity and consent; later ending layers cannot rewrite the core. |

## 7. Bounded cross-document repair

This gate changed only the following directly related documents:

- [Core Story Bible](../../story/01-core-story-bible.md): reconciled the three
  visible surfaces, replaced the visible umbrella cutaway with the approved
  offscreen Day 2 causal history and bounded facets, removed its anchor
  eligibility, and replaced the anomaly quota with functional law.
- [Character & Relationship Handbook](../../story/02-character-relationship-handbook.md):
  replaced the stale “Bible budget” checklist item with the same functional and
  credible-danger test.
- [Working decision ledger](2026-09-02-narrative-constitution-working-decision-ledger.md):
  changed the umbrella conflict's status from deferred to reconciled while
  retaining the former cutaway language in historical provenance.
- [Seven-Day Causal Matrix](../../story/07-seven-day-causal-matrix.md): updated
  its umbrella-law cross-reference to the reconciled Core Bible heading without
  changing a placement or plot premise.

It did not reconcile the remaining romantic-asymmetry language, exact
promotion valves, or every distributed reference to “ordinary scene.” Those
changes are connected to unfinished relationship and seven-day architecture
and remain for the coordinated canon pass. It also did not edit the August
mechanical design or runtime code.

## 8. Rules that survived pressure

The pass found no sufficient reason to remove:

- nondiegetic, scene-local board interpretation;
- complete authored coverage for every reachable terminal cause;
- two to four response families as a soft complexity ceiling;
- semantic convergence only when reusable consequence and evidence agree;
- concrete pair residue without mutation of the frozen pair form;
- rare, fully authored impulse atoms with immediate aftermath;
- practically conclusive, interpretively open core endings;
- Group/Two-friends non-dominance;
- counter-facets for materially incriminating clues;
- coherent physical history despite plural motives or unresolved global cause;
  and
- targeted removal tests as an adversarial tool rather than a ritual.

## 9. Stop condition

After reconciliation and a final craft/runtime, local/cross-route, and
core/conditional-card reread, no further high-confidence defect whose repair
belongs inside Sections 9–11 remained. Remaining issues are Manual Sections
12–13, the open distributed Bible drift, exact scene allocations, complete
ending-family auditions, the Day 1–7 plot, and the future raw-conversation audit
of the complete Priscilla–Lavinia bar-and-residue event. Under the bounded
ablation rule, this gate stops after one cycle and does not make the whole
manual final authority.

# Room-Owned Aperture Caption Material Disposition

## 1. Accepted direction

The project owner selected every recommended **A** in the witnessed-scene
material batch on 2026-08-21. The caption region uses a **Registered Material
Field** joined to the Room-Owned Aperture by one clean registered seam. Caption
cards become nearly flush registered leaves; History becomes a full-canvas
institutional reading ledger; inactive material carries only quiet maintained
wear.

This disposition settles material presentation beside Room-Owned Aperture. It
does not change story content, route logic, Minesweeper mechanics, caption
publication, transport behavior, focus order, persistence, localization, or
recovery. It authorizes no Godot implementation.

Partial visual evidence for the substrate and boundary comparison is:

`.superpowers/brainstorm/32727-1787290229/content/tableau-caption-substrate-directions-v5.html`

That disposable companion is evidence rather than authority. It does not lock
the old portrait-register geometry, final colours, final card backing, or any
runtime behavior. The conversational approval and the decisions restated here
own the complete material direction.

## 2. Registered Material Field and seam

Authored room pixels end at the accepted Room-Owned Aperture boundary. The
functional caption field begins at native `y = 224`, `196`, or `164` for the
100%, 125%, or 150% text preset and continues to the pinned rail at `y = 328`.
The solid functional rail continues from `y = 328` to `360`.

The caption field is one fixed, low-value, UI-owned maintained-university
material. It is independent of room, location, character, relationship,
route, result, challenge state, and whether the aperture currently contains
zero or several visible bodies. It never becomes a scene clue, Angela-absence
signal, anomaly, technical status, or emotional meter.

Exactly one clean native-pixel seam separates the aperture from the caption
field. The seam remains stationary and geometrically exact. It does not react
to speaker, Minesweeper result, relationship state, Observer state, route,
failure, or narrative pressure.

The field is not:

- a continuation of environment art beneath captions;
- a paper folio, evidence board, transcript, or visiting document;
- a translucent surveillance pane or recording surface;
- a generated character, route, or result material; or
- an excuse for scene pixels, clues, bodies, or objects to appear beneath
  caption glyphs.

## 3. Flush registered caption leaves

The accepted bottom-up caption stack remains unchanged. Each current or
retained caption occupies a restrained, nearly flush leaf within the same
Registered Material Field family. The leaves read as maintained reading
planes, not loose paper, chat bubbles, subtitles floating over art, or movable
windows.

The current caption remains the lowest card. Retained captions recede through
their accepted material hierarchy, spacing, and depth while preserving the
same selected text size and required contrast. They are never made secondary
by shrinking, fading, blurring, tint-only evidence, or reduced legibility.

Caption text and its functional backing remain fully protected from scene art.
Exact leaf value steps, backing opacity, seams, insets, and microgeometry remain
for later tuple and native-pixel calibration; those details may not turn the
leaves into paper slips or alter the accepted caption rhythm.

## 4. History reading material

History retains its accepted full-canvas, inspect-only reading-sheet behavior.
Its visual direction is an institutional reading ledger made from the same
Registered Material Field family, with a denser but calm reading anatomy.

The frozen scene does not show through History. History does not resemble an
evidence wall, archive collectible, dialogue-choice record, or forensic
comparison, and its material never adds a verdict, route explanation, speaker
solution, or relationship interpretation to witnessed lines.

## 5. Quiet maintained wear

Wear is stationary, sparse, and nonsemantic. It may live only on inactive
material planes where it cannot interfere with captions, glyphs, controls,
focus, selection, seams, targets, scrolling, or technical truth.

Wear never intensifies with pressure, relationship, route, result, health,
Observer state, failure, or anomaly. High Contrast may simplify or omit it.
Reduced Motion needs no alternate treatment because the wear never moves.

## 6. No-choice authority boundary

This material direction creates no dialogue-choice surface. The accepted UI
foundation remains controlling: canonical Dating, visible Priscilla-Lavinia,
Hospital, and ordered-ending playback publish no response lists, route
selectors, default answers, or player-authored Angela lines.

Minesweeper play remains the sole in-scene agency and
relationship-consequence mechanism. The player operates the board; the board
engine derives and durably commits board-result truth; the coordinator consumes
that truth, commits the registered consequence, and freezes the validated
post-scene context. The narrative host publishes only that context, without
offering the result as a selectable answer, previewing its consequence, or
displaying a result grade or relationship summary. Operational application
controls and accepted board terminal operations are not dialogue choices.

## 7. Deliberately unlocked details

This disposition does not lock:

- final raw colours or any complete theme/accessibility tuple;
- exact caption-leaf or History-sheet opacity, backing, value steps, borders,
  insets, spacing, or texture asset;
- typography metrics, final localized copy, or line wrapping;
- final wear density, dither pattern, or material export;
- transition, sound, ambience, or authored scene art; or
- Godot scenes, resources, scripts, tests, assets, imports, or compatibility
  behavior.

These details may be calibrated only inside the accepted material hierarchy.
They may not reopen the Registered Material Field, single seam, flush-leaf
relationship, opaque full-canvas History boundary, quiet nonsemantic wear, or
no-inline-scene-choice law.

## 8. Reopening rule

The rejected hard-nocturnal-field, visiting-folio, direct-abutment,
double-instrument-lip, cardless-text, paper-slip, translucent-History, and
pressure-reactive-wear directions remain comparison history only. Reopening
any of them as an equal production candidate requires a new explicit owner
decision.

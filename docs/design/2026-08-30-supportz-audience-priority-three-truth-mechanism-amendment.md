# Supportz Audience Priority and Three-Truth Mechanism Amendment

## 1. Status and Objective

This document records the owner-approved conversational design for four related
changes:

1. Supportz cannot be purchased on Day 1 and first becomes eligible on Day 2;
2. successful Day 2 and Day 6 R3 group activations preserve a separate
   audience-priority history;
3. successful activation in both windows makes Priscilla's and Lavinia's Day 7
   invitations literally absent; and
4. Angela's lawful knowledge of Priscilla and Lavinia remains separate from
   both pair progress and audience-visible evidence.

The desired audience-facing effect is severe but lawful. On each pair day, the
audience receives Priscilla's and Lavinia's ordinary named invitation notices.
Continuing to the Supportz-enabled third desktop round while both invitations
remain unread may supersede them through the existing group protocol. Repeating
that priority on both pair days changes what enters the world on Day 7. The game
does not accuse, congratulate, explain, or label the audience's conduct. The
missing invitations are the consequence; the independently qualified
Priscilla-Lavinia ending is its later answer.

`Supporter` remains optional writer-facing shorthand for Angela's local
dramatic function in an attended Group. It is not a player-facing identity,
moral award, relationship state, save flag, dialogue mandate, or implementation
architecture. The approved architecture retains three distinct truths instead.

The conversational decisions and this exact written specification were approved
by the owner on 2026-08-30. This document authorizes design documentation only:
it does not authorize Godot, GDScript, Dialogic, test, save-schema, requirement,
Beads, localization, UI, asset, implementation-plan, implementation-commit, or
remote-push changes.

## 3. Approved Decisions

1. **Day 1 Supportz absence:** Supportz cannot be purchased on Day 1, even
   after two valid desktop completions and even when every other predicate
   passes.
2. **Day 2 onward:** From Day 2 through Day 7, the existing Supportz
   eligibility law applies after the new day predicate passes.
3. **Purchase is not the meaningful audience act:** buying Supportz records a
   capability transaction only. It creates no pair progress,
   audience-priority credit, Angela knowledge, or Day 7 silence.
4. **Successful R3 activation is the structural fact:** audience-priority
   credit arises only when the existing third-round activation succeeds while
   both same-day Priscilla and Lavinia solo offers are available and unread.
5. **Both pair days are required:** Day 7 silence requires one successful R3
   activation on Day 2 and one on Day 6 in the active saved continuation of the
   same run.
6. **Literal silence:** when that conjunction is true, neither Priscilla's nor
   Lavinia's Day 7 invitation is generated. There is no substitute message,
   joint invitation, toast, warning, locked row, gap, expiry trace,
   explanation, or moral label.
7. **Pair count remains broader:** two counted pair meetings still qualify the
   P-L ending whether either meeting was Group, Missed, Private-visible, or
   Private-offscreen.
8. **Sylvia and Alone remain independent:** Sylvia's Day 7 invitation follows
   its existing tier and round law. Done with no selected destination still
   produces Alone. The independently qualified P-L ending remains the latest
   applicable layer.
9. **Angela's knowledge remains lawful:** audience-visible evidence does not
   give Angela private knowledge. Group attendance may authorize a sharper
   later observation; an audience-only pair surface does not.
10. **Pair-aware dialogue is contextual:** Angela may occasionally observe
    Priscilla-Lavinia evidence she lawfully knows. No scene must name the absent
    woman, and no generic insertion may force Angela to interpret, endorse, or
    manage their relationship.

## 4. Governing Terms

### 4.1 Supportz-Eligible Day

A Supportz-eligible day is a current logical day numbered 2 through 7 while the
run remains in its ordinary playing state. The existing current-day completion,
daily cap, branch cap, quote, funds, and transaction predicates remain jointly
necessary.

A Day 2 purchase can authorize that day's third desktop round and remains
durable for later days under the existing capacity law. A second purchase is
not required merely because the calendar reaches Day 6.

Day 1's fixed Supportz area remains nonsemantic blank capacity. It receives no
lock, hint, error, hover meaning, accessible secret, or preview of later use.

### 4.2 Successful R3 Activation

A successful R3 activation is the existing August group-activation transaction
at the third-completed-round boundary on Day 2 or Day 6. It succeeds only while
both same-day solo offers remain available and unread, then atomically
supersedes those offers and creates the single group action for that window.

Purchasing capacity, starting a third round, or forfeiting one is insufficient.
The existing completion boundary remains required. Opening either solo offer
before that boundary prevents activation because the two offers no longer
satisfy the existing predicate.

### 4.3 Literal Day 7 Silence

Literal silence means non-generation. It is not a hidden generated entry,
suppressed toast, already-read invitation, expired action, inaccessible
destination, placeholder, or alternate joint correspondence.

Rounds 1 and 2 still complete normally and retain their ordinary desktop
consequences. They simply generate no Priscilla or Lavinia ending invitation
under the double-activation condition. Round 3 may still generate Sylvia's
eligible invitation under existing law.

## 5. Three-Truth State Model

The three truths may coincide, but none may stand in for another.

| Truth | Authoritative evidence | Consequence | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pair-meeting truth** | Existing Day 2 and Day 6 counted-window receipts and their lawful modes | Both counts qualify the P-L counter-ending | Audience priority, Angela witness, Day 7 silence, affection, tier, or consent |
| **Audience-priority truth** | Durable proof that the existing R3 activation transaction succeeded on each pair day | Day 2 plus Day 6 success suppresses P/L Day 7 invitation generation | Angela knows the pair scene, the audience morally supports a pairing, or the pair has an exclusive label |
| **Angela-knowledge truth** | Angela's direct Group witness or a later lawful record, object, changed circumstance, message, or dialogue carrier for the exact fact | May authorize a contextual, individually auditioned observation in a later Angela scene | Pair count, activation, private motive, omniscience, mandatory naming, or Day 7 suppression |

For specification reasoning only:

```text
pair_ending_eligible =
    pair_meeting_counted(day_2)
    and pair_meeting_counted(day_6)

audience_priority_complete =
    r3_activation_succeeded(day_2)
    and r3_activation_succeeded(day_6)

priscilla_day7_invitation_eligible =
    priscilla_tier in {Ambiguous, Love}
    and not audience_priority_complete

lavinia_day7_invitation_eligible =
    lavinia_tier in {Ambiguous, Love}
    and not audience_priority_complete

sylvia_day7_invitation_eligible =
    sylvia_tier in {Ambiguous, Love}
```

These names express semantic facts, not approved runtime fields, APIs, resource
keys, save keys, or Dialogic variables.

### 5.1 Relationship Between Pair Modes and Truths

| Pair mode | R3 activation succeeded | Pair meeting counted | Angela directly witnessed the pair scene |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Group | Yes | Yes | Yes, bounded to what she physically perceived |
| Missed | Yes | Yes | No |
| Private-visible | Yes | Yes | No |
| Private-offscreen | No | Yes | No |
| Prevented | No | No | No pair scene exists |

Missed or Private-visible material may later create an ordinary lawful carrier.
That later carrier grants only its own bounded fact; audience visibility itself
is never the carrier.

## 6. Lifecycle and Receipt Contract

### 6.1 Supportz Transaction

On Day 1, the Supportz slot cannot open its confirmation surface.

On Days 2 through 7, a successful purchase retains the existing order:

1. validate the day gate and all existing eligibility predicates;
2. revalidate quote, funds, run, branch, day, daily cap, and branch cap;
3. spend exactly `$45`;
4. add one durable future desktop-round capacity;
5. close the day's Supportz purchase opportunity; and
6. retain one idempotent transaction receipt.

Failure mutates nothing and spends nothing.

### 6.2 Audience-Priority Evidence

The existing successful R3 activation transaction is the source event. Its
day-specific provenance must remain durably distinguishable after the group
action later resolves.

Implementation may reuse an existing durable transaction receipt if it
preserves that provenance. If current state loses it, future implementation
must add the minimum evidence required. It must not infer activation from:

- Supportz ownership or purchase count;
- entering, starting, forfeiting, or completing a third round alone;
- a pair meeting count;
- a visible pair scene or pair-board result;
- Angela's attendance;
- the drawn pair combination; or
- a Day 7 result.

Day 2 and Day 6 activation facts are independently idempotent inside the active
continuation. Replay, resume, or repeated signal delivery cannot duplicate,
erase, or move one fact to the other day. A single terminal group-action object
must not make a resolved Day 2 action block or overwrite the separate Day 6
activation window.

Activation history follows the existing Load replacement law rather than
inventing a new profile-monotonic milestone:

- an ordinary Save made after activation contains that day-specific fact;
- loading that post-activation save copies and remaps the selected fact into the
  fresh branch continuation;
- loading a pre-activation save truthfully rewinds the fact along with that
  snapshot's ordinary contact future;
- activation facts from an abandoned future or sibling branch are not unioned
  into the selected continuation; and
- New Run creates no activation history from the prior `run_id`.

To make the post-activation Load case lawful, this amendment adds the selected
snapshot's day-specific audience-priority activation receipt to the closed
generation-remap allowlist in August 11 section 9.3. Restore preserves its
source transaction provenance and pair-day meaning while remapping the branch,
continuation-generation, and causal-day identities required by the fresh active
continuation. It does not admit any other new receipt category to that allowlist.

The Day 7 test therefore uses the two facts present in the active restored
continuation. It does not require their source receipts to share one historical
`branch_id`, because every Load issues a fresh branch identity.

### 6.3 Day 7 Candidate Freeze

After due follow-ups and the unavoidable echo fallback complete, the Day 7
resolver evaluates durable activation history before creating each P/L ending
invitation.

If both activation facts exist:

- Priscilla's Round 1 invitation is not created;
- Lavinia's Round 2 invitation is not created;
- no corresponding contact entry, unread mark, notification, action, schedule
  destination, History projection, or expiry record exists;
- Sylvia's Round 3 law remains unchanged; and
- Done still selects at most one available destination or Alone.

The filter changes neither woman's affection, dark count, tier, attitude, solo
history, desire, nor prior invitation history.

### 6.4 Angela's Knowledge and Later Observation

A baseline Angela scene may contain an occasional Priscilla-Lavinia observation
grounded only in Bible-established history, a fact Angela independently learned,
or conduct she directly witnessed. Baseline does not mean that Angela knows the
shared flat, argument, Room encounter, private state, or motive unless a source
separately establishes that exact fact.

An Angela-attended Group may authorize a sharper later variant about one exact
visible gesture, wording pattern, arrangement, contradiction, or jointly
produced object. Every such variant still requires an individually suitable
scene and character-faithful wording.

Missed, Private-visible, and Private-offscreen presentations grant no Group
witness to Angela. A later ordinary carrier may teach her a bounded fact, but it
grants only what it physically conveys.

Angela may report evidence or decline to answer for the absent woman. She may
not be compelled to declare a motive, diagnose a relationship, explain the
route, or mention the other woman in every solo scene.

## 7. Invariants

The following remain true in every branch:

1. Supportz remains fixed at `$45`, once per logical day and at most three
   times per saved branch.
2. Supportz adds desktop-round capacity only; it never directly activates a
   group action.
3. Day 1 contains no Supportz purchase, preview, lock explanation, or
   foreshadowing.
4. Successful activation remains possible only under the existing
   both-available-and-unread predicate.
5. Opening either solo invitation remains its acceptance action, not harmless
   inspection.
6. Pair-meeting count and audience-priority history remain separate.
7. Pair boards change only legibility and mastery evidence.
8. No pair event changes Angela's affection, dark, tier, attitude, or
   invitation consent.
9. No `supporter` relationship tier, global flag, route badge, moral score, or
   player-facing label exists.
10. The pair's desire, state, tone, and label are not derived from audience
    priority.
11. Audience-visible private evidence never becomes Angela knowledge without a
    lawful carrier.
12. Two pair counts can qualify the P-L ending without causing Day 7 silence.
13. Supportz ownership can exist without causing Day 7 silence.
14. Two successful R3 activations complete audience priority even when their
    later pair presentations use different visible modes.
15. Sylvia's invitation, Special trigger, faint precedence, and ending law stay
    unchanged.
16. Alone remains deliberate rather than failed or punitive.
17. A qualified P-L ending remains later than Angela's solo, Sylvia's sequence,
    or Alone and does not replace that committed result.
18. This mechanism approves no final dialogue, prose, scene placement, or Day 2
    Group adaptation.

## 8. Presentation Law

The mechanism relies on existing evidence rather than explanatory UI:

- Priscilla's and Lavinia's pair-day solo deliveries retain their existing
  first-name notification and binary unread behavior.
- Successful group activation retains the existing absence of a group toast.
- Supportz retains its blank-card and unexplained transaction presentation.
- Day 7 suppression appears only as the nonappearance of two invitations.
- No later text says that Supportz, the audience, Angela, Priscilla, or Lavinia
  caused the silence.
- The P-L ending may make the route retrospectively legible through ordinary
  character action, but it does not decode the mechanism.
- Gallery, History, replay, accessibility, or save UI may not expose a hidden
  supporter, priority, or suppressed-invitation label merely because internal
  state must be deterministic.

## 10. Rejected and Retired Mutations

The approved design excludes:

1. one global `supporter` Boolean controlling mechanics, knowledge, and
   dialogue;
2. Supportz purchase alone suppressing Day 7 invitations;
3. pair count alone suppressing Day 7 invitations;
4. requiring Angela's Group attendance before the audience-priority consequence
   can occur;
5. allowing audience-visible private scenes to become Angela's memory;
6. requiring Angela to mention the absent woman in every later solo;
7. treating Angela as the pair's spokesperson, matchmaker, permission-giver,
   or relationship manager;
8. generating and then visibly locking, expiring, or erasing the Day 7
   invitations;
9. replacing the absent invitations with a joint message or destination;
10. explaining the consequence through an error, toast, codex, Gallery label,
    accessibility-only spoiler, or ending summary;
11. changing pair state, tone, desire, consent, or label from audience-priority
    history; and
12. treating one pair-day activation as sufficient.

These ideas may survive in conversation history but must not re-enter later
work as implied requirements.

# Narrative Style Manual Sections 12–13 Audit

## 1. Outcome and limitation

Sections 12–13 of the
[Narrative Style Manual](../../story/08-narrative-style-manual.md) survive one
bounded adversarial cycle after thirteen finding clusters and two retained trade-offs. The
former temporary review diary is now a durable authority-maintenance protocol,
and the obsolete “finish the manual later” checklist is now a production-use
and bounded-review cadence. The complete manual can therefore function as a
reconciled writer-facing reference.

The owner has chosen not to use subagents or cross-model review. This is a
deliberately degraded same-context audit rather than an independent
fresh-context verdict. The claim and source contract were fixed before the
doubt pass, every objection was checked against the current files, and the stop
condition appears below. Shared-context blind spots remain possible.

This gate does not approve an exact Day 1–7 plot, ending family, scene-local
response map, progression predicate, bar/residue chronology, dialogue, DTL, or
implementation. The complete Priscilla–Lavinia bar-and-residue audit still
requires the raw chronological user-and-assistant conversation rather than an
automatic summary.

## 2. Claim under test

**Claim:** Sections 12–13 can preserve decision authority and guide the project
from plot through dialogue without making the manual a second Bible, turning
traceability into canon, endlessly re-auditing unchanged rules, or silently
promoting open material.

**Why it matters:** A style manual that explains only how it was assembled
becomes obsolete as soon as assembly ends. A manual that absorbs every route
fact becomes a competing canon source. These final sections must instead say
how a later correction moves through owning and derived documents, when a gate
reopens, which evidence is required, how the manual enters production, and
when review stops.

## 3. Review contract

The audit compared Sections 12–13 against:

- Sections 1, 9, 14, 14.1, and 15 of the
  [working decision ledger](2026-09-02-narrative-constitution-working-decision-ledger.md),
  covering authority, romantic geometry, document workflow, bounded ablation,
  special raw-conversation evidence, and the open-decision queue;
- the [Core Story Bible](../../story/01-core-story-bible.md) and
  [Character & Relationship Handbook](../../story/02-character-relationship-handbook.md)
  as the owning and derived narrative documents affected by settled laws;
- the [August 7 design](2026-08-07-seven-day-dialogic-flow-design.md),
  [runtime behavior record](2026-09-02-relationship-progression-runtime-behavior-record.md),
  and [Causal Matrix](../../story/07-seven-day-causal-matrix.md) where style and
  plot-facing mechanical claims meet; and
- the three earlier sectional audits as evidence of what had already survived,
  not as authority to approve new plot material.

The fixed contract was: convert one-use process language into durable law;
retain detailed provenance because loss of conversational detail is a stated
project risk; distinguish normative instruction from traceability; close only
settled cross-document conflicts; preserve open exact plots and implementation;
and stop after a clean bounded reconciliation rather than manufacturing more
review work.

## 4. Findings and reconciliation

### M1213-01 — A temporary audit procedure masqueraded as permanent craft law

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The former Section 12 began “for each
candidate manual rule” but did not state what happens after the manual becomes
usable. It is now a bounded update transaction that locates authority,
classifies material, updates the owning document first, repairs affected
derivatives, preserves provenance, records unresolved collisions, and invokes
ablation only when risk justifies it.

### M1213-02 — The conflict rule still required deferral after the gate had closed

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Section 1 told writers to defer all
distributed repair until the manual cluster was reviewed. Retaining that
sentence after the final gate would institutionalize known drift. The repair
now requires an owning-document-first bounded transaction when a decision is
settled and an explicit open marker when it is not.

### M1213-03 — Traceability tables could be mistaken for an authority hierarchy

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Coverage and source-classification
tables remain useful, but listing a source can look like promotion. Both tables
now state that they are provenance maps only and cannot approve material left
upstream.

### M1213-04 — The raw-conversation rule was buried inside a universal workflow

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The complete Priscilla–Lavinia
bar-and-residue audit has an unusually strict owner-confirmed evidence
requirement. Moving it into a named special-evidence subsection preserves that
law without implying that every routine maintenance correction requires a full
conversation export.

### M1213-05 — The source index stopped before the final gate

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The classification table could not
explain the authority of its own completion or the coordinated canon repair.
It now links this audit and the bounded post-manual reconciliation record while
explicitly denying whole-repository coverage.

### M1213-06 — The drift register mixed completed and future work

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Romantic openness, exact progression
windows, and writer-facing anchor declaration were still marked open despite
owner approval and authorization to edit the Bibles. They are now marked
reconciled with exact preserved boundaries. The umbrella and abnormality
repairs remain recorded as completed.

### M1213-07 — A known residence conflict was omitted from the drift register

**Classification: Valid + actionable, guarded rather than migrated here.** The
Core Bible preserves inherited cohabitation language while the later working
decision makes Lavinia the sole resident and displaces the Packed Room carrier.
That is a genuine canon migration, but it is bound to the complete bar/residue
history and the owner's raw-conversation evidence rule. The old subsection is
now visibly historical and barred from forward use, while the Handbook
withholds un-migrated residence knowledge. This style gate does not reconstruct
the missing chronology or erase its provenance.

### M1213-08 — Exclusion language could be read as tacit possibility

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The prior list said what the manual did
not promote but did not expressly deny that an excluded item was therefore
available for use. It now states that neither exclusion nor presence in the
open-work list grants canon status.

### M1213-09 — Section 13 described work that had already happened

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The old “Manual review sequence” told a
future reviewer to audit remaining sections and only then use the manual. After
this gate, that instruction would be false. The replacement records all four
gates, states the same-context limitation, and gives a plot-to-dialogue use
sequence compatible with the project's end-first hybrid.

### M1213-10 — There was no precise reopening or stop rule

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** Without a trigger, maintenance becomes
either neglect or ritual review. The repair reopens only for later owner
correction, verified evidence, concrete cross-document collision, or repeated
draft failure, and stops when the named claim has no further high-confidence
in-scope defect. Open questions and provenance remain visible rather than being
forced into false closure.

### M1213-11 — Active Day 1 documents silently defeated the selective reset

**Classification: Valid + actionable cross-document conflict.** The current
decision ledger explicitly reopened every exact Day 1–7 message, dating premise,
response mapping, mystery carrier, and stale approval, yet the Core Bible still
called three mystery anchors fixed, the Causal Matrix still marked two Day 1
solos and one message `APPROVED`, and the Beatbook still called their complete
constellation approved. That would make the manual's promised Day 1 plot audit
fictional. The repair preserves the playable container and historical work but
marks the former anchors, Day 1 rows, message premise, and six-family reaction
grids as reopened reference. It selects no replacement fact or scene.

### M1213-12 — The runtime record promoted an unaudited P–L candidate

**Classification: Valid + actionable cross-document conflict.** The runtime
behavior record correctly left exact pair-ending residue rules open, yet a
separate subsection, three drift rows, and eleven “required” checks treated the
Day 2 bar confrontation, cheek injury, Day 6 continuation split, and Angela's
first-recognition carriers as already approved. That contradicted both the
ledger's own `unapproved bar-return audition` labels and the owner's requirement
to audit the raw chronological conversation. The repair retains the frozen
four-form mechanism and generic pair lifecycle, but replaces plot-specific
requirements with an explicit hold and marks their historical checks dormant.
No candidate detail was rejected or promoted.

### M1213-13 — A hard duration line survived a later soft-target ruling

**Classification: Valid + actionable cross-document conflict.** The decision
ledger says the former two-hour first-clear figure applies at most as a soft
target for plot content, may be exceeded when earned, and is not canon or a
compatibility constraint. The Core Bible and derived Public Profile still
presented two hours for a first clear and five hours for completion as project
facts. The repair converts the Core figure into nonbinding plot-length guidance,
removes the unsupported five-hour hard target, and leaves public runtime unfixed.
This changes no scene length by itself.

## 5. Retained trade-offs

### T1213-01 — Detailed provenance costs reading space

The two tables and drift register are longer than a minimalist style manual
requires. They survive because this project has accumulated many approved,
reopened, and superseded decisions, and the owner explicitly identified detail
loss across long conversations as a risk. The guardrail is categorical: tables
are labelled non-normative provenance, while reusable craft law stays in the
manual's earlier sections.

### T1213-02 — “Reconciled” may sound stronger than same-context evidence permits

The final document status could be mistaken for independent validation. It
survives because the four bounded gates and directly coupled document repairs
are complete, making “working audit draft” inaccurate. The manual and each audit
explicitly disclose that subagent and cross-model review were declined, preserve
the right to reopen a concrete defect, and deny approval of exact plots or code.

## 6. Seven-question ablation result

| Test | Result after repair |
|---|---|
| Removal | Removing the owning-document-first transaction restores silent drift; removing the special evidence rule or runtime hold permits a summary-backed bar candidate to become code law. Those remain load-bearing. The public duration promise was not load-bearing and was removed; the two-hour figure survives only as flexible internal plot guidance. |
| Simplification | One update transaction, one special evidence exception, and one explicit plot-dependent runtime hold are simpler than mixing all audits or candidate receipts into the permanent writing and code contracts. |
| Collision | Romantic openness, reopened progression windows, anchor declaration, selective-reset status, residual umbrella language, the runtime record's accidental P–L candidate promotion, and the stale hard duration line received bounded repairs. Residence history remains an explicit open migration rather than a concealed collision. |
| Variation | A small wording correction need not trigger a whole-manual audit; a physical, agency, progression, surface, or ending change still receives the module proportional to its risk. Plot length may exceed the former target when earned rather than forcing every route into one duration. |
| Evidence | Source listing no longer counts as promotion. Owner-confirmed sources govern; the exceptional bar/residue audit still requires its raw chronology. |
| Maintenance | The final section now tells future writers how to use and reopen the manual after compilation, and it prevents ritual review of unchanged gates. |
| Agency | The transaction cannot make a character, route, desire, clue, or ending true merely to resolve a documentation inconvenience. Open material stays open. |

## 7. Bounded cross-document repair

The coordinated repair is recorded separately in
[Post-Manual Canon Reconciliation](2026-09-06-post-manual-canon-reconciliation.md).
It changes only directly coupled active guidance: romantic geometry,
progression-window status, writer-facing anchor declaration, selective-reset
status, a remaining active umbrella-cutaway contradiction, the forward status
of inherited ending summaries, and the runtime record's P–L plot-dependent
hold, plus the stale hard duration claim. No code, DTL, replacement scene premise, exact progression predicate,
ending carrier, or residence history was implemented or approved.

## 8. Rules that survived pressure

The pass found no sufficient reason to remove:

- owner approval as the highest project-specific decision authority;
- separate owners for canon, performance guidance, style, and code behavior;
- explicit classification before promotion;
- preservation of superseded evidence rather than historical rewriting;
- detailed cluster and source traceability with non-normative labels;
- the raw chronological evidence requirement for the complete bar/residue audit;
- clustered rather than sentence-by-sentence ablation;
- an end-first hybrid in which endings constrain but do not invent their routes;
  and
- the sequence from essential plot through targeted scene cards to dialogue.

## 9. Stop condition

After the maintenance/use rewrite, coordinated bounded reconciliation, link and
stale-term checks, and one final status/authority reread, no further
high-confidence defect whose repair belongs inside Sections 12–13 remained.
The complete manual is therefore ready to judge ending-family and Day 1 plot
work. The open Priscilla–Lavinia residence/bar migration, exact response maps,
progression windows and predicates, ending plots, Day 1–7 architecture,
dialogue, DTL, and implementation remain outside this gate.

# Backup Archive Cabinet Standard Palette and State Disposition

## 1. Accepted decision and objective

The project owner approved the complete recommended Backup Standard-master
design batches on 2026-08-23. This disposition does not redesign Backup. It
translates the accepted nine-record archive, shared inspector, transaction
truth, confirmation grammar, and current Unclassified Transfer Bloom direction
into one closed visual mapping for After-Hours Standard and Midnight Standard.

The private mnemonic is:

> **The cabinet remembers nothing; it records exactly.**

Backup therefore shows public evidence without interpreting it. It may identify
a drawer, publish `Day N · HH:MM`, `Empty`, `Unavailable`, a prepared fallback
target, a real operation in progress, or a proven result. It never explains what
a record means, assigns a speaker, gives a narrative hint, or turns save-system
behavior into a selectable story choice. Minesweeper outcomes remain the game's
sole choice-like narrative agency under their own authority.

This is a finishing visual-system layer, not a feature, storage, or
implementation layer. It closes:

- one exact Backup body and its title, desktop, and Pause mounting;
- the two Standard primitive-to-semantic mappings consumed by Backup;
- component bindings for the cabinet, drawers, inspector, mode cluster, action
  dock, confirmations, custody, and recovery;
- the coexistence of occupancy, Selected, Focus, Hover, Press, Disabled,
  Unavailable, Warning, Danger, Destructive action, Busy, Success, Error, and
  Information;
- the distinct projection laws for drawer fingerprints and transfer blooms; and
- the first native-pixel proof family for the Backup Standard cohort.

It creates no record, save field, operation, random generator, bloom allocation
algorithm, narrative sign, public explanation, assistive interpretation, host,
or implementation authority.

## 2. Authority and narrow visual supersession

### 2.1 Retained owners

The accepted Backup Save/Load UI/UX amendment remains authoritative for:

- exactly seven numbered records plus Quick and Autosave, in the fixed row-major
  order;
- `Save`, `Load`, and title `Log in` behavior;
- the action matrix, fallback preparation, confirmations, transaction binding,
  atomic publication, recovery, and exact public copy;
- saved-time truth, Empty and Unavailable meaning, inspector facts, view cache,
  shortcuts, focus order, localization, assistive parity, and input parity; and
- the rule that technical failure is nonfiction and Backup never manufactures
  a story anomaly.

The Backup Transfer Bloom disposition retains the Unclassified Transfer Bloom
as the current direction. Its Distributed Transfer Field remains a deferred
candidate that requires a new explicit owner decision.

The Functional-State Morphology and Legal-Overlap disposition retains typed
fact families, smallest-owner attachment, invariant state morphology, stable
visual zones, collision law, and custody arbitration. The Global Functional
Semantic Alphabet retains complete-tuple governance and the primitive-to-role-
to-component dependency.

The Haunted Instrumentarium foundation retains the Four-Lens theory-to-form
covenant. The shared shell and host amendments retain title, desktop, app-strip,
Pause, foreground, confirmation, route, and restoration ownership. The deferred
feature register remains closed.

### 2.2 Exact visual scope won here

Within Backup only, this disposition resolves:

1. the exact 400-by-328 Backup body and internal arithmetic;
2. title centring and lawful inert side and lower fields;
3. After-Hours Standard and Midnight Standard semantic mappings;
4. component tokens for the cabinet, drawer, inspector, actions, state
   attachments, confirmation, and trusted recovery;
5. exact Standard carriers for Backup-owned state collisions and custody;
6. unchanged authored-pixel law for drawer fingerprints and record-bound
   transfer blooms; and
7. the required Backup Standard proof family.

It narrowly supersedes two earlier visual details:

- the exploratory whole-paper Hover tone becomes one one-native-pixel leading
  contact rule on an unchanged Worn Cream face; no lawful second paper fill is
  introduced; and
- drawer fingerprints no longer recolour with the base palette. Their authored
  pixels remain unchanged across theme, host, text master, locale, and
  functional state.

It also closes the title composition by using the same 400-by-328 Backup body
as every other host. Title supplies two inert 40-pixel habitat margins; Backup
does not stretch to 480 pixels or create a title-only layout.

The owner also approved two narrow UI-custody closures:

- a Backup-local operation that truthfully remains Busy transfers Focus to the
  readable pinned status target, then restores a lawful semantic anchor after
  custody ends; and
- a projected local error-recovery dock begins on `Cancel` and repeats the
  explicit risky command verb where the coordinator actually offers re-entry.

For a visible-duration inspector-dock Quick Save, the first closure narrowly
supersedes the older blanket statement that Quick Save never changes current app
Focus. Immediate Quick Save still causes no Focus transfer.

F5 remains entirely shell-status-owned and never duplicates its edge status in
the pinned Backup region. Immediate F5 causes no Focus transfer. If F5 becomes
visibly pending, current Focus remains only when its exact owner is still a
lawful, meaningful, nonconflicting read-only inspection or scroll target. If the
old owner becomes inert, Focus moves to the already-selected drawer as a
read-only inspection target without changing selection. The exact pre-F5 owner
is restored when Quick custody settles if it is lawful; otherwise Focus remains
on the selected drawer. This is the sole F5 exception to the older blanket
no-Focus-change statement.

The second closure changes only error-dock projection and initial Focus. It does
not preserve a consumed confirmation, prepared target, document revision, or
transaction admission. Every re-entry passes the retained action matrix,
revalidation, and fresh consent again.

Except for those exact UI-custody closures, these are visual closures only. The
retained behavioral owner wins any reading that would change a command, record
state, confirmation, transaction, visible fact, focus order, assistive fact,
route, persistence rule, or failure outcome.

### 2.3 No behavior reopening

The fixed geometry does not authorize per-drawer action buttons, a tenth record,
sorting, filtering, record names, file browsing, double-click Load, hidden
metadata, or a new navigation edge. The state mapping does not make an action
legal, classify a record, start or delay an operation, or choose a recovery
result.

No decorative mark is evidence that a save is safe, recent, important,
compatible, narratively charged, or connected to a character. If a visual token
and the Backup behavior authority disagree about a fact, the visual token is
invalid.

> `72 + 4 + 72 + 4 + 72 = 224`

> `88 + 4 + 88 + 4 + 88 = 272`

> `224 + 8 + 152 = 384`

The cabinet and inspector therefore occupy approximately 59.6 percent and 40.4

|---|---:|

| Pinned status region | `152×48` |
| Pinned action dock | `152×56` |

The information region alone may expose genuine vertical overflow. The status
region and action dock never scroll away. No essential text scrolls
horizontally.

### 3.2 Drawer construction

Every drawer uses the same construction:

1. one controlled-dark chassis occupying the complete 72-by-88 drawer;
2. one Worn Cream paper face inset two native pixels from that chassis; and
3. one four-native-pixel filing lip inside the stable drawer composition.

The paper face carries the literal drawer identity, its one public primary state
line, and any lawful authored material. The chassis carries commitment and
outer functional geometry. No state changes the drawer's bounds, paper inset,
filing-lip allocation, text bands, hit target, or neighbour graph.

### 3.3 In-run desktop mounting

The in-run desktop mounts the 400-by-328 body in the established 400-pixel
computer workspace beneath the host-owned app strip. Backup uses the mode
cluster at the top, then the 8-pixel gap and the complete main region.

The separate Angela/presence field remains host-owned. Backup never overlaps,
recolours, frames, explains, or reacts to authored character or scene art.

### 3.4 In-run Pause mounting

Pause mounts the same opaque 400-by-328 Backup body in its accepted right
workfield. This is not a second Backup implementation. The left Pause pane
retains preview, entry, source-row, and restoration ownership.

Before entry, the right workfield has no active Backup focus or action. After
the accepted entry command, the same Backup body owns its normal active subtree.
Closing returns through the Pause owner's exact source anchor. Pause glass never
becomes Backup's functional substrate.

### 3.5 Title Log in mounting

The title host centres the same 400-by-328 body inside its 480-by-328 workfield.
The body begins at host-local x=40 and leaves exactly 40 native pixels of title
habitat on both sides.

Those side fields are inert negative space. They contain no seam, frame, label,
focus, Hover, Press, hit target, assistive node, status, art, placeholder, or
suggestion of a missing panel.

Title `Log in` has no mode cluster at all. The 384-by-272 main region moves to
y=8. The region from y=280 through the body bottom is a 48-pixel quiet lower
field, including the retained lower body perimeter. It contains no phantom tab,
mode-shaped gap, hidden control, rule, label, or assistive residue.

The 480-pixel title host strip remains host-owned. Only the Backup content body
is centred beneath it.

### 3.6 Text and target masters

The fixed topology survives the accepted 100, 125, and 150 percent text masters
and both 24- and 32-native-pixel target modes. Internal copy may wrap, and the
information region may scroll when measured content truly overflows. The body,
main split, cabinet topology, drawer size, drawer order, inspector width, and
pinned status/action regions do not reflow.

All native evidence uses the established 640-by-360 canvas at 1:1 native
pixels. The default 1280-by-720 presentation is exact 2x integer scaling.
Fractional placement, resampling, local zoom, shrink-to-fit, or target-mode
substitution is forbidden.

## 7. Functional states and visual-zone composition

### 7.1 Exact Focus geometry

Backup Focus never changes hit bounds, target size, drawer size, scroll
position, selection, or neighbour graph. From semantic body toward the
outermost edge, the detached double perimeter contains:

1. one native pixel of owning-substrate detachment;
2. one-native-pixel inner rail;
3. one native pixel of owning-substrate separation; and
4. one-native-pixel outer rail.

Unselected controlled-dark targets use `backup.focus_dark`. Selected Filed
targets use `backup.focus_filed`. A paper inspector custody or scroll target uses
`backup.focus_paper`. The owning component chooses the complete semantic role,
never either rail directly.

Mode controls instantiate `backup.mode.focus_dark` or
`backup.mode.focus_filed`. The active Busy custody target instantiates
`backup.status.focus_paper`. Drawer, action, mode, inspector, and status Focus
therefore always pass through an exact component token.

The perimeter draws into reserved same-substrate space or inward after one
protected row where host bounds require it. It never lands on authored pixels,
clips at a viewport or host edge, reduces the accepted target, or erases
Selected, risk, availability, or status evidence.

### 7.2 Hover and Press

Drawer Hover draws one one-native-pixel leading rule on the unchanged Worn Cream
paper face using `backup.state.hover.edge_paper`. It does not replace the paper
fill. Other exact dark, paper, or Filed targets use their matching Hover token.

Hover confirms only lawful pointer inspection. It never selects, focuses,
loads, saves, deletes, confirms, opens hidden help, previews a save, or changes
geometry.

Press replaces Hover during physical contact with the matching one-native-pixel
trailing or reversed seam. It does not move, scale, depress, fill, select,
commit, or claim a result. Disabled, Unavailable-for-the-required-command,
Busy-locked, inert, recovering, or covered actions cannot become Pressed.

### 7.3 Disabled and Unavailable

Disabled belongs to one action that currently cannot accept input. It remains
readable and uses one closed blocked-action edge plus only the literal reason
already owned by Backup. It never relies on opacity and never turns the drawer
container into a disabled no-op.

Unavailable belongs to the record capability. The drawer remains present,
occupied, labelled, selectable, focusable for inspection, and paired with the
separately legal action set. Its paper face uses exact public copy plus the
dashed boundary and stable hatch without obscuring copy, authored material,
Selected chassis, or Focus.

An Unavailable record may therefore own:

- an inspectable and Selected drawer container;
- a disabled Load action;
- an enabled confirmed Delete action; and
- in Save mode, the separately retained overwrite legality for Quick or
  numbered records.

Those are typed facts, not one dominant drawer skin.

### 7.4 Busy, temporarily inert, and custody

Busy appears only when a real accepted operation remains in progress across
visible frames. The pinned inspector status region shows the exact present-tense
fact already owned by Backup:

- `Saving…`;
- `Loading…`; or
- `Deleting…`.

It adds one static custody seam. There is no Busy copy for an operation that

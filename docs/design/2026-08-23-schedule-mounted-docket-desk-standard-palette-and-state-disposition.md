# Schedule Mounted Docket Desk Standard Palette and State Disposition

## 1. Accepted decision and objective

The project owner approved every recommended Schedule Standard design batch and
the complete consolidated design on 2026-08-23.

Schedule becomes one **Mounted Docket Desk**: a dark, maintained-instrument mat
holding two separate Worn-Cream documentary sheets. `Available` records the
authoritative current source projection, including any explicitly Unavailable
source. `Docket` preserves the exact sequence that has been drafted. Filled
entries are restrained index folios; unused capacity is quiet ruled paper. The
apparatus records availability, order, inspection, refusal, and custody without
explaining what any of them mean.

The private material shorthand is:

> Night holds the desk; the desk holds two papers; one records what may be
> drafted, and one preserves the order that was drafted.

That sentence is a design covenant, never player-facing copy, assistive copy,
metadata, a tooltip, or a localization key. Its phrase `may be drafted` names
the ledger's documentary category, not current actionability; an explicit
Unavailable fact remains completely truthful.

The selected direction translates the accepted Focused Docket Folio through
the Haunted Instrumentarium and current Standard grammar. It rejects a generic
calendar, a task planner, a soft card dashboard, a romance chooser, a horror
prop, a meaning-coded invitation board, and a single cream-dominant whole-app
sheet.

This disposition is a finishing visual-system layer. It does not redesign:

- Schedule eligibility, availability, costs, capacity, repetition, ordering,
  validation, execution, warning, Dark refusal, persistence, recovery, or
  routing behavior;
- invitation generation, reading, acceptance, missed-date consequences,
  Hospital, dating scenes, Minesweeper, or ending resolution;
- player agency, narrative choice, relationship logic, or result ownership;
  or
- shell geometry, Angela's panel, global chrome, Settings, localization
  authorship, save ownership, or the deferred anomaly and Observer systems.

It closes only:

- exact 100-percent native geometry for the two sheets and pinned dock;
- the Available source row, Docket occurrence, focused folio, command stack,
  Day-7 reduction, and warning-sheet anatomy;
- the Schedule-local projection of Standard materials and functional states;
- scrolling, focus, art, status, refusal, warning, and custody composition; and
- the first Schedule Standard native-pixel and real-locale proof family.

No visual affordance introduced here is a player-selectable narrative choice.
The things that act like choices remain the accepted Minesweeper results. The
Schedule UI exposes draftable evidence and accepts an ordered draft; it does
not supply an interpretive choice, recommendation, forecast, or ending label.

On 2026-08-24, the owner clarified that the follow-up approval remained
design-only. It did not request or authorize Godot implementation,
implementation-plan execution, Beads mutation, registered-asset integration,
test work, localization mutation, save or mechanics work, or a release claim.
No implementation plan was authored in that exchange. Any plan created later
is reference material only until the owner separately approves its exact
execution scope.

### 2.3 Narrow clarifications and supersessions

The following owner-approved Standard clarifications are exact:

1. **Two separate sheets.** `Available` and `Docket` are independent
   Worn-Cream documentary sheets mounted on a dark application mat. They are
   not regions of one cream card or one surrounding cream window.
2. **Stable native mounting.** At 100 percent, the Schedule-owned work area is
   the exact pair of sheets and dock in section 3. Text and target modes may
   create honest local overflow; they do not collapse, reorder, or swap the
   two-column topology.
3. **Docket split.** The Docket interior permanently reserves a 96-pixel order
   lane, a 6-pixel unprinted-paper gutter, and a 135-pixel focused-folio bay.
   The 6-pixel gutter is paper, not exposed dark mat.
4. **Day-7 topology.** Day 7 retains that 96-plus-6-plus-135 interior. It does
   not become a full-width single folio. Its compact chosen occurrence and
   large descriptive folio are two visual projections of one draft entry, not
   two entries or two focus targets.
5. **Selected ownership.** On Days 1-6, Selected belongs only to the inspected
   compact Docket occurrence. On Day 7, Selected belongs only to the chosen
   Available source. The large folio is never Selected.
6. **Two scroll owners.** Available owns only its source-list body beneath its
   pinned heading. Docket owns its complete interior composition as one
   vertical scroll owner. The focused bay does not create a third owner.
7. **Pinned dock.** The local status region and Done remain in the fixed dark
   dock outside both paper scroll owners. Idle status is literal absence, not
   an empty status component.
8. **Warning habitat.** Days 1-6 warnings use one fixed opaque Worn-Cream sheet
   above the dock. The surrounding desk remains visible, undimmed, and inert.
   Day 7 never instantiates this sheet.
9. **Unavailable remains evidence.** An unavailable source retains its true
   name and authored art and adds literal `Unavailable` plus exact morphology.
   It is not removed, dimmed, or assigned a reason.
10. **No synthetic operation.** An immediate command publishes immediately.
    It never shows `Saving...`, `Saved`, `Ready`, a spinner, progress, success,
    or any other false duration or evaluation.
11. **Unavailable traversal.** The current Standard narrows the older generic
    visible-source focus wording: an Unavailable source remains fully readable
    in visual and assistive reading order but leaves the action-focus graph and
    owns no Hover or Press. This changes no availability or command result.
12. **Current-v1 overlap.** The current Schedule projection never supplies
    Selected plus Unavailable on one source. In particular, a chosen Day-7
    source remains the Selected same-source no-change target rather than being
    restyled Unavailable. This changes no one-choice or admission law.

Native notation in this disposition is evidence-space notation. One native
pixel remains two logical pixels. It does not replace the retained logical
canvas or target contracts.

## 3. Exact native mounting and macro geometry

### 3.1 Coordinate contract

All rectangles in this section are 100-percent native-pixel rectangles in the
Schedule-local coordinate space already mounted by the shell. Coordinates use
the half-open form `(x, y, width, height)`. Edges are hard-aligned to native
pixels. No fractional placement, subpixel transform, scaling, or resampling is
permitted.

The accepted shell supplies an 800-by-656 logical Schedule body below its
global strip. Schedule retains the 16-logical-pixel inset and uses the
corresponding 384-by-312 native working rect from `(8,8)` through `(392,320)`.

> 125 Available + 6 exposed mat + 253 Docket = 384.

> 274 paper + 6 exposed mat + 32 dock = 312.

There is no outer Schedule window, title bar, title, close button, footer,
drop shadow, floating card, or additional app-local frame.

### 3.2 Sheet insets and inner rectangles

Each sheet has an exact 8-pixel native inset on all sides.

- Available inner rectangle: `(16,16,109,258)` in Schedule-local coordinates.
- Docket inner rectangle: `(147,16,237,258)` in Schedule-local coordinates.

The Docket inner width closes exactly as:

> 96 order lane + 6 unprinted-paper gutter + 135 focused-folio bay = 237.

The gutter is contiguous Worn Cream with no printed rule, dark cut, shadow,
bevel, or independent node. The Available/Docket macro gap is exposed mat; the
Docket interior gutter is paper. They must not be conflated in token mapping,
hit testing, accessibility, or screenshots.

### 3.3 Scaling contract

The native mount, sheet edges, macro gaps, and column order remain fixed across
100, 125, and 150 percent text and ordinary or Large Targets. Growth is
accommodated by:

- real text wrapping;
- row-height expansion;
- the two approved vertical scroll owners;
- reserved target and focus clearance; and
- decorative art yielding before mandatory text or controls.

No mode may create horizontal scrolling, shrink essential copy, ellipsize a
name or command, scale the app, overlap the dock, move Done, collapse the
focused bay, or turn Day 7 into a new topology.

### 3.4 Native target modes

The retained 48-logical-pixel and 64-logical-pixel target modes resolve to:

- ordinary target: 24 native pixels minimum; and
- Large Target: 32 native pixels minimum.

Visible faces may be smaller only where the retained semantic target includes
the required clear hit region. Every focusable Schedule target reserves four
native pixels beyond its semantic body for the detached Focus construction in
section 8.3. A nonsequential pointer subregion such as the registered grip does
not instantiate a second focus body. The exact fixture manifest records
semantic body, hit rectangle, and focus expansion separately.

### 5.2 Source order and row rhythm

Days 1-6 retain the authoritative source order:

1. Training;
2. Working;
3. Rest; and
4. accepted date sources in fixed invitation-generation order.

There is no category separator between ordinary actions and dates. Locale,
affordability, unavailability, focus, inspection, art availability, and
scroll position never sort or group sources.

Let `T` be 24 native pixels in ordinary target mode or 32 in Large Target mode.
Let `N_i` be the fully wrapped integer-native height of source name `i`, and
let `U_i` be the height of its localized `Unavailable` literal when present.
The source target has:

- ordinary available height `H_i = 8 + max(T,N_i)`;
- unavailable height `H_i = 8 + max(T,N_i+4+U_i)`;
- first sheet-local target origin `Y_1 = 40`; and
- recurrence `Y_(i+1) = Y_i + H_i + 4`.

The ordinary unwrapped result is 32 pixels and the Large result is 40 pixels.
Every target is sheet-local `B_i = (12,Y_i,101,H_i)`. Its detached Focus
rectangle is exactly `B_i` expanded by four pixels on every side, so the first
Focus top is the source-list lane top at `y=36`.

Rows are never distributed to fill the sheet height. Unused paper stays
unevenly and truthfully blank beneath the source list.

- one fixed 24-by-24 native art aperture beside the name; and
- any applicable functional-state morphology defined in section 8.

It contains no type, category, participant role, description, price, cost,
effect, formula, route, eligibility reason, consequence, relationship state,

The four pixels between the name measure and availability field remain bare
paper. The field contains the exact eight-pixel hatch aperture plus its
perimeter-owned trailing column. The art aperture is structural and fixed; it does not cause even
distribution, row stretching, or name truncation. The name wraps only in its
published measure. The literal status ends before the reserved gutter. Source
art and name remain visually associated at every text and target tuple.

### 5.4 Source interaction state

On Days 1-6, Available source rows never receive Selected. Activating a legal
source applies the authoritative draft command; it does not turn that source
into a selected option. Repeatable ordinary sources remain available according
to the retained rules. Nonrepeatable date or group sources project their
authoritative availability without inventing a local count.

An unavailable source remains readable at full-strength text and art. It is
nonfocusable and has no Hover or Press. It receives the exact unavailable
morphology in section 8.5 and literal localized `Unavailable`, with no reason,

### 5.5 Day-7 source reduction

Day 7 projects only eligible Priscilla, Lavinia, and Sylvia sources, in that
fixed order. An ineligible or unread person leaves no row, gap, explanation,
placeholder, faded portrait, locked entry, or assistive node.

If nobody is eligible, the Available sheet contains only its heading, bottom
rule, four-pixel bare gap, and bare paper. It does not substitute Training,
Working, Rest, a disabled friend, an empty-state sentence, or an ending label.

When a Day-7 source is chosen, that source alone receives Selected. The source
remains in its stable Available position; no sorting or transfer animation
occurs.

## 12. Lawful-absurdity guardrail

### 12.1 Object before explanation

Schedule presents paper, order, gaps, repeated acts, names, a refusal, a
warning edge, and an unchanged Docket as evidence. It does not explain their
meaning. The system remains administratively competent even when the content
that passes through it is impossible.

The accepted theoretical references function as private design tests, not as

### 12.2 McKee boundary

choice, relationship chooser, or outcome selection. Schedule orders the draft

choice-like agency named by the project.

### 12.3 Robbe-Grillet boundary

Placement, repetition, omission, material continuity, and the exact survival
of the Docket do the descriptive work. Empty Day-7 paper, the fixed order lane,

### 12.4 Barthes boundary

The apparatus supplies no authorial hierarchy of friends, recommended source,
preferred order, moral label, correct route, intended emotion, or explanatory
legend. Stable registry order is evidence and determinism, not value.

### 12.5 Fisher boundary

The university instrument treats unavailable invitations, impossible terminal
weight, a Dark refusal, and transaction failure with the same maintained
procedural exactness as Training or Rest. It does not announce horror through
glitch, corruption, occult imagery, dramatic colour, broken layout, or fake
technical behavior.

### 12.6 Design rejection test

A proposed Schedule detail fails this guardrail if it:

- tells the audience how to interpret an absence, repetition, friend, warning,
  or outcome;
- uses visual emphasis to recommend a source or disclose narrative value;
- makes the apparatus theatrically react to impossible content;
- adds a choice not produced by authoritative gameplay; or
- makes the interface lie about time, saving, availability, progress, or
  consequence for atmosphere.

# Narrative Style Manual Sections 1–4 Audit

## 1. Outcome and limitation

Sections 1–4 of the
[Narrative Style Manual](../../story/08-narrative-style-manual.md) survive one
bounded adversarial cycle after eight actionable wording repairs and one
explicitly bounded trade-off. The repairs clarify existing owner-confirmed
decisions; they do not approve exact plot, dialogue, staging, ending
eligibility, or implementation.

The owner has previously chosen not to use subagents or cross-model review for
this work. This pass is therefore a deliberately degraded same-context audit,
not an independent fresh-context verdict. To reduce confirmation bias, the
claim and source contract were fixed before the doubt pass, each objection was
classified against exact project text, and the stop condition is recorded
below. A future independent review may still find defects this pass shares with
the drafting context.

## 2. Claim under test

**Claim:** Sections 1–4 provide a coherent, actionable foundation for authority,
experiential style, the three observable surfaces, audience agency, evidence,
and perceptual anchoring without silently deciding open plot or code questions.

**Why it matters:** If this foundation is vague or internally inconsistent,
later plotting can invent a fourth surface, confuse inference with fact, assign
the wrong consequence to a board, leak knowledge across characters, minimize
harm in the name of subtlety, or turn *La Jalousie* into a free camera effect.

## 3. Review contract

The audit compared the manual against:

- the owner-confirmed authority, craft, surface, co-authorship, board-class,
  Group/Two-friends, and evidence decisions in the
  [working decision ledger](2026-09-02-narrative-constitution-working-decision-ledger.md);
- the retained physical-reality and perceptual laws in the
  [Core Story Bible](../../story/01-core-story-bible.md);
- the character-facing anchor and knowledge safeguards in the
  [Character & Relationship Handbook](../../story/02-character-relationship-handbook.md);
- the earlier
  [Narrative Constitution ablation](2026-09-02-narrative-constitution-ablation-pass-01.md),
  especially its seven-question gate and finding `ST-14`; and
- the owner-accepted primary-source adaptation in
  [*La Jalousie*: Unmarked Focal Presence](../research/2026-09-06-la-jalousie-unmarked-focal-presence-vn-adaptation.md).

This was a style-foundation audit, not the later raw-conversation audit of the
complete Priscilla–Lavinia bar-and-residue event. No plot claim from an
automatic conversation summary was promoted here.

## 4. Findings and reconciliation

### M14-01 — Authority order permitted convenient cherry-picking

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The manual correctly placed later
explicit approval above older documents, but did not say when a rule in a
working manual was usable. The repair requires an owner-confirmed or explicitly
retained upstream status, keeps open decisions open, and treats an unrepaired
older sentence as documented drift rather than an optional alternative.

### M14-02 — The North Star could become an adjective quota

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The effect list was accurate but could
pressure every scene to perform dreamlike, noir, sweet, dark, ridiculous, and
surreal qualities at once. The repair identifies it as a palette and requires
only locally concrete pressures.

`Dramatically earned` and `subtle` also carried avoidable moral ambiguity. The
repair defines earned as traceable character pressure, choice, and consequence,
never deserved victimization or pardon through attachment. Subtlety now limits
sensational emphasis, not the seriousness or duration of harm.

### M14-03 — Theory shorthand could masquerade as scholarship

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The named lenses are useful, but five
compressed bullets cannot summarize five bodies of work. The repair labels them
as project-specific translations. They remain private diagnostics, not author
impersonation, dialogue content, or a claim of scholarly completeness.

### M14-04 — Surface, passage, and carrier were conflated

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** “Exactly three surfaces” could be
misread as forbidding a passage boundary inside one dating scene, while the
approved Day 6 Group form requires an Angela-bound passage followed by a
Priscilla-bound pair-only tail. The repair distinguishes surface class,
passage, anchor, carrier, UI, and location. It also locates ending preludes,
pair or Sylvia layers, Observer codas, and postscripts inside the ending surface,
and treats Gallery or Rehearsal as replay rather than a new class. A lawful
boundary does not create a fourth surface.

### M14-05 — Inference was hidden inside “character knowledge”

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The former four-lane list called a
lawful inference knowledge and mentioned audience inference only afterward.
That blurred received claims, authenticated facts, beliefs, and conclusions.
The repair separates production truth, character-held evidence, character
inference, audience evidence, audience inference, and deliberately unresolved
cause or interpretation. It also records that unresolved canon may deliberately
have no single private answer.

### M14-06 — “Eligible dating-board results” was too broad

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** The phrase could collapse desktop,
solo-challenge, and pair-challenge Minesweeper. The repair names the only three
local response selectors: ordinary-message reply, canonical solo result, and
visible pair result. Desktop Minesweeper remains outside dating-response
selection, and no board chooses another woman's feelings.

### M14-07 — The anchor channel omitted three safeguards

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** “Bodily sensation” did not expressly
belong only to the anchor, and the manual omitted the Bible's distinction
between perceptual prose and separately authorized visual or audio evidence. It
also failed to say what happens when material is deliberately presented without
ownership by any consciousness. The repair assigns sensation and immediate
judgment to the active anchor, gives unowned material no character-bound prose,
preserves the external-channel distinction without granting character
knowledge, and disallows that allowance inside the bounded Day 6 unmarked
passage.

### M14-08 — The focal cutoff appeared to forbid the approved tail

**Classification: Valid + actionable.** “The passage ends when Angela's sight
and hearing end” was correct locally but could be read as ending the entire
dating scene. The repair ends only the Angela-bound passage, permits the already
authorized Priscilla-bound pair-only tail after a clear boundary, and preserves
the rule that Group and Angela-absent Two-friends forms may not subsume one
another.

### M14-09 — External visual/audio evidence remains a dangerous exception

**Classification: Valid trade-off.** A visual or audio channel that exceeds
what an anchor notices can drift toward an omniscient camera. The rule survives
because it is retained Bible law and can keep subtle evidence visible without
granting character knowledge. Its safeguards are now local and explicit: the
channel must already be authorized, cannot be restated as perception, cannot
grant knowledge, and is unavailable to the Day 6 unmarked construction.

## 5. Seven-question ablation result

| Test | Result after repair |
|---|---|
| Removal | Removing the three-surface law, stable anchor, or evidence separation reopens fourth-surface and knowledge-leak failures; each remains load-bearing. |
| Simplification | The six evidence lanes are prose-only distinctions, not six runtime states; merging them recreates the exact ambiguity repaired in M14-05. |
| Collision | No unresolved internal collision remains in Sections 1–4. Older Bible cutaway, abnormality-budget, romantic-asymmetry, and progression text remains separately logged drift. |
| Variation | The North Star is a palette, lenses are diagnostic, and unmarked focal presence is bounded to one current use; two scenes can obey the foundation without sharing a rhythm. |
| Evidence | Physical fact, received carrier, character conclusion, audience observation, audience conclusion, and deliberate non-resolution no longer masquerade as one another. |
| Maintenance | The repairs add no variables, branches, UI surfaces, assets, or implementation commitments. |
| Agency | Audience inputs select only registered authored continuations; Angela and the other women retain exact action, motive, desire, consent, and decision. |

## 6. Rules that survived pressure

The pass found no sufficient reason to remove:

- the authority split by responsibility, with latest explicit approval first;
- exactly three current observable surface classes;
- constrained audience–Angela co-authorship;
- truthful admitted input under `trust the hand; question the trace`;
- dialogue-led evidence and sparse, anchor-bound perceptual prose;
- one stable writer-side anchor per continuous passage;
- audience-facing non-certification of the Day 6 Group anchor; or
- the ban on head-hopping, invisible observers, and omniscient explanation.

## 7. Stop condition

After reconciliation and a final anchor-channel reread, no further
high-confidence defect whose repair belongs inside Sections 1–4 remained. Remaining
issues are either already logged distributed Bible drift, exact Day 1–7 plot
questions, final Day 6 staging, or material owned by later manual sections.
Under the project's bounded-ablation rule, this gate therefore stops after one
cycle. It does not convert the whole manual from working draft to final
authority.

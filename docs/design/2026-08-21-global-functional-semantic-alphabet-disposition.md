# Global Functional Semantic Alphabet Disposition

## 1. Accepted direction

The project owner selected **A: Filed Semantic Ledger** on 2026-08-21.

The interface uses one restrained, institution-wide functional alphabet. Every
authored theme and colour-differentiation tuple may change the colour assigned
to a semantic role, but the player's learned evidence for that role remains
stable through outline, edge, fill, glyph, label, line, shape, or pattern.
Individual applications do not invent conflicting meanings for the same state.

This disposition locks the global role grammar. It does not approve final raw
hexadecimal values, complete any of the sixteen theme/accessibility tuples, add
gameplay features, or authorize Godot implementation.

The primary approved visual evidence is:

`.superpowers/brainstorm/581-1787233443/content/global-functional-semantic-alphabet-directions-v9.html`

Companion HTML is visual evidence rather than runtime or design authority, so
every binding decision is restated below. Earlier exploratory versions and the
rejected alternatives do not override this disposition. In particular,
`global-functional-semantic-alphabet-directions-v8.html` contains truncation
damage and is invalid evidence.

## 2. Narrow authority boundary

This disposition decides how already-owned functional facts are visibly
encoded. It does not decide when a state exists, what that state means, or what
it does.

The retained application authorities continue to own narrative facts,
mechanics, screen topology, labels, input and focus order, persistence,
recovery, failure behavior, localization, assistive semantics, and gameplay
consequences. Within visual encoding only, this newer global grammar wins a
conflict with exploratory app-specific semantic colour or morphology.

It does not reopen the Settings anatomy, shell composition, Contacts topology
or avatarless direction, Schedule behavior, or Minesweeper board topology and
rules.

## 3. Locked governance law

Each effective functional theme is one complete, explicitly authored semantic
record. The required matrix remains:

> 2 base palettes × 2 contrast states × 4 colour-differentiation presets = 16
> authored tuples.

The runtime may select a validated tuple; it may not manufacture one by stacking
filters, applying a global recolour shader, or silently substituting a nearby
tuple. Components consume semantic roles rather than introducing local raw
colours.

Across all sixteen tuples:

- colour may accelerate recognition but never owns meaning alone;
- simultaneous states remain simultaneously legible;
- selection never becomes focus;
- warning, danger, and destructive action remain distinct;
- unavailable content remains readable and is never represented by opacity
  alone; and
- friend identity never becomes relationship progress, route, mood, unread,
  warning, or anomaly evidence.

## 4. Primitive extension law

The ten Haunted Instrumentarium primitives remain the palette's named emotional
anchors. They are not a closed implementation swatch box.

Where the anchors cannot provide sufficient contrast, separation, or
simultaneous-state legibility across the approved substrates, the project may
register a small, governed family of calibrated functional derivatives. Every
such derivative must:

- be declared once at the primitive layer before any semantic or component use;
- use a material or value-family identity rather than a route, character,
  ending, mood, or component-specific meaning;
- exist for a demonstrated pairing, contrast, or collision need rather than
  decorative variety;
- remain shared wherever the same calibrated value is suitable, instead of
  creating a separate derivative bank for each application;
- preserve the established palette hierarchy and the identity of all ten
  original anchors; and
- pass the same contrast, collision, colour-differentiation, and native-pixel
  proofs as the complete tuples that consume it.

The binding dependency direction is:

> registered primitive → semantic role → component token.

Components never contain an undeclared raw hexadecimal value and never reach
past semantic roles to choose a primitive directly. A semantic role may map to
different registered primitives in different complete tuples; its meaning and
non-colour morphology do not change.

This policy accepts the right to register necessary derivatives, not any
particular candidate derivative. All additional values shown in companion
evidence remain unapproved until the relevant tuple cohort is calibrated and
accepted.

## 5. Internal orthogonal state-composition contract

**Orthogonal Fact Ledger** is a private design-system term. It is not a literal
player-facing ledger, application, screen, menu, statistic, explanation,
diagnostic overlay, or story fact. The audience sees only the ordinary visual
and assistive results of truthful state composition.

A projected component may carry independent facts on separate axes:

1. **projection:** present or absent;
2. **content or occupancy:** app-owned states such as Empty, readable, filled,
   or fallback-loadable;
3. **actionability:** enabled, disabled, or unavailable;
4. **commitment or location:** selected or current;
5. **navigation:** focused, hovered, or pressed;
6. **risk:** warning, danger, or destructive action;
7. **execution custody:** idle, busy, temporarily inert, recovering, or
   terminal-locked where an accepted app authority owns that fact; and
8. **public metadata:** identity, unread, and other explicitly disclosed
   app-owned facts.

No dominant visual state silently erases a compatible fact on another axis.
The accepted simultaneous-state evidence therefore includes:

- an Empty Backup drawer that remains selected;
- launcher Home that remains current while disabled;
- one control that is selected and focused;
- one Contacts row that retains identity, unread, selection, and focus; and
- a focused action that retains its truthful warning or destructive scope.

An absent component cannot be selected or focused because it does not project.
A component cannot be pressed while unavailable, and mutually exclusive values
on the same axis cannot coexist. Application authorities continue to decide
their exact focusability, action legality, visible copy, and domain-specific
combinations. A later global overlap matrix records those retained laws; it
does not invent new behavior.

This contract affects design tokens, composition, assistive projection, and
verification only. It adds no player command, mechanic, state, knowledge, or
interface surface.

## 6. Locked invariant morphology

The following functional evidence survives every palette, contrast state,
colour-differentiation preset, and host:

- **Selected:** a committed fill plus a stable filing seam.
- **Focus:** a detached double outline, visibly separate from selection and
  never clipped by the focused control.
- **Warning:** a literal warning glyph and one governing edge rule.
- **Danger:** a danger glyph and a double rule distinct from warning.
- **Destructive action:** an explicit destructive verb, a filled action key,
  and the accepted safe confirmation flow.
- **Unavailable:** a literal `Unavailable` label, dashed boundary, and stable
  hatch or equivalent pattern; never mere dimming.

If selected and focused coincide, both the selected fill/seam and detached focus
outline remain visible. Press, hover, busy, disabled, error, success,
information, and unread states retain their accepted product behavior and must
be completed under the same redundant-evidence principle during tuple
authorship. This disposition does not invent morphology for states the approved
companion did not fully resolve.

## 7. Identity law

Priscilla, Lavinia, and Sylvia each retain:

- their literal localized name;
- a stable, distinguishable non-colour rail morphology; and
- an authored identity colour where the effective tuple can support it without
  semantic collision.

The current morphology direction is solid, notched, and twin evidence
respectively. Exact pixel geometry and final colours remain part of the tuple
and component proof. High Contrast may collapse identity colours to the active
foreground while the names and rail morphologies continue to distinguish all
three friends.

## 8. Cross-application custody

Settings is the calibration laboratory, not the sole owner of the alphabet.
Contacts, Schedule, Minesweeper, Backup, Gallery, narrative hosts, and shared
chrome consume the same global semantic meanings.

- Schedule may retain domain-specific action, date, inspection, and ordering
  marks, but focus, warning, danger, destructive, and unavailable evidence use
  the global grammar.
- Minesweeper may retain digits, flag silhouettes, forced-cell brackets, and
  exploded-cell marks, but focus and shared status meanings use the global
  grammar.
- App-specific material dialects may alter substrate and composition; they may
  not redefine shared state semantics.

This adds no application, command, state, statistic, or player-facing feature.

## 9. Recolouring boundary

Functional controls and overlays use the selected semantic tuple. Authored art
pixels, portraits, narrative tints, item art, backgrounds, environmental art,
and decorative ink or transfer material are outside functional recolouring.

Decorative material never becomes the only evidence of a functional state. A
functional overlay placed over art still owns its required contrast, shape,
label, and focus evidence without modifying the art beneath it.

## 10. Deliberately pending work

Approval of Filed Semantic Ledger does not lock:

- any candidate hexadecimal value shown in the companion;
- the final After-Hours or Midnight surface mappings;
- High Contrast, Protan, Deutan, or Tritan role assignments;
- information, success, unread, busy, or application-specific state details
  not fully resolved by the approved comparison;
- the complete legal-overlap matrix for every app-owned state;
- final component pixel geometry, typography, sound, animation, or artwork; or
- Godot scenes, resources, scripts, shaders, tests, assets, imports, or runtime
  configuration.

Before any tuple is called production-ready, all sixteen complete records must
pass automated contrast checks, colour-collision analysis, colour-vision
simulation used only as a design aid, and native-pixel proofs for representative
shared, Schedule, and Minesweeper states. Those checks support design review;
they do not by themselves establish a public accessibility-conformance claim.

## 11. Reopening rule

**B: Bone-and-Carbon Proof** and **C: App Custody Inks** are rejected as equal
candidates. They remain historical comparison evidence only. Reopening the
global alphabet requires a new explicit owner decision. Until then, Filed
Semantic Ledger wins conflicts with exploratory companion styling while all
retained product, truth, input, and accessibility authorities continue to
govern behavior.

# Priscilla–Lavinia Packed Room Ablation

> **Superseded working path (2026-09-05):** The owner replaced irregular
> cohabitation with an access-without-residence premise. This file remains an
> audit history for reusable constraints; its move-out carrier, room model, and
> residence-dependent actions are no longer forward plot authority.

## 1. Outcome and authority

This record tests the current Packed Room ending audition after its first
cluster of owner-confirmed constraints and actions. It does not approve the
Packed Room as the Priscilla–Lavinia ending carrier, fix finished dialogue, or
authorize implementation.

The owner previously requested no subagent or cross-model review. This is
therefore a same-context adversarial pass rather than a fresh-context review;
that limitation is recorded rather than disguised. The owner's standing choice
also skips another cross-model offer for this cycle.

The candidate survives removal and differentiation pressure, but it is not yet
ready for carrier approval. The inherited duplicate-key Persistence collision
is resolved at architecture level, and Priscilla now has an owner-confirmed
independent motive plus a late-vacancy student-residence destination envelope.
The exact housing form, calendar placement, finite tether, and physical
logistics remain unresolved.

## 2. Claim under test

**Claim:** One genuine, practicable move from the former shared flat can carry
four substantively different Priscilla–Lavinia resolutions without turning
Ambiguous/Love or Sweet/Dark into a moral or romantic ladder.

**Why it matters:** Selecting the carrier will determine which necessities and
residues must be backchained into the two counted pair encounters and their
messages. A weak carrier would make six days of plot serve a finale that either
recolours one outcome or contradicts a later ending layer.

## 3. Artifact under test

The shared premise is that Priscilla independently intends and can complete a
move from her bedroom in the family-provided two-bedroom flat. She has a usable
destination and operational arrangements. The move is not a bluff, ultimatum,
disappearance, rescue demand, or test of Lavinia.

- **Ambiguous + Sweet:** the move completes. Lavinia gives a bounded invitation
  to dinner at the former shared flat. Priscilla accepts and later arrives;
  Lavinia opens the door and admits her as a guest. Neither defines the meeting.
- **Love + Sweet:** the move completes. After every moving task ends, Priscilla
  plainly invites Lavinia to the new home. She sends the address only after
  Lavinia accepts; the visit grants no key or continuing access.
- **Ambiguous + Dark:** the move completes, but both knowingly leave one finite,
  genuinely wanted and nonurgent category of Priscilla's belongings behind,
  manufacturing a deniable future reason for contact.
- **Love + Dark:** before execution, Lavinia explicitly asks Priscilla to cancel
  the usable destination. After making the loss of exit legible, Priscilla
  knowingly cancels it herself. The boxes remain packed.

## 4. Contract

The audition must satisfy all of the following:

- one shared terminal situation supports four material resolutions;
- Ambiguous/Love governs mutual legibility rather than amount of passion;
- Sweet/Dark governs interruption or knowing reinforcement of destructive
  anticipation rather than virtue or truth;
- both women author consequential conduct and retain the applicable capacity to
  refuse;
- no injury, bodily crisis, institutional task, Angela decision, or audience
  operation makes them remain;
- every form settles visible conduct and a material consequence through an
  ending surface;
- Priscilla's independent move remains genuine before Lavinia responds;
- the misaligned-anticipation lens does not become one repeated prop trick;
- the pair layer composes after any qualified Angela or Alone core without
  cancelling or interpreting it; and
- physical housing, scheduling, and service claims are verified before premise
  approval.

## 5. Adversarial findings and reconciliation

### PLR-01 — Duplicate-key Persistence collision

**Classification: Valid + actionable; resolved at architecture level.** The
inherited coda has Priscilla
return a familiar key that Lavinia says was already returned, after which an
identical key is visible. A return is locally natural after the three
move-complete forms but lacks a clean motive after Love + Dark preserves
residence. Restricting Observer to Sweet would restore a privilege the newer
Constitution deliberately supersedes; forcing a pointless key return into the
Love + Dark form would make characters serve the anomaly.

The base Packed Room forms correctly avoid using a key. The owner approved
form-aware staging of one semantic contradiction: completed-move forms may use
incompatible return history, while Love + Dark requires a locally motivated
discovery of duplicate custody without pretending Priscilla has left. Every
variant must preserve materially identical keys and incompatible custody
histories without changing the gate, relationship result, or unresolved cause.
The owner selected one Persistence and Gallery identity with exactly two
structural staging classes: move completed, or move cancelled/residence
retained. Quadrant-specific reactions are optional residue, never four new
codas. Exact staging and the Observer gate remain open.

### PLR-02 — A genuine move still needs an independent motive

**Classification: Valid + actionable; motive and destination envelope resolved,
timetable open.** “Not a test” prevented one false motive but did not explain
why Priscilla wanted a separate residence. The owner has now fixed that motive
inside the audition:
Priscilla refuses to keep her residence's family-facing legitimacy and staged
domestic continuity dependent on Lavinia's voluntary silence. She wants an
address she can truthfully describe without using Lavinia as evidence.

This choice remains morally mixed rather than completing a growth lesson.
Leaving ends one selective-truth performance, but it also pre-empts Lavinia's
power to decide when the family account collapses and restores Priscilla's sole
authorship over its timing. The move therefore survives Lavinia's total
nonresponse without becoming flight, punishment, generosity, or a covert test.
An external deadline may constrain execution but may not manufacture the
desire. Her usable destination now comes from a late-vacancy student-residence
opportunity for which she applied before Day 1. Whether that residence is a
conventional hall, university-administered off-campus flat, partnered private
residence, or another evidenced form remains open alongside its tenure, cost,
family explanation, and credible timetable.

### PLR-03 — Sweet differentiation survives removal pressure

**Classification: Valid trade-off; retained inside the audition.** Removing the
enacted Ambiguous dinner leaves only an untested promise. Moving that dinner to
Priscilla's new home collapses its spatial consequence into Love + Sweet.
Removing Priscilla's explicit post-move invitation from Love + Sweet reduces
Love to a warmer instance of Lavinia's invitation. The present contrast—one
bounded return to familiar space versus explicitly offered access to a new
independent space—therefore performs distinct work.

The former flat still risks reading as restored cohabitation. The confirmed
guest entrance, absence of luggage, omitted bedroom, and lack of standing
access are load-bearing safeguards rather than decorative staging.

### PLR-04 — The finite Dark tether can be too weak or too symbolic

**Classification: Valid + actionable; exact carrier open.** If the retained
belongings are disposable, no real future reason exists. If they are medically,
legally, or urgently necessary, the act becomes a crisis or coercive trap. If
they are sentimental shorthand, the object announces the theme. The later
selection must be ordinary, finitely enumerable, genuinely wanted, nonurgent,
and capable of later retrieval through more than one practical arrangement.

### PLR-05 — Love + Dark can masquerade as the greatest romance

**Classification: Valid trade-off.** Cancelling a home is the largest sacrifice
in the matrix and can make Dark look more passionate or canonically romantic
than Sweet. Retain it only with the confirmed safeguards: no deception, forced
cancellation, emergency, celebratory unpacking, destiny language, or claim that
cohabitation authenticates love. Priscilla's direct invitation in Love + Sweet
must remain equally legible as exceptional desire even though it preserves
exit.

### PLR-06 — The four branches currently end at different times

**Classification: Valid + actionable; open.** Love + Dark concludes before the
move, Ambiguous + Dark can conclude during its execution, and both Sweet forms
require post-move contact. This variation is not itself a defect, but the final
ending plot must establish a common opening and a comprehensible interval for
each consequence. It must also state whether the pair layer occurs later on Day
7 or projects beyond the seven-day calendar after the preceding core.

### PLR-07 — Agency and character specificity presently survive

**Classification: No defect found in the current envelope.** Priscilla authors
the genuine move in every form and performs every alteration to it herself.
Lavinia asks rather than covertly operating Priscilla's housing. Their actions
remain grounded in Priscilla's control of arrangements and Lavinia's demand for
evidence. Later dialogue can still break this result and must be audited again.

### PLR-08 — Housing and moving plausibility is not fully established

**Classification: Valid + actionable; room model selected, finance, contract,
and timetable open.** The retained Bibles establish the family-provided
two-bedroom flat,
permission, separate bedrooms, irregular cohabitation, and estrangement. Current
Hong Kong university sources establish that student housing can include
university-administered off-campus flat-type residences, partnered privately
operated co-living accommodation, and self-arranged private renting. They also
show that off-campus does not automatically mean private: real schemes commonly
use double or larger shared rooms, while some administered flat-type residences
provide single rooms. Relevant examples include [PolyU off-campus
housing](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/sao/student-resources-and-support-section/residential-life/university-managed-accommodations/off-campus-housing/),
[HKUST Sunny House](https://shrl.hkust.edu.hk/off-campus-housing/test), and
[HKU Ching Lin Terrace Residence](https://housing.cedars.hku.hk/university-accommodation/residence-options/ching-lin-terrace-residence).

This evidence supports the approved envelope, not a specific East Harbour
policy. The owner has selected a fictional but physically possible explicit
policy: a university-arranged, privately operated off-campus residence lets
Priscilla rent an entire twin room for sole registered occupancy at the
full-room or two-bed rate. She does not reserve an otherwise assignable
university bedspace. This selection fixes room occupancy but not her lawful
source of funds, the affordability sacrifice, eligibility, offer and check-in
dates, payment period, notice or refund rule, visitor and overnight boundary,
collection service, or credible seven-day timetable. The model does not pass
final plausibility review until those linked claims are checked as a whole.

# Shop Maintained Campus Stores Standard Palette and State Disposition

## 1. Accepted decision and objective

The project owner approved the complete recommended Shop Standard design
batches, including the final removal of the redundant Shop-local title and
balance header, on 2026-08-23.

Shop becomes one **Maintained Campus Stores** counter: a competent dark service
instrument holding pale laminate specimen cards, literal object apertures, one
inspector, and a restrained transaction dock. Coffee, wine, bandages, a plush,
a Debug Key, keepsakes, and one blank capacity receive the same sincere
administrative treatment. The catalog never comments on that coexistence.

The private material shorthand is:

> The counter records the object, the price, and the present transaction fact;
> it does nothing else to them.

This disposition is a finishing visual-system layer. It does not redesign the
catalog, economy, item effects, Supportz eligibility, transaction order,
condition routing, save data, story, or audience agency. It creates no choice,
hint, anomaly, fictional fault, extra status, object provenance, speaker
identity, or public explanation. Minesweeper results remain the sole
choice-like narrative agency under their own authority.

It closes only:

- the exact headerless Shop plate and native geometry;
- the After-Hours Standard and Midnight Standard material mappings consumed by
  Shop;
- component bindings for cards, inspector, dock, paging, confirmations, and
  Shop-owned operational states;
- the visual and semantic boundary between Supportz absence and missing art;
- immutable specimen-art placement; and
- the first Shop Standard native-proof family.

It authorizes no Godot implementation, asset production, localization writing,
High Contrast or colour-vision tuple, audio work, task creation, or release
claim.

### 2.2 Exact owner-approved Standard supersessions

Within Shop presentation only, this disposition supersedes these older details:

1. The Shop-local header is removed completely. Its duplicate title, money
   balance, Minesweeper-coin balance, rectangle, following gap, seam, focus
   stop, hit target, assistive node, and cache state have zero presence. The
   shared app strip is the sole Shop-title projection. Angela's persistent HUD
   is the sole ordinary desktop projection of current money and coins.
2. Older references to refreshed Shop balances mean truthful HUD refresh after
   commit. Balance value, ownership, timing, and assistive publication do not
   move into Shop.
3. “Below the header” now means directly inside the Shop plate's retained
   eight-native-pixel inset. “Phone-like app” authorizes no second title bar,
   floating window, local frame, close control, or duplicated chrome.
4. The former approximate 144-by-156 logical card and remainder-distribution
   allowance become one exact 72-by-88 native card, equivalent to 144-by-176
   logical pixels. Every page uses identical rectangles and gaps.
5. Whole-paper Hover tone becomes one leading contact rule on an unchanged
   laminate face. The old charcoal double inset for Selection becomes the
   global Selected commitment chassis and filing seam. Focus remains the
   detached double outline. Press uses the shared reversed contact seam.
6. An ineligible or exhausted Supportz slot is no longer described as inert.
   It is nonsemantic blank capacity. “Temporarily inert” is reserved for a real
   execution-custody fact.
7. The damaged cached-view sentence is completed as: “This is presentation
   state. It cannot affect purchase eligibility or canonical run state.”
8. The older phrase that texture stays outside hit boundaries is read as an
   exclusion from text, controls, visible target-edge evidence, and
   Focus/Selected clearance. The whole ordinary card remains its one hit
   target.
9. Previous and Next remain fixed in the page rail. A direction with no target
   page is a Disabled, nonfocusable action rather than an absent or apparently
   enabled action. The page indicator remains a noninteractive fact.
10. MIN and Minus are Disabled at quantity one; Plus and MAX are Disabled at
    the current legal maximum; and all four are Disabled when that maximum is
    zero. A single product or sold-out product still instantiates no irrelevant
    quantity or purchase controls.
11. A refusal caused by inspector Buy remains in the inspector dock. A refusal
    caused by a confirmation-sheet Yes remains in that exact foreground sheet,
    so Supportz evidence can never appear attached to an unrelated ordinary
    item's inspector.
12. A confirmation sheet remains the exclusive foreground owner while its Yes
    command is genuinely unresolved. Direct Buy uses the selected card as its
    retained read-only anchor; a confirmation-originated command never moves
    active Focus behind its still-visible sheet.

The disposable material-study prototype is design history, not Standard proof.
Its scaled canvas, nonproduction fonts, raw colours, green availability copy,
and `BUY ×1` substitution do not survive. The authoritative action string
remains localized `Buy xN`.

No other behavior, art, state, palette, copy, command, persistence, or
implementation authority changes.

An ordinary double-click confirmation or Supportz confirmation replaces the
complete inspector rectangle `(240,8,152,312)` with one trusted foreground

truthful beneath foreground custody.

requires it; the action row never moves.

### 3.5 Control hit and face geometry

Every action rectangle in section 3 is its semantic hit rectangle, not an
undersized visible key. A target whose shortest dimension is 24 pixels insets
its controlled-dark face three pixels on every side. A target whose shortest
dimension is 32 pixels insets that face four pixels on every side. The detached
one-pixel Focus rails sit one and three pixels outside the face, with the
offset-two substrate left unpainted; all three pixels remain inside the full hit
rectangle. Hover and Press attach to the face, while the hit rectangle and
label never move.

This rule covers Previous, Next, MIN, Minus, Plus, MAX, Buy, No, and Yes in
ordinary and Large Targets. It preserves every 24- or 32-pixel minimum target,
all declared inter-target gaps, and the confirmation sheet boundary. The card
Focus geometry in section 6.1 is the deliberate outside-target exception.

remain authored object pixels outside functional recolouring.

### 4.2 Component material hierarchy

The plate perimeter uses habitat. The catalog bed, gutter, page rail, card
chassis, transaction dock, and action keys use controlled-dark instrument
material. Each ordinary card uses one pale laminate face inset two pixels into
its chassis and one completely enclosed Worn Cream object aperture. The
inspector repeats pale laminate at larger scale; its dock returns to controlled
dark. Trusted confirmation uses Worn Cream with contextual-dark ink and
controlled-dark action keys.

This is a maintained service counter, not a glass vending bay or an archival
leaf. It adds no decorative serial, provenance, offset paper, torn edge,
performed damage, effect colour, rarity treatment, or marketing emphasis.

Buy, quantity actions, Previous, Next, and `1 / 2` receive no permanent accent
or commitment plane. Fixed position and literal text establish their function.
Selected cards alone consume Filed Verdigris.

### 4.4 Flat first masters

The first Shop Standard masters use flat fills and hard raster rules. Optional
grain, soot, registration wear, laminate noise, paper fibres, and runtime
randomness are absent. Authored object dithering remains confined to object
pixels and is not surface texture.

Later material work may introduce deterministic static wear only after the flat
masters pass. It must remain outside text, prices, controls, state marks,
target-edge evidence, object exclusion masks, and Focus/Selected clearance. It
cannot vary with item, price, availability, eligibility, purchase history,
theme, route, relationship, anomaly, or random seed.

## 5. Card, inspector, and object-art anatomy

### 5.1 Ordinary card

An ordinary 72-by-88 card is one selectable inspection target. All rectangles
in this subsection are card-relative. Its complete controlled-dark chassis
contains a laminate face `(2,2,68,84)` and protected content field
`(3,3,66,82)`. The fixed Worn Cream object aperture is `(21,3,30,30)`; its
one-pixel structural perimeter encloses the immutable 28-by-28 card export at
`(22,4,28,28)`.

Content remains in this vertical order:

1. centred literal object aperture and art;
2. complete localized generic name, wrapping to at most two lines;
3. exact literal unit price and currency; and
4. literal `Available` or `Sold out`.

At 100, 125, and 150 percent text, Fusion Pixel uses native 8-, 10-, and
12-pixel masters. The object export remains the same 28 native pixels at every
master. Text and state rectangles close exactly as follows:

| Text master | name, at most two lines | unit price | availability | state rail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100% | `(4,37,64,16)` | `(4,57,64,8)` | `(4,69,60,8)` | `(4,84,64,1)` |
| 125% | `(4,36,64,20)` | `(4,59,64,10)` | `(4,72,60,10)` | `(4,84,64,1)` |
| 150% | `(4,34,64,24)` | `(4,59,64,12)` | `(4,72,60,12)` | `(4,84,64,1)` |

The coordinates, including their deliberate blank rows, replace all automatic
gap distribution. The open state rail is present on every ordinary card. Sold
out closes its trailing end with `(67,80,1,5)`; the availability band reserves
the final four horizontal pixels for that mark. Neither rail nor cap enters the
object aperture.

Names, price, currency, and availability never clip, ellipsize, abbreviate,
scroll horizontally, become art, or shrink below the active master. The real
locale proof gate may reject this provisional mapping; runtime must not invent a
fallback layout that violates it.

### 5.2 Inspector

The inspector presents the selected ordinary item as:

1. its larger literal object export;
2. its complete localized generic name;
3. one neutral description of physical form only;
4. pinned exact unit price and currency; and
5. pinned `Available` or `Sold out`.

Art, name, and description share the sole vertical scroll region. Selecting a
different item resets that region to the top. Hiding and reopening the unchanged
same-day cached selection may restore its scroll position. Price, availability,
quantity, total, refusal, and Buy never scroll away.

Coordinates here are relative to the 152-pixel-wide scroll-document origin,
not the viewport's Shop-plate position. The fixed Worn Cream aperture is
`(44,8,64,64)` and encloses the immutable 56-by-56 inspector export at
`(48,12,56,56)`. The complete localized name begins in `(8,80,136,H_name)`;
`H_name` is its integer wrapped height at the active registered master. The
description begins four pixels after that band and uses the same 136-pixel
measure. Only document height varies. Aperture, art rectangle, horizontal
insets, and relative order remain identical across text master, target mode,
theme, state, and locale.

### 5.3 Object-art protection

Exactly seventeen ordinary items own object art. Each uses one canonical source
with two purpose-authored exports:

- one 28-by-28 native card export; and
- one 56-by-56 native inspector export.

The exports preserve one pose and silhouette. The larger export may add literal
construction detail only. Neither export is produced by runtime scaling.

Within each purpose-authored export, object pixels remain unchanged across
theme, text master, target mode, locale, Selected, Focus, Hover, Press,
availability, and Sold-out. The card and inspector exports are not required to
be byte-equal to each other because their approved sizes and literal detail
differ. Neither export is scaled, cropped, padded, regenerated, or exchanged to
fit a text master. No tint, opacity, filter, mask, palette remap, shader,
antialiasing, or mipmap changes either export. Functional marks compose outside
its declared artwork exclusion mask.

Object art owns no independent focus or assistive node. The owning card or
inspector exposes the approved operational facts. If a primary export is
missing, the retained art resolver may use only a manifest-declared same-item
neutral render with its own approved 28-by-28 and 56-by-56 exports. If neither
lawful same-item pair exists, Shop enters trusted technical recovery. Missing
art never creates a blank card, wrong object, generic silhouette, or
broken-image badge.

The fixed sizes, one-pose-and-silhouette relation, permitted larger-export
detail, no-runtime-transform law, exclusion-mask law, and cross-presentation
invariance above apply independently to a resolved neutral fallback pair. A
fallback is not a relaxed asset class.

Pixel-equality evidence pins one resolved asset/export ID. It compares that
same resolution across presentation states; it never requires a primary export
and its lawful same-item neutral fallback to be byte-equal to each other.

## 6. Functional states and transaction truth

### 6.1 Card state composition

Selected, Focus, Hover, Press, availability, and actionability are independent
facts:

- **Selected** fills the card chassis with Filed Verdigris and adds the stable
  two-pixel filing seam `(0,86,72,2)`. The laminate face, copy, aperture, and
  art remain unchanged.
- **Focus** uses one-pixel concentric rails at one and three pixels outside the
  card rectangle; the offset-two pixels remain an unpainted substrate gap. Both
  unselected and Selected cards use the dark Focus pair because those rails
  rest on the controlled-dark catalog bed. This three-pixel footprint fits the
  fixed four-pixel card gaps without touching another card. Selected and Focus
  remain simultaneously visible.
- **Hover** uses one one-native-pixel leading contact rule on the unchanged
  face at `(2,2,1,84)`. It does not select, focus, reveal, or purchase.
- **Press** replaces Hover's shared contact carrier with the reversed inward
  trailing seam `(69,2,1,84)`. It causes no movement and proves no outcome.
- **Sold out** retains the literal label, selectable card, readable copy, and
  unaltered art. The exact closed end-cap in section 5.1 supplies non-colour
  redundancy. Purchase controls are absent.

`Available` and `Sold out` use contrast-safe contextual ink. They do not borrow
Success, Error, Warning, mint, warm colour, opacity, or animation.

### 6.2 Quantity and action states

For a batchable product:

- MIN and Minus are Disabled at quantity one;
- Plus and MAX are Disabled at the current legal maximum;
- all quantity actions are Disabled when the legal maximum is zero; and
- Buy remains enabled for an otherwise Available product when funds are
  insufficient, so the accepted literal refusal can publish.

Disabled actions retain readable labels and the shared blocked-action edge.
They cannot Hover as an inspection promise, Press, or admit input. Single items
instantiate no quantity targets. Sold-out items instantiate no Buy target.

### 6.3 Expected refusal and success

The inspector total/refusal band always retains the exact total for an
available selected item. Its reserved second line may contain exactly one
current direct-Buy refusal:

- `Insufficient funds`; or
- `Unavailable`.

This line is a quiet Shop-local Information attachment: primary dark-surface
copy plus one registration boundary. It is not Error, Warning, generic
Unavailable morphology, a toast, or a focus target. Selection or quantity
change clears it. Page change necessarily changes/restores selection and clears
it. A new request replaces it. It is not cached or saved, so hide, Load, New
Run, and logical-day reconstruction cannot restore it.

Direct Buy refusal leaves Focus on Buy when that target remains lawful and
moves Focus to the selected card when revalidation removes Buy.

Each confirmation factual body reserves one otherwise blank line for the same
two literal refusals. A Yes request clears its prior line. Expected refusal
keeps the sheet open, publishes the exact line there, and returns Focus to Yes
when that command remains admissible or No when it does not. The refusal is
exposed by the same sheet's assistive subtree. It never appears in the ordinary
inspector dock, on the blank card, or as a Shop-wide status. A new Yes request
replaces it; No, Back, or Close clears it with the sheet.

A successful purchase creates no Shop Success status, toast, receipt screen,
effect copy, or celebration. Refreshed HUD balance, availability, quantity
bounds, condition presentation, and any permitted keepsake projection are the
complete evidence.

### 6.4 Neutral confirmations

The ordinary double-click and Supportz sheets are neutral consent, not Warning,
Danger, or Destructive action.

At rest, the ordinary sheet contains only generic item name, relevant quantity,
exact total, No, and Yes. At rest, the Supportz sheet contains only `$45`, No,
and Yes. After a rejected Yes, either may additionally contain the one exact
current refusal defined in section 6.3. Neither adds a visible title, icon, art,
invented question, hint, placeholder, or special material. No is initially
focused. Focus is trapped. Back or Close means No.

### 6.5 Real transaction custody

An immediate purchase publishes its result directly and receives no Busy frame.
A transaction that genuinely remains unresolved across visible frames owns
silent static custody only for its real lifetime. It adds no spinner, pending
copy, progress claim, animation, heartbeat, or minimum dwell.

For direct Buy custody, selection changes, quantity changes, purchase actions,
page actions, shortcuts, Home, and route-changing controls are temporarily
inert, not Disabled. Current selection, cards, inspector facts, prices, and HUD
remain readable. The selected ordinary card becomes the meaningful read-only
Focus anchor without changing selection. In addition to the resting dock rule,
the direct-custody token draws `(240,226,152,1)` at ordinary targets or
`(240,194,152,1)` with Large Targets. The one controlled-dark row between the
parallel rules remains unpainted.

For confirmation-originated custody, the foreground sheet remains present and
exclusive. No and Yes become temporarily inert, the sheet's factual body is its
meaningful read-only Focus anchor, and the visible background remains absent
from active Focus and assistive-action traversal. Active Focus never moves to a
background card while the sheet remains. In addition to the resting
body/action rule, the sheet-custody token draws sheet-relative
`(0,277,152,1)`; Worn Cream row 278 remains the gap. In both paths, held input
and contact are cancelled and no activation queues. These parallel static
seams are the sole visual custody evidence.

Settlement applies these exact retained outcomes:

- direct success or refusal returns lawful Focus to Buy when it remains and the
  selected card otherwise;
- ordinary-sheet success closes the sheet and returns Focus to its originating
  card;
- Supportz success closes the sheet and moves Focus to Spa Coupon;
- a sheet-owned expected refusal follows section 6.3 without closing the sheet;
- a condition-driven route owns its destination instead of any Shop fallback;
  and
- technical failure replaces the current custody owner with trusted recovery
  and transfers Focus there.

No settlement creates a Shop-local horror or Error skin.

## 7. Supportz absence boundary

Page-one slot nine always retains its blank laminate structural shell.

When ineligible or exhausted, the location is nonsemantic blank capacity. It
has no art aperture, component state, hit target, Hover, Press, Focus, Selected,
price, availability, hint, assistive node, fallback, placeholder, or asset
record. It is neither Disabled, Unavailable, nor temporarily inert.

When eligible, the same resting pixels become an unselected blank-card action.
It may own Hover, Press, and Focus while the previously selected ordinary card
remains Selected. The card target's sole assistive identity is
`Blank shop card`. Activation opens the Supportz sheet; it never changes
ordinary selection or opens the inspector.

The ineligible shell, eligible shell before Hover/Press/Focus, and exhausted
post-purchase shell are pixel-identical. Eligibility changes only semantics and
lawful interaction until a real navigation/contact state must be drawn.

No, Back, or Close restores Focus to the blank-card action when it remains
eligible and to Spa Coupon when revalidation has removed that semantic target.
Successful purchase removes the target and likewise moves Focus to Spa Coupon
when no route takes custody. Ordinary selection remains unchanged. No name
reveal, status, receipt, history entry, explanation, or celebration appears.

Supportz owns no source art, export, manifest record, fallback, exclusion mask,
or assistive art node. Its deliberate blankness can never be used as the
missing-art presentation of another item.

## 8. Focus, input, typography, and accessibility

### 8.1 Focus graph

Fresh or reconstructed Shop begins on Coffee. Home precedes the Shop root in
host traversal. Sequential traversal visits:

1. focusable cards in row-major order;
2. the inspector's single scroll target only when genuine overflow exists;
3. instantiated transaction controls in visible order; and
4. enabled page actions.

Reverse traversal is exact. Ineligible Supportz, nonsemantic blank capacity,
noninteractive copy, totals, the page indicator, and absent controls never
enter focus order.

Spatial arrows and D-pad retain the accepted 3-by-3 graph. Right from the third
column enters the inspector's first actionable control, never its scroll target;
if no action exists, Right is a no-op. Left from an inspector action returns to
the selected card. Down from the bottom row enters enabled page actions. Page
activation focuses the remembered target card. A direction with no lawful
destination is a no-op. Interactive modal traversal contains only No then Yes;
genuine sheet custody temporarily transfers the single read-only Focus anchor
to the sheet body as defined in section 6.5.

Pointer Hover never selects or steals Focus. Pointer, touch, keyboard,
controller, and supported assistive activation retain the same complete visible
select–inspect–quantity–Buy path. Double-click remains an optional mouse
accelerator only.

### 8.2 Typography and currency

Shop inherits Fusion Pixel proportional builds for names, descriptions,
availability, paging, and actions. Monospaced tabular builds are limited to
prices, quantities, totals, and `1 / 2`. Source Han Serif has no Shop role.

MIN and MAX are localized text. Decrement uses Fusion Pixel U+2212 minus;
increment uses U+002B plus. Their semantic names are localized equivalents of
Less and More. `Buy xN`, Previous, Next, dollar prices, and coin prices resolve
from registered strings. Shop adds no cart, bag, rarity, currency, or status
icon. Item cards, inspector, total, and confirmations use literal `$20`,
`1 coin`, `3 coins`, or their approved localized equivalents.

### 8.3 Assistive projection

An ordinary card exposes only its generic name, exact price/currency,
`Available` or `Sold out`, Selected state where true, and control identity. The
inspector exposes the same operational facts, selected quantity, total, and
literal refusal where present. It never exposes effects, caps, remaining stock,
friend association, keepsake mapping, route, or anomaly interpretation.

Object art receives no invented equivalent evidence or separate traversal node.
Supportz uses only the approved `Blank shop card` identity while eligible.
Absence remains absent from the assistive tree.

Both confirmation sheets retain the Shop authority's neutral dialog role and
localized neutral dialog identity without gaining a visible title. The ordinary
sheet may expose only its visible item, quantity, total, actions, and current
literal refusal. The Supportz sheet may expose only its visible `$45`, actions,
and current literal refusal; it never exposes the withheld item identity or
effect.

The design defines semantic and proof obligations but makes no certification
claim about a particular Windows assistive product before that exact
combination passes evidence.

### 8.4 Text size and localization

The same macro topology survives 100, 125, and 150 percent text and ordinary or
Large Targets. Text wraps vertically. It never clips, ellipsizes, scrolls
horizontally, becomes art, or shrinks below the selected master. Only inspector
information and a confirmation factual body with genuine supported-copy
overflow may scroll; their action rows never move.

Real final descriptions and complete `en`, `zh_CN`, and `zh_HK` catalog strings
are not yet approved or registered. Native locale fixtures therefore remain
blocked. This disposition invents no product prose or translation and claims no
initial 200-percent support.

## 9. Cache, persistence, and reconstruction

Same-day cache may retain only the accepted current page, remembered selected
slot per page, selected card, current selected quantity, lawful scroll
positions, and last meaningful Focus target. Selecting a different item resets
inspector information to the top. Reopening the unchanged cached selection may
restore its prior position.

Refusal, modal, Hover, Press, custody, Shop-local balance copy, and Supportz
interaction state are not cached. Cached presentation cannot affect purchase
eligibility or canonical run state.

The last-Focus key may name page-one slot nine only when that blank-card target
was eligible and lawfully focused; the key does not preserve eligibility or a
component state. Reopening recomputes Supportz first. If the target still
exists, Focus may restore there. If it does not, Focus moves to the Spa Coupon
card without changing ordinary selection. A completed un-routed Supportz
purchase has already moved Focus to Spa Coupon, so its later cache key is Spa
Coupon rather than the absent slot.

Canonical persistence remains unchanged. It owns balances, stats, purchase
counts, capabilities, keepsake ownership, Supportz day/branch facts, and any
durable in-flight transaction or condition handoff. It does not save Shop page,
selection, quantity, scroll, Focus, refusal, or modal state.

Logical-day change, Load, and New Run reconstruct page one with Coffee selected,
quantity one, and Coffee focused from validated canonical state. Supportz and
ordinary availability are recomputed; cached component state cannot influence
legality.

# *La Jalousie* and the Steam store-page voice: primary-source check

Date: 2026-08-30  
Status: non-authoritative external research. This note does not amend canon,
approve public copy, authorize implementation, or attribute this project to
Alain Robbe-Grillet. The rewrite samples are auditions only.

## Question

How can *La Jalousie* and Robbe-Grillet's stated craft principles inform a
short Steam description without direct imitation, affectation, or commercial
obscurity?

## Conclusion

Borrow **discipline of attention**, not Robbe-Grillet's sentence texture.

The useful transfer is narrow:

- name observable things and state how they relate;
- let a repeated thing return in a changed arrangement;
- avoid assigning a final psychological label to a character;
- keep the factual referent clear while leaving motive and interpretation open.

The current three-paragraph opening already does most of this. It should not be
rewritten merely to advertise literary influence. At most, audition a slightly
flatter statement of the disagreeing records. The game's own plain, intimate
voice should win whenever a Robbe-Grillet-inflected line sounds performed.

## What the primary material supports

### Observable surfaces and relations

The official Minuit excerpt of *La Jalousie* begins by locating a shadow in
relation to a pillar, roof, terrace, walls, and sun before describing A...'s
entrance and turn. The objects are not an ornamental list: their positions
establish a vantage and a field of possible observation. [Éditions de Minuit,
official opening-pages excerpt, pp. 7-9](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/images/extrait_2731.pdf)

Minuit's presentation likewise describes the unseen focal presence as the
point from which surrounding lines acquire direction and solidity. It also
emphasizes that apparently innocent perceptions remain registered. That
supports a store voice built from **messages, appointments, records, presence,
and elsewhere** rather than adjectives announcing that the situation is
"surreal," "obsessive," or "unreliable." [Éditions de Minuit, *La
Jalousie*](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/livre-La_Jalousie-2731-1-1-0-1.html)

This does **not** support a supposedly neutral camera. Minuit's author page
identifies "objectivity" and the absolute primacy of sight as persistent
misreadings of Robbe-Grillet, whose work instead claims radical subjectivity
and repeatedly complicates sight with hearing. For this game, concrete surfaces
should therefore reveal a situated attention, not remove human pressure.
[Éditions de Minuit, Alain Robbe-Grillet author page](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/auteur-Robbe_Grillet_Alain-1445-1-1-0-1.html)

### Repetition with alteration

In a 1978 first-person interview, Robbe-Grillet calls repetition essential to
strongly structured composition, but immediately places the interest in the
textual shifts between recurring themes. In the same interview he describes
meaning as mobile and resists imposing one authorized reading. The responsible
store-copy analogue is a small changed recurrence—*week* or *appointments*
returning under a new relation—not a slogan repeated for atmosphere. [Michel
Rybalka's interview with Robbe-Grillet, *Le Monde*, 22 September
1978](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/1978/09/22/robbe-grillet-commente-par-lui-meme_2987950_1819218.html)

### Withholding psychological labels

In a 1965 interview, Robbe-Grillet argues that conventional psychological
analysis freezes passions when it labels, classifies, names, and orders them.
His distinction is between declaring a character to *be* a jealous type and
showing a person in the process of living jealousy. Applied here, the page
should not pre-classify a character as controlling, possessive, seductive, or
safe. It can show appointments overlapping and records disagreeing, then allow
the game to disclose what those facts mean. [Jacqueline Piatier's interview
with Robbe-Grillet, *Le Monde*, 9 October
1965](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/1965/10/09/alain-robbe-grillet-presente-la-maison-de-rendez-vous_2194074_1819218.html)

This is not a reason to remove the phrase **romantic psychological-horror
visual novel**. That phrase labels the product and genre, not a character's
inner life. Steam's own review standard requires a description to be detailed
and coherent enough that customers know what to expect. A store page cannot
delegate all product comprehension to inference. [Valve, Steamworks *Review
Process*](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/review_process?language=english)

### A method, not a costume

Robbe-Grillet's publisher-hosted statement for *Pour un nouveau roman* says the
essays are not a theory of the novel and "Nouveau Roman" is not the name of a
school. That directly cautions against treating a few recognizable devices as
a compulsory house style. [Éditions de Minuit, *Pour un nouveau
roman*](https://www.leseditionsdeminuit.fr/livre-Pour_un_nouveau_roman-2229-1-1-0-1.html)

## Actionable boundary

| Transfer responsibly | Becomes unnatural in short store copy |
| --- | --- |
| Concrete nouns: messages, appointments, records, boards | Inventories of shadows, angles, furniture, or distances merely to resemble the novel |
| Exact relations: arrive, overlap, agree, attend, miss, continue elsewhere | Vague pronouns such as "it happens again" whose practical referent is hidden |
| One recurrence whose relation changes | Repetition that functions only as a literary wink or ominous slogan |
| No final personality diagnosis | Removing the genre and playable-form information shoppers need |
| Flat statement beside one distinctive hook | Geometric syntax, conspicuous fragments, or an imitation "absent observer" voice |

The key distinction is: **facts may be definite while their emotional causes
remain non-final**. Obscuring both fact and cause produces a riddle or an
aesthetic pose, not Robbe-Grillet's precision.

## Evaluation of the current opening

> During a seven-day university Open Week, messages arrive, appointments
> overlap, and ordinary records refuse to agree. Angela is present for only
> part of what happens.
>
> The week unfolds through messages, appointments, and compact
> Minesweeper-style logic boards. Angela may answer, revisit, attend, miss, or
> leave things alone. Events continue elsewhere.
>
> A romantic psychological-horror visual novel about attention, absence, and
> the danger of being understood too precisely.

**Assessment: keep it unless a live page audition proves a revision better.**

- Paragraph one is already surface-led: three ordinary objects/actions acquire
  tension through their relations. "Refuse" is the only conspicuous
  personification. It is a voice choice, not a defect.
- Paragraph two supplies the commercial grounding that a literary novel does
  not have to supply. The action list is legible and the final sentence extends
  the presence/absence relation without explaining its cause.
- Paragraph three is deliberately abstract, but usefully so: it names the genre
  and gives the page one memorable interpretive hook. Removing it in pursuit of
  surface purity would make the page less truthful and less distinctive.
- The passage contains no ornamental obscurity, psychological character label,
  second-person command, city, major, gender classification, route catalogue,
  or plot synopsis.

## Restrained rewrite auditions

These preserve the approved disclosure boundary. None is automatically better
than the current version.

### 1. Flatter contradiction — preferred audition

> During a seven-day university Open Week, messages arrive, appointments
> overlap, and ordinary records do not always agree. Angela is present for only
> part of what happens.
>
> The week unfolds through messages, appointments, and compact
> Minesweeper-style logic boards. Angela may answer, revisit, attend, miss, or
> leave things alone. Events continue elsewhere.
>
> A romantic psychological-horror visual novel about attention, absence, and
> the danger of being understood too precisely.

This changes only the authored flourish. It is more observational, but less
alive; the original "refuse" may still be the better game voice.

### 2. More visible interfaces

> During a seven-day university Open Week, messages arrive. Appointments
> overlap. Calendars and contact lists do not always agree. Angela is present
> for only part of what happens.
>
> The week unfolds through messages, appointments, and compact
> Minesweeper-style logic boards. Angela may answer, revisit, attend, miss, or
> leave things alone. Events continue elsewhere.
>
> A romantic psychological-horror visual novel about attention, absence, and
> the danger of being understood too precisely.

This is the clearest surface version. It should be used only if **calendars and
contact lists** are both visibly present in the launch build and the extra
specificity does not disclose more than intended.

### 3. Controlled recurrence

> During a seven-day university Open Week, messages arrive and appointments
> overlap. Ordinary records preserve the same week differently. Angela is
> present for only part of it.
>
> The week continues through messages, appointments, and compact
> Minesweeper-style logic boards. Angela may answer, revisit, attend, miss, or
> leave things alone. Elsewhere, the week continues without her.
>
> A romantic psychological-horror visual novel about attention, absence, and
> the danger of being understood too precisely.

The altered return of "the week continues" demonstrates the device most
plainly. It is also closest to sounding written-for-effect; reject it if that
echo is perceptible before its meaning is.

## Recommendation

Retain the current opening as the baseline. If a one-line experiment is wanted,
test **"ordinary records do not always agree"** against **"ordinary records
refuse to agree"** in the actual Steam layout. Do not add geometric description
or further repetition. The deeper *La Jalousie* inheritance should live in the
game's arrangements of evidence and absence; the store page needs only its
clarity and restraint.

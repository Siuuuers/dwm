# Seven-day candidate mechanisms: physical and institutional plausibility audit

Date: 2026-08-31  
Sources accessed: 2026-08-31  
Status: **NONCANONICAL RESEARCH.** This note does not approve, place, rewrite,
or canonize any scene, message, consequence, character authority, East Harbour
procedure, or exact dialogue. It tests only whether the named provisional
mechanisms could work in an ordinary physical institution if their listed
guards are adopted.

## Scope and reading rule

This audit checks eleven provisional mechanisms against official observatory,
university, event-safety, privacy, dance, conservatoire-adjacent, and equipment
sources. It also checks the proposed actions against the role and knowledge
limits in the [Core Story Bible](../../story/01-core-story-bible.md) and
[Character & Relationship Handbook](../../story/02-character-relationship-handbook.md).

- **Verified general practice** means a first-party institution, public body,
  professional organization, or manufacturer documents that practice.
- **Reasonable project inference** means a conservative East Harbour workflow
  could be built from those practices, but no source establishes the fictional
  institution's exact rule.
- **Unsupported story-specific invention** means the proposal needs a new
  fictional policy, delegation, system state, or causal link before it can be
  treated as ordinary institutional fact.

The non-Hong-Kong sources below demonstrate general event or production
practice, not Hong Kong law. The Hong Kong sources establish local analogues for
Information Day routing, incident reporting, and personal-data handling. No
source establishes East Harbour's exact titles, delegations, forms, rooms, or
software.

## Bottom line

Ten candidates are physically plausible either as stated or with a narrow
guard. **Routed Fallback Case fails in its current form**: universities do use
confirmed locations, work orders, delivery/pickup, equipment custody, and
holding areas, but this pass found no support for an ordinary location
check-in prompt itself automatically dispatching an equipment case or turning
expiry into a `manual-check` routing state. That causal bridge needs an explicit
pre-authorized work-order channel and an authorized custodian.

A later replacement is audited in the addendum below. **Late Work Check earns
PASS WITH GUARD** because it changes only the first destination of an
already-authorized human check; it does not revive or repair the failed
equipment-dispatch chain.

The recurrent risk is **authority inflation**, not physical impossibility.
Students can issue assigned queue tokens, audit a route, time a transition,
stop their own movement, update a document they own, or submit a form through a
defined channel. Those actions do not make them the building-capacity officer,
accessibility certifier, clinician, artistic director, facilities dispatcher,
or owner of another person's confidential record.

## 1. Numbered telescope access is ordinary; the exact authority split is local

### Verified general practice

Lick Observatory has used numbered passes issued at the door for telescope
access, admitting lower-numbered pass holders first. That is direct precedent
for **Numbered Turns** as a practical way to order a scarce telescope-viewing
opportunity. [Lick Observatory, *Summer Series*](https://ucolick.org/summer/music/july15.html)

The University of Washington's Theodor Jacobsen Observatory limits dome access
to twelve visitors at a time. A student coordinator checks visitors in and
assigns them to telescope-access groups on a first-come basis. It therefore
supports both a small fixed capacity and student-run group issuance, while also
showing that the capacity belongs to the venue's established operation rather
than to a student's improvised judgment. [University of Washington, *Jacobsen
Observatory*](https://astro.washington.edu/jacobsen-observatory)

Mount Wilson separates the experienced telescope-operator role from the
session-director role and requires visitors to follow staff instructions.
Boston University's public-night account likewise records a capacity limit,
ticketed and unticketed admission, staff briefing, queues at the instruments,
and a no-touch rule for visitors. Together these sources support distinct
queue, visitor-management, and instrument-custody responsibilities. [Mount
Wilson Observatory, *Public Ticket Nights*](https://www.mtwilson.edu/public-ticket-nights/)
and [Boston University, *Public Open Night at BU's Coit
Observatory*](https://www.bu.edu/federal/2016/03/16/nightlife-public-open-night-at-bus-coit-observatory/)

### Reasonable project inference

East Harbour can credibly use one fixed batch of numbered cards or tokens for a
small demonstration. Priscilla's canon role in programme communications and
student organization can support issuing them **if that duty is assigned**.
Angela's canon competence with astronomy demonstrations can support stating how
many people the telescope operator can process per turn, what visitors may
touch, and when the operator must stop.

The clean division is:

- the venue or approved event plan supplies the room/dome occupancy limit;
- Angela supplies the instrument-side throughput and operating constraints;
- Priscilla issues the already authorized sequence and maintains the ordinary
  registration record; and
- telescope custody stays with the assigned operator.

### Unsupported story-specific invention

No source requires a sealed remainder of tickets, an evidentiary custody test,
or a second fairness method. Those may be story objects, but they are not safety
necessities. Priscilla cannot derive a legal capacity from her communications
role, and Angela's telescope competence does not by itself make her a building
occupancy or crowd-safety authority. If Angela is absent, a documented default
may operate only if the institution or assigned technical lead established it
beforehand; absence cannot silently convert Priscilla into that technical lead.

## 2. Wayfinding, welfare, protected forms, breaks, and public water

### Verified general practice

HKU's 2025 Information Day supplied a campus map identifying information
counters, first-aid stations, shuttle stops, washrooms, and water dispensers.
Its transport page gave distinct routes from MTR exits to groups of buildings,
warned visitors to allow extra time for crowds, asked them to follow staff
instructions, and published fixed shuttle routes, capacities, and intervals.
Large university information events therefore do turn route facts into public
maps, staffed directions, and operational route records. [HKU, *Information Day
Campus Map*](https://admissions.hku.hk/sites/default/files/2025-10/IDAY-2025-Campus-Map.pdf)
and [HKU, *Information Day Transportation*](https://infoday.hku.hk/transportation)

The University of Missouri's event guide calls for an advance route walkthrough
and maps that identify accessible facilities and routes. Hampden-Sydney
College's event-planning guide describes wayfinding as a continuous visitor
journey that should orient, route, and repeatedly confirm a first-time visitor.
These sources support a route audit, but they also show why a casual walk cannot
be promoted into a formal accessibility certification. [University of
Missouri, *Student Group Event Planning
Guide*](https://getinvolved.missouri.edu/student-group-event-planning-guide/)
and [Hampden-Sydney College, *Event Planning
Guide*](https://www.hsc.edu/documents/calendar/EventPlanningGuide.pdf)

HKU directs university accident reports to its Safety Office. Hong Kong's
Privacy Commissioner recommends restricted, need-to-know access for paper
personal data and secure transmission such as sealed envelopes that do not
expose sensitive fields. An ordinary incident-form handoff therefore has both a
destination and a privacy boundary. [HKU Safety Office, *Reporting
Accidents*](https://safety.hku.hk/reporting-accidents) and [Office of the Privacy
Commissioner for Personal Data, *Data Protection Principles in the Personal
Data (Privacy) Ordinance*, pp. 70–72](https://www.pcpd.org.hk/english/publications/files/Perspective_2nd.pdf)

Official event guidance assigns information/welfare roles in advance and asks
that welfare services be clearly signposted, accessible, and properly lit.
Separate stewarding guidance calls for relief workers so event staff receive
regular breaks. HSE guidance also treats a clean place to eat and drink during
breaks as ordinary staff welfare. These establish the workflow behind
**Coverage Window** and **Caretaker's Break**, though the cited UK timings are
not Hong Kong legal requirements for a fictional student volunteer. [Dover
District Council, *Event Planning: Information and
Welfare*](https://www.dover.gov.uk/Community/EventPlanning/A-Z/InformationandWelfare.aspx),
[Midlothian Council, *Selecting and Training
Stewards*](https://www.midlothian.gov.uk/info/200269/licences_and_permits/533/organising_an_event/4),
and [UK Health and Safety Executive, *Employee
Welfare*](https://www.hse.gov.uk/event-safety/employee-welfare.htm)

Public-event health guidance permits either water bottles or dispensers and
requires drinking water to be safe and well signposted. HKU's Information Day
map provides a direct local example of water dispensers and first-aid points
being visible public facilities. This supports a self-service welfare-table
view without turning a bottle into medical care. [UK Health Security Agency,
*Hot Weather Advice: Mass Gatherings and Planning
Events*](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/hot-weather-advice-mass-gatherings-and-planning-events/)

### Candidate implications

**Coverage Window — verified core.** Sylvia may state an assigned room-check or
welfare interval and may separately offer her own availability. The first is a
roster fact; the second is a personal request or offer. Neither lets her promise
diagnosis, ongoing care, private access, or an outcome on behalf of the
institution. Angela can refuse the personal offer without losing ordinary
public welfare service.

**Route Card Audit — conservative inference.** A one-page `route card` is a
local name for a combination of documented map, destination, checkpoint, and
direction facts. Sylvia's canon access to public schedules, assigned rooms, and
check-in procedures supports checking those fields. Angela can traverse the
route and report time, obstruction, signage, or a mismatch she actually sees.
An official accessibility or emergency-route conclusion still belongs to the
responsible office. If a corrected card exists, the old one should be visibly
superseded or removed; production-document guidance treats current version
control as an ordinary safety against conflicting instructions. [University of
Alaska Fairbanks, *Stage Manager's
Handbook*](https://www.uaf.edu/theatrefilm/stage-managers-handbook.php)

**Slip Leaves Her Hand — protected handoff, not open circulation.** Sylvia may
submit a form through her assigned incident workflow. The physically safest
visible carrier is face-down, covered, sealed, or entering a designated secure
collection point. Lavinia may receive or inspect information about herself
under an appropriate procedure; Angela and Priscilla do not acquire the
contents merely by being nearby. If the scene needs only the slip's passing
presence, a generic form edge, envelope, or destination is enough. Showing the
name, symptoms, or help field to an unrelated observer would require a separate
lawful reason and would not be ordinary good handling.

**Caretaker's Break — ordinary if coverage pre-exists.** Sylvia can hand over an
assigned station to pre-rostered relief or use an authorized temporary closure.
Angela can notice the missed break and offer bounded food, water, or company.
She cannot assume the welfare post, receive confidential forms, or supervise
visitors solely because the relationship scene needs Sylvia to sit down.

**Public welfare-table view — ordinary public access.** Sylvia may place an
unopened bottle or point to a dispenser at an assigned, reachable public table.
Exact bottle position is staging, not verified procedure. It must not imply a
prescription, diagnosis, exclusive reservation for Angela, or an arrangement
that blocks circulation. The ordinary public service must remain available
whether Angela answers Sylvia's message or not.

## 3. Movement observation, transition timing, load adjustment, and deletion

### Verified general practice

The University of Iowa Department of Dance requires consent each time an
instructor considers touch useful and requires a non-contact instructional
alternative when touch is declined. The Royal Academy of Dance separately
frames safe touch around consent, professional boundaries, risk identification,
and respectful use. Observation without touch is therefore not an artificial
escape hatch; it is an ordinary safe alternative. [University of Iowa,
*Instructional Touch Best Practices*](https://callboard.performingarts.uiowa.edu/dance-callboard/dance-policies-and-additional-guidelines)
and [Royal Academy of Dance, *Safe Touch in Dance: Boundaries, Consent and
Communication*](https://www.royalacademyofdance.org/event/safeguarding/)

One Dance UK calls for a clean, appropriate sprung floor, clear surrounding
space, suitable height and ventilation, workload planning, rest, and access to
dance-aware health professionals. Its guidance distinguishes artistic practice
from health assessment. The Royal Ballet School likewise places training and
rehabilitation decisions within an integrated artistic and multidisciplinary
healthcare system while encouraging dancers to take charge of their wellbeing.
[One Dance UK, *Industry Standards for
Dance*](https://www.onedanceuk.org/programmes/industry-standards) and [The Royal
Ballet School, *Healthy Dancer
Programme*](https://www.royalballetschool.org.uk/train/dancer-training/healthy-dancer-programme/)

IADMS describes jumps and rapid direction changes as high-load lower-limb work,
while barre and adagio are lower-load examples. It recommends individualized
load modification and consultation with a physiotherapist rather than a
universal pain rule. That supports reducing or stopping a test, but not Angela
declaring a movement safe. [International Association for Dance Medicine &
Science, *Managing Tendinopathy in Dancers and Dance
Teachers*](https://iadms.org/media/5780/iadms-resource-paper-managing-tendinopathy-in-dancers-and-dance-teachers.pdf)

UAF's production handbook documents paper tech, dry tech, and cue-to-cue work.
Cue-to-cue skips nontechnical material to refine cue timing and transitions; a
stopwatch or timing device is standard stage-management equipment; current
documents use version control. It also gives the director the artistic plan and
stage management the organization and communication of that plan. SUNY New
Paltz documents the same use of cue-to-cue rehearsal to develop timing and
accuracy. These sources support **Five-Minute Audience** and the dependent
document changes in **Nothing Replaces It**, but not unilateral programme
authority for every participant. [University of Alaska Fairbanks, *Stage
Manager's Handbook*](https://www.uaf.edu/theatrefilm/stage-managers-handbook.php)
and [SUNY New Paltz, *Production
Handbook*](https://www.newpaltz.edu/fpa/theatre/productions/production-handbook/)

### Candidate implications

**Borrowed Gravity — viable bounded observation.** Use a simple movement whose
load is evident from the staging: for example, a small weight shift or marked
transition rather than a jump, catch, lift, forced balance, or endurance test.
Lavinia specifies the action, space, stop condition, and whether touch is
offered. Angela may observe or decline. If touch occurs, it is not rescue,
assessment, or proof of trust; consent can change immediately.

**Five-Minute Audience — viable timing task.** A short comparison can establish
which of two transitions fits a known cue interval or creates an observable
collision. Angela can operate a timer and report the result. Lavinia retains the
professional judgment about which version she will use. The five-minute label
is a bounded scene duration, not evidence that the movement is safe, complete,
or artistically approved.

**Before It Hurts — viable self-authored reduction.** Lavinia may recognize her
own functional change and reduce, mark, stop, or alter her work before it
becomes a crisis. Angela may name a visible difference and ask what is wanted.
The title must not imply that Angela predicts pain, that no pain exists yet, or
that one successful adjusted pass provides medical clearance. A formal
longer-term modification plan remains a conservatory/healthcare matter.

**Nothing Replaces It — viable only with source ownership.** A performer can
decline her own optional demonstration, and a current running order can delete
the resulting item and its dependent cue. The institutionally load-bearing fact
is not the deletion itself but **who is authorized to make it current**. The
proposal needs either:

- a pre-existing record that labels the unit optional and within Lavinia's
  delegated ownership; or
- an ordinary approval/update in the shared current record from the role that
  owns the public programme.

After that, Priscilla may update programme language/run order within her
assigned remit, and Angela may delete only the corresponding item from her own
operator list. Without that channel, `Lavinia removes it; Priscilla must comply;
nothing replaces it` overstates all three students' authority. The sources also
do not require a dramatic blank, held silence, or symbolic gap; an ordinary
omission in the current version is sufficient.

## 4. Weather-safe fallback equipment and the location prompt

### Verified general practice

NASA's Night Sky Network advises safely stashing a telescope when rain or
inclement weather arrives and supplies downloadable presentations for indoor or
cloudy-day outreach. A weather fallback can therefore be a real, pre-existing
source package rather than an improvised substitute. [NASA Night Sky Network,
*Ready-Made Presentations for Meetings, Class Visits, and Cloudy
Days*](https://nightsky.jpl.nasa.gov/news/208/)

University event-services offices use approved requests and work orders before
delivering equipment. The University of Florida requires event approval and an
accepted estimate before a work order, then offers delivery and pickup. Baylor
checks portable equipment out to a responsible individual. UCCS records the
confirmed venue, requested equipment, primary contact, authorized proxy, and
approved changes; it also limits unarranged storage. The University of
Cincinnati uses a designated receiving point and requires event/contact labels
and approved storage. UT Austin exposes approval and work-order states rather
than treating an informal message as a facilities instruction. [University of
Florida, *Event Equipment and
Services*](https://www.facilitiesservices.ufl.edu/get-help/event-services/),
[Baylor University, *Event
Technology*](https://events.web.baylor.edu/plan-your-event/event-technology),
[UCCS Event Services, *Guidelines and
Procedures*](https://eventservices.uccs.edu/guidelinesandprocedures), [University
of Cincinnati, *Event
Policies*](https://www.uc.edu/about/event-services/resources/event-policies),
and [UT Austin, *Event & Moving
Services*](https://facilitiesservices.utexas.edu/services/ems)

A genuinely IP67-rated closed hard case can protect sensitive equipment from
water and dust, but that is a property of a specified case and correct closure,
not of the generic phrase `weather-safe`. [Pelican, *Protector
Cases*](https://www.pelican.com/us/en/discover/protector-case)

### Reasonable project inference

It is plausible for an already approved astronomy fallback pack to wait in a
closed protective case at a designated receiving/holding point, then be moved
to one of a small number of pre-approved event locations after the responsible
contact confirms the destination. If the destination is not confirmed, leaving
the case with an authorized custodian while the work order remains unresolved
is more ordinary than sending it speculatively through campus.

### Unsupported story-specific invention and failure reason

This pass did not verify any general practice in which:

1. an attendee/operator's ordinary **location check-in prompt** doubles as an
   equipment-dispatch authorization;
2. allowing that prompt to expire automatically routes the case elsewhere;
3. expiry creates a standard `manual-check` tag; or
4. any of the four principal students can see, infer, or control the unseen
   facilities movement without being the authorized contact.

Those are plausible **fictional system rules**, but they are not presently
ordinary facts. To become physically defensible, the premise must separately
establish a pre-approved work order, pre-approved destination choices, the
authorized contact, the staff or assigned operator who may move the case, the
secure holding point, and which resulting status is visible to Angela. That is
a substantive causal addition, so **Routed Fallback Case remains failed rather
than provisionally passed by research alone**.

## Addendum — `Late Work Check` replacement audit

Date: 2026-08-31  
Status: **NONCANONICAL RESEARCH ADDENDUM.** This addendum tests a replacement
for `Routed Fallback Case`. It does not approve the candidate, change the Day 6
anchor, select an encounter, name an unseen worker, or authorize a new Day 7
surface.

### Exact verdict

**PASS WITH GUARD.** A pre-authorized end-of-day check, a timed phone check-in,
a known or updated work location, a scheduled in-person visit, and a response
to failed contact are all documented university practices. It is therefore
credible for confirmation to change the **first stop** of an already-scheduled
check, and for non-confirmation to leave an already-recorded default room as
that first stop. The exact two-outcome East Harbour routing rule remains a
reasonable local inference rather than a verified universal procedure.

This verdict does not alter the original **FAIL** for `Routed Fallback Case`.
The replacement passes precisely because no equipment moves, the prompt does
not create the physical check, and expiry does not create an emergency,
diagnosis, or new authority.

### Verified general practice

UCL requires departments to authorize routine specified lone work, plan the
time spent working alone, and establish periodic checks at agreed intervals.
Those checks may occur in person, by telephone, or by radio; the procedure must
also address failed contact and keep appropriate records. This supports a check
that exists before Angela's phone response rather than being summoned by it.
[UCL Safety Services, *Lone Working
Standard*](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/safety-services/policies/2022/may/lone-working-standard)

A UCL School of Pharmacy procedure supplies the closest physical analogue. A
worker tells the duty porter where in the building the work will occur, updates
the porter when changing locations, and gives an expected duration. The porter
then phones at fixed intervals and must investigate if there is no response.
This verifies the combination of pre-notification, current-location updates,
scheduled contact, and required follow-up investigation; it does not prescribe East Harbour's
exact room-selection interface. [UCL School of Pharmacy, *Code of Practice for
Lone Working and Out of Hours
Working*](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/life-sciences/sites/life_sciences/files/cop_lone_working_out_of_hours.pdf)

UIC's official Work Alone function lets a campus user communicate where and
when they are working, uses recurring phone prompts, and notifies a contact if
a response is missed. Louisville's SafeZone check-in can share location, be
scheduled in advance, run for a set duration, and carry a note; its missed-timer
path calls the user and can escalate if the user remains unreachable. These are
direct precedents for a timed phone check and distinct confirmation/nonresponse
states. Louisville's stronger emergency consequence is **not** evidence that
East Harbour must infer an emergency from expiry. [University of Illinois
Chicago, *UIC SAFE App*](https://ready.uic.edu/safetyresources/uic-safe-app/)
and [University of Louisville Public Safety,
*SafeZone*](https://safety.louisville.edu/police-department/safezone)

The University of Wisconsin–Eau Claire requires a working-alone check-in plan
to identify the main and backup contacts, the communication schedule, whether
contact is verbal or visual, periodic calls or visits, a possible written log,
and a separate action plan when contact is not achieved. RIT likewise requires
the buddy to know the worker's specific location and, after lost contact, tell
Public Safety where the worker can be found. These sources support a physical
checker using the currently authorized location, while keeping the response to
continued noncontact separate from the first visit. [University of
Wisconsin–Eau Claire, *Working Alone
Safely*](https://kb.uwec.edu/articles/uwec-620a) and [Rochester Institute of
Technology, *Buddy System*](https://www.rit.edu/ehs/buddy-system)

Sussex requires risk-assessed check-in and supervision procedures. Its missed
check-in guidance calls for a set response that may begin with another contact
attempt and may later include a Security welfare check or police involvement.
It therefore supports `unconfirmed` as a trigger for an ordered procedure, not
as proof that an emergency or injury has occurred. [University of Sussex,
*HS G402 Guidance on Personal Safety & Lone
Working*](https://www.sussex.ac.uk/hso/documents/hs-g402-guidance-on-personal-safety-and-lone-working-v10.pdf)

Assigned event locations and accountable leads are also ordinary. Cornell
volunteers check in, meet a group leader, and walk to an assigned location;
ASU recommends a check-in/out process, area leads, role limits, maps, and a
reference list organized by volunteer, area, and job. These sources support a
finite event-location roster and role-based custody without requiring a named
extra to appear on screen. [Cornell University, *Commencement
Volunteers*](https://commencement.cornell.edu/may/vol/) and [Arizona State
University, *Volunteer
Management*](https://eventguide.engineering.asu.edu/planning-process/volunteer-management/)

### Reasonable project inference

East Harbour may pre-authorize a low-risk end-of-day operator check with two or
another small number of approved work locations. The check stays on the rota in
every branch. Angela's confirmation updates only the checker's first
destination; expiry records `unconfirmed` and leaves the already-declared
default room as the first destination. If Angela is not found there, the
separate failed-contact procedure continues.

No reviewed source documents that exact combined state transition. It is a
defensible local rule because each component is documented and because the
reply changes neither whether the check occurs nor who is authorized to perform
it. `Late Work Check` should therefore be described as an East Harbour
operator/check-in procedure, not as the behavior of UIC SAFE or Louisville
SafeZone.

The astronomy label does not broaden the permission. The checked activity must
already be assessed as safe for one student to perform at that time. Hazardous
maintenance, heavy lifting, high-power lasers, energized work, unprotected
height, or any task whose emergency controls require a second person cannot be
smuggled into the candidate merely because a later check is scheduled.

### Minimum guards and operational fields

The following fields are the minimum defensible internal record. They need not
all appear to the audience.

| Field group | Minimum content |
| --- | --- |
| **Preauthorization** | Stable check-plan or risk-assessment ID; approved low-risk activity; Day 6 date; scheduled check time/window; version; authorizing unit. |
| **Subject and role** | Protected Angela/operator identity mapping; designated checker role plus backup role; working contact method. The prose may omit a personal name, but the institution cannot omit responsibility. |
| **Location** | Finite whitelist of access-controlled work-location IDs; one default assigned room; any access or phone-coverage constraint. No free-text destination and no inferred GPS route are required. |
| **Prompt** | Prompt ID; issue time; expiry time; allowed location choices; advance display of the default-first rule. |
| **Outcome** | Exactly `confirmed(location_id, timestamp)` or `unconfirmed(expired_at)`; derived first-stop destination; immutable receipt ID. Expiry means only that confirmation was not received. |
| **Continuation** | Maximum ordinary first-stop delay; failed-contact procedure ID; permitted retry/escalation sequence; eventual check result stored separately from the prompt receipt. |
| **Privacy** | Role-limited access to the location, proportionate retention, and no disclosure to Sylvia, Priscilla, or Lavinia merely because they appear elsewhere that day. |

The checker and backup must be assigned before the prompt. Angela can truthfully
confirm one offered location or allow the deadline to pass; she cannot appoint
the checker, rewrite the approved location list, cancel the check, or create an
emergency. Sylvia, Priscilla, and Lavinia neither control the process nor learn
its result without a separate lawful carrier.

### Day 6 route-survival audit

The route test below applies the fixed mode and Hospital law in the [Seven-Day
Causal Matrix](../../story/07-seven-day-causal-matrix.md) and [August 7
dialogic-flow design](../design/2026-08-07-seven-day-dialogic-flow-design.md).

| Route | Verdict | Required behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Zero dates** | **PASS WITH GUARD** | The already-scheduled prompt still appears. Angela may confirm an occupied approved work location or let it expire; neither outcome requires an encounter. |
| **One or two solos / Prevented pair** | **PASS WITH GUARD** | Solo attendance does not cancel the check. The prompt window must begin after selected encounter work finishes, or every possible current work site must already be on the whitelist. A date cannot silently become an approved safety location. |
| **Group** | **PASS WITH GUARD** | Angela's attendance does not cause the prompt. Either the Group work location is pre-approved, the prompt follows the Group work, or nonresponse leaves the default-first path. The receipt carries no Priscilla–Lavinia interpretation. |
| **Missed** | **PASS WITH GUARD** | Angela's absence from the accepted Group is unrelated to check-in status. Confirmation or expiry still commits independently; the pair scene supplies no location information. |
| **Private-visible** | **PASS WITH GUARD** | The audience-visible Priscilla–Lavinia scene neither blocks nor explains Angela's separate phone state. Audience observation grants no participant or checker knowledge. |
| **Private-offscreen** | **PASS WITH GUARD** | No pair presentation is needed. The phone receipt and the offscreen pair/count receipt remain distinct records; neither proves the other. |
| **Hospital** | **PASS WITH GUARD** | Hospital remains caused only by the fixed condition resolver. A confirmation already committed remains fixed; otherwise the phone may expire while Angela is unavailable and the check begins at the default room. Finding her absent proves neither Hospital nor diagnosis and does not reveal her treatment location. |
| **Day 7 passive read** | **PASS WITH GUARD** | An existing resolver may consume the immutable Day 6 prompt receipt as state. It must not create a new `day_7.dtl` entry, notification, scene, reply, check-completion claim, or Day 8 debt. Any fact about what the unseen checker later found requires its own authorized receipt or witness. |

The prompt must remain an institutional check-in, separate from Sylvia's Day 6
ordinary message, both solo invitations, and the pair action. Otherwise an
ordinary-message expiry would be wrongly promoted from its invisible sequence
tombstone into route logic, or one relationship path would acquire control of a
protected anchor.

### Unsupported story-specific invention or failure conditions

`Late Work Check` would fail this audit if any later draft asserts that:

- the prompt creates the check rather than merely selecting its first stop;
- an arbitrary/free-text or GPS-inferred location becomes authorized by reply;
- expiry proves danger, injury, Hospital, guilt, intent, or diagnosis;
- the default room was not declared before expiry;
- absence at the first room completes the procedure without the documented
  failed-contact continuation;
- Angela, Sylvia, Priscilla, or Lavinia silently acquires dispatcher, Security,
  facilities, supervisory, or diagnostic authority;
- the prompt receipt proves the physical check was completed;
- a checker knows Angela's Hospital location without a separate lawful channel;
  or
- Day 7 presents a new bridge scene or message instead of passively reading the
  already-committed Day 6 receipt.

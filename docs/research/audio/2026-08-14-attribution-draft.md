## Change-notice template

```text
<Title / exact filename> — <Creator / performer>
Source: <direct exact asset page>
License: <exact license name and direct link>
Changes: <truthful list, for example: selected excerpt, trimmed, gain-adjusted,
faded, gently EQ-adjusted, and converted for game use>.
No endorsement by the creator or source platform is implied.
```

Do not say “edited” if nothing changed, and do not omit resampling, downmixing,
loop construction, stereo narrowing, noise reduction, or other material edits.

# UI and Gameplay Candidate Ledger

**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Status:** Pack-level shortlist; no asset downloaded or selected

This ledger closes the five `ui.` and three `gameplay.` roles with two related
Kenney CC0 families. The official pages expose pack identity, file count,
version, category, tags, and license, but not individual filenames or audible
previews. Each row is therefore `CONDITIONAL`: an exact member can be named only
after the user authorizes acquisition and the archive is inventoried and
auditioned. Silence remains every role's fallback.

Kenney's official support page states that game assets on its asset pages are
CC0, are usable in commercial projects, and do not require attribution. It also
asks users not to use the Kenney logo because that mark is reserved for official
studio projects ([Kenney support](https://kenney.nl/support)). The controlling
[CC0 1.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits
commercial copying, adaptation, distribution, and performance without asking
permission and prohibits implying endorsement.

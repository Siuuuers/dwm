# Human Nonverbal Candidate Ledger

**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Status:** Conservative first-pass research; no acquisition authority

This ledger covers the three closed `human_nonverbal.` roles. Because a breath,
hum, or choir-like sound readily invents an unseen person, intimacy, distress,
religion, or supernatural agency, human provenance and a permissive license are
necessary but not sufficient. Silence is the present recommendation for all
three roles until a candidate survives direct listening and blind scene tests.

Every external asset below comes from its exact Freesound page. The displayed
[CC0 1.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits
commercial copying, adaptation, distribution, and performance without legally
required attribution. Voluntary creator/source credit remains production policy.

# Audio Conditional Audition-Priority Packet

**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Decision state:** Complete role-first research; zero downloads and zero assets approved

This is the user-review surface for the Acoustic Memory Atlas search. It names
the strongest evidence-backed prospect, a fallback, or silence for all 35
registered roles. “Preferred” means **first to audition if acquisition is later
authorized**. It does not mean the sound has been heard, approved, or integrated.

## Why no numeric artistic scores appear yet

The plan's five-point scoring dimensions include emotional fit, restraint, loop
quality, mono behavior, repetition fatigue, and comfort. Those are auditory
facts. Scoring them from titles, tags, or wave metadata would manufacture
evidence. The audition form at the end preserves the required dimensions, but
scores remain blank until the exact master is heard in mono first and then in
scene with dialogue and uniform TTS ducking.

## Audition scorecard for later authorized acquisition

Score each exact file from 1 (fails) to 5 (excellent) only after source/license
capture and full listening. Legal status remains a gate and is never averaged.

```text
Exact file:
Role:
Physical credibility: _/5
Emotional fit without instruction: _/5
Restraint: _/5
Editability: _/5
Loop suitability or finite-tail quality: _/5
Mono safety: _/5
Repetition comfort: _/5
Spoiler/importance neutrality: _/5
Source reliability: _/5
TTS ducking behavior (-12 dB, 120 ms down, 180 ms up): pass / fail
No painful frequency, loud attack, or jump-scare contour: pass / fail
Decision: reject / fallback / preferred
Notes:
```

## Recommendation

Approve the **order of audition**, not the assets. Begin with the quiet physical
core—bedroom/university/hospital beds, paper, pencil, mug, switch, umbrella,
door, and neutral UI clicks—before testing music or human voice. Music enters
only after the game already works in ambience, Foley, and silence. Human choir
does not enter this acquisition pass.

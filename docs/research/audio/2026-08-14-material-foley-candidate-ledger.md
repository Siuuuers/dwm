# Material and Character Foley Candidate Ledger

**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Status:** First-pass source and license research; no acquisition authority

This ledger covers all twelve closed `foley.` roles. It searches by visible
object and physical action, not by character emotion. Every surviving recording
is `CONDITIONAL` until the exact file is acquired with authorization and passes
source-match, transient, distance, room, mono, repetition, and comfort tests.
Silence remains valid whenever a sound would exaggerate the visible action.

All external candidates below are exact Freesound assets displayed under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), which permits
commercial copying and modification without required attribution. Production
should nevertheless retain voluntary creator/source credit and a checksum.

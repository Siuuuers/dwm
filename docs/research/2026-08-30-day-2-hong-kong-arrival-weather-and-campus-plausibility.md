# Day 2 Hong Kong arrival, weather, and campus-event plausibility

Date: 2026-08-30  
Sources accessed: 2026-08-30  
Status: non-authoritative external research. This note does not amend story
canon, select a Day 2 premise, approve dialogue, or authorize implementation.

## Scope and reading rule

This note checks three narrow physical-world questions against first-party
Hong Kong sources: late-October/early-November weather; arrival at Hong Kong
International Airport (HKIA) and onward travel through Kowloon; and ordinary
university or conservatoire open-event operations. No guidebook, news outlet,
weather blog, or fan source is used as authority.

**Verified fact** below means a first-party source states the fact. **Project
inference** means a bounded scene implication drawn from those facts; it is not
itself a claim made by the source. Dates from real institutions are examples,
not a claim that the fictional institution shares their calendar.

## Bottom line

1. A passing Day 2 shower is physically credible in either late October or
   early November. It should not make the whole week continuously wet by
   default: November is materially drier than October in the Hong Kong
   Observatory (HKO) normals.
2. A Kowloon-to-HKIA pickup cannot read as instantaneous. The Airport Express
   rail segment alone is 22 minutes from Kowloon Station, before access to that
   station, waiting, terminal movement, and the arriving traveller's
   immigration, baggage, and customs process.
3. The terminal-to-Airport-Express connection is a same-level circulation
   route. A long umbrella is therefore most convincing as something needed for
   a later exposed transfer or walkway, or as the deliberately awkward object
   Lavinia names when she wants Priscilla to come—not as equipment they must
   open on the airport platform.
4. Late-October university admissions events and performing-arts information
   days are well supported. Their ordinary texture includes programme proofing,
   room schedules, talks, booths, facility tours, workshops, student/faculty
   staffing, backstage access, and transport planning. `Open Week`, however,
   is not established here as a standard Hong Kong event name or a standard
   seven-day public format; it should remain institution-specific fictional
   branding.

## Weather

### Verified facts

The HKO's 1991–2020 Hong Kong reference normals give the following monthly
values:

| Month | Rainfall | Days with at least 0.1 mm | Days with at least 25 mm | Mean daily max / mean / min | Mean relative humidity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| October | 120.3 mm | 7.83 | 1.33 | 28.1 / 25.7 / 23.9 °C | 73% |
| November | 39.3 mm | 5.70 | 0.40 | 24.5 / 22.2 / 20.3 °C | 72% |

These are climatological averages, not a forecast for a particular fictional
date. The same HKO page records only 0.83 October thunderstorm days and 0.17
November thunderstorm days on average. [HKO, *Monthly Meteorological Normals for
Hong Kong (1991–2020)*](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/cis/normal/1991_2020/normals.htm)

HKO describes November and December as generally having pleasant breezes,
sunshine, and comfortable temperatures, and says roughly 80% of Hong Kong's
annual rain falls from May through September. It also notes that a sufficiently
close tropical cyclone can still produce heavy, widespread, multi-day rain.
[HKO, *Climate of Hong
Kong*](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/cis/climahk.htm)

### Project implications

- A brief shower, wet pavement, a recently used umbrella, or rain beginning
  during an onward transfer is ordinary and needs no storm explanation.
- Light rain should not automatically bring thunder, flooding, cancelled
  transport, soaked interiors, or a week-long storm palette. Those are separate
  weather decisions with larger causal consequences.
- Late October can still feel warm; early November can feel milder. Either can
  support strong indoor-air-conditioning contrast, but the normals do not prove
  conditions inside any particular building.
- If the exact story year and dates are later fixed, the scene needs a fresh
  check against that date's observed weather and warning history. Monthly
  normals cannot establish whether it rained at the arrival hour.

## HKIA arrival, Kowloon transit, and the umbrella

### Verified facts

HKIA gives the arrival sequence as immigration, baggage reclaim, customs, then
the Arrivals Hall. Checked baggage is collected after immigration and before
customs. [HKIA, *Arrivals Passenger
Guide*](https://www.hongkongairport.com/en/passenger-guide/arrivals/)

HKIA says the Airport Express platform is reached by a walkway outside the
Arrivals Halls with no change of level. Its accessibility guide separately
places bus and taxi access after the Meeters and Greeters Hall. [HKIA, *Special
Needs Access—Arrivals*](https://www.hongkongairport.com/en/passenger-guide/arrivals/special-needs-access.page)

The MTR timetable effective 1 June 2026 gives a 22-minute journey between
Kowloon Station and Airport Station. The first city-bound train from the
airport is listed at 05:54 and the last at 00:48; MTR warns that exact times are
subject to change. [MTR, *Airport Express
Timetable*](https://www.mtr.com.hk/en/customer/services/timetable_index.html)
MTR's current 2026 Airport Express material describes trains as departing about
every ten minutes. [MTR, *Airport Express 50% off JoyYou Card
Offer*](https://www.mtr.com.hk/en/customer/tickets/ael-joyyou.html)

Kowloon Station is a specific West Kowloon station, not shorthand for every
origin in Kowloon; MTR describes it as served by onward public transport and
integrated facilities. [MTR, *Kowloon Airport Express
Station*](https://www.mtr.com.hk/en/customer/services/aestations_kln.html)

### Project implications

- `22 minutes` is the train segment, not a landing-to-campus duration. A
  credible pickup also needs Priscilla's travel to Kowloon Station (or another
  departure point), a possible train wait, airport circulation, and Lavinia's
  variable immigration/baggage/customs time.
- If Priscilla takes Lavinia's heavier checked bag, stage that action in or
  after the public Arrivals/Meeters and Greeters area. Priscilla cannot
  ordinarily take it from the baggage hall before Lavinia clears customs.
- A request sent while Lavinia is still completing arrival formalities can let
  those processes overlap with Priscilla's trip. A request sent only after
  Lavinia reaches the public hall creates a more conspicuous wait. Neither
  version supports Priscilla appearing immediately.
- Keep the long umbrella closed in the terminal, on the platform, and in the
  train unless an exact exposed threshold is established. Its practical payoff
  can occur at a taxi or bus transfer, station exit, campus approach, or other
  outdoor segment. The official sources do not identify which onward segment
  the fictional campus requires.
- The umbrella request remains physically useful even if the pickup itself is
  indoors: rain may be visible outside, forecast for the onward leg, or have
  already begun elsewhere. The source facts do not determine Lavinia's motive;
  the emotional pretext remains a story decision.
- Same-day arrival followed by campus activity is possible in principle, but
  the exact flight arrival time, clearance delay, campus location, and event
  slot must be fixed before assigning a credible clock time. Avoid wording such
  as “twenty-two minutes after landing.”

## University and conservatoire open-event operations

### Verified facts

Late October is demonstrably active teaching term in at least one current Hong
Kong university calendar: CUHK's 2025–26 first term ran from 1 September through
29 November, with its undergraduate Information Day on 11 October. [CUHK
Registry, *Full-time Undergraduate Programmes—Teaching
Terms*](https://www.res.cuhk.edu.hk/general-information/almanac/university-almanac-2025-26/full-time-undergraduate-programmes-teaching-terms/)

HKU held its 2025 undergraduate Information Day on Saturday 25 October from
09:00 to 18:00 across its campuses. Its official programme highlights included
mock lectures, laboratory visits, interactive workshops, and teacher/student
sharing, with Cantonese, English, and Mandarin listed. [HKU Admissions, *HKU
Information Day for UG Admissions
2025*](https://admissions.hku.hk/events/hku-information-day-ug-admissions-2025)

HKBU held its 2025 Information Day on Saturday 4 October from 10:00 to 17:30 at
its Kowloon Tong campus, advertising more than 100 activities and talks. The
creative-arts programme included a piano masterclass, recording-studio
workshop, film-scoring workshop, student/faculty interaction, facility access,
and a shuttle from Kowloon Tong MTR station. [HKBU, *Information Day 2025 press
release*](https://www.hkbu.edu.hk/en/whats-new/press-release/2025/0925hkbu-information-day-to-feature-latest-undergraduate-programmes-and-admissions-information-with-jockey-club-campus-of-creativity-to-open-to-the-public-for-the-first-time.html)

The Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts (HKAPA) held an Info Day on Saturday
14 October 2023 with all six Schools and supporting units involved, serving
onsite and online prospective students. [HKAPA, *Info Day 2023—A Heartfelt Event
to Those Love Performing Arts*](https://www.hkapa.edu/srce/news/ID2023) Its
separately branded 2026 Open Day was a one-day Sunday public event with more
than 100 activities, including concerts, workshops, backstage visits, sets,
props, costumes, technical work, and stage/project-management discussion.
[HKAPA, *Open Day 2026*](https://www.hkapa.edu/event/hkapa-open-day2026)

### Project implications

- Programme-copy revisions, room allocation, visitor routes, rehearsal or
  workshop preparation, staff/student briefings, accessibility planning,
  equipment checks, and a late public-facing schedule are all ordinary work.
  They can exist without inventing a special mystery mechanism.
- Real examples strongly support a concentrated Saturday or Sunday public
  event. To make a seven-day `Open Week` feel ordinary, define most days as
  preparation, internal run-throughs, classes, room turnover, or staged public
  sessions rather than seven identical all-day open houses.
- A performing-arts strand can credibly expose rehearsal rooms, backstage
  areas, recording spaces, and student work, but access should be scheduled and
  supervised rather than implying every restricted room is continuously open.
- Student/faculty labour and ordinary support units are visible in the official
  examples. A roster, programme proof, internal version, room check, or shuttle
  plan can therefore carry story evidence without requiring an individuated
  administrator to appear onscreen.
- `Information Day`, `Info Day`, and `Open Day` are all evidenced names.
  `Open Week` is still a fictional institutional name in this research set. Do
  not present it as a standard Hong Kong-wide term unless a separate primary
  source is found.

## Confidence and limitations

**High confidence:** the HKO monthly normals; HKIA's order of arrival procedures;
the same-level Airport Express access; the current MTR station-to-station time;
and the dates/activities stated by the named institutions.

**Moderate confidence:** the scene-blocking implications above. They are
physically conservative, but exact plausibility depends on facts not yet fixed:
the story year and weekday, flight and terminal arrival time, immigration and
baggage delays, Priscilla's starting location, the fictional campus location,
the exposed part of the onward route, and which Open Week day is public.

**Not established by this pass:** an exact door-to-door airport/campus time;
rain at a specific story hour; the umbrella's dimensions or airline handling;
the precise public-holiday pattern for an unfixed year; a standard Hong Kong
`Open Week`; or the fictional institution's authority and room-access rules.
Those details should remain unclaimed until the relevant date, route, and
institutional model are selected.
